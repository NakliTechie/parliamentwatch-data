Pattukottai for Railway Lines were commissioned at the time of the British, more than 90 years back and still hasn?t been taken up by the Railway Board. This will also enable the railways to provide more rail services to this region and to provide freight services to the farmers in the delta region. These railway lines should be commissioned at the earliest to ease the travel of the people of the region. Also, Villupuram ? Thanjavur Section of Main Line should be doubled as the line has already attained saturation and is running at more than 100% of track capacity, leading to numerous delays. Moreover, A New Vande Bharat Train from Thanjavur Jn to Mysore should be introduced to connect these two cultural and heritage cities and cater to the increased demand of this rail route.
