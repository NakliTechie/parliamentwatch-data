Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office (DMEO) 
 
5.
 
The Committee noted that the Development Monitorin
g and Evaluation Office

programmes and initiatives. DMEO was mandated with monito

discussions held quarterly by PMO to review the various sectors, with no rol

fiscal and the same appeared to be in line with the expansive work profile proposed to be

8
-
2019 was submitted to the Dept. of

d.

ial unintended consequences of each of the schemes have been

e.

-
19
.

NITI Aayog has also developed a dashbaord for FY 2018
-
19 Outcome monitoring
