II.   The existing companies, which were paying tax at the highest rate of 30 % 
(effective rate of tax 34.94%) and have opted for the concessional tax regime of 22%

boost in demand.  The results of these measures are expected to be visible in the 
medium to long run. 
 
III
.   As the rate of MAT has been reduced even for existing companies which do not 
avail of the concessional tax regime (from 18.5% to 15%), they will also get the

IV.   In case of new manu
facturing companies, the concessional tax rate of 15% 
(effective tax rate of 17.16%) is expected to boost fresh investment in manufacturing 
which will also stimulate demand.   The new manufacturing companies are required

year and coming financial years.

-

rs.

not
ed that, the exemptions and deductions available to individuals/HUF under the 
Act have not been withdrawn.
 
VIII.  As has already been mentioned earlier, the proposed new concessional tax

IX.   The new concessional taxation regime will not disincentivize growth of financial
