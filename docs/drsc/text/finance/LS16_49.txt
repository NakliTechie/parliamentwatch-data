for their livelihood. As per the latest survey of NSSO, about 31% of the rural house

As regards term loans for farmers, the Committee` are of the view that the 
instrument of KCCs can be modified and used for this purpose by providing enhanced 
credit, say five or six times the crop loan, after every five years or so. Farmers can then 
be per

conveniently linked to land records, Jandhan Yojana bank account; Aadhaar etc. Such a 
wid
ening of KCC ambit will not only enhance access of farmers to institutional credit but 
also boost the confidence of financial institutions with regard to the borrowers
.
