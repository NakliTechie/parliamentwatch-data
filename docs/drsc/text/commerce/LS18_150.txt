2.   During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Committee ha s noted some cases 
where there has been a considerable time lag between the date of notification of the Order 
and the date of its laying. The annexed statement also indicates the period of delay in 
cases where notifications have not been laid as per the Co mmittee’s guidelines and 
whether such notifications were accompanied by a statement indicating the reasons for 
the delay in laying. In some of the cases the delay has been quite abnormal. The 
observations/recommendations of the Committee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows:-    
  
(i)  Ministry of Agriculture 

    (Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying) 
  
         The Committee notes that the laying of   notification at Sl. No.7 of the  
Annexure by   the Ministry of Agricultur e (Department of Animal Husbandry and 
Dairying)  has been delayed   by 6 months.   Having been  notified on the 19 th February, 
2003 the regulation could have been laid during the Budget Session itself. The Ministry 
have even failed to append the delay statement to the notification.  The Committee hopes 
that the Ministry of Agriculture (Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying) would 
adhere to the time schedule as recommended by it in the matter of laying of 
the  Subordinate Legislation pertaining to their Department.  
  
(ii)     Ministry of Civil Aviation 
  
      The Committee regrets to note that the Ministry of Civil Aviation have delayed the 
laying of one of their notifications (Sl. no. 9) out of the 10 notifications laid by them during 
the 199 th  session.  The Committee recommends that the Ministry   of Civil Aviation 
should stick to the time schedule as recommended by the it in its 131 st  Report in laying 
the Subordinate Legislation before the Parliament. 
  
(iii)       Ministry of Communications and Information  Technology 
  
            The Committee regrets to find that out of the 5 notifications laid by the Ministry 
of Communications and Information Technology, there has been  a delay in two cases (Sl. 
Nos.32 & 33 of the Annexure) and no delay statement has been appended therewith.  Two 
notifications (Sl. 35 & 36) laid by the Ministry of Communication and Information 
Technology bear identical notification Nos.. Such notifications in future should bear the 
distinct  Nos.35 and 36 for easier identi fication. The Committee expects the Ministry of 
Communications and Information Technology, etc. to exercise caution in such   matters 
for future and ensure that the schedule as prescribed by it in the matter of laying of 
subordinate legislation framed under various Acts of Parliament is followed meticulously. 
  
(iv)       Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution 
  
            The Committee is unhappy to note that the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food 
& Public Distribution have delayed the laying of 3 of their notifications  (Sl.No. 37, 41 & 
42) out of the 8 notifications laid by them during the Session. The Ministry have not 
appended delay statement with two notifications which are  at Sl. no.   37 and 41. The 
Committee would like to draw attention of  the Ministry towards the schedule  prescribed 
by it in the matter of laying of Subordinate Legislation and it hopes that this would be 
complied with in future. 
  
(v)        Ministry of Environment & Forests 
  
      The Committee notes that the Ministry of Environment and Forests have delayed 
laying of 1 of their notifications (Sl. no 49) out of the 7 notifications (Sl. nos. 46 to 52) laid 
by them during the session and no delay statements had been appended therewith. The 
Committee hopes that the Ministry of Environment and Forests would observe the time 
schedule prescribed by it for laying of Subordinate Legislation in future.  
  
  
  
  

  
(vi)                   Ministry of Finance 
                         
            The Committee is unhappy to no te that the Ministry of Finance have delayed 
laying of 33of their notifications (Sl. No. 56, 63, 65, 78-99, 103, 170,171 and 233-236),  out 
of 236 notifications laid by them during the 199 th Session.  The above notifications has 
been delayed by the period ranging from 4 month to 2 years and 7 months.  The Ministry 
of Finance have not appended delay statement  in some of the cases.  In this connection it 
may be mentioned here that there was   delay in laying in case of 77 and 47 notification 
laid during the 197th & 198th Session respectively.  Attention of the Ministry is also invited 
to the G.S.R. 115 (E)   dated the 21 st February, 2003 and G.S.R. 217 (E) dated the 17 th 
March, 2003 (Sl. No. 170 and 171 respectively).  Both the notifications bear identical short 
title.  It would be more appropriate if such notifications are distinguished by numbering 
as ‘first amendment’ and ‘second amendment’ and so on. The Committee obse rved that 
the Ministry of Finance do not strictly adhere to the time schedule prescribed in its 135th 
Report.   The Committee hopes that the Ministry of Finance would make special efforts 
to avoid  such instances of delay in future. 
  
(vii)      Ministry of Home Affairs 
  
            The Committee regrets to note that the Ministry of Home Affairs have 
delayed  laying of two notifications (Sl. No. 305 and306) out of   twelve notifications laid 
during the Session. The Ministry have failed to enclose the delay statement  except one 
notifications at Sl. No. 306 of the Annexure.    The Committee hopes that the Ministry of 
Home Affairs shall in future comply with the schedule prescribed by the Committee in 
this behalf in its 131st Report presented to House on 15th December, 2000. 
  
  
(viii)      Ministry of Human Resource & Development 
            (Department of Elementary Education and Literacy) 
  
            The Committee regret to note that the Ministry of Human Resource Development 
(Department of Elementary Education and Literacy) have again delayed laying of   two 
out of three notifications (Sl.nos. 307 & 308) laid during the Session by more than 5 
months and no delay statement is appended therewith. The Committee impressed upon 
the Ministry of Human Resource Development (Department of Elementary Education 
and Literacy) to pay adequate attention towards   the timely laying   of the subordinate 
legislations. 
  
  
 (ix)      Ministry of Labour 
  
            The Committee notes with regret the delay of 4 months in laying of one notification 
(Sl. no. 313) out of 3 notifications laid by the Ministry.   The Ministry have also not 
appended the delay statement.   The Committee    hopes that the Ministry would take 
precaution to avoid recurrence of such instances. 
  
(x)        Ministry of Law & Justice 
  
The Committee notes with regret that out of two notifications laid during the 
Session, there is delay of almost 6 years in case of one notification which is not justified. 

(Sl. no. 314). The Committee hopes that the Ministry  shall adhere to the time schedule as 
recommended by it for laying  the Subordinate Legislation before the Parliament.  
  
(xi)       Ministry of  Personnel, Public Grievances & Pension 
  
            The Committee notes with regret that the Ministry of Personnel, Public 
Grievances & Pension have laid 6 notifications during the  session out of which 3  (Sl. Nos. 
316 to 318) have been delayed by a period  of 4 months.  Further,  the Ministry have not 
appended delay statement, there wer e instances of delay during the 191 st to 193 rd and 
197th Session also. The Committee expects the Ministry to be cautious in such matters and 
ensure compliance of time schedule for laying of the notifications in future.  
  
  
(xii)      Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas 
  
            The Committee regrets to note that the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas have 
laid two notifications during the 199th Session and  one of them has been delayed by more 
than 7 months (Sl. No. 322). The Committee directs the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural 
Gas to avoid recurrence of such instances in future. 
  
(xiii)      Ministry of Power 
  
The Committee notes with regret that there is delay in laying   of one  out of the 4 
notifications laid by the Ministry of Power by 4   months (Sl. No. 324) and the Ministry 
have not appended any delay statement. The Committee also finds that Sl. nos. 324 & 326 
and Sl. nos. 325 and 327 are showing similar notification numbers.   The notification 
should bear distinct numbers for easier identifica tion.  The Committee hopes that the 
Ministry would   ensure strict compliance of the schedule for laying Subordinate 
Legislation as prescribed by the Committee  in its 131st report.  
  
  
(xiv)     Ministry of Road Transport & Highways 
  
            The Committee notes that the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways  have  again delayed in laying of 18 notifications (Sl. Nos. 328 -340, 365-367 & 
393 and 394) out of the total of 77 notifications laid. The delay in laying   ranges from 3 
months to one year 4 months and they have not   appended delay statements in case of 5 
notifications (Sl. nos. 365-367,393 & 394). The Committee expects the Ministry to be more 
prompt and vigilant in laying the rules/regulations within the stipulated period to obviate 
such instances of delay in future. 
  
            Besides the above,   the Committee also notes with regret   the isolated cases of 
delayed laying of notifications by the Ministry of Urban Development and Poverty 
Alleviation (Sl. No. 410 of the Annexure).   The Committee would like the Ministries to 
take note of the time schedule which is reproduced hereinafter and ensure that the same 
is complied with meticulously in    future.  The Committee, accordingly,   reiterates its 
observations made in its 135th Report. 
  
  
“The Committee has, time and again, emphasized that it is essential that in case there is 
a delay in the laying of Statutory Orders, the concerned Ministry/Department should 
invariably append a delay statement along with the notifications being laid so as to 

apprise Parliament of the causes of delay. The Committee desires all 
Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the intention is that through the 
process of formulation of a delay statement the Ministry is able to introspect, identifying 
bottlenecks and devise remedial measures”.  
  
.           The Committee impresses upon all the Ministries/Departments to take immediate 
steps to avoid delay in laying of Subordinate Legislation. The Committee, accordingly, 
wishes to bring to the notice of all Minis tries/Departments of the Government of India, 
particularly, the defaulting ones as enumerated above the following recommendations, as 
contained in its 131 st Report wherein a time schedule for the laying of Subordinate 
legislation has been prescribed,  as follows: 
  
“Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the House is not in 
Session, during the Session immediately following the date of publication of the Order in 
the Official Gazette and (ii) if the House is in Session, on the date of the publication of the 
Order, during its continuance and in case the time lag between the date of publication 
and the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the expiry of 
the Session immediately following the said Session . All the Ministries/Departments of 
Government of India should henceforth comply with this schedule in the matter of laying 
of the orders issued under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha.”
