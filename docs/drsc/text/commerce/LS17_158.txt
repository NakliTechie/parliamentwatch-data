INTRODUCTION 
  
1.   I, the Chairman of the Committee on Subordinate Legislation, having been authorized 
by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, do hereby present this  Hundred 
and Fifty Eight Report of the Committee. 
. 
1.2  The Committee considered and adopted the Report in its meeting held on         August 
23, 2005. 
  
1.3       Notifications containing Rules/Regulations, etc.   laid on the Table of the Rajya 
Sabha during its 204 th session  (February-May, 2005) have been perused by the 
Committee in the light of the provisions of the Acts under which these were framed and 
also with a view to seeing whether these have been laid properly and   within the 
prescribed time-frame.  The Committee’s observations/recommendations are contained 
in the report that follows.

2.                  During the course of scrutiny of these Notifications/Orders/Scheme, the 
Committee has noted few cases of abnormal delay ranging from 1 to 3 years between the 
date of notification of the Order and the date of the laying.   The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of delay in cases of notifications which have not been laid as per the 
schedule prescribed by the Committee and also whether such notifications were 
accompanied by any statement indicating the reason for th e delay in laying.   The 
observations/recommendations of the Committee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows: 
  
(i)                  Ministry of  Agriculture 
  
The Committee notes with regret that the Ministry of Agriculture have delayed 
the laying of 07 of their notifications (Sl. No. 01 to 06 and 09) out of the 9 notifications 
laid by them during the 204th Session.  The delay ranges between 11months to 1 year and 
7 months.  Further, the Ministry have not appended delay statement in the case of the 
notification at Sl. No.9 with a delay of 11months     The Committee recommends that 
the  Ministry of Agriculture  should strictly adhere to the time schedule as recommended 
by it in its 131st Report in laying of the Subordinate Legislation before Parliament and in 
case of any delay in laying of the notifications, a delay statement should also invariably 
be appended therewith. 
  

(ii)               Ministry of Civil Aviation 
  
The Committee notes with regret that the Ministry of Civil Aviation have delayed 
the laying of all the five notifications (Sl No 11 -15)laid by them and have not 
appended  delay statements therewith. The Committee recommends that the Ministry of 
Civil Aviation should strictly adhere to the time schedule as recommend by t he 
Committee.  The Committee recommends that the   Ministry of Civil Aviation   should 
strictly adhere to the time schedule as recommended by it in its 131 st Report in laying of 
the Subordinate Legislation before Parliament and in case of any delay in laying  of the 
notifications, a delay statement should also invariably be appended therewith. 
  
(iii)       Ministry of Commerce & Industry 
  
The Committee is unhappy to note that the Ministry of Commerce & Industry 
have delayed laying of 4 notification (Sl Nos. 16,21,23) out of 14 notification laid by them 
during the session. Out of above, they have not appended delay statement alongwith  the 
three notification (Sl. Nos. 21, 23).   The Committee also notes that the notific ation at Sl. 
No. 26 has been notified   in the official   Gazette without allotting any distinctive 
number.  The Committee therefore recommends that the Ministry of Commerce & 
Industry should make all out effaces to adhere to the time schedule prescribed for  the 
purpose and in case of delay in laying of any notified a delay statement must be appended 
therewith . The Committee also recommends that each notification shall be allotted 
distinctive number while notifying in the Gazette    
  
(iv)       Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution 
                  
           The Committee regrets to note that the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & 
Public Distribution have delayed the laying of 8 of their notifications (Sl. No. 46  to 
48,53,56-59) out of the 17 notifications laid by them during the 204th  session. Four of such 
notifications at Sl. Nos. 53, 56,57 and 58 have not been accompanied by delay statements. 
The Committee recommends that the Ministry   of Consumer Affairs, Foo d & Public 
Distribution should stick to the time schedule as recommended by the it in its 131st  Report 
in laying the Subordinate Legislation before the Parliament. 
  
(v)        Ministry of Finance:  
  
            The Committee regrets to note that the Ministry Finance have delayed laying   of 
13 notifications (Sl. Nos75-76,88-94,144,171, and 185-186) out of the 183 notifications laid 
during the session. There was also delay in laying of 11 notifications during the previous 
session .Out of 13 notifications delayed in this session there were 8 cases where delay 
statement was not appended therewith (Sl. No. 88-94 and 171). The Committee hopes that 
the Ministry of Finance would make more effects to avoid any delay in laying of the 
notification and in case of an y delay in   laying thereof, a delay statement shall be 
appended  therewith. 
  
(vi)                      Ministry of Human Resource Development 
  
                 The Committee regrets to note that the Ministry of Human Resource 
Development have abnormally delayed laying of 24 out of 31 notifications (Sl. Nos. 
283,284,286 to 301,307 to 311 and 313 laid during the Session by more than 6 months and 
no delay statements were appended along with 17 notifications. The Committee also notes 
that the notification at Sl. No 308 has been notified without giving any specific 

number.  The Committee recommends that the Ministry of Human Resource 
Development should take necessary corrective steps in the right earnest so that they are 
able to lay their Subordinate Legislatio n on time.  The Committee further recommends 
that the Ministry should invariably apprise the House about the reasons for delay by 
appending a delay statement in case there was any delay in laying thereof. The Committee 
also recommends that   the Ministry sh all take all precautions in allotting a distinctive 
number to each of the notification while notifying  it in the official Gazette. 
  
(vii)             Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport & Highways   
  
                 The Committee is not happy to note undue delay in laying   of 38 notifications 
(Sl Nos.343 to 352,356,388 -392,406,408,415-422,439-447,475,476 and 481)out of 146 
notifications laid during this session and  no delay statements were appended  along with 
three notifications (Sl.Nos. 444,475 and 476). The Committee recommends that   the 
Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport   and Highways   shall make all out efforts   for 
avoiding any delay  in laying of the notifications and in case of any delay in laying thereof, 
a delay statement shall be appended therewith. 
  
3.         The Committee also notes that there has been delay in laying of some notifications 
each by the Ministries of   Company Affairs (Sl. No.43), Environment & Forests 
(Sl.No.71), Health of Family Welfare (Department of Health Sl. Nos. 258 ,259, Home 
Affairs (Sl. No.276), Information   and Broadcasting (Sl. No. 314), Personnel, Public 
Grievances and Pensions (Sl. No. 319), Railways (Sl.No 333), Urban Employment and 
Poverty Alleviation (Sl.No.487) .Further notifications at Sl.No.9,11 to15, 21 to 23, 43, 258, 
259, 276, were not accompanied by delay statements.   The Committee hopes that the 
Ministries shall be particular  in  timely laying of the notifications  and a delay statement 
in case the laying of subordinate legislation is delayed. 
  
4.Besides, the timely laying of Subordinate Legislation, the Committee has been 
consistently reiterating that each notification should have a distinctive number so that 
the same is identifiable.   However, during the 204 th Session ,the Committee has come 
across three instances where the following notifications were not numbered:- (i) Ministry 
of Commerce and Industry (Sl. No.26(ii) Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) 
at Sl.No.147(iii) Ministry of Human Resource Development (Department of Secondary & 
Higher Education) at Sl. No.308. As regard the notification pertaining to the Ministry of 
Shipping, Road Transport & Highways (Department of Shipping), both 
the  notifications  at Sl. Nos.475 & 476 bear the same notification number and date 
but  publish differ ent notifications.    The Committee reiterates that every notification 
should have a distinct number to give it a separate identity.  The Committee recommends 
that such instances should not be repeated and Ministries/Departments should ensure 
that every not ifications is assigned a distinct number so that the same is identifiable at 
any later stage. 
  
5 .The Committee at the end  impresses upon all the Ministries/Departments to take 
immediate steps to avoid delay in laying of Subordinate Legislation and brings  to their 
notice the following recommendations, as contained in its 131st 135th and 152nd  Reports: 
  
“Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the House is not 
in Session, during the Session immediately following the date of publication of the 
Order in the Official Gazette and (ii) if the House is in Session, on the date of the 
publication of the Order, during its continuance and in case the time lag between 
the date of publication and the date of the closing of the Session is less  than 15 

clear days, before the expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. 
All the Ministries/Departments of Government of India should henceforth comply 
with this schedule in the matter of laying of the orders issued under various Acts 
of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha.” 
(131st Report) 
  
"The Committee has, time and again, emphasized that it is essential that in case 
there is a delay in the laying of Statutory Orders, the concerned 
Ministry/Department should invariably append a delay statement along with the 
notifications being laid so as to apprise Parliament of the causes of delay. The 
Committee desires all Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the 
intension is that through the process of formulation of a delay statement the 
Ministry is able to introspect, identifying bottlenecks and devise remedial 
measures". 
(135th Report) 
  
“In the opinion of the Committee, every notification should be assigned a 
distinctive number  in order to give it a separate identity and similarly, no 
notification should be published/laid which does not carry a notification number. 
The Committee expects all Ministries/Departments of Government of India to 
exercise caution in this regard. In case where there are more than one notifications 
under a single file, the Ministries/Departments should ensure that the file number 
on each notification should be suitably distinguished from one another”. 
        
(152nd Report)
