2. During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Committee has noted 
some cases where there has been a cons iderable time lag between the date of 
notification of the Order and the date of its laying. The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of dela y in cases where notifications  have not been laid as 
per the schedule prescribed by the Committee and where such notifications 
were accompanied by a statement indicating the reasons for the delay in laying. 
In some of the cases the delay has been quite long. The 
observations/recommendations of the Co mmittee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows: -    
  
(i) Ministry of Agriculture (Department of Agriculture & 
Cooperation) 
  
 The Ministry of Agriculture (Department of Agriculture & 
Cooperation) have laid 7 no tifications out of which 2 (Sl. Nos. 1 to 2) have 
been delayed. Out of those none of th e  delayed notifications have been 
accompanied by any delay statements. The delay ranges from 1 year 5 months 
to 1 year 10 months.  
 
  

: 3 : 
 
 The Committee notes with regret that  the Ministry of Agriculture 
have made inordinate delays in laying  of notifications as set out by the 
Committee in its recommendations. There is no delay statement appended 
to know why such delay has occurred . The Committee impresses upon the 
Ministry to ensure timely laying of notifications and where there is delay, 
delay statements should invariably be appended therewith. 
 
(ii) (Ministry of Civil Aviation)  
 
The Ministry of Civil Aviation have laid five notifications out of which 
one notification has been delayed.  Th e delay period extend s upto 1 year and 
one month.  There is no delay statement  appended either. 
 
 The Committee expresses its concern over such inordinate delay in 
laying of notification a nd urges upon the Ministry of  Civil Aviation to 
avoid extreme delays. A de lay statement should invariably be appended 
where laying of subordinate legislation is delayed. 
 
(iii) Ministry of Communicati on and Information Technology 
 
 (Department of Telecommunication) 
 
Department of Telecommunication ha ve laid 2 notifications one of 
which has been delayed. The delay ra nges upto five months and no delay 
statement have been attached. 
 
The Committee impresses upon the Ministry of Communication 
and Information Technology (Department of Telecommunications) to 
evolve suitable mechanism to avoid su ch delays and also to append delay 
statements where there has been a delay, in laying of the notifications. 
 
(iv) Ministry of Consumer Affairs,  Food and Public Distribution  
 
(Department of Consumer Affairs)  
 
Department of Consumer Affairs have  laid five notifications four of 
which have been delayed. The delay period ranged from four to five months.  
There is no delay statement  appended with the delayed notifications. 
 
 The Committee reiterates its recommendation that in case of delay 
in laying, notifications should be a ccompanied by a  delay statement. The 
Ministry should take care that repet ition of such delay is avoided in 
future.  
 

: 4 : 
 
(v) Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
 
  Ministry of Corporate Affairs have laid 11 notifications out of which  4 
notifications  have been delayed by two to three months. These have been 
accompanied with delay statements. The reason for delay given by the 
Ministry is as under:- 
 
 “The notifications could not be laid before both the Houses of 
Parliament within the prescribed period as while the Ministry was in process of 
authenticating and laying the notification in both the Houses of Parliament it 
was adjourned sine die before its due date of adjournment.” 
 
  The Committee observes that sessions are declared much in 
advance and had the Ministry of Corp orate Affairs taken prompt steps 
for laying of the notifications, th e delay could have been avoided. It 
appears to have wanted till last  movement. The Committee recommends 
that the Ministry should take the fi rst available opportunity for laying of 
their notifications during the session. 
 
(vi)  Ministry of Defence 
 
  The only notification that the Ministry  of Defence have laid has been 
delayed by seven months without any delay statement being appended 
therewith. 
 
  The Committee emphasizes that the Mi nistry should take steps to 
avoid delay in laying of notifications and where there is delay the 
notifications should be accompanied by delay statement/explaining the 
reasons therefore. 
 
(vii) Ministry of Finance 
 
 (Department of Economic Affairs) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Departmen t of Economic Affairs) have laid 8 
notifications out of which  2 notifications have not been laid within time. There 
has been a delay of four to five mont hs in laying of notifications. No delay 
statement is appended with either of these notifications. 
  
 The Committee recommends that the Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Economic Affairs) should evolve an effective mechanism 
to obviate possibility of such delays and in case of delay in laying of 
notifications, delay statements should invariably be attached with them.

 (viii)-A       Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services) 
  
 The Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services) have laid 3 
notifications out of which  one notifica tion have been delayed by five months. 
There is no delay statement appended with the notification. 
 
 The Committee desires that the Mi nistry of Finance (Department 
of Financial Services) wo uld take action accordingly, in order to comply 
with the prescribed schedule in the matter of laying of Statutory 
Notifications. 
  
(viii)-B       Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Departm ent of Revenue) have laid 66 
notifications and 4 of them have been delayed, with delay of 4 months to 1 
year and 1 month. There is no de lay statement  appended with two 
notifications and two notifications have been accompanied with delay 
statements. The reasons given by the Mini stry for delayed  notifications that 
the Ministry is involved in the Budget making exercise relating to the Direct 
Taxes. 
  
 Though the Ministry of Finan ce appended delay statements with 
two notifications, rest two other notifications were laid without and 
proper delay statements which w ere delayed for 4 and 5 months 
respectively. The  Committee  feels that though the delay period is 
negligible even then Ministry should invariably append the delay 
statement. However, the Committee is happy to note that the Ministry of 
Finance has appended delay statement with 62 notifications and the 
reasons given therein one quite convincing. 
 
(ix) Ministry of Health & Family Welfare 
(Department of Health & Department of Health & Family Welfare) 
 
 The Department of Health have la id 10 notifications out of which 7 
have been delayed, 3 notifications out of delayed notifica tions have no delay 
statement upended with them and four  notifications are accompanied with 
delay statement. The delay period extends from five months to eleven months. 
  
Department of Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, 
Homeopathy have laid one notificati on which has been delayed by four 
months. There is delay statement appended with it.

The Committee directs the Ministry of  Health & Family Welfare 
(Department of Health) to take correct ive measures to obviate such delay 
in future and in case such delays beco me unavoidable  in future, it should 
invariably be accompanied by statemen ts giving the reasons for delay in 
laying. 
 
(x)      Ministry of Home Affairs  
 
The Ministry of Home Affairs have laid 16 notifications. 3 of these 
have been delayed. The delay period ranges from 7 months to 1 year & 3 
month. All 3 notifications have been attached with delay statement. The 
reasons for delay given is as under:- 
 
The Notification G.S.R. 228 (E) and G.S.R. 255 (E) were published in 
the Gazette of India (Extraordinary) Part -II, Section 3(i). The rules could not 
be laid down before each House of Parl iament in the Budget Session due to 
adjournment of both the Houses. 
 
 The Committee recommends that Mi nistry should take the first 
available opportunity for laying the st atutory Notifications and, should 
not postpone this work for the later part of the session so that the 
eventualities like early prorogation of the House, which is of course rare, 
could be avoided. 
 
(xi) Ministry of Labour and Employment 
 
 (Directorate General of Employment and Training) 
   
 The Directorate General of Empl oyment and Training have laid 3 
notifications and one of these have b een delayed by 5 months. There is no 
delay statement appended with the notification. 
  
 The Committee invites attention to  its recommendations made in 
135
th Report, wherein it is laid down that it is essential to append a 
statement in case of delay in laying  of statutory orders. The Committee 
therefore, impresses upon the Ministry of Labour and Employment to 
strictly adhere to the time schedule in  laying of notifications and should 
appended delay statement in case of any delay.

The Committee recommends that Mi nistry should take the first

are taken care of.

 “Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the 
House is not in Session, during the Se ssion immediately following the date 
of publication of the Order in the Offici al Gazette and (ii) if the House is 
in Session, on the date of the pub lication of the Order, during its 
continuance and in case the time l ag between the date of publication and 
the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the 
expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Governme nt of India should henceforth 
comply with this schedule in the matt er of laying of the Orders issued 
under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha. 
(131st Report) 
(Presented on 15 December 2000)

statement along with the notificati ons being laid so as to apprise 
Parliament of the causes of delay. The Committee desires all 
Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the intension is that 
through the process of formulation of a delay statement the Ministry is 
able to introspect, identify bottlenecks and devise remedial measures”.
