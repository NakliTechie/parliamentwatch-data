2.  While scrutinizing these Orders, the Committee noted that there was a considerable time lag 
between issue  of Notifications and their laying in  some cases. The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of delay in cases where Notifi cations have not been laid as per the time 
schedule prescribed by the Committee. The Committee  observed that out of the total 200 delayed 
Notifications, there were 115 Notifications where n o delay statement was appended to. However, 
the bulk of delayed Notifications were from the Min istry of  Road Transport and Highways which 
comes to around 74.5% of the total delayed Notifications. 
 
The observations/recommendations of the Committee i n respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments where delay in laying of Notifications has been noticed, are as follows: -  
 
(i) Ministry of Commerce and Industry 
 (Department of Commerce; & Department of Industrial Policy and  Promotion) 
  
The Ministry of Commerce and Industry (Department o f Commerce) have laid 9 
Notifications (Sl. Nos. 11-19) out of which 2 Notif ications have been delayed by 4 months each. 
These Notifications (Sl. Nos. 
14-15) have no delay statement attached.  
Department of Industrial Policy and Promotion have laid 3 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 20  –  
22). Out of these 2 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 21 – 22 ) have been delayed. The delay period ranges 
from 4 months to 8 months and there is  no delay st atement  appended to both of the delayed 
Notifications. 
 

 The Committee find that Ministry of Commerce and I ndustry (Department of 
Commerce & Department of Industrial Policy and Prom otion) have not only delayed the 
laying of Notifications but also failed to appended  delay statement to that. The Committee, in 
the absence of delay statements, are not able to kn ow the reasons behind this delay in laying 
of Notifications. The Committee have clearly mentio ned in its 131 st  Report that delay 
statements should invariably be appended where ther e is delay in laying of Notifications. The 
Committee, therefore, impress upon the Ministry of Commerce and Industry to strictly 
adhere to the time schedule as prescribed in Committee’s Report and in case of any delay in a 
laying, delay statement should invariably be appended thereto. 
 
(ii)  Ministry of Communications and Information Technology 
 (Department of Telecommunications) 
 
Only one  Notification that the Ministry of Communi cations and Information Technology 
(Department of Telecommunications) (Sl. No. 24) hav e laid is delayed by 5 months and there is no 
delay statement appended thereto. 
     
 The Committee are not happy over the delay of 5 mon ths on the part of the Ministry. 
Further there is no reason given as to what caused this delay. The Committee therefore, 
impress upon the Ministry to  take care so that suc h instances of delay do not recur in future 
and in case of any delay in laying,  a delay statement should invariably be appended. 
 
(iii) Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public  Distribution 
 (Department of Food and Public Distribution)  
 
 Ministry of Consumer Affairs  (Department of Food and Public Distribution) have laid 3 
Notifications (Sl. Nos. 28 to 30). Only 1 Notificat ion (Sl. No. 29) has been delayed by 4 Months 
and there is no delay statement appended to it.  
  
   The Committee notes that there has been delay in la ying of one Notification and no 
delay statement has been attached to it. The Commit tee reiterates its recommendation 
contained in its 135 
th  Report that in case of delay in laying of Notifica tions etc., invariably a 
delay statement should be attached. The Ministry should ensure such compliance in future.  
 

 
(iv) Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate C hange 
 
Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change  have laid 8 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 
44 to 51) on the Table of the House during the Sess ion. Three of  them  (Sl. Nos. 44, 47 - 48) have 
been delayed by 4 to 8 months. One Notification (Sl . No. 47) has delay statement appended to it 
whereas no delay statement  was appended with other  two notifications (Sl. Nos. 44 & 48). The 
reason forwarded by the Ministry for delay is as under:- 
 
“Laying of Gazette Notification No. S.O. 3111 (E) d ated 10 
th  December, 2014 bringing in 
an amendment to Rule 3 of the National Board for Wi ldlife Rules, regarding enabling continuity of 
the tenure of the members nominated in accordance to Rule 3(4) of the National Board for Wildlife 
Rules, 2003, till such time the nomination of the n ew members comes into effect, on the Table of 
both Houses of Parliament. 
The notifications could not be laid in the Budget S ession of Parliament 2015, as it was the 
first full Budget Session of the new Government and  several issues of the Ministry were being 
considered during the Session. Besides, the Ministr y was also actively involved in the process of 
withdrawal of the Wild Life (Protection) Bill, 2013  and matter regarding laying of this notification 
was inadvertently missed.” 
 
The Committee notes the reason given by the Ministr y of Environment, Forests and 
Climate Change that caused delay in laying of the N otifications. The Committee, however, 
emphasises that tabling of Gazette Notification is of utmost public importance and it is prime 
responsibility of the Ministry to discharge its duty to Parliament and to the public. 
 The Committee, therefore,  impresses upon the Mini stry to adhere to the time 
schedule and in case, there is a delay in the layin g of any Statutory Notification, the same 
should be accompanied by a delay statement while it  is being laid. The Ministry should 
clarify the reasons for delay in laying and also wh y the delay statement has not been 
appended to the Notifications. 
  
(v) Ministry of Finance 
(a)    (Department of Economic Affairs) 
 

Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs ) have laid 18 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 
53 to 70) out of which 7 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 53   – 55, 66 & 68  – 70)  have been delayed by 4 to 
11 months. Moreover, there is  no delay statement  appended to any of these notifications so 
delayed.  
 
The Committee takes a serious view not only of the delay in laying of seven 
Notifications (by the Department of Economic Affair s) on the Table of the House but also for 
its failure to append the required delay statement.  The Ministry/Department must know 
about the possible implications of failing to meet this requirement that tantamounts to 
diluting Executive’s accountability to Parliament. It must ensure to append a delay statement 
in all such cases in future. 
 
(b)    (Department of Financial Services) 
 
Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services) have laid 33 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 
71 to 103), of which 13 Notifications have been del ayed.  Out of these 13 delayed Notifications,  2  
Notifications (Sl. Nos. 98 to 99) have  been delaye d each by 4 months with delay statement 
appended thereto. Whereas in the 11 Notifications w hich have been delayed  by 4 to 7 months, 
there is  no delay statement attached. The reasons forwarded by the Ministry for delay is as under:- 
 
“At the time of publication of Notifications Nos. 2 69 (E) and G.S.R. 274 (E) of the 
insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (Sal ary and Allowance payable to and other 
Terms Conditions of Service of Chairperson and othe r Members) Rules, 2000, in the official 
Gazette of India, the Parliament was not in session. In the subsequent sessions of the Parliament the 
Notifications were inadvertently not laid on the Table of the Houses due to an oversight.” 
 
 The Committee cannot accept the reply given by the Ministry of Finance (Department 
of Financial Services)  that the Notifications could not be laid on time due to oversight. It only 
reflects the lack of seriousness on the part of the  Ministry in fulfilling its constitutional 
responsibility to Parliament. The Committee expects  the Ministry to be more careful about 
laying of its papers and see that persons responsible for this are held accountable. 
 
 (c)  (Department of Revenue) 
 

 Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) have la id 86 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 104 to 
189) on the Table of the House. Out of these 10 Not ifications have been delayed by 4 to 5 months 
respectively and no delay statement have been attached to any of these Notifications. 
 
 The Committee opines that had the Department of rev enue exercised a bit caution, 
they would have avoided such instances of delay as delay period is not exceptionally long. 
Further, delay statement is also not appended. The Committee, therefore,  impresses upon 
the Ministry to avoid such instances of delay and i n case there is a delay in the laying of any 
Statutory Notifications, the same should be accompa nied by a delay statement while it is  
being laid. The Departments’ attention is drawn tow ards recommendations in this regard as 
contained in 135 
th  Report of the Committee.

 Department of Health & Family Welfare have laid 3 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 192-194). All

(viii) Ministry of Human Resource Development 
  (Department of Higher Education) 
 
 The Ministry of Human Resource Development (Depart ment of Higher Education) have 
laid 2 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 224 - 225). Out of t hese 1 Notification (Sl. No. 224) has been delayed 
by 4 months with delay statement appended to it. Th e reason for delay given by the Ministry is as 
under:- 
 
 “As per  the recommendations of the Committee on P apers Laid on the Table of Lok 
Sabha/Rajya Sabha, the Rules are required to be lai d on the Tables of the Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha 
with a period of 15 days after their publication in  the Gazette, if the House is in session and if the  
House is not in Session the orders should be laid on the Table of the House as soon as possible (but 
in any case within 15 days) after the commencement of the following session. 
 However, due to lacunae leading to corrigendum, th e said rules could not be laid in the 
following sessions of Parliament. The said rules al ongwith the corrigendum notified vide G.S.R. 
244 (E) incorporating the omissions/infirmities poi nted out by the Committee on Subordinate 
Legislation will therefore be laid in the Monsoon Session, 2015 of the Parliament.” 
 
 The Committee find that delay was caused due to lac unal  in the Notification leading 
to corrigendum. The Committee observes that Ministr y have been following the practice of 
laying Notification on the first available opportun ity and laying corrigendum as and when 
published. However, in the instant case, the minist ry has waited for four months for 
publication of corrigendum and thereafter they have  laid both Notifications and 
Corrigendum simultaneously. The Committee recommend s that Ministry should lay the 
Notification at the first opportunity available and  thereafter lay the corrigendum as and 
when it has been published and not to wait for both of them to be laid together.

 3.   Committee impresses upon all the Ministries/De partments to take immediate steps to ensure

, 135

 “In addition to the concern expressed by the Commi ttee over delay in laying of 
Notifications on the Table of the House, the Commit tee also express their deep concern over 
delay in framing of rules/regulations under differe nt Acts. The Committee in its 47
th  report 
presented to the House on 14.9.1981 has recommended  that the rule-making process should 
be completed within a period of six months of the c oming into force on an Act. The 
Committee has further recommended that in case a Ministry is not able to adhere to this time 
limit, it should seek extension of time for complet ing the rule-making process. The 
recommendation of the committee reads as follows:- 
 
  “The Committee feels that the rule-making process c ould well have been 
completed within the time limit of six months which  the Committee has laid down for 
making of rules under an Act after coming into forc e of that Act. Where however, a 
Ministry does not find it possible to adhere to the  time limit, they should inform the 
Committee of the reasons why they cannot make rules  within the time limit and seek 
extension of time for completing the rule-making process.” 
 The Committee is constrained to take note of the f act that the Ministries/Departments
