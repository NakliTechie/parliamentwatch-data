INTRODUCTION 
  
      I, the Chairman of the Committee on Subordinate Legislation, having been authorized 
by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, do hereby present this Hu ndred 
and Forty Fifth  Report of the Committee. 
. 
1.2  The Committee considered and adopted the Report in its meeting held 
on  December  9, 2002 
  
1.3  The notifications containing Rules/Regulations, etc. laid on the Table of the Rajya 
Sabha during its 196th session have been perused by the Committee in the light of 
the provisions of the Acts under which these were framed and also with a view to 
seeing whether these have been laid within the prescribed time -frame and 
properly.  The Committee’s observations/ recommendations are contained in the 
report that follows.

 Report 
I 
Laying of Statutory Orders on the Table of  Rajya Sabha 
  
      During the 196 th Session of the Rajya Sabha, 78 Statutory Orders were laid on the 
Table of the House by the various Ministries/Departments.   The details of the Gazette 
Notifications in which these were published and the date of their laying on the Table of 
the Rajya Sabha have been indicated in the Ministry-wise statement placed at Annexure. 
  
2.   During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Comm ittee has noted some cases 
where there has been a considerable time lag between the date of notification of the Order 
and the date of its laying. The annexed statement also indicates the period of delay in 
cases where notifications have not been laid as pe r the Committee’s guidelines.  In some 
of the cases the delay has been quite abnormal.   The observations/ recommendations of 
the Committee in respect of specific Ministries/Departments are as follows: - 
  

I     Ministry of Commerce & Industry  
      The Committee notes that out of the five notifications laid by the Ministry of 
Commerce and Industry, there has been a delay in two cases (sl. nos. 6 and 7 
)  of  the  Annexure. The Ministry have also not enclosed the delay statement giving the 
reasons for  the delay in laying. The Committee expects the Ministry of Commerce & 
Industry to exercise caution in such matters and ensure that the   schedule as prescribed 
by the Committee in the matter of laying of Subordinate Legislation framed under 
various Acts of Parliament is followed meticulously in future . 
  
II    Ministry of Finance & Company Affairs 
  
      The Committee is unhappy over the fact that for the seventh consecutive session, 
the  Ministry  of Finance and Company Affairs are  
coming under its particular notice on account of delay in the laying of Subordinate 
Legislation on the Table of the Rajya Sabha. The Ministry have laid 30 rules/regulations 
during the 196 th Session and there has been delay in case of 27 ranging from 1 year 5 
months to 3 months as shown in the Annexure and in none of the cases, delay statement 
has been appended.  The Committee   recommends that the Ministry of Finance and 
Company Affairs  should take immediate corrective action and ensure that henceforth 
the subordinate legislation is laid before the Parliament on time. 
  
III  Ministry of Home Affairs 
  
      The Committee notes with regret that out of the eleven notifications laid by the 
Ministry of Home Affairs during the 196 th Session, there has been delay in case  of four 
(sl. nos. 54 to 56 and 58).   The Committee would like the Ministry to streamline its 
procedures so as to obviate such instances in future.  
  
IV  Ministry of Information & Broadcasting 
  
      The Committee notes with regret that there has been a delay of five months in laying 
two of the notifications (sl. nos. 61 and 63) and no delay statement was sent 
therewith.  The Committee hopes that the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting 
would take adequate steps to ensure compliance with the time sche dule as laid down by 
the Committee for the laying of Subordinate Legislation. 
  
V    Ministry of Law and Justice  
      The Committee notes with regret that there has been a delay of seven months on the 
part of the Ministry  of Law  and Justice  in laying   
their only   notification (sl. no. 64)   and  no  delay  statement  has been enclosed. The 
Committee expects the Ministry to be cautious in such matters and ensure compliance 
with the time schedule for laying of notifications in future.  
             
VI  Ministry of Urban Development & Poverty Alleviation 
  
      The Committee regrets to note that out of two notifications laid during the 196 th 
Session, one has been delayed by four months.   The Committee expects the Ministry to 
take necessary corrective action in order to ensure that such delays do not recur and the 
schedule as prescribed by the Committee in the matter of laying of Subordinate 
Legislation is followed strictly. 
  

3.   The Committee also notes with regret the delayed laying of notification at s l. No. 10 
of the Annexure by the Ministry of Civil Aviation by four months and that too without 
any delay statement.   
  
4.   The Committee impresses upon all the Ministries/Departments to take immediate 
steps to avoid delay in laying of subordinate legisl ation. The Committee, accordingly, 
wishes to bring to the notice of all Ministries/Departments of Government of India, 
particularly, the defaulting ones as enumerated above the following recommendations as 
contained in its 131 st Report wherein a time sched ule for the laying of subordinate 
legislation has been prescribed as follows: 
  
“Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid  (i) if the House is not 
in Session, during the Session immediately following the date of publication of the 
Order in the Official Gazette and (ii) if the House is in Session, on the date of the 
publication of the Order, during its continuance and in case the time lag between 
the date of publication and the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 
clear days, before the expiry of   the  Session immediately following the said 
Session.   
All the Ministries/Departments of Government of India should henceforth comply 
with this schedule in the matter of laying of the orders issued under various Acts 
of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha.” 
  
 5.  The Committee also reiterates its following observations made in its 135 th Report 
regarding appending of delay statement where there has been delay in the laying: 
  
“ The Committee has, time and again, emphasized that it is essential that in case 
there is a delay in the laying of Statutory Orders, the concerned 
Ministry/Department should invariably append a delay statement along with the 
notifications being laid so as to apprise the Parliament of the causes of delay.  The 
Committee desires all Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the 
intention is that through the process of formulation of a delay st atement the 
Ministry is able to introspect, identifying bottlenecks and devising remedial 
measures”.

2.       The Committee took up for consideration its draft 145 th Report on the Statutory 
Orders laid on the table of the Rajya Sabha during the 196 th Session.  While considering 
the Report, the Committee also had a look into the delay in the laying of Statutory Orders 
by the various Ministries/Departments during the previous five to six Sessions and it 
noted that the delay in laying had been quite persistent.   The Committee further noted 
that in majority of such cases of delay, the Ministries/ Departments had even failed to 
append a delay statement explaining the reasons for delay in the laying of 
the  notifications despite Committees’ specific  and  clear recommendations to that effect  
in its 131 st Report which had been reiterated a number of times.  The Committee, 
accordingly, decided to hear the representatives   of the Ministries of Finance and 
Company Affairs and Home Affairs on the question of persistent delay in the laying of 
their notifications. 
  
3.   The Committee, thereafter, adopted its draft 145th Report and decided to present 
it to the House on the 12th December, 2002.  The Committee authorized the Chairman 
and in his absence Shri B.P.Singhal and Shri Ravula Chandra Sekar Reddy MPs to 
present the Report. 
  
4.   The Committee also decided to meet again at 3.00 p.m. on the 9th January, 2003. 
  
5.  The meeting then adjourned at 4.30  p.m.
