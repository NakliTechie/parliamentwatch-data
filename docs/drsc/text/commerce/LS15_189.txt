2. During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Committee has noted 
some cases where there has been a cons iderable time lag between the date of 
notification of the Order and the date of its laying. The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of dela y in cases where notifications  have not been laid as 
per the schedule prescribed by the Committee and where such notifications 
were accompanied by a statement indicating the reasons for the delay in laying. 
In some of the cases the delay has been quite long. The 
observations/recommendations of the Co mmittee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows: -    
  
(i) Ministry of Agriculture (Dep artment of Animal Husbandry, 
Dairying & Fisheries) 
  
 The Ministry of Agriculture (D epartment of Animal Husbandry, 
Dairying & Fisheries) have  laid one  notification (S l. No. 12) which has been 
delayed by 10 months. The notification is  accompanied by a delay statement. 
The reasons of  delay given are as under:- 
 
 “The Parliament passed the Coastal Aquaculture Authority, 2005 in 
June, 2005 and the Coastal Aquaculture Authority was set up in December, 
2005. The Coastal Aquaculture Authorit y Rules, 2005 was notified in the 
Official Gazette on 22.12.2005 including guidelines for shrimp farming. No 
major policy initiative could be in troduced in 2005-06. The regulations 
concerning the functioning of the Authorit y and recruitment rules for the staff 
of the Authority were finalized and no tified only in May, 2008. The Authority 

 
has therefore been functioning on a skeleton staff borrowed from the sister 
organizations under the Department of Animal Husbandry, Dairying & 
Fisheries. Even with the limited staff strength the Authority could process all 
the applications received from States for registration of shrimp farms which 
was the main task assigned. 
 Under Coastal Aquaculture Aut hority (Amendment) Rules, 2009, 
guidelines for regulating hatcheries and  farms for introduction of Litopenacus 
vannamci was notifie d in Gazette on 1
st May, 2009. After getting printed 
version, it was circulated to concerned ag encies. Since it is a new concept, the 
reaction of the hatchery ope rators and other organizat ions had to be obtained 
so that necessary amendments if needed could be carried out. This delay 
occurred essentially due to the above procedure which had to be followed.” 
  
 The Committee note the reply given by the Ministry regarding the 
reasons of delay and advise the Minist ry to take precaution so as to 
obviate such bottle necks such as sk eletal staff or delay in receiving 
printed copies of notifications, getting reactions from organization etc. 
The Committee hope that the Ministry of Agriculture  would cut on such 
delays in future by evolving an effective mechanism and would adhere to 
the time schedule as prescribed in Committee’s 131
st Report. 
 
(ii) Ministry of Chemical & Fert ilizers (Department of Fertilizers) 
 
 Only one notification (Sl. No. 13)  that Ministry of Chemical & 
Fertilizers (Department of Fertilizers) ha ve laid is delayed by 8 months. There 
is no delay statement  appended thereto. 
 
The Committee emphasise that the Ministry of Chemical & 
Fertilizers (Department of Fertilizers), strictly adhere to the time schedule 
as recommended by it in its 131 st Report  in laying of the Subordinate 
Legislation before Parliament and wh ere there is delay in laying of the 
notifications, a delay statement sh ould also invariably be appended 
therewith. 
 
 
(iii) Ministry of Communicati on and Information Technology 
 
The only notification that the Ministry of Communication and 
Information Technology have laid has been delayed by 6 months. The Ministry 
has attached a delay statement with th e notification explaining the reasons of 
delay. The reasons given by the Ministry is as under:-. 
 
 “The copies of the Gazette Notification which was published in the 
Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II – Section 3, sub section (i) vide No. 626 
dated October 29, 2009 regarding introduc tion of Overnight Parcel Service 
could not be laid on the Ta ble of Rajya Sabha due to the fact the service could 

 
not be launched due to rescheduling of the India Post freighters being operated 
by Air India. The notification is now being laid on the table of the Rajya Sabha 
during the Budget Session of Parliament.” 
 
The Committee impress upon the Mi nistry of Communication and 
Information Technology  to take necessary steps so as, to ensure that their 
notifications are laid well in time during the course of the Session. 
 
(iv) Ministry of Defence 
 
 Only one notification (Sl. No. 48) that  Ministry of Defence have laid is 
delayed by 9 months. There is no  delay statement  is appended thereto. 
  
  The Committee urge upon the Ministry of Defence to invariably 
append the delay statement explaining reasons of delay therein and in case 
of any delay taking place, in laying of  the notifications, take steps as to 
avoid recurrence of such delay in future. 
 
(v)   Ministry of Finance 
 
 (A)  (Department of Financial Services) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Departmen t of Financial Services) have laid 
19 notifications. A total of 6 notificatio ns have been delayed 2 notifications 
(Sl. Nos. 80 and 93) have delay of 4 to 6 months and no delay statement is 
attached thereto and 4 notifications (Sl.  Nos. 88 to 91) have delay statements 
appended with delay period of 1 year an d 5 months giving th e reason that it 
took sufficient time to carry out amendm ents in the State Bank of India Act, 
1955 which caused delay. 
 
 The Committee take note of the reply given by the Ministry of 
Finance (Department of Financial Services) and desire that such delays do 
not recur in future.  
 
 
(B)  (Department of Revenue) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Departm ent of Revenue) have delayed the 
laying of 4 notifications (Sl. Nos. 1 38-139, 159 and 240) by 4 to 8 months out 
of 154 notifications laid by them during the Session. The Ministry have failed 
to append delay statement to any of them. 
  
 
 
 

 
 The Committee appreciate the effo rts made by Department of 
Revenue in having laid maximum of their notifications without delay 
except a few of them. The Committee ho pe that the Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Revenue) would see that  in case of any delay actually 
taking place in laying, a delay statement is invariably appended therewith. 
 
 (vi)      Ministry of Home Affairs  
 
 
The Ministry of Home Affairs have laid 26 notifications out of which 5 
notifications have been delayed. The delay period ranges from 4 months to 6 
months. Only one notification has de lay statement appended thereto. The 
Ministry have given reason for delay as non-availability of requisite number of 
printed copies from the Press. 
 
 The Committee take serious note of the fact that delay was caused 
due to late receiving of  printed copi es of notifications from Government 
Press. Ministries have been taking reco urse to this plea very frequently. 
This possibility  should have been  assessed well before and adequate 
arrangements should have been made  in respect thereof. The Committee 
advise the Ministry of Home Affairs to take care so that such delays do not 
recur in future. 
 
(vii) Ministry of Human Resource Development 
  
 (Department of Higher Educat ion & Department of School 
Education and Literacy) 
 
The Ministry of Human Resource De velopment (Department of Higher 
Education) have laid 3 notifications (S l. Nos. 284-286) and all of them have 
been delayed by 7 months to 10 months. There is no delay statement appended 
with any of  them. 
   
  Department of School Education and Literacy have laid one 
notification which has been delayed by 8 months and no delay statement 
appended with it. 
 
 The Committee are unhappy to note that all the notifications (3 by 
the Department of Higher Education and 1 by Department of School 
Education and Literacy) laid by  Ministry of Human Resource 
Development have no delay statement appended with them. The 
Committee impress upon  the Ministry to  devise suitable mechanism to 
avoid such instances of delay in future a nd in case of any delay in laying,  
a delay statement should invariably be appended. 
 
 

 
 
 
(viii) Ministry of Law and Justi ce (Department of Legal Affairs)  
 
 Ministry of Law and Justice (Department of Legal Affairs)  have laid 2 
notifications (Sl. No. 298-290)  both of them have been delayed by 5 months to 
7 months. There are no delay statements with therewith. 
 
 The Committee are unable to understand why the Ministry of Law 
and Justice has not appended any de lay statement with the delayed 
notifications.  The Committee reiterate  its recommendation contained in 
its 131 st Report that in case of delay in  laying, notifications should be 
accompanied with delay statement. The Committee further desire that 
Ministry of Law and Justice should take care that repetition of such delay 
may not be happen. 
 
(ix) Ministry of Power 
 
 The Ministry of Power have laid  15 notifications out of  which 1 
notification (Sl. No. 366) has been delayed by 5 m onths and   delay statement 
is appended thereto. The reason for delay given by the Ministry is as under:- 
 
 “The Regulation was published in the Gazette of India Extraordinary 
dated 23
rd October, 2009 and printed copies of the same were received by the 
Central Electricity Regulatory Commission only on 18 th December, 2009 from 
the Press. Copies of the Regulation were  received in the Mi nistry of Power on 
18th December, 2009. The House was adjourned on 21.12.2009. since only one 
day was available before the House was adjourned, the Regulation could not be 
laid in the winter session 2009.” 
  
 The Committee observe that sessions are declared much in advance 
and had the Ministry of Power take n prompt steps for laying of the 
notifications, the delay could have been avoided. It appears to have waited 
till last moment. The Committee, therefore,  recommend that the Ministry 
should take the first available opportunity for laying of their notifications 
during the session. 
 
 
(x)     Ministry of Road Transport & Highways 
 
 The Ministry of Road Transport & Highways have laid 445 
notifications
 on the Table of the House. 87 notif ications have been delayed. 
Out of which  64 notifications which have been delayed and have no delay 
statements appended to them. The dela y period ranges from 4 months to 5 
months. 23 notifications  have been  accompanied by delay statements. The 
delay period ranges from 4 months to 9  months. The reasons given by the 

 
Ministry is that it took some time to complete the procedural formalities and 
therefore, papers could not be laid in time. 
  
 
 The Committee take note of the fact that Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways have done mammoth work in laying such huge 
volume of notifications on the table of the House.  The Committee 
however regret that the Ministry h ave not appended any delay statement 
with any delayed Notifications/Rules. Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways should see or look into  the matter seriously eliminate 
recurrence of such delays completing the procedural formalities. 
 
 
(xi) Ministry of Shipping 
  
The Ministry of Shipping  have laid 8 notifications out of which 1 
notification (Sl. No. 834) has been dela yed by 4 months and delay statement is 
appended thereto. The reason of delay given by the Ministry is as under:- 
 
“Notification No. GSR 847 (E) could not be laid on the Table of the 
Houses as immediately after its publication in the Gazette, within the stipulated 
period due to administrative reasons.” 
  
 The Committee are not happy with the reply given by the Ministry 
of Shipping that the notifications coul d not be laid due to administrative 
reasons which is a vague or evasive  reply. The Committee advise that 
lacunae such as administrative or other should be plugged to avoid 
recurrence of such instances in future. 
 
 
(xii) Ministry of Textiles 
 
 The Ministry of Textiles have la id 5 notifications. Out of which 2 
notifications have been delayed. One notification (S l. No. 836)  have been 
delayed by 5 months. There is no dela y statement appended thereto. Another 
notification which has delay statement has a delay period of 11 months. The 
Ministry have cited administrative reas ons for not laying the notification in 
time. 
 
 The Committee hope that the Mini stry would avoid such instances 
of delay in laying of subordinate le gislation in future. The Committee also 
impress upon the Ministry to take note of the fact that in  case there is a 
delay in the laying of any statutor y notification, the same should be 
accompanied with a delay statement while it is being laid. The Committee 
also advise the Ministry to avoid recurrence of such delays due to 
administrative reasons. 

 
 The Committee is not happy at the casual manner in which the 
Ministries have treated the recomme ndations of the Committee with 
regard to the delay in the laying of the Notifications despite the 
Committee’s reiteration in all its reports. The Committee is unable to 
accept the reasons given by the Ministri es for the delay in laying such as 
late receiving of printed copies of  Notifications from the Government 
Press, administrative reasons, time taken to complete procedural 
formalities, etc. The Committee also take  serious note of the fact that, in 
most cases, the Ministries fail to append delay statement explaining the 
reasons for the delay. Therefore, the Committee reiterates, that the 
Ministries should take proactive measures so as to avoid any delay, 
whatsoever, in the laying of the No tifications and that the Committee is 
unable to accept the flimsy grounds gi ven by the Ministries regarding the 
reasons for delay. 
 
3. The Committee impresses upon all the Ministries/Departments to take 
immediate steps to ensure timely and pr oper laying of subordinate legislation. 
The Committee, accordingly, wishes to bring to the notice of all 
Ministries/Departments of the Government of India, particularly, the defaulting 
ones, the following recommendations, as contained in its 131
st
,135
th and 152nd 
Reports: 
 “Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the 
House is not in Session, during the Se ssion immediately following the date 
of publication of the Order in the Offici al Gazette and (ii) if the House is 
in Session, on the date of the pub lication of the Order, during its 
continuance and in case the time l ag between the date of publication and 
the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the 
expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Governme nt of India should henceforth 
comply with this schedule in the matt er of laying of the Orders issued 
under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha. 
(131st Report) 
(Presented on 15 December 2000) 
 “The Committee has, time and again,  emphasized that it is essential 
that in case there is a delay in th e laying of Statutory Orders, the 
concerned Ministry/Department should invariably append a delay 
statement along with the notificati ons being laid so as to apprise 
Parliament of the causes of delay. The Committee desires all 
Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the intension is that 
through the process of formulation of a delay statement the Ministry is 
able to introspect, identify bottlenecks and devise remedial measures”. 
(135th Report) 
(Presented on 27 July 2001) 

 
 “In the opinion of the Committee,  every notification should be 
assigned a distinctive number in order to give it a separate identity and 
similarly, no notification should be published/laid which does not carry a 
notification number. The Committee ex pects all Ministries/Departments 
of Government of India to exercise caution in this regard. In case where 
there are more than one notification under a single file, the 
Ministries/Departments should ensu re that the file number on each 
notification should be suitably distinguished from one another. 
(152
nd
 Report) 
(Presented on 26 August 2004)
