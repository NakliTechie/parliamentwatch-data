2. During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Committee has noted 
some cases where there has been a cons iderable time lag between the date of 
notification of the Order and the date of its laying. The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of dela y in cases where notifications  have not been laid as 
per the schedule prescribed by the Committee and where such notifications 
were accompanied by a statement indicating the reasons for the delay in laying. 
In some of the cases the delay has been quite long. The 
observations/recommendations of the Co mmittee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows: -    
  
(i) Ministry of Agriculture (Department of Agriculture & 
Cooperation) 
  
 The Ministry of Agriculture (Department of Agriculture & 
Cooperation) have laid 9 notifications out of whic h 5 (Sl. Nos. 1,2,5,6 and 8) 
have been delayed. Out of those, 4 delayed notifications  have not been 
accompanied by any delay statements. Only  one notification has been attached 
with delay statement (5 months). The delay ranges from 5 months to 1 year and 
9 months. The reasons for delay given by the Ministry are as under: 

: 3 : 
 
 “The Notification G.S.R. 717 (E), dated the 15 th November, 2007  was 
published in the Gazette of India (Extra ordinary) Part-II, Section 3(i). The 
rules could not be laid down before each  House of Parliament in the Winter 
Session of Parliament as sufficient numbe r of copies of the Rules were not 
received in time from Government of India Press, New Delhi.” 
  
 The Committee notes with regret that  the Ministry of Agriculture 
have again failed to strictly adhere to the time schedule as prescribed in its 
131st Report that is reiterated as follows:- 
 
  “Orders required to be laid before  the House should be laid (i) if 
the House is not in Session, during the Session immediately following the 
date of publication of the Order in the Official Gazette and (ii) if the 
House is in Session, on the date of the publication of the Order, during its 
continuance and in case the time l ag between the date of publication and 
the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the 
expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Governme nt of India should henceforth 
comply with this schedule in the matt er of laying of the Orders issued 
under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha.” 
 
 The Committee hopes that the Ministry of Agriculture would put 
an effective mechanism in place to e liminate such instances of delay in 
future, particularly on account non-rece ipt of sufficient number of  copies 
of the notifications from the Govern ment of India Press. The Committee 
hopes that the Ministry of Agricultu re would be able to ensure that such 
delays do not recur in future.  
 
(ii)  Ministry of Chemical and Fertilizers (Department of Fertilizers) 
   
 Only one  notification that the  Mi nistry of Chemical and Fertilizers 
(Department of Fertilizers) have laid  on the table of the House has been 
delayed by 5 months and no delay statement has been appended therewith. 
 
 The Committee urges the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers to  
append the delay statement where there has been a  delay in laying of the 
notifications. 
 
(iii)  Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
 
  Ministry of Corporate Affairs have laid 9 notifications out of which  4 
(Sl. Nos. 26,28,29 and 31) have been delayed by 4 to 10 months and no delay 
statement has been attached therewith. 
   

: 4 : 
   
 
 The Committee hopes that the Minist ry of Corporate Affairs would 
take care for  timely laying of the notifications and append delay 
statements in all such cases where the laying of subordinate legislation is 
delayed. 
 
(iv)   Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs) 
 
  The Committee regrets to note that Mi nistry of Finance (Department of 
Economic Affairs) has laid 27 notifications  out of which 12 (Sl. Nos. 49 to 56, 
67-67, 70 and 72) have been delayed by 4 months to eight months. The 
Ministry has not appended delay statem ents to 11 notifications which have 
been delayed and only one notification is  accompanied with delay statement. 
The reasons given by the Ministry are as follows: 
 
 “Notification No. AX1/ST/DM/ 173/2007 published in the Gazette 
Notification No. 2 GI/2007 Published in Pa rt III, Section 4, vide no. 37 dated 
15-21 September, 2007 containing Bank of  Maharashtra Officer Employees 
(Discipline and Appeal) (Amendment) Re gulations, 2003 could not be laid on 
the Table of Rajya Sabha in time due to non receipt of Gazette copies from 
Bank of Maharashtra well in time.” 
 
 The Committee recommends that the Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Economic Affairs) should evolve an effective mechanism 
to obviate such delays as non-recei pt of Gazette copies from nodal 
agencies in future and in case of del ays, delay statement be appended in 
order to apprise the Parliament of the true picture. 
  
 
(iv)-A      Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services) 
 
  The Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Service) have laid 
14 notifications out of wh ich 4 (Sl. Nos. 78,82,84 & 89 ) have been delayed. 
Two notifications are accompanied with delay statements and two notifications 
are not accompanied by any delay statement. The reasons for delay given by 
the Ministry is as under: 
 
 “Corrigendum rectifying the erro r was published in the Gazette of 
India. The delay in laying the notifications occurred due to delay in issue of the 
corrigendum.” 
  
  The  Committee notes with regret that Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Financial Service) have failed to strictly adhere to the time 
schedule as prescribed in its 131
st Report that is reiterated as follows:- .  

: 5 : 
 
 “Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the 
House is not in Session, during the Se ssion immediately following the date 
of publication of the Order in the Offici al Gazette and (ii) if the House is 
in Session, on the date of the pub lication of the Order, during its 
continuance and in case the time l ag between the date of publication and 
the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the 
expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Governme nt of India should henceforth 
comply with this schedule in the matt er of laying of the Orders issued 
under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha.” 
 
 The Committee hopes that the Mini stry of Finance would put an 
effective mechanism in place to eliminate such instan ces of delay in future 
particularly on account of not receiving printed copies of notifications in 
time from Government of India Pre ss. The Committee hopes that such 
delays would not recur in future. 
 
(iv)-B  Ministry of Fina nce (Department of Revenue) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) have laid 137 
notifications and only 3 of them have been delayed, with delay of 11 months in 
each case (Sl. Nos. 200, 201 and 202); the reasons of delay is given as under: 
  
 “The notifications nos.  G.S.R.  386(E), 387(E) and 388(E) were 
inadvertently not laid during the last se ssions  of the Parliament and is now 
being laid in the Budget Session of Parliament. The delay may be condoned.” 
 
 The Committee notes the reasons given by the Ministry of Finance 
(Department of  Revenue) for delay in laying of notifications and hopes that 
such delays do not happen further. 
 
(v) Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (Dental Education Section) 
 
 The Committee is unhappy to note th at the Department of Dental

with them. 
 
 The Committee directs the Ministry of  Health & Family Welfare 
(Dental Education Section) to take corrective measures to obviate such 
delay in future and in case such del ays become unavoidable  in future, it 
should invariably be accompanied by statements giving the reasons for 
delay in laying.

immediate steps to ensure timely and pr oper laying of subordinate legislation. 
The Committee, accordingly, wishes to bring to the notice of all 
Ministries/Departments of the Government of India, particularly, the defaulting 
ones, the following recommendations, as contained in its 131
st
,135
th and 152nd 
Reports: 
 “Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the 
House is not in Session, during the Se ssion immediately following the date 
of publication of the Order in the Offici al Gazette and (ii) if the House is 
in Session, on the date of the pub lication of the Order, during its 
continuance and in case the time l ag between the date of publication and 
the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the 
expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Governme nt of India should henceforth 
comply with this schedule in the matt er of laying of the Orders issued 
under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha. 
(131st Report) 
(Presented on 15 December 2000) 
 “The Committee has, time and again,  emphasized that it is essential 
that in case there is a delay in the laying of Statutory Orders, the 
concerned Ministry/Department should invariably append a delay 
statement along with the notificati ons being laid so as to apprise 
Parliament of the causes of delay. The Committee desires all 
Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the intension is that 
through the process of formulation of a delay statement the Ministry is 
able to introspect, identify bottlenecks and devise remedial measures”. 
(135th Report) 
(Presented on 27 July 2001)

of Government of India to exercise caution in this regard. In case where 
there are more than one notification under a single file, the 
Ministries/Departments should ensu re that the file number on each 
notification should be suitably distinguished from one another.
