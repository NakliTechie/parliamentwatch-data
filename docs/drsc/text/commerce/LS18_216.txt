2.  While scrutinizing these Orders, the Committee note d that there was 
considerable time lag between issue  of Notificatio ns and their laying in some 
cases. The annexed statement also indicates the per iod of delay in cases where 
Notifications have not been laid as per the time sc hedule prescribed by the 
Committee. The Committee observed that of the total  352 Notifications that were 
delayed, there were 329 Notifications where no dela y statement was appended to. 
However, the bulk of delayed Notifications were fro m the Ministry of  Road 
Transport and Highways which comes to around 93.4% of the total delayed 
Notifications. 
 
The observations/recommendations of the Committee i n respect of the 
specific Ministries/Departments where delay in layi ng of Notifications has been 
noticed, are as follows: -  
 
(i) Ministry of Civil Aviation  
 
 The only one notification that the Ministry of Civ il Aviation (Sl. No. 8) 
have laid, was delayed by 8 months and no delay sta tement has been appended 
thereto. The Committee is concerned over the delay in laying  of the only  
Notification by the Ministry of Civil Aviation by  8 months. Further there is 
no delay statement appended to it citing details as  to what caused such delay. 
The Committee, therefore, impresses upon the Minist ry to take care that 
such instances of delay do not recur in future and in case of any delay in 
laying, a delay statement should invariably be appended thereto. 
 

  
(ii) Ministry of Commerce and Industry (Department of Co mmerce and 
Department of Industrial Policy & Promotion)  
 
Ministry of   Commerce and Industry (Department of Commerce) have 
laid 3 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 9-11). Out of these,  two Notifications (Sl. No. 9-10) 
were delayed by 4 to 5 months and  there is no dela y statement appended to both 
of them. Whereas the Ministry of Commerce and Indus try  (Department of 
Industrial Policy & Promotion)  have laid 5 Notific ations  out of which  one was 
delayed by 7 months with no delay statement attached to it.  
 
The Committee expresses its unhappiness  over the d elay in laying of 
the above mentioned Notifications by the Ministry o f Commerce and 
Industry (Department of Commerce and Department of Industrial Policy & 
Promotion) resulting in a delay of  4 to 7 months. Further, no delay statement 
have been appended thereto  giving reasons for the delay. The Committee, 
therefore, would like  the Ministry to ensure that there are no delays in 
future and at all if these is a delay in laying, th ere should be a delay 
statement attached with it. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
(iii) Ministry of Communications and Information Te chnology 
 
(a)   (Department of Electronics and Information Technology) 
  
 Ministry of Communications and Information Technology  (Department of  
Electronics and Information Technology)  have laid 10 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 17 
to 26).  Out of these, 6 Notifications have been de layed by 5 to 14 months. Only 
three  delayed Notifications (Sl. Nos. 17 and 19) w ere attached with delay 
statement  whereas the  other three Notifications ( Sl. Nos. 20 to 22) did not have 
any delay statement citing the exact reasons for de lay. The reason for delay given 
by the Ministry is as under:- 
  
 “The Electronics and IT Goods (Requirement of Comp ulsory Registration) 
Order, 2012 under the Bureau of India Standards Act , 1986 in English and Hindi 
versions, could not be laid on the Table of both th e Houses of parliament within 
the specified period as further amendments to these  Rules/ Subordinate 
Legislations were being issued. These documents are  now being laid on the Table 
of both the Houses of Parliament. 
 In future, all efforts will be made to ensure that  the documents are laid on 
the Table of the Houses by due date.” 

  
 The Committee regrets the delay in laying of Notifi cations due to 
amendments taking place. Instead of waiting for ove r a year to lay the 
notifications, the Ministry should have laid the no tifications on the first 
available opportunity. These amendments would have followed  suit. But the 
Ministry thought it would carry out the amendments and then  lay the 
Notifications. The Committee exhorts the Ministry t o lay the Notifications 
well in time in future at the first available opportunity, to avoid any delay. 
 
(b)  (Department of Telecommunications) 
 
Ministry of Communications and Information Technolo gy (Department of 
Telecommunications) have laid 7 Notifications (Sl. No. 27 to 33)  out  of which 
three Notifications were delayed by 3 to 11 months.  One of the Notification (Sl. 
No. 30) had a delay statement attached to it wherea s the other two did not have 
any delay statements with them. The reasons for del ay given by the Ministry are 
as under:- 
 
“The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) i s a statutory body 
set up under the Telecom Regulatory Authority of In dia Act, 1997. In exercise of 
the powers conferred upon it under section 36 of th e TRAI Act, 1997, the 
Authority may, by notification, makes regulations c onsistent with the Act. Under 
section 37 of the Act, the regulations notified by TRAI are required to be laid 
before each House of Parliament. 
The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) in  exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 36, read with sub-clauses (i), and 
(v) of clause (b) of sub-section (1) of Section 11 of the TRAI Act, 1997 notified 
“Standard of Quality of service (Duration of Advert isements in Television 
Channels) (Amendment) Regulation, 2013.” On receipt  of the copies of the 
gazette notification on 15 
th  April, 2013 from the Government of India Press, 
TRAI sent the Regulation to the Department on 16 th  April, 2013. Since  the 
subject matter of the Regulation i.e. television br oadcasting falls within the 
business allocated to the Ministry of Information &  Broadcasting as specified in 
the first schedule to the Govt. of India (Allocatio n of Business) Rules, so it was 
decided in the Department of Information and Broadcasting for laying of the same 
on the table of both Houses of Parliament by them. The same was forwarded to 
them on 25 th  April, 2013. 
Ministry of Information & Broadcasting has returned  the above said 
regulation to this department on 10 th  October, 2013, after the lapse of approximate 
6 months from the date of forwarding of said regula tion to them without laying or 
the same on the Table of the both Houses of Parliam ent. While returning of the 
regulation, the Ministry of Information & Broadcast ing has said that the said 
regulation is to be laid in both Houses of the Parl iament by the Department of 
Telecommunications as regulations of TRAI, notified  in the past, have not been 
referred to their Ministry for laying in the Houses of the Parliament. 

The matter was referred to Cabinet Secretariat, for  their advice, Reply 
from the Cabinet Secretariat has been received vide their O.M. No.1/21/112/2013-
Cab. Dated 13.12.2013. 
Accordingly, the Regulation titled “Standard of Qua lity of service 
(Duration of Advertisements in Television Channels)  (Amendment) Regulation, 
2013” is now being laid on the Table of the Rajya Sabha.” 
 
The Committee notes with surprise the reasons of de lay given by the 
Ministry of Communications and Information Technolo gy  (Department of  
Electronics and Information Technology and Departme nt of 
Telecommunications) where there was no clarity of j urisdiction  between the 
Ministries in laying of the Notifications. It respe cts that the Ministry  sticks 
to the schedule of laying of the Notifications as g iven in Committee’s 131 
st  
Report.

50) on the Table of the House during the Session. O ut of  these,  2 Notifications 
(Sl. Nos. 44 and 45) have been delayed by five  mon ths and three months 
respectively and there is no delay statement attached to either  of them. 
    
 The Committee notes with concern that two of the  N otifications laid 
by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs were not only delayed by three and  five 
months respectively  but also did not have any dela y statement attached to 
them. The Committee views it seriously  and reitera tes its recommendations  
as contained in its 135 
th  Report that in case there is delay in laying of 
Statutory Orders, the concerned Ministry/Department  should invariably 
append a delay statement. The Committee further imp resses upon the 
Ministry that it is their obligation towards the Pa rliament to invariably 
apprise it about the reasons for delay through dela y statements in case they 
are unable to lay any Subordinate Legislation on time.

“The Notification G.S.R. 37 (E) dated 18/01/2012 wa s challenged before

Further, the Order of Allahabad High Court, dated 3 1.01.2013 has been 
challenged before the Supreme Court of India vide S LP (C) No. 14258/2013-
Sayyed Idris Hussain Zaidi and others Vs UOI and ot hers. The matter is pending 
before the Hon’ble Court. 
Since the matter was subjudice, it was felt that th e placing of notification 
in the Houses of Parliament would attract attention  of the Hon’ble Court. The 
matter was consulted with the Advocate, Supreme Cou rt of India, pleading the 
case on behalf of UOI. He advised to place the noti fication on the table of both 
the Houses of Parliament. 
The delay in laying these rules on the Tables of bo th the Houses of 
Parliament is regretted and may kindly be condoned.” 
 
 The Committee notes the reply given by the Ministr y of Culture, 
continues it to be alert   and hopes that in future  they should endeavour to 
strictly adhere to the schedule as recommended by t he Committee in its 131 
st  
Report in matters of  laying of subordinate legislation.

(x) Ministry of Power 
 
 The Ministry of  Power have laid 12 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 365 to 376).  
Out of these 3 Notifications have been delayed from  5 months to 7 months and 
two of them (Sl. No. 368 and 371) are also accompan ied with delay statements. 
The reason given by the Ministry for delay is as under:- 
 
 “Although the Notification No. JERC-16/2013 was pu blished in the 
Gazette of India Extraordinary dated 9 
th  March, 2013, copies of the Notifications 
could not be laid on the table of the Rajya Sabha b efore conclusion of the 
Monsoon Session 2013 as printed copies of the same were received by Joint 
Electricity Regulatory Commission for Goa & UTs fro m the Department of 
Publication on 11 th  September, 2013 and JERC Joint Electricity Regulat ory 
Commission for Goa & UTs forwarded the same to Mini stry of Power on 13 th  

September, 2013. The Monsoon Session concluded on 3 0 th  August, 2013. Hence

 “Although the Notification No. L-1/12/2010-CERC, w as published in the 
Gazette of India Extraordinary dated the 11 
th  July, 2013, copies of the 
Notification could not be laid on Table of the Rajy a Sabha before conclusion of

situation. 
 
The Committee, therefore,  directs all the Ministri es that in case there 
is delay in procuring printed copies in time, they may obtain G.S.R. and S.O. 
numbers from the Government of India Press and prep are requisite number 
of copies in cyclostyled form for laying in the Par liament as suggested by 
Secretary, Ministry of  Urban Development, the cont rolling Ministry of 
Government of India Press, in its meeting held on 3 0  July, 2013. The 
Committee also recommends that this process may be adopted by Ministries 
suo moto  on immediate basis so as to avoid such delays henceforth.

time limit, it should seek extension of time for co mpleting the rule-making 
process. The recommendation of the committee reads as follows:- 
 
  “The Committee feels that the rule-making process could well 
have been completed within the time limit of six mo nths which the 
Committee has laid down for making of rules under a n Act after 
coming into force of that Act. Where however, a Ministry does not find 
it possible to adhere to the time limit, they shoul d inform the 
Committee of the reasons why they cannot make rules  within the time 
limit and seek extension of time for completing the  rule-making 
process.”

Ministries/Departments of Government of India are n ot complying with the
