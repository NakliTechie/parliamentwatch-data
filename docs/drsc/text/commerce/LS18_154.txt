INTRODUCTION 
  
         I, the Chairman of the Committee on Subordinate Legislation, having been 
authorized by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, do hereby present this 
two Hundred and First Report of the Committee. 
2.      The Committee considered and adopted the Report  in its meeting held on the 16 th 
September, 2004. 
3.      The notifications containing Rules/Regulations, etc. were laid on the Table of the 
Rajya Sabha during its 201st Session (January-February, 2004) have been persued by 
the Committee in the light of the provisions of the Acts under which these were framed 
and also with a view to seeing whether these have been laid properly and within the 
prescribed time-frame. The Committee's observations/recommendations are contained 
in the report that follows.

REPORT 
I 

LAYING OF STATUTORY ORDERS ON THE TABLE OF RAJYA SABHA 
        During the 201 th Session of the Rajya Sabha, 42 Statutory Orders were laid on the 
Table of the House by various Ministries/Departments. The details of the Gazette 
Notifications in which these were publis hed and the date of their laying on the Table of 
the Rajya Sabha have been indicated in the Ministry-wise Statement (Annexure). 
2.     Upon scrutiny of the Orders laid in the Session, the Committee notes that out of 42 
notifications laid, only one notifica tion at Sl. No. 13 laid by the Ministry of Human 
Resource Development, Department of Women and Child Development has been delayed 
by five months and no delay statement has been appended therewith. The Committee 
impresses upon the Ministry of Human Resource  Development (Department of Women 
and Child Development) to pay adequate attention towards the timely laying of the 
subordinate legislation and to append the delay statement in case there is a delay in laying. 
3.     The Committee further notes that three notifications (Sl. No. 3, 7 and 8) laid by the 
Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (Department of 
Telecommunications) bear identical reference number. In this context, the Committee 
would like to invite the Ministry's attention to its fol lowing 
observations/recommendations as contained in the 152nd Report. 
"In the opinion of the Committee, every notification should be assigned a distinctive 
number in order to give it a separate identity and similarly, no notification should 
be published/la id which does not carry a notification number. The Committee 
expects all Ministries/Departments of Government of India to exercise caution in this 
regard. In case where there are more than one notifications under a single file, the 
Ministries/Departments s hould ensure that the file number on each notification 
should be suitably distinguished from one another". 
The Committee hopes that the Ministry of Information Technology (Department of 
Information Technology) would take suitable corrective measures for future.
