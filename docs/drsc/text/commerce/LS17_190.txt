2. During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Committee has noted 
some cases where there has been a cons iderable time lag between the date of 
notification of the Order and the date of its laying. The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of dela y in cases where notifications  have not been laid as 
per the schedule prescribed by the Committee and where such notifications 
were accompanied by a statement indicating the reasons for the delay in laying. 
In some of the cases the delay has been quite long. The 
observations/recommendations of the Co mmittee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows: -    
  
(i) Ministry of Communication  and Information Technology 
 (Department of Posts) 
 
Ministry of Communication and In formation Technology (Department 
of Posts) have laid 3 notifications. One notification (Sl. No. 20) has been 
delayed by 1 year and 3 months and ha s delay statement at tached with it. 
Explaining the reasons of delay, the Ministry has stated as under:- 

 
 
 “The copies of the Gazette Notification which was published in the 
Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II – Section 3, sub section (i) vide No. 626 
dated October 29, 2009 regarding introduc tion of Overnight Parcel Service 
could not be laid on the Ta ble of Rajya Sabha due to the fact the service could 
not be launched due to rescheduling of the India Post freighters being operated 
by Air India. The notification is now being laid on the table of the Rajya Sabha 
during the Budget Session of Parliament.” 
 
The Committee have noted the reply  given by the Ministry of 
Communication and Information Tec hnology (Department of Posts) and 
hope that the Ministry would take  adequate precaution to avoid such 
delays in future. 
 
(ii) Ministry of Defence 
 
 Ministry of Defence have laid 2 not ifications (Sl. Nos. 39 and 40). Out 
of these one notification (Sl. No. 39) is delayed by 5 months and  no  delay 
statement  is attached  thereto. 
  
  The Committee urges the Ministry of Defence to append the delay 
statement where there is delay in laying of notifications and avoid 
recurrence of such delays in future. 
 
(iii) Ministry of Environment and Forests 
 
 Ministry of Environment and Forest s have laid 14 not ifications (Sl. 
Nos. 41 to 54). 5 notifications have been delayed. The delay period ranges 
from 5 months to 1 year. No delay statement has been attached to any of these 
delayed notifications. 
 
The Committee in the absence of de lay statements are not able to 
know the reasons behind this del ay. Therefore, the Committee invite 
attention to its recommendation contained in its 131
st R e p o r t   i n  t h i s  
connection where it is observed to append invariably delay statement in 
case of delay in laying of notifications by  the Ministry. 
 
(iv)   Ministry of Finance 
 
 (A)  (Department of Financial Services) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Departmen t of Financial Services) have laid 
14 notifications (Sl. Nos. 72 – 85). Out of these 5 notifications have been 
delayed and no delay statements  is appended to them. The delay period ranges 
from 4 months to 10 months. 
 

 
 The Committee takes note of the fact and advices Ministry of 
Finance (Department of Financial Servi ces) shall ensure timely laying of 
their notifications as per th e schedule prescribed in its 131 st Report. 
Therefore,  the Committee reiterate its directed that the Ministry should  
keep the Parliament informed of the reasons of delay in laying of their 
subordinate legislation.  
 
(B)  (Department of Revenue) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) have laid 117 
notifications out of  which 20 notifications (Sl. Nos. 86-92, 101-103, 138-139, 
154-160 & 177) have been delayed. The delay ranges from 3 to 5 months. The 
Ministry have failed to append delay statement to any of them. 
  
 
 The Committee invite attention to  its recommendations made in 
135
th Report, wherein it is laid down that it is essential to append a 
statement in case of delay in laying  of statutory orders. The Committee 
therefore, impress upon the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) 
to append delay statement in case of any delay and strictly adhere to the 
time schedule in laying of notifications so as to avoid any delay in laying of 
notifications. 
 
(v) Health and Family Welfare 
 
  The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare have laid 13 notifications
 
on the Table of the House. 7 notifications have been delayed. Out of these  3 
notifications which have been delayed have no delay statements appended to 
them. 4 notifications  have been accompanied by delay statements. The delay 
period ranges from 4 months to 1 year . The reasons given by the Ministry is 
that it took some time to complete the procedural formalities and therefore, 
papers could not be laid in time. 
 
 The Committee are not convinced of the vague reasons of delay 
given by the Ministry of Health a nd Family Welfare that due to time 
taken by the procedural formalitie s, the Ministry could not lay the 
notifications in time. If, it was so, th e Ministry should have foreseen the 
things beforehand and would have acted  promptly. The Committee 
castigate this lackadaisical approach by  the Ministry and advise them to 
take care in future in laying of notifications.  
 
(vi)      Ministry of Home Affairs  
 
The Ministry of Home Af fairs have laid 25 notifications out of which 
11 notifications have been delayed. The delay period ranges from 4 months to 
1 year. Eight notifications (Sl. Nos. 217-219, 221, 225, 228-229 and 235) have 

 
no delay statement attached. Three no tifications have delay statement 
appended. The reasons forwarded by the Ministry for delay is as under:- 
 
“The Daman & Diu Private Security Agencies Rules, 2009 were 
notified by the UT Administration of Daman & Diu vide Notification 
No. 1/DC/Home/7/DDPSAR/2009-2010/42 dated 18.9.2009 for 
carrying out the provisions of the Private Security Agencies 
(Regulation) Act, 2005. As per Sec tion 25($) of the Act, the above 
Rules are proposed to be laid before  both Houses of Parliament during 
the current Monsoon Session of Parliament. 
The UT Administration had expe ditiously notified and printed 
the English version of the Rule s on 21.8.2009. Translation of the Rules 
into Hindi, however, took considerable time and the UT Administration 
was able to publish the Hindi version in November, 2009. This coupled 
with other administrative reasons re sulted in delay in laying of the 
aforesaid Rules before each House of Parliament”. 
 
 The Committee take serious note of the fact that delay was caused 
due to administrative reasons. Ministries have been taking recourse to this 
plea very frequently. This possibility   should have been assessed well 
before and adequate arrangements sh ould have been made in respect 
thereof. The Committee advises the Minist ry of Home Affairs to take care 
so that such delays do not recur in future. 
 
(vii) Ministry of Human Resource Development 
  
 (Department of Higher Educat ion & Department of School 
Education and Literacy) 
 
The Ministry of Human Resource De velopment (Department of Higher 
Education) have laid 5 notifications (Sl. Nos. 242-246). Out of these 3 
notifications have been delayed, 2 no tifications have no delay statement 
appended with them and only 1 notif ication is accompanied with delay 
statement. The delay period ranges from  5 months to 1 year and 11 months. 
The Ministry have given reason for de lay as non-availabili ty of requisite 
number of printed copies from the Press. 
   
  Department of School Education and L iteracy have laid 2 notifications 
and both of these have been delayed by 3 months and no delay statement is 
appended thereto. 
 
  The Committee are not happy over th e inordinate delay extending 
upto about 2 years in laying of some of these notifications. Further the 
reasons given such as non-availability of requisite number of printed 
copies appear to be lame excuse. The Committee therefore, impress upon  
the Ministry to  devise suitable mechanism to avoid such instances of delay 

 
in future and in case of  any delay in laying,  a delay statement should 
invariably be appended. 
 
 (viii) Ministry of Power 
 
 The Ministry of Power have laid  21 notifications out of  which 5 
notification have delayed, 4 notifications (Sl. Nos. 309-312) have been delayed 
by 1 year and these have no delay statement appended with them. One 
notification (Sl. No. 304) has delay period of  4 months and is attached with  
delay statement. The reason for delay given by the Ministry is as under:- 
 
 “The Regulation was published in the Gazette of India Extraordinary 
dated 23
rd October, 2009 and printed copies of the same were received by the 
Central Electricity Regulatory Commission only on 18 th December, 2009 from 
the Press. Copies of the Regulation were  received in the Mi nistry of Power on 
18th December, 2009. The House was adjourned on 21.12.2009. since only one 
day was available before the House was adjourned, the Regulation could not be 
laid in the winter session 2009.” 
  
 The Committee observe that sessions are declared much in advance 
and had the Ministry of Power take n prompt steps for laying of the 
notifications, the delay could have  been avoided. It reflects the bad 
management planning on the part  of the Ministry. The Committee, 
therefore,  recommend that the Ministry should take the first available 
opportunity for laying of their notifications during the session. 
 
 
(ix)     Ministry of Road Transport & Highways 
 
 The Ministry of Road Transport & Highways have laid 317 
notifications
 on the Table of the House. Out of  these 102 notif ications have 
been delayed. All of these have no de lay statements appended thereto and  
delay period ranges from 3 months to 8 months.  
  
 The Committee take note of the fact that Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways have done mammoth work in laying such huge 
volume of notifications on the table of the House.  The Committee 
however regret that the Ministry h ave not appended any delay statement 
with any delayed Notifications. Ministry of Road Transport and Highways 
should see or look into the matter seriously to eliminate recurrence of such 
delays in future. 
 
 
 
 

 
(x) Ministry of Science and Technology (Department of Science and 
Technology) 
 
 The Ministry of Science and Technology (Department of Science and 
Technology) have laid only one notification (Sl. No. 630) which has been 
delayed by 4 months. The Ministry have  appended delay statement with the 
notification and the reasons given by the Ministry is as under:- 
 
 “As per section 22 of the Scien ce and Engineering Research Board 
(SERB) Act, 2008 (9 of 2009), the Scie nce and Engineering Research Board 
Rules is required to be laid on the Table of Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha. Though 
the papers pertaining to SERB rules were  sent to the Parliament Secretariat 
during the last Parliament session, in advertently, the papers regarding 
enforcement of the Act (No. G.S.R. 212 (E) dated the 29
th March, 2010) were 
laid on the table of both the Houses, in stead of SERB Rules (Notification No. 
G.S.R. 213 (E) dated the 29 th March, 2010). Hence the delay in laying the 
papers in respect of the SERB Rules.” 
 
 The Committee have noted the rep ly given by the Ministry of 
Science and Technology (Department of  Science and Technology) and 
hope that the Ministry will adh ere strictly to the schedule as 
recommended by the Committee for timely laying of Subordinate 
Legislation so that such lapses do not recur in future. 
 
 
(xi) Ministry of Shipping 
  
The Ministry of Shipping  have la id 5 notifications ( Sl. Nos. 631-635) 
out of which 2 notifications have been  delayed. The delay period ranges  from 
3 to 5 months and both of them have delay statements appended. The reasons 
for delay  given by the Ministry is  that the time taken to  get sufficient number 
of printed copies caused delay. 
  
 The Committee find that the reply given by the Ministry as to the 
reasons of delay is not convincing a nd appears to be evasive. Delay could 
be avoided, had precaution been taken by  the Ministry in this regard. The 
Committee hope that the Ministry of Shipping would cut on such delays in 
future particularly on account of de lay in receiving printed copies of 
notifications by evolving and effective mechanism and would adhere to the 
time schedule as prescribed in Committee’s 131
st Report. 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
(xii) Ministry of Textiles 
 
 The Ministry of Textiles have laid  2 notifications (Sl. Nos. 636-637) 
one of which (Sl. No. 636) has been delayed. The delay ranges upto 3 months 
and no delay statement has been attached. 
 
 The Committee impress upon the Mini stry of Textiles to evolve 
suitable mechanism to avoid such de lays and also to append delay 
statements where there has been a delay, in laying of notification. 
  
3. The Committee impresses upon all the Ministries/Departments to take 
immediate steps to ensure timely and pr oper laying of subordinate legislation. 
The Committee, accordingly, wishes to bring to the notice of all 
Ministries/Departments of the Government of India, particularly, the defaulting 
ones, the following recommendations, as contained in its 131
st
,135
th and 152nd 
Reports: 
 
 “Orders required to be laid before the House should be laid (i) if the 
House is not in Session, during the Se ssion immediately following the date 
of publication of the Order in the Offici al Gazette and (ii) if the House is 
in Session, on the date of the pub lication of the Order, during its 
continuance and in case the time l ag between the date of publication and 
the date of the closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the 
expiry of the Session immediately following the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Governme nt of India should henceforth 
comply with this schedule in the matt er of laying of the Orders issued 
under various Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha. 
(131st Report) 
(Presented on 15 December 2000) 
 
 “The Committee has, time and again,  emphasized that it is essential 
that in case there is a delay in th e laying of Statutory Orders, the 
concerned Ministry/Department should invariably append a delay 
statement along with the notificati ons being laid so as to apprise 
Parliament of the causes of delay. The Committee desires all 
Ministries/Departments to take a note of this, since the intension is that 
through the process of formulation of a delay statement the Ministry is 
able to introspect, identify bottlenecks and devise remedial measures”. 
(135th Report) 
(Presented on 27 July 2001) 
 

 
 “In the opinion of the Committee,  every notification should be 
assigned a distinctive number in order to give it a separate identity and 
similarly, no notification should be published/laid which does not carry a 
notification number. The Committee ex pects all Ministries/Departments 
of Government of India to exercise caution in this regard. In case where 
there are more than one notification under a single file, the 
Ministries/Departments should ensu re that the file number on each 
notification should be suitably distinguished from one another. 
(152
nd
 Report) 
(Presented on 26 August 2004)
