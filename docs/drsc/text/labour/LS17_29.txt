5.
 
In their earlier report, the Committee had appreciated that NIFT 
Alumni occup

and Fashion Industry not only in India but in South East Asia and 
remained instrumental in shaping the future of Fashion Business. 
Courses offered by NIFT in the Under Graduate Bachelor of Design 
i

Communication; Post Graduate Masters Degree include Fashion 
Technology, Fashion Management and Design Space; Short Duration 
Part Time Courses in the form of Continuing Education; Bridge 

3

developing Cutting Edge Technologies

super

undoubtedly put the Indian Fashion Industry on a level playing field for 
which NIFT as the pioneering Fashion Institute had an even greater role 
to play.
 
The Committee, therefore, recommended that NIFT leverage and 
expand the courses and programmes offered as per the global standard 
so as to cater to the high expectations of the Nation and rise further on 
the Global Fashion Map.

BUDGETARY SUPPORT
 
 
(Recommendation Para No. 15)

In their original report, the Committee were deeply concerned to 
note that NIFT used to receive various grants from the Government of 

10
 
 
India since 1986
-

-

support for carrying out research and development activities, the 
Committee exhorted the Ministry to strive to ensure adequate fin
ancial 
support to NIFT enabling it to excel in its mission.

In their Action Taken Note furnished to the Committee, the 
Ministry of Textiles have stated as follows:
-
 
“National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) was set up under 
the Ministry of Texti
les, Government of India as a statutory 
Institute governed by NIFT Act, 2006. NIFT, today, has spread its 
wing across the length and breadth of the country.
 
NIFT was started by the Govt. of India in 1986, however, after the 
year 2012
-
2013 no revenue grant 
is provided to NIFT. NIFT is 
meeting revenue expenditure through its internal sources i.e. fee 
income, interest income and research project income etc. NIFT is 
also involved in Research. It will be appropriate if for research in 
Management, Design & Techno
logy Govt. funds are provided to 
have world class lab in its Campuses. Fund support is required for 
research work.”
 
 
17.
 
The Committee are deeply concerned to note that after the 
year 2012
-
13, no revenue grant is provided to the NIFT by 

11
 
 
Government of India
 
and all the revenue expenditure is met through 
NIFT’s internal sources 
i.e
 
fee income, interest income, research 
project income etc. In view of the constraints being faced by NIFT in 
furtherance of its research in management, design and technology 
related
 
activities due to inadequate funding support, the Committee 
once again exhort the Ministry of Textiles to take up the matter 
with the Ministry of Finance to obtain requisite budgetary support 
for NIFT so as to enable the Institute to establish world class
 
labs 
and further excel in its mission.
