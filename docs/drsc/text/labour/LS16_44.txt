2.  The Committee were infor

existing ‘The Cine

3.  The Committee have observed that as per Gazette Notification No. GSR

lump sum or in installments.
 
4.  The Committee observed that the

all the powers exercisable by it, except power to make rules, under this 
Act, to the State Government.  However, the Committee, made it clear 
that the Ministry of Labour and Employment being the nodal Ministry

Ministry of Information & Broadcasting in good time.

the Ministries work in tandem with all concerned agencies who accord

to ensure that infrastructure of all

is  adequately taken care of.
