handling work pertaining to invitation of applications, scrutining, inspection and giving 
accreditation based upon which the NCVT woul

comprehensive portal was created for processing of ITI applications to independent

QCI would 
accredited

online submission of applications, desktop assessment, site assessment, placing eligible

"The Committee are concerned to note that the QCI had compromised with the quality
