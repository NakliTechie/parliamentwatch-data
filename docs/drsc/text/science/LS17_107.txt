R E P O R T
 
The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Ocean Development on the
 recommendations contained in the One Hundred-first Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee
 on Science & Technology, Environment & Forests on the Demands for Grants (2002-2003) of the Department of Ocean
 Development which was presented/laid to both Houses of Parliament on 7
th May, 2002.  There are fifteen recommendations
 contained in One Hundred-first Report of the Committee. Action Taken notes on the recommendations of the Committee
 were received from the Department  on 27
th August, 2002.
The Committee’s recommendations, action taken thereon and comments of the Committee on the action taken by the
 Department of Ocean Development are set out in the succeeding paragraphs: -
 
BUDGETARY ALLOCATION
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that there were major variations in the budgetary allocations and their utilization.  The non-
utilisation of the allocation meant less or reduced activities of the Department.  The Committee is not happy with the reasons
 advanced by the Department for the non-utilization of funds which includes delay in transfer of foreign consultation, change
 of priorities, delay in initiating discussion with the neighbouring countries, non approval and uncertainty of elements.  The
 Committee feels that the Department should have foreseen these difficulties before asking for budgetary allocation and
 should have evolved suitable mechanism to monitor the budgetary proposal and its allocations.
(Para 7)
Action Taken by the Department
 
Regarding the major variations in the budgetary allocations and their utilization, all  efforts will be made to minimize
 such variations.  In respect of National Institute of Ocean Technology where there has been an increase in the actual as
 compared to the BE, the main reason was on account of the additional requirement of the OTEC project.  For this, all
 necessary approvals including for the supplementary demand for grants was taken.
 
ICMAM (World Bank assisted project) Rs. 3.11 Crore
 
The tasks relating to collection of data for ICMAM Plan of Goa and Gulf of Kachchh could not be initiated in 2000-2001
 as more time was taken to finalise the contracts. The Integrated Coastal and Marine Area Management programme being a
 World Bank assisted project, approvals needed to be taken from both government and the world Bank. Since there was
 variance in opinions there was delay in awarding the contracts. Hence the entire funds could not be utilized in the financial
 year under mention. Further, the savings are also on account of reduction in the scope of work in the foreign consultancies.
 
PMN-Unmanned submersible Rs. 4.09 crore
 
The project on the development of a unmanned submersible was approved by the EFC at an estimated cost of Rs. 18.28
 crores. At the time of approval, the Department had a proposal in hand from an institute (EDBOE) under Russian Academy
 of Sciences to develop the equipment/vehicle in technical collaboration with National Institute of Ocean Technology
 (NIOT). Later on, it was felt that it could be better to explore possibilities of technical collaboration from other sources and

Oceanographic Survey – Continental Survey – Continental Shelf –Rs.. 43 crore
 
The unspent balance in relation to the continental shelf programme is on account of various factors. It may be noted that
 Delineation of Outer Limits of Continental Shelf is the international programme and India is expected to gain about 1
 million square km of area in the sea based on the supporting scientific data. The data acquisition is based on the Scientific
 and Technical Guidelines of the Commission on the Limits of Continental Shelf. The issue of the guidelines were delayed
 by the UN Commission on the Limits of Continental Shelf which was beyond the control of Department of Ocean
 development. The scientific and technical guidelines were received towards the end of calendar year 1999 and various
 parameters of the project such as the quantum of work, technical parameters and their specifications, area of operation, types
 of survey and survey requirement were worked out keeping in mind the guidelines as well as the national interest.
Further the Department kept major provision of the programme in the budget for the year 2000-2001 as the work was
 required to be completed on priority basis due to fast approaching last date of submission of claim in June 2005. Later on,
 somewhere towards middle of the year 2000, it was learnt that the extension of the last date for submission of claim is being
 considered and Department felt that it would be appropriate to slow down the pace of ground work and watch for
 development at international level. Thus, the efforts of the Department were diverted towards detailed examination of the
 guidelines, specification of surveys, and inter-departmental and international dialogues instead of taking up the surveys for
 which the budget provision was made. Hence the provision kept for the survey work was surrendered.
 
Comments
 
The Committee has observed that the budgetary support has been surrendered regularly at the end of each financial year
 without spending the single paise by the Department as the project undertaken were not completed for which budgetary
 allocation was sought. Although it is true that the completion of the project was beyond the control of the Department.  In
 this connection Committee would like to suggest that 50% of budgetary allocation should be surrendered at R.E. stage
 instead of holding of budgetary allocation for the full year, if possible.
 
COASTAL RESEARCH VESSELS
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee commends the efforts being made by the Department in utilising the services of two vessels.  However,
 in view of decommissioning of existing vessels after another 10 years, it is time to plan for acquiring new vessels which can
 cater to shallow water survey, data buoy maintenance and provide a platform for various coastal and deep ocean activities
 and other requirements which could reasonably be foreseen in the years ahead. The new vessels would not only serve as a
 platform for the programmes envisaged in the Tenth plan but also serve as a utility vessel which might augment the capacity
 requirements for programmes on the marine living and non-living resources.
(Para 10)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
Noted.  The Department has proposed for a new research vessel in the Xth Plan to be used as a platform for its various
 technology development activities and to augment the capacity requirements for its scientific programmes.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply.  However, it would like the Department to see that the plan for the acquisition of a new
 research vessel is implemented in a time bound manner.

Recommendation
 
The Committee is of the view that in addition to on-going programmes in various domains of polar sciences, National
 Centre for Antarctic Ocean Research (NCAOR) should also initiate cutting-edge experiments in such frontier areas as
 assessment of biotechnological potential of Antarctic microbes, biodegradation of organic wastes using microbial

 consortium and productivity and ecosystem dynamics in the Antarctic lakes.  It should also envisage initiating meaningful
 logistics operation with other countries which could lead to operational and financial cooperation.
(Para 16)
Action Taken by the Department
 
The National Centre for Antarctic & Ocean Research (NCAOR) has been directed by the Department to develop short
 and long term scientific programmes including frontier areas such as assessment of biotechnological potential of Antarctic
 microbes, etc.  Efforts are being made by NCAOR to develop such programmes.  The possibilities of meaningful logistics
 operations with other countries are also being explored.
 
Comments

 of these programmes and see if some more programmes could be taken in hand and provided funds.

The Committee is of the view that the National Centre for Antarctic Ocean research (NCAOR), being the nodal
 Department to execute the Antarctic Programmes should strive to further expand its infrastructure facilities for carrying out
 research in the realms of Polar sciences and Southern Ocean Oceanography.  Besides, the Centre should also initiate its
 activities related to establishing the Country’s first ice-core repository and analytical facilities with focus on high-resolution
 histories of fact changes in climate, atmospheric composition and ice-cover in Antarctica.
(Para 17)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The infrastructure in the form of laboratories and other equipments are being acquired at National Centre for Antarctic &
 Ocean Research and it is expected that construction of ice-core laboratory will be completed within this financial year
 (2002-03).
 
Comments
           
The Committee notes the reply.

 establishments to facilitate treatment of soldiers stationed in cold
regions or for such other uses as might be required in the national interest.
(Para 18)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The data collected so far on impact of cold climate on human body in Antarctica are being transferred to the different
 agencies for their views to facilitate treatment of soldiers stationed in cold regions.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the position and would like to be apprised of the further development in this regard.

 special note of the fact that unless additional information is generated by way of implementation of its recommendation in
 respect of survey, mining, extraction of metals and environmental impact assessment, the over all picture would remain
 incomplete and inconclusive.  Thus a knowledge based upgradation would be necessary with the launching of chain of

COASTAL OCEAN MONITORING AND PREDICTION SYSTEM (COMAPAS)
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that the efforts of the Department to monitor pollution in the coastal areas need to be continued
 vigorously.  The Department should identify locations for periodical monitoring alongwith stipulated parameters for
 diffusion and dispersion characteristics.  Besides, the stations for collection of near-shore samples must be planned with a
 view to reflect the pollution load that was occurring there and the data collected should be processed and forwarded to State
 Pollution Control Boards for taking remedial measures.
(Para 28)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department has been regularly surveying a number of locations for periodical monitoring of marine pollution. 
 These locations are reviewed periodically to focus attention on the areas that are becoming or likely to become contaminated
 in the near future.  Under the revised programme, collection of near shore samples to analyse pollution and related
 parameters in biological organisms has been taken up.  These data are being sent regularly to the State Pollution control
 Boards for taking remedial measures.
 
Comments
           
The Committee notes the reply.

NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF OCEAN TECHNOLOGY (NIOT)
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC) has a rich potential to provide pollution free
 renewable energy to meet the power needs of coastal region as well as island groups.  Therefore, it recommends the
 Department to build energy plants of higher capacity as this might lessen the demand on fossil fuel for power generation. 
 Besides, the higher capacity plants may cost comparatively cheaper in view of the economies of scale.
(Para 36)
 
Action Taken by the Department
Noted.  After the trials are completed on the 1 MW floating plant, plan for upscaling to meet the requirements will be
 worked out.
 
Comments
           
The Committee notes the reply.
 
Recommendation
 The Committee would like the National Institute of Ocean Technology (NIOT) to ensure that its activities especially in
 the area of Deep Sea Mining did not pollute the ocean floor and depletion of fish stocks from their habitats which could
 affect the fishing communities.
(Para 37)
 
Action Taken by the Department
           
Noted.  Environmental Impact  Studies (EIA)is  a part of the Polymettallic Nodules Programme for mining of nodules in
 the seabed.
 
Comments
           
The Committee notes the reply.
 
 
ISLAND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee is of the view that the Department should undertake research on formulation of appropriate feeds for
 maximum growth of lobsters and also develop the larval rearing technology in Indian spiny lobster.  Efforts should also be
 made to implement projects on development of technology for sustainable shrimp farming, lobster and seaweed culture and
 polyculture of marine organisms.
(Para 42)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
Noted.  This is part of the NIOT implemented programme on Ocean Science & Technology for Islands.

INTEGRATED COASTAL AND MARINE AREA MANAGEMENT (ICMAM)
 
Recommendation
      
The Committee observes the need to focus on programmes aimed at imparting training to coastal states on use of
 geographic information system (GIS) for management of critical habitats.
(Para 48)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The training on use of Geographic Information System (GIS) for management of critical habitats are being organized to
 various groups including for the Coastal States.  The Coastal States are approached periodically to avail the training
 facilities.
 
Comments
           
While the Committee notes the reply, it would like an effective mechanism to be evolved to see that the programme
 reaches the targeted groups.  The Department should devise better ways and means to achieve this objective.
 
OCEAN OBSERVATIONS AND INFORMATION SERVICES (OOIS)
 Recommendation
 
The Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS), being the nodal agency for providing coastal and
 ocean data and value added products to the users, the Department should monitor the performance and work-mechanism of
 the INCOIS to process and disseminate ocean data and data products to user communities like fisheries, weather, climate,
 ports shipping etc. on a regular basis so that ocean weather forecasting is precise.
(Para 57)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The activities of the Indian National Centre for Ocean Information services are being monitored through the Governing
 Council of the institute.  Evaluation of performance of the institute and its programme are being done in every meeting of
 the Council.
 
Comments
           
The Committee is of the view that being the nodal agency for providing coastal and ocean data and value added products,
 the INCOIS should play a leading role in bringing better coordination with user agencies so as to achieve optimum results
 for the programme.
 
MARINE RESEARCH AND CAPACITY BUILDING
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee feels that the Department should focus its efforts in assisting Universities to create infrastructure
 facilities and building up a skilled human resource base in Marine Sciences.  Moreover, the Department should also
 participate and showcase its activities in national and international level exposition, exhibition, fairs etc. from time to time. 
 Inter-active computer generated information on Ocean science & Technology could perhaps help educate the public on the
 ocean and its resources.
(Para 59)

Action Taken by the Department
 
Assistance to universities is being given through the MRDF programme in which Ocean science & Technology Cells
 have been set up in universities on specialized topics.  As a result of this scheme there would be specialized facilities,
 equipments besides building up of skilled human resource base.
The Department has been participating in national and international level exhibitions.  During the year 2001-02 the
 Department had participated in India International Trade Fair (IITF)-2001, India International Maritime Expo and the
 Swadeshi Vigyan Mela.  As far as international exhibitions are concerned, the Department had participated in ‘Made in
 India’ Show at Johannesburg, South Africa and ‘India Trade Exhibition’ at Sao Paulo, Brazil.  The Department will
 continue to participate in similar events.  For information to the public on ocean and its resources the Department has also
 developed an interactive computer quiz.
The suggestions of the Committee have been noted for further action.
 
Comments
                       
The Committee notes the reply.
