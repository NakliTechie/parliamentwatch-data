67th Report on Demands for Grants of Department of Electronics
 
The Department of Electronics (DOE)  is responsible for the development of the electronics industry formulation,
 promoting application  of electronics and informative technology,  implementation and review of various programmes/schemes
 in the field of electronics.  These schemes are implemented both in-house as well as through PSUs /Societies  under DOE and
 also through various sponsored programmes being funded under R&D.
2.         The Department is a medium not only to provide a focal point of Government’s attention to electronics sector and also to
 function as a coordinator within and outside the Government, but is also responsible for bringing the benefits of electronics to
 every walk of life, and play a catalytic role to make the Indian Electronics Industry a global player  in the light of the new
 Industrial and Trade Policy.
3.         With delicensing of the entire electronics industry (except aerospace and defence electronics), liberalisation in foreign
 investment and Export-Import policies, the country is witnessing new investments, restructuring of existing activities,
 diversification of the product range and an intensely  competitive environment.  All the renowned global brands have either
 established production facilities in the country or are present in the market through technical/financial collaborations, thus
 giving consumer a wider choice in terms of product features, technology quality and competitive prices.
4.         The thrust , according to  DOE, during the Ninth Plan, would be on Export, Employment and productivity-led growth of
 Electronics and Information Technology.  The electronics and Information Technology sector has the propensity to increase the
 overall growth rate to higher level.
5.            Electronics is the fast growing sector of the Indian industry.  During the Eighth Plan, the electronics industry had a
 cumulative annual growth of 20 per cent in production and over 40 per cent in exports.  The electronics industry achieved a
 production level of Rs. 32,070 crores and an export performance of Rs. 9, 500 crores during the year 1997-98 and is likely to
 achieve a production level of Rs. 41,000 crores during the year 1998-99 registering a growth of over 28% and export
 performance of Rs.12800/- showing a net increase of 70 per cent.  A goal of annual electronics production of Rs. 1,38,350
 crores and exports of about Rs. 49,000 crores has been set for the terminal year of the Ninth Plan (2001-02), representing a
 growth rate of 39% and 52% respectively.

th Five Year Plan, out of which budgetary support

 crores Non-Plan)(See Appendix –II).  The allocation for the year is thus Rs. 51.43 crores more than RE 1998-99.

 has been provided  for setting up of  an  IT Venture Capital Fund .  Besides, an amount of  Rs. 5.00 crores are likely to be spent
 for Electronic Governance. A sum of Rs. 5.09 crores has been provided towards  reimbursement of Central Sales Tax to
 STPs/EHTPs in accordance with the Exim Policy.    This scheme was being handled by Ministry of Commerce till 1996-97 and
 has subsequently been transferred to the Department of  Electronics.  TDC projects, STQC Labs/centre and Ernet India would
 require an additional outlay of Rs. 5.50 crores, 4.00 crores , 5.50 crores and 10.00 crores respectively for their plan expenditure
 and the additional allocation has been proposed  to give thrust to the technology development in the electronic sector  and to
 meet requirements of the approved projects

 earmarked against any of the Ministry/Department of Government of India.  The Department is working on so many

NATIONAL INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE FOR NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT
10.        It has been stated  that during 1998-99 Department of Electronics had initiated a national focus on National Information
 Infrastructure with the aim of promoting use and proliferation of Information Technology.  Some of the sectors proposed to be
 covered  included – literacy, education and life-long learning leading to increase in employment opportunities, better health
 care, environment friendliness, opportunities for rural populace for acquiring new skills, etc.
11.        A multi-institutional National Resources Centre for low cost multimedia content creation has been set up and projects
 for ‘Object oriented toolkit’ for developing Multimedia and Virtual Reality applications and development of an ‘ATM test-bed’
 have been initiated.  In association with the Government of Andhra Pradesh, the Department has supported development of two
 test-beds in the area of Electronic Governance, namely, ‘Development and deployment of multi-purpose, multi-lingual,
 multimedia, scaleable information warehouse for state level governance of Andhra Pradesh’ and ‘Community Information
 Network for Vijaywada Municipal Corporation (VMC)’. 
12.        While the Committee appreciates the efforts made by DOE in initiating a focussed attention on National
 Information Infrastructure in Andhra Pradesh, it would like the Department to continue its efforts in spreading the
 same culture to other states.
 
 
 
 
NATIONAL TASK FORCE ON INFORMATION TECHNOLOY & SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT
13.          The Government has announced promotion of Information technology as one of the five top priorities of the country
 and constituted a National Task Force on Information Technology and Software Development .  Information Technology
 provides an opportunity to achieve the goal of becoming a strong, prosperous and  self-confident nation.   The Task Force
 submitted  108 point Action Plan  which seeks to accomplish inter-alia
(i)            Infrastructure Drive,
(ii)            Target of IT Exports of $ 50 ,
(iii)            IT for all by 2008,
(iv)            opening of Internet Gateway access,
(v)            encouragement to private STPs,
(vi)            zero customs and excise duty on IT software,
(vii)    income tax exemptions to software and service exports,
(viii)            decision to set up a Venture Capital Fund ,
(ix)            A separate fund to handle the Y2K problem,
(x)            gift tax exemption on computers,
(xi)            1-3% of budget of every Ministry/Department for IT applications,
(xii)            networking of all Universities and research institutions,
(xiii)      Dollar linked Stock Option to employees of Indian Software Companies, Sweat Equity,and
(xiv)     new schemes proposed for students which include attractive financial package for buying computer etc.

TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL PROJECTS
17.        The TDCP programme aims to promote and support research, design, development and engineering in the areas of
 computer and computer communications, control and instrumentation, Consumer Electronics , Telecommunication and
 broadcasting.
18.        Asked about the grounds on which the allocation for the Technology Development Council Projects has been increased
 to Rs. 6.80 crores from Rs. 2.50 crores allocated in the Budget Estimate of 1998-99,  the Committee was informed that the
 enhancement of the budget allocation for the Technology Development Council has been sought in view of the recent emphasis
 on developing technologies relevant to the industry.  This would entail funding the projects above the critical level.  Further,
 DOE have recently identified thrust areas of R&D in order to meet  the needs of the industry/users.  In the next year, projects
 would be developed for filling up the technology gaps.

 evinced by the industry/users in the exhibition.  The Committee would like the Department to continue such exhibitions 
 from time to time in the overall interests of the consumers  as well as to promote trade.

 Power, Jute, Textile, Pulp and Paper and Food Processing Industry for modernisation and technology advancement.

 which was raised to Rs. 4.71 crores in the RE of the same year but has been reduced to Rs. 2.90 crores in the current year.

 the  Programme, during the  current financial year  will  concentrate in the area of Plant Wide-Networking, Total Plant
 Solutions - System Engineering, Integration and Services and IT Solutions for the Process Control Sector.  As regards  the
 specific projects envisaged in the above focussed areas the Department  will  endeavored to bring in supplementary financial
 contributions from users/manufacturers to be  tied up as development partners.  Accordingly they have sought reduced
 allocation  in the B.E. of 1999-2000 .

 Regions and the North East.  The objective of the Scheme is to generate  employment opportunities for the educated
 unemployed youth by harnessing the potential of Electronics and Information Technology.  Under the Scheme, free courses are
 offered to SC, ST and OBC candidates.
35.        The Committee was informed the Department of Electronics has identified  certain computer hardware and software
 training courses as well as Electronics related repair and maintenance courses for the educated unemployed youth under its
 Employment Generation Training Scheme.   Efforts are beinginitiated to spread theEmployment Generation Training Scheme
 in deep regions of the North-East, and for this purpose North-eastern Universities have been directed to shortlist the names of
 the affiliated colleges which are conducting B.Sc (Computer Science) and B.Sc.(Electronics) courses.  These colleges would be
 conducting DoE –ACC ‘O’ and ‘A’ Level courses apart from Repair and Maintenance courses in the Electronics area.  Besides,
 a number of new institutions in the North-East have also been short-listed to conduct  training programmes under the DoE’s
 Scheme for Employment Generation.
36.            Department of Electronics is also initiating steps to start non-English language computer training programme in the
 Backward regions and Hindi Heartland States like Rajasthan, UP, Bihar, Orissa and Madhya Pradesh and a number of
 Government educational institutions apart from reputed NGO’s, which are working for the benefit of weaker sections of the
 Society would be included in the Scheme.
37.        The Committee notes with satisfaction the courses proposed to be  started for Physically Handicapped and
 Educated Unemployed women.  The Committee recommends that this programme should be continued with much more
 vigour for these and other deprived sections of the society.
                       
ELECTRONICS TRADE & TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION LTD.(ET&T)
38.        The Electronics Trade and Technology Development Corporation Ltd. (ET&T) was established in 1974 with twin
 objectives of import/export trade and technology development in the field of electronics .  Its initial thrust was to build up
 bilateral rupee trade with the then USSR and other East European countries and to serve as a nodal agency for inter-
governmental electronic trade agreements.  Keeping in view the globalisation of Indian economy and the ideological social and
 economic changes taken place in the East European countries, the company redefined its mission as follows:
            “To provide product and services in the field of electronics, telecommunication and information technology support to
 the end-user sectors with contemporary technology, material and training inputs”.                
39.        ET&T has also successfully completed three months’ full time computer course for 26 foreign students from 14
 developing countries under ITECH SCAAP programme sponsored by Ministry of External Affairs.  Under the aegis of
 Employment Generation Training Scheme of  ET&T started 7 different training programme for Scheduled Castes/Tribes
 candidates free of cost and the scheme is being funded by the Department of Electronics.
40.          ET&T is also conducting various training programmes under “Computer Literacy and Studies in School” (CLASS) at
 more than 200  schools in the States of Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala and schools of Kendriya Vidyalaya
 Sangthan at various locations.
41.        ET&T entered the field of Computer Education & Training during the Year 1994-95 by setting up a modern centre and
 is offering courses in hardware and software.  During early 1996, a new dimension was given to the programme by introducing
 the concept of countrywide setting up of “Licencee Training Centres’.  Currently, the computer education and training activities
 of the Company comprises of:
*            Own Training Centres
*            Country-wide licencee training centres
*          Implementation of Government schemes like CLASS, employment generation schemes, development of North-
East Region etc.
42.        As on 31st March, 1999 , ET&T is having 150  training centres  spread throughout the country.  Currently the computer
 education and training programmes of ET&T are aimed at providing skills in the field of computerisation at the induction level
 and the manpower trained under various programmes are capable of undertaking employment .  ET&T has a Placement Cell for
 providing with the information regarding the employment opportunities, guidance for suitable placement, etc.

 NATIONAL INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE
 
45.        In order to promote use and proliferation of Information Technology the Department has initiated a national focus on
 National  Information Infrastructure which would  cover –literacy, education and life-long learning  to pave the way for 
 increase in employment opportunities, better health care, environment friendliness, opportunities for rural populace for
 acquiring new skills, etc.
46.        A number of  initiatives have been taken in the area of multimedia promotion, electronic governance and ATM test bed.
47.        The DOE propose to undertake the following new programmes
( i)            Technologies for E-Commerce.
(ii)            Online Learning/Distance Education.
(iii)            Digital Library and Contents Creation related initiatives.
48.        In order to increase efficiency in the Government Departments the DoE propose to initiate institutional mechanism
 aimed at facilitating greater use of IT as an enabling tool:-
(1)            Application of  IT to the processes of Government functioning to bring about Simple, Moral, Accountable,
 Responsive, Transparent government (SMART);
(2)            Formulation of comprehensive policy of Electronic Governance (EG);
(3)            Building a comprehensive inventory of initiatives taken up in the country in this regard with a similar effort at
 an international level.
(4)            Development of environment and platform independent workable EG modules for seamless adoption.
(5)            Incubating efforts both in the Ministries in the GOI as well as the State level towards adapting and adopting
 processes of electronic delivery of public services; and
(6)            Comprehensive synergic collaboration of concepts and implementation mechanisms/methodologies are being
 firmed up with professional organisations of international repute.
49.        These efforts are expected to lay the ground work for sustained appropriate and workable models of Electronic
 Governance.  Efforts are also being made  to coordinate initiatives taken by other committees like the High Powered Committee
 under the chairmanship of the Cabinet Secretary as also the IT- Citizen interface Sub Working Group set up under the aegis of
 the National Task Force on IT and Software Development.
50.        It was stated that the initiatives would result in quick and appreciable benefits of delivering public services as well as
 promote efficiency in government functioning through the use of enabling tool of Information Technology.
51.        The Committee observes that against  an allocation of Rs. 24.00 crores in BE of 1998-99 ,   which was
 subsequently  reduced to Rs. 10.00 crores at RE stage an amount of Rs. 20 lakhs only had been utilised .  A provision of
 Rs. 10.00 crores has been made in the current year.  Either the programmes were not realistic or there was under
 utilisation due to other reasons.  The Committee would like to emphasize that having once drawn up the programmes
 the Department should aim at full utilisation of the budgetary provisions.
