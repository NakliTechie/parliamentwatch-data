CENTRAL  SECTOR  SCHEMES/PROJECTS
( A) Space Technology
Recommendation/Observation
1.9 The Committee notes the physical progress made by the Department of Space under the head space
technology during the last three years with satisfaction. The Committee also notes the roadmap shared by the
Department with regard to its proposed activities under this head for the year 2018-19.  The Committee
hopes that the Department would continue its efforts towards making the Indian space technology as pioneer
in the world and make earnest efforts to achieve the targets set for the year 2018-19.
(Para 3.8)
Action Taken
1.10 The observations of the Committee are noted. The Department shall make efforts to achieve the
targets set under the head “Space Technology” for the year 2018-19.
Recommendation/Observation
1.11 On scrutiny of the overall fund utilisation performance of the Department with regard to the budgetary
allocation made under the head Space Technology during the year 2017-18 (upto December, 2017), the
Committee observes that against the total amount of 
` 5984.42 crore, the Department could manage to
spend ` 4336.12 crore which is 72.45 percent utilisation of the budgetary allocation. The Committee, however,
finds that though the overall fund utilisation has been satisfactory yet the fund utilisation under some of the
component of the Space Technology such as ISRO Propulsion Systems Centre (IPSC), Master Control Facility
(MCF), Cartosat-3, RISAT- 1A, OCEANSAT-3&3A, Resourcesat-3S&3SA, Resourcesat-3&3A and High
Resolution Satellite Constellation has been more prudent while utilizing the budgetary allocation for the
intended purpose. (Para 3.10)
Action Taken
1.12 The observations of the Committee are noted. As observed by the Committee, the Department shall
make earnest efforts to ensure that allocations made in 2018-19 are fully utilised under the above mentioned
schemes/projects.
Recommendation/Observation
1.13 The Committee is of the considered view that the realization of financial targets set by any organisation
speaks volume of its financial prudence in the form of making best use of funds made available to it. The
poor funds utilization of the above mentioned components is also indicative that the Department has lost
sight of its financial targets. The Committee, therefore, recommends the Department to do an internal
assessment of its fund utilisation capacity and take desired remedial measures to ensure the optimum utilisation
of the budgetary support. (Para 3.11)

` 6576.02 crore made in BE 2018-19 by making earnest efforts towards the judiciously utilisation of this

(I) Indian Institute of Space Science & Technology (IIST)
Recommendation/Observation
1.29 The Committee, therefore, expresses its displeasure over the routine approach of the Department
which also gets reflected in its inability to utilise the budgetary support made available for IIST year
after year . The Committee, in the light of the inability of the Department to make best use of the budgetary
allocation, opines that the Department lacks a focused approach towards the completion of the developmental

1.30 The recommendations of the Committee regarding the completion of Campus development work
are noted. Considerable progress has been achieved towards the campus development work. Out of the

campus development work and upgradation of Laboratory by optimally utilising the allocation of  ` 110
crore in BE 2018-19.
(II) Semi-Conductor Laboratory (SCL)
Recommendation/Observation
1.31 The Committee notes that the Semi-Conductor Laboratory (SCL) against the budgetary allocation of
` 320.00 crore could manage to utilise only ` 157.50 crore in 2017-18 (upto December, 2017) which is just
49.21 per cent utilisation of the funds earmarked for SCL. The Committee further notes the fact that the
Department in its written reply to the Committee submitted that it had sought 
` 190.00 crore to cater to the
running cost of the facilities and Annual Comprehensive Maintenance of the Fab facility, procurement of
consumables & Spares and outflow on Power, Security, etc; 
` 47.00 crore for creation of Capital Assets
towards Augmentation of VLSI fabrication facility; and  ` 83.00 crore to cater to the annual increments and
DA, however, the Department failed drastically to utilise the funds for the intended purposes. The Committee

7
is of the considered view that had the Department shown little concern towards meeting the obligations for
which it had sought `320.00 crore at RE stage, the fund utilisation performance could have been improved
to a great extent. The Committee strongly feels that the Department needs to shrug off this complacency. The
Committee, accordingly, recommends the Department to take immediate action in the matter and make
sincere and earnest efforts toward optimum utilisation of the RE allocation of ` 320.00 crore during the
remaining period of the financial year 2017-18.                                                                      (Para 4.9)
Action Taken
1.32 Against an allocation of ` 320.00 crores in RE 2017-18, the expenditure under Semi-Conductor
Laboratory (SCL) was ` 157.50 crore upto December, 2017. However, at the end of FY 2017-18, the
expenditure under the scheme was ` 329.00 crore which is about 102.8 % of the RE allocations.
(III) North-Eastern Space Applications Centre (NE-SAC), Shillong
Recommendation/ Observation
1.33 The Committee appreciates NE-SAC for the physical activities being undertaken by it for the overall
growth of the Region. However, scrutiny of the fund utilisation capacity of NE-SAC during the year 2017-18
(upto December , 2017) does not present a very encouraging picture. The Committee notes that out of total
budgetary allocation of 
` 30.00 crore for the year 2017-18, NE-SAC could utilise `18.57 crore only upto
December, 2017. The Committee is not pleased with the fund utilisation capacity of NE-SAC and is of the
considered view that given the track record of good work being done by NE-SAC in the overall well being of
the NER, had NE-SAC been more financially prudent while making optimum utilisation of the funds allocated
for its activities, the funds utilisation would not have been to the tune of just 61.9 per cent upto December ,
2017. The Committee, therefore, recommends NE-SAC to revisit its fund utilisation mechanism and take remedial
action to factor out the reasons leading to underutilisation of the budgetary allocation. The Committee also
recommends the Department to keep a vigil over the financial performance of NE-SAC and ensure that the
budgetary allocation of 
 ` 35.00 crore allocated in BE 2018-19 is utilised fully and optimally.
(Para 4.15)
Action Taken
1.34 As observed by the  Committee, the expenditure under NE-SAC was ` 18.57 crore upto December,
2017 against an allocation of ` 30.00 crores in RE 2017-18. However, at the end of FY 2017-18, NE-SAC
could utilise the entire allocation and the expenditure under the scheme was Rs 32.40 Crore which is about
108% of the RE allocations. As suggested by the Committee, the Department shall keep vigil over progress
of financial performance of NE-SAC and ensure that the budgetary allocation of  ` 35.00 crore in BE 2018-
19 is optimally utilised.

8

fund utilisation capacity of the laboratory.
