REPORT 
 The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Science and Technology 
on the recommendations contained in the One Hundred Seventieth Report of the Department-related 
Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science and Technology, Environment and Forests on the Demands for 
Grants (2007-08) of the Department of Science and Te chnology, which was presented to both the Houses of 
Parliament on 26th April, 2007. There were ten recommendations in the One Hundred Seventieth Report of the 
Committee. Action Taken Notes on the recommendati ons of the Committee were received from the 
Department on 9th August, 2007. 
 The Committee’s recommendations, action taken thereon and comments of the Committee on the Action 
Taken by the Department of Science and Technology are set out in the succeeding paragraphs:----  
NANO SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY INITIATIVE (NSTI) 
1. Recommendation  
 The Committee desires that the Nano Mission proposal made by the Department should be finalised soon. 
The Committee appreciates Indian Institute of Scien ce, Bangalore and Indian Institute of Technology, 
Guwahati for the breakthroughs made on the respective projects assigned to them. It also desires that such 
success stories encourage other institutes to follow suit. 
 The Committee is of the opinion that futuristic technology based on Nano Science holds an important key 
to future development in the world of science. In an increasingly interdependent world where the dividing line 
between academic research and commerc e is increasingly getting blurred, futuristic projects like those based 
on Nano-science can not only raise our living standards but such potential areas can also create wealth for the 
society. The Committee welcomes the idea of separate budget allocation for Nano-mission in budget 2007-08 
as it believes that such a step would help bring the focus back on Nano-research.  
 (Para 9.4.5) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department has mounted National Mission on Nano Science and Nano Technology (Nano-Mission). 
It includes components; such as, Basic Research, Human Resource Development and Education, Nano Centres 
of excellence, Public-Private Part nerships in Industrially important sectors and International R&D 
collaboration. The Mission also plans to put in special  efforts towards development and commercialization of 
Nano Technology, not only through public private partnerships, but also by encouraging and enabling the 
private sector to invest in, and leverage, this sunrise technology. Several potential projects have already been 
sanctioned under the Mission. 
Comments 
 The Committee is of the opinion th at the sanctioned projects should  be put in fast track as the 
advantages in such futuristic project s can only be harnessed if they are completed in competitive time 
framework so that benefits from such innovation can be reaped to the maximum. 
S & T PROGRAMMES FOR SOCIO-ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT 

2. Recommendation  
 The Committee is of the opinion that agriculture sector continues to be the Achilles heel of our economy. 
With very little growth over the year s, we are entering the danger zone of  increased poverty levels in the rural 
sector. It is in this context th at Science and Society Programme of the Department acquires immense 
importance. The idea of eleven scien ce based organizations to nurture S&T personnel to take up challenges to 
work on rural problems and evolving innovative, S&T based solutions can go a long way in addressing the 
failure of agriculture extension services which is one of the reasons for slow growth of agriculture. 
 The Committee also expresses its satisfaction on the work done by the Department in field-testing and 
adaptation of water filter and desires that such projects should be replicated elsewhere. (Para 10.8) 
Action Taken by the Department 
 The Science and Society Programme of the Depart ment continues to strive to set up S&T-based 
organizations to address rural problems through innova tive S&T-driven solutions. Many more core-groups are 
being considered for support for taking up problems in farm and non-farm sectors. These core-groups will also 
nurture S&T personnel to take up challenges to work on rural problems. In addition, the rural needs are also 
being addressed through Women Technology Parks. 
 The Department has undertaken a demonstration project on the use of satellite data on cooperative 
planning and management in Haryana successfully. There is plan to implement successful experiment to other 
neighbouring States as well. 
 The Department would continue to field-test indigenously-developed technologies for extensive 
utilization of such technologies in different parts of the country. 
Comments 
 The Committee would like the Department to facilitate the use of satellite data for end users so that 
such sophisticated technological fa cilities do not remain esoteric and become end-user friendly for the 
masses. The Committee feels that indi genously developed technologies su itable/useful for local needs of 
farming community should be encouraged in coordination with ICAR as much as possible.

TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT BOARD (TDB) 
4. Recommendation  
 The Committee is of the opinion that in a competitive economy assistance to technology entrepreneurship 
is of utmost importance. Symbiosis between research initiatives and industry participation is essential for any 
research to become fruitful. The Committee notes the e fforts made in the area of effluent treatment, water 
treatment and power from municipal solid waste. (Para 13.3) 
Action Taken by the Department 
 TDB is a unique organization under Department of Science and Technology, which has played an 
important role in developing technology oriented ventur es in the country, thereby giving boost to Industry as 
well as techno-entrepreneurs. TDB also has been working on development of symbiosis between research 
institutions and industry by not only providing financial support but also provides guidance through experts for 
research to become fruitful by way of commercializat ion of R&D results from various sources. TDB has been 
making efforts by initiating new initiatives to increase th e reach of TDB to build partnerships with academic, 
research institution and industry. 
 TDB has provided seed support funding to Technology Business Incubators (TBIs) Science and 
Technology Entrepreneurship Parks (STEPs) for pr oviding requisite seed support to incubatees for 
development of their projects, which will ultimately be considered. Further, it has investment in few Venture 
Capital Funds to provide support to commercialize innovati ve and technology oriented ventures as well as to 
seed and early stage ventures. 
 TDB by providing financial assistance to the tune of Rs. 800 crore, created-symbiotic partnerships with 
about 180 industrial concerns and 70 national laboratories and academic institutions in the country. The source 
of technology support by TDB is as under: 
(i) In-house R&D centres of industry and commercialized by

(iii) Individuals  10.5%

(vi) Academic Institution 3.8% 
 Total : 100.0%                           
 
 TDB is committed to promote development and commercialization of innovative technologies in all 
sectors of industry for overall development of the nation.

SCHOLARSHIPS FOR SCIENCE IN HIGHER EDUCATION 
6. Recommendation 
 The Committee supports the idea of widening and streng thening the base of scientific research in the 
country through capacity building in schools, colleges  and universities. The idea  of forming, ‘Science and 
Engineering Research Board’ to fulfil the above objective can be useful and might help in plugging the gaps in 
the supply of quality human resource. (Para 15.3) 
Action Taken by the Department 
 The Department has noted the recommendations of the Committee. As recommended, the Department is 
confident that the establishment of the proposed ‘Science and Engineering Research Board’, which is presently 
at the inter-ministerial consultation level, would widen and strengthen the research base in the country and 
would help reduce the gap between requirements of quality human resources and its supply. 
Comments 
 The Committee desires that the ‘Science and Engi neering Research Board’ provide the necessary 
institutional back up to plug the gap between the requirement and availability of research professionals. 
WATER TECHNOLOGY INITIATIVE 
7. Recommendation  
 The Committee desires that the steps taken by the Department in designing water technologies should 
bear fruits in the near future. Water pollution being a major problem in our country and providing safe drinking 
water a major challenge, the need of the hour is easy availability of low cost domestic water purification 
technology so as to Save people from polluted water and the resultant water borne diseases.  
    (Para 18.4) 
Action Taken by the Department 
 The Department would consciously strive for development of water purification technologies at 
household level so that availability of adequate and safe drinking water is demonstrated widely. The 
Department has already organized a national consultation with scientists and technologists on 28-29 June, 
2007 at Lucknow, which has resulted in identification of specific technologies which could be adopted at field 
level. 

Comments 
 The Committee would like the Department to popularize water purificat ion technologies in 
coordination with all other Depart ments involved in the field so that  they can benefit people in smaller 
towns where drinking water is not purified either  because of lack of  awaren ess or because of costly 
technologies available. 
INNOVATION CLUSTERS 
8. Recommendation 
 The Committee believes that in the fast changing world of today where the growth of technology is 
exponential, both generation of innovative ideas along with their adaptations in the work environment and their 
commercial viability is essential. Helping firms adapt to new production practices is not only important for the 
firms to stay in business but also important to spread a .culture of flexibility and learning so as to remain 
efficient in the long run. The Committee in its interaction with the Department has highlighted the importance 
of not just attracting young people but also empowering them so as to involve them in decision making and 
helping them take up responsibilities so that they can become future leaders. The Committee appreciates the 
idea of innovation clusters to attract young minds. The Committee believes that science research being a 
dynamic field, young scientist should be trained to take fast decision and show functional flexibility.  
 (Para 19.5) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The suggestion of the Committee has been noted by the Department. The Department would integrate the 
suggestion during the implementation of the project. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and woul d like to be apprised in the matter. 
PATENT  FACILITATION  CENTRE (PFC) 
9. Recommendation  
 The Committee attaches great importance to the creati on of Intellectual Property Rights and recommends 
the Department to take necessary steps to ensure that all novel scientific efforts should get translated into 
intellectual property. The Committee would like the Department to set up Technology Intelligence Cell in the 
Department so as to keep track of directions which contemporary science is taking, based on IPR activities. 
    (Para 20.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 Patent Facilitating Centre (PFC) has been providing technical and financial support to all educational 
institutions in the-country for intellectual property rights emanating from innovative research conducted in 
these institutions in India and elsewhere through patents, designs and. copyrights. PFC also helps other 
Government Ministries/Departments in this regard. PFC has been helping DRDO in filing all their patent 
applications and maintaining them. The Department is planning the establishment of an in-house Technology 
Intelligence Cell while engaging also other S & T institutions for data compilation and assessment for tracking 
technology trends and development of technology foresight. 
Comments 

 The Committee notes the reply.
