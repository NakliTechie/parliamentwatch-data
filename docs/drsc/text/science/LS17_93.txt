INTRODUCTION
 
 I, the Chairman of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science & Technology, Environment &
 Forests, having been authorised by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, present this Ninety-third Report on the
 Action Taken by the Department of Biotechnology on the recommendations contained in the Eighty-seventh Report of the
 Committee on Demands for Grants (2001-2002) of the Department of Biotechnology.
 
            The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Biotechnology on the recommendations
 contained in the Eighty-seventh Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science & Technology,
 Environment & Forests on the Demands for Grants (2001-2002) of the Department of Biotechnology which was presented to the
 Rajya Sabha on 18
th April, 2001.

 recommendations of the Committee, were received from the Department on 23rd July, 2001.

 same.

R E P O R T
 
The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Biotechnology on the
 recommendations contained in the Eighty-Seventh Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee
 on Science & Technology, Environment & Forests on the Demands for Grants (2001-2002) of the Department of
 Biotechnology which was presented to the Rajya Sabha on 18th April, 2001 and laid on the Lok Sabha on the 18 th April,
 2001.  There are sixteen recommendations contained in the eighty-Seventh Report of the Committee. Action Taken notes
 on the recommendations of the Committee were received from the Department on 23rd July, 2001.
 
The Committee’s recommendations, action taken thereon and comments of the Committee on the action taken by
 the Department of Biotechnology are set out in the succeeding paragraphs: -
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that it is not opposed to enhanced allocations to the Department. While it is necessary
 that the Department keeps itself posted with the developments abroad in the field of biotechnology, it should
 choose projects with utmost caution with a view to ensure that they are in the best interests of the country.
                 
(Para 10)
 
Action Taken by the Department

The Department has been from the very beginning interacting with all the concerned scientific agencies, user
 Ministries/Departments, national laboratories, universities, state level institutions and NGOs to identify and prioritize
 programmes considering needs and interests of the country. The department is advised by a Scientific Advisory
 Committee. There is also a Standing Advisory Committee (Overseas), a novel feature of this department with non-resident
 Indian scientists as its members to give advice and inputs on the developments abroad in the field of biotechnology. The
 Biotechnology Research and Promotion Committee (BRPC), a High Powered Committee of inter-disciplinary experts and
 16 subject specific Task Forces are also operational to identify, recommend, review and monitor projects in the best
 interest of the country. Every effort has been made to keep itself abreast with latest development world over.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply and commends the efforts being made by the Department in
 involving non-resident Indians in its programmes.
 
Recommendation
 
As regards the programmes under the forest trees the same need to be developed extensively with a view to
 increase the forest cover. This, in turn, would help in arresting ecological imbalances. Besides, the technology be
 made available for social forestry programmes.                 
       
(Para 17)
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department has a major programme on forest tree improvement. Tissue culture regeneration protocols have been
 standardized for a large number of economically important tree species and these are now being adopted for large scale
 multiplication. The tissue culture pilot plants set up by the Department at Tata Energy Research Institute, New Delhi and
 National Chemical Laboratory, Pune which were later converted to Micro propagation Technology Parks are producing
 large quantities of elite planting material of forest trees. Approx. 70 lakhs plants have been produced so far and field
 planted over an area of 8000 ha. at multi- locations in 17 states across the country.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply. However, it would like the Department to focus on the States facing problems of
 deforestation or environmental degradation in any other form.
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee is constrained to observe that the leprosy vaccine developed by the National Institute of
 Immunology is yet to be included by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare in its leprosy control programme.
 The Committee has been given to understand that voluntary agencies have made use of 24 lakhs doses of the
 vaccines whereas the Ministry of Health continues to import the vaccines instead of making use of the one
 developed by the National Institute of Immunology. If it is really so, the Committee observes that it constitutes
 draining out of the precious foreign exchange from the national exchequer. This needs to be pursued with the
 Ministry of Health on priority basis.
 
                                                                                                                        (Para 19)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department has already approached Ministry of Health & Family Welfare at the highest level with the request
 to make use of, LAPROVAC in the National Leprosy Eradication Programme, as a therapeutic immunomodulator.

Comments
 
            The Committee notes with concern that the outcome of the efforts made at the highest level with the
 Ministry of Health and Family Welfare to make use of LAPROVAC in the National Leprosy Eradication
 Programme has not been indicated.  The Committee apprehends that the Department has either not succeeded in
 persuading the Ministry of Health to make use of LAPROVAC or something is amiss that Ministry of Health finds
 it difficult to make use of indigenous supplies of LAPROVAC.  The Committee would like the Department to take
 up the matter again with the Ministry of Health again and, if necessary, approach the Cabinet Secretariat for
 advice or for placing the matter before the Cabinet.  The Committee would like to be apprised of the outcome.
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee commends the work done by the Department in the field of rice genome sequencing and observes that more
 such programmes need to be undertaken especially in crops like pulses which are in short supply in the country, maize, oilseeds,
 and other dry farming crops keeping in view the extremely varied climate, cropping patterns, variety of crops available in the
 country.
                                                                                                 (Para 22)  
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Rice Genome is the smallest of the most economically important cereals like wheat, barley, maize and
 sorghum. There is considerable conservation of gene order among these genomes and hence the information gained from
 rice will be helpful in understanding other cereal genomes. The outcome of sequencing  programme would be
 identification of many genes of interest particularly yield component, plant architecture, resistance to stresses and pests,
 diseases, drought, salinity etc.
 
So far as legumes are concerned, functional genomics of chickpea has received high priority at National Centre for
 Plant Genome Research (NCPGR), New Delhi. The EST, sequences, characterization of putative chickpea disease
 resistant gene and cloning of molecular markers associated with plant differentiation are the ongoing activities. The
 Department at the moment does not have any programme on genomics of maize. However' it is intended to have a
 collaborative programme with CIMMYT, Mexico - the applied Biotechnological Centre which is working on molecular
 markers, genetic engineering and apomixis of maize.
 
In the area of wheat genomics, one of the Department supported Centres has recently become a member of
 International Wheat Microsatellite Consortium (IWMC). This initiative has received positive response from the other
 members of IWMC.
 
Comments
 
The Committee commends the efforts made by the Department in the field of Rice and wheat genomics.  The
 Committee observes that the Department should continue to explore more areas of Genetic Engineering in
 furthering the research.
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee has perused the information available in respect of Patent Cell over the internet. While all the
 institutions etc. can be accessed and through them the homepage of the Department, the home page is not directly
 accessible for some reason of confidentiality attached to it or because of some technical reasons. This needs to be
 looked into. The Committee is also of the view that the efforts be made to make the scientists aware of the need for
 taking patents and procedures for applying a patent must be simplified and displayed over the Internet along with
 facility for downloading of forms as far as possible. The information about the patents applied must also be made
 available which is perhaps not available at present.
                                                                                                                                            (Para 25)

Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department has easily and directly accessible website over the internet. Apart from all the relevant information
 about Department, it contains the format of the application for patent filing both in pdf and html. formats. The hard copy
 of the format is circulated to interested organizations, as and when they require and for reference in their libraries. To
 encourage scientists to patent their innovations and create awareness on the procedures four National Roving seminars
 and an equal number of lectures were organized in the last two years. Two publications brought out for this purpose have
 been widely circulated. A simplified and supportive system is already in place to facilitate patenting. The information
 regarding the patents applied cannot be made available on the internet for the reasons of confidentiality. The Department
 is having bilingual (English and Hindi) website with the addresses www.dbtindia.nic.in
 and www.dbtindia.org
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply and would like to point out that the Committee has not at all questioned the
 confidentiality about the patents applied or otherwise.  What was pointed out is that the homepage of the website
 should be easily accessible.  If this too is confidential then there is no point in having any website whatsoever.
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that efforts be made for developing cheaper technologies in waste treatment to enable
 industries to readily accept and install the same. As oil spill/sludge has become an important cause of pollution in
 the high seas in the recent years and is liable to affect the marine life, Oil Zapper could be marketed worldwide to
 contain oil spills.
                                                                                                                                            
 (Para 27)
Action Taken by the Department
 
Oilzapper technology developed by Tata Energy Research institute (TERI), New Delhi has already been transferred
 to Sriram Biotech Ltd. Hyderabad and Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (BPCL), Mumbai. Sriram Biotech Ltd. is
 already supplying oilzapper within the country. In addition, these companies have received requests from Pakistan, Saudi
 Arabia, Philippines, Malaysia which are being negotiated. Further, cost effective technologies for ecorestoration of mine
 dumps, starch factory waste water treatment, dye industry effluent treatment, desulphurisation of biogas have been
 developed and demonstrated to user industries.
 
Comments
 
            The Committee notes with satisfaction the efforts made by the Department. 
Recommendation
The Committee commends the progress made in the field of vaccine development specially the edible vaccines. 
 The Department should endeavour to pass on these technologies to industry for marketing purposes. The
 Committee observes the need to develop more such vaccines for diseases like Hepatitis B virus, Hepatitis E virus
 that are highly infectious.
                                                                                                                                  (Para 30)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department has already taken action to expand the programme on edible vaccines so as to include important
 vaccines for several infectious diseases. As and when technology is ready for transfer to the industry, appropriate action
 will be taken.
 
Comments

Recommendation
 
While the Committee is satisfied with the programme related to setting up of Biotechnology Parks, it
 emphasizes the need for such Parks if they help the cause of the development of wasteland in villages which could
 possibly be another area of augmenting the income of farmers/women in rural areas.
                                                                                                                                            (Para 32)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
Several projects are operational for the development of wasteland in villages for economic benefit to the farmers
 and women in rural areas. The Department is framing more programmes/schemes for the benefit of the farmers, women
 and weaker sections of the society, keeping in view their location specific problems and circumstances.    As and when the
 proposals will be received from the State Governments and other organizations, emphasis will be given on these
 recommendations.
 
Comments
 
            The Committee notes the reply.
Recommendation
 
The Committee commends the concept of Biovillage taken up by the Department. The Committee however feels the
 need for more such projects being undertaken in those villages where chemical fertilizer is abundantly used. Training also
 needs to be provided for use of the bio-fertilizers in view of their minimal impact on environment.
                                                                                                                                            (Para 34)
Action Taken by the Department
 
Training and use of biofertilizers is an integral part of Biovillege project. Programmes are implemented in Madhya
 Pradesh and West Bengal. A network project on Integrated Nutrient Management has also been implemented at 7 centres
 based on important cropping patterns of the country. Through this initiative, various packages have been developed for
 effective use of biofertilizers, resulting in reduction of chemical fertilizer consumption.
 
Comments
 
            The Committee notes the reply.
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee feels that the country must aim to regain its lost glory in silk trade as it has the advantage of
 production of all four own commercial silk varieties viz; mulberry, tusser, eri and muga. As sericulture is suitable
 for marginal, small and landless farmers, it has tremendous scope for expansion in view of its employment
 generation potential. Efforts should therefore be made to strengthen the linkages between extension and R&D
 units. The technology delivery systems should be streamlined by providing need based training and effective field
 demonstrations by using various communication methods.
                                                                                                                                            (Para 36)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department is working closely with the Central Silk Board (CSB)- the user agency for sericulture from
 identification of R&D programmes to their implementation. The department is providing R&D inputs to CSB. Further

Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that India is one of the first countries in the world to initiate a Bioinformatics
 programme. Besides, given the advantages the country has in IT sector and its expertise in mathematics and high
 knowledge base, special emphasis needs to be given to this aspect as sustainability and dissemination of the data is
 necessary for the success of plans. The researchers should also have the access to updated knowledge to keep
 themselves abreast of research elsewhere in the world. This would help avoid duplicating the research and in
 patenting the research already conducted.
                                                                                                                    (Para 38)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The department has so far established 55 Bioinformates Centre spread across all over the country with necessary
 computer and communication infrastructure and sufficient manpower to handle the IT applications in the area of
 Biotechnology and modern Biology. These centre are well equipped to collect, analyse and disseminate information
 recourses to researchers both from national and international information sources. The department also provides support
 to R&D activities  in Bioinformatics to utilise the expertise available from the IT sector towards development of software
 packages and other tools for bioinformatics and biotechnology applications.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply but it would like the Department to continue efforts aimed at providing the
 latest information in relevant fields to help researchers.
 
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that studies conducted under the bilateral cooperation should aim at research in
 developing drugs for diseases that are particular to our country such as Kala-azar, plague, tuberculosis & malaria
 etc.
                                                                                                                                            (Para 41)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The efforts have been made under the bilateral /multilateral cooperation to collaborate in tropical diseases prevalent
 in India and the region. Under bilateral collaboration with Sri Lanka malaria is an area of priority research. Under INDO-
US Vaccine Action Programme vaccines and diagnostics are targeted towards major tropical diseases like tuberculosis,
 malaria, HIV, hepatitis virus, laishmaniasis etc.
 
Comments
 
            The Committee notes with satisfaction the efforts made by the Department and hopes that more and more
 countries would be invited to join bilateral/multilateral cooperation to fight the menace of the diseases affecting the
 region.

Recommendation
The Committee expresses its satisfaction with the work being done by the Centre for DNA Fingerprinting and
 Diagnostics (CDFD) in the field of Automated Genome Analysis,  Bioinformatics   Diagnostics,   DNA
 Fingerprinting, Instrumentation and Research in, among other areas, cancer biology, computational biology and
 Immunology etc. The Committee however feels that the country's population having touched 1020 million and as
 the crimes, conflicts and paternity disputes are expected to arise with more frequency with the rising pace of
 population, it was time to consider whether there was need to set up full fledged institutes to provide the services to
 the institutions and people expeditiously and with minimum of costs.
                                                                                                                        (Para 45 & 46)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The Department envisages CDFD to become a referral centre for DNA fingerprinting activities. It is expected that
 the institute will provide guide to other DNA fingerprinting laboratories. An Advisory Committee is being set up to assess
 the requirement of DNA fingerprinting, profiling and diagnostic activities.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply.
Recommendation
The Committee commends the progress made by BIBCOL from a company facing financial crunch to revenue
 generating one. The Committee is happy to note that it is able to sustain its regular activities. The Committee
 reiterates its earlier recommendation that efforts must be made to produce new & improved vaccines and
 immunodiagnostics such as rabies measles, recombinant Hepatitis 'B' vaccine, etc.
                                                                                                                                            (Para 53)
 
Action Taken by the Department
 
The company has been registered under Board for Industrial and Financial Reconstruction (BIFR). The BIFR has
 appointed IDBI as Operating Agency to prepare the revival scheme of the company. The Operating Agency submitted
 financial restructuring and product diversification plan to BIFR. Action will be taken on this as and when the BIFR and
 Govt. approve.
 
Comments
 
The Committee notes the reply.
Recommendation
 
The Committee observes that as the programmes of the Department are liable to benefit different sections of the
 society, the Department must take initiative to approach Doordarshan for at least a few minutes slot to disseminate
 information on at least one programme every day in a bid to reach the sections of the society for whom it was meant.
                                                                                                                                    (Para 54)
Action Taken by the Department
The Department has taken initiative for dissemination of information of its programmes and schemes over
 Doordarshan in its Turning Point and Gyan Darshan sections. The Department will make all efforts for giving wide
 publicity over Doordarshan. A major programme on popularization of biotechnology has been formulated for
 implementation.
