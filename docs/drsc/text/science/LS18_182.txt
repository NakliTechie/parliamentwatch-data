REPORT 
 The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Space on the 
recommendations contained in the One Hundred Seventy-fifth Report of the Department Related 
Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science and Technology, Environment and Forests on the Demands for 
Grants (2007-2008) of the Department of Space, which was presented to both the houses of Parliament on 26th 
April, 2007. The One Hundred Seventy-fifth Report of the Committee contained ten recommendations. Action 
Taken notes on the recommendations of the Committee were received from the Department on 14th August, 
2007. 
 The Committee’s recommendations, action taken thereon and comments of the Committee on the Action 
Taken by the Department of Space are set out in the succeeding paragraphs:- 
ELEVENTH FIVE YEAR PLAN (2007-12) PROPOSALS 
1. Recommendation 
 The Committee desires that the overall thrust of the Space Programme during Eleventh plan should be to 
sustain and strengthen the already established space based services towards socio-economic development of 
the country. Efforts should also be made towards increasing the commercial prospects of the developments of 
the country in the field of space science specially in the area of Launch Vehicle Development. The Committee 
also desires that the country scale new heights during the Eleventh plan period in the space applications for 
communication, broadcasting, remote sensing, launch vehicle technology, etc. (Para 13.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 Noted. The Eleventh five year plan of the sp ace programme envisages major thrust on societal 
applications of space technology in cluding Tele-education, Tele-medicine and Village Resource Centres. 
Several new initiates in the areas of Satellite Navigation, advanced communication technologies, planetary 
exploration, microwave remote sensing and advanced launch vehicle systems have been planned during the 
Eleventh plan which would upgrade India’s technological capability in Space Research. The successful launch 
of Italian Agile satellite onboard PSLV C8 on 23rd April, 2007 from Sriharikota is a land mark achievement in 
commercialization which has enhanced the marketing prospects of Indian launch vehicles. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and is of the view  that during the Eleventh  Plan Period, efforts 
should be made towards ensuring th at benefits of the Space programme percolate to all the sections of 
the society, which would help in the overall socio-economic development of the country. 
MONITORING MECHANISM FOR ONGOING SCHEMES 
2. Recommendation  
 The Committee desires that the existing reviewing mechanisms developed by the Department to monitor 
and review the progress of the ongoing schemes/projects before formulating/finalizing proposals for budgetary 
support should be strengthened to eliminate the scope of shortage or surrender of funds at a later stage.  
 (Para 14.2) 

Action Taken by the Government 
 Noted. The Department has initiated a system of reviewing the targets for commitments and expenditure 
of various projects/schemes on quarterly basis from the very beginning of the year to ensure that funds 
allocated for plan schemes are not surrendered and shortage of funds, if any, for the approved plans and 
schemes. is met through appropriate savings/reappropriations. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and wo uld like that compliance is ensured. 
SPACE CAPSULE RECOVERY (SRE-l) EXPERIMENT 
3. Recommendation 
 The Committee commends the role of the Indian Space Research Scientists in successfully conducting 
the SRE-I experiments and desires that the technology demonstrated through the experiment should provide 
inputs for the design of reusable launch vehicles and manned space missions in near future. (Para 15.2) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 SRE was intended for demonstrating the capability to recover an orbiting space capsule. SRE-l which 
was launched using PSLV-C7 was successfully recovered on January 22, 2007 after being maneuvered to re-
enter the earth’s atmosphere and descend over Bay of Bengal about 140 km east of Sriharikota. A number of 
critical technologies have been tested and validated th rough this experiment The re-usable thermal protection 
system (TPS), Navigation, guidance and control, Hypersonic aerothermodynamics, managing communication 
blackout, deceleration and recovery operations are some of the technologies which were demonstrated. The 
data which have been obtained from this SRE-l experiment are being studied in detail for upgrading the 
standards and technological requirements of future missions. Some of these inputs will assist in the 
development of basic technologies needed for future lifting body and winged re-entry mission such as RLV 
(Reusable Launch Vehicle) and manned space mission. 
 RLV - TD (Reusable Launch Vehicle Technology Demonstrator) project is progressing well. The design 
Phase has been completed and sub-systems are in various stages of realization. 
 A study team has been constituted for manned mission programme. The pre project activities have been 
initiated. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would lik e to be apprised of the progress made. 
CHANDRAYAAN-I 
4. Recommendation 
 The Committee desires that the Chandrayaan-l mi ssion progresses as per sc hedule and the spacecraft 
should be ready for launch in 2008. The Committee de sires that the Chandrayaa n-l mission should upgrade 
India’s space technological capabilities and would in future help in launching manned missions to moon.  
 (Para 16.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 

 The setting-up of deep space communication network required for Chandrayaan-l is in advanced stages. 
The fabrication of spacecraft and scientific instruments of Chandrayaan-1 are in pr ogress. As per the present 
status, Chandrayaan-l is targeted for launch onboard India’s PSLV in 2008. As planned, the Chandrayaan-l 
will pave way for development of several new technologies including advanced propulsion technologies, 
hyper-spectral sensors, spacecraft control and mission analysis, large diameter antenna technologies for ground 
communication systems and scientific data processing/analysis. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like to see that Chandrayaan- 1 will be launched during 
the targeted period i.e., 2008. 
TELE-EDUCA TION 
5. Recommendation  
 The Committee feels that through the EDUSAT a long-felt need to provide connectivity and transfer of 
knowledge among the technical/medical institutes would be met. This would help the students in 
technical/medical institutes which lack infrastructure to get quality education. The Committee further desires 
that the proposal for connecting schools through the EDUSAT programme should be expedited in coordination 
with the Ministry of Human Resource Development and attention should be paid towards including schools in 
the backward and rural areas. (Para 17.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 EDUSAT besides connecting technical and medical institutions also provides services to primary 
and secondary schools. 
 EDUSAT school network has been gathering momentum and many State Governments are coming 
forward for implementing EDUSAT network for schools and provide quality education for primary 
and secondary school children. 
 Government of Haryana has provided EDUSAT services to about 9000 schools which cover all 
Government schools in the remote areas. 
 Government of Andhra Pradesh has already connected 1800 schools and is expanding their network 
to 5000 schools in the first phase and 3000 more schools in the second phase. 
 Government of Karnataka has provided EDUSAT connectivity to 2000 schools and 1000 more 
schools are being added in the next one year. 
 About 900 schools in Bihar, Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh are receiving EDUSAT network 
programmes in their school network. 
 About 600 schools in Kerala are receiving education through EDUSAT network. 
 About 30 schools in Lakshadweep Isla nds are provided with EDUSAT facility. 
 About 1000 schools in Gujarat are having connectivity to receive EDUSAT programmes. 

 ISRO is coordinating with Ministry of Human Resource Development to expand all school 
networks in all these States. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes that the EDUSAT programme  for the primary and secondary schools is 
being implemented in a few States /Union Territories only. The Commi ttee would like that the reach of 
the EDUSAT programme should be  extended expeditiousl y, to cover the remaining States/Union 
Territories specially –– the remote, backward and rural areas. 
TELE-MEDICINE 
6. Recommendation 
 The Committee feels that in a country like India, invest ment in a highly capital intensive area like Public 
Health may not be always possible, particularly in the backward areas of the country. Tele-medicine provides 
an excellent alternative option to provide the services of the best medical specialists to the farthest corner of 
the country. The Committee therefore recommends that th e tele-medicine network should be expanded further 
to include the areas which have been left out so far. (Para 18.2) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 Tele-medicine network is being expanded to cover more remote areas and also use of Mobile Tele-
medicine Vans which can reach interiors is being enco uraged. About 30 more Tele-medicine nodes have been 
added in the Tele-medicine network in the last 3 months. Tele-medicine has also been added as a component of 
the services provided in the Village Resource Centre  (VRC) Network. Presentl y about 200 VRC networks 
have been provided with Tele-medicine facility. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like specific information about the progress made regarding 
the expansion of the tele-medicine network. 
DISASTER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM 
7. Recommendation  
 The Committee understands that a lot of economic devastation and human loss is caused regularly by the 
floods, cyclones, storms, etc. In th is direction, the Committee recommends that the work relating to the 
development of the Airborne Synthetic Aperture Radar for aircraft survey for di saster management and the 
setting up of Virtual Private Network (VPN) may be expedited, particularly in view of the increased budgetary 
allocations, so that the country may derive the benefits of the Disaster Management System (DMS) at the 
earliest. The Committee also recommends that when th e DMS is in place, proper attention may be given 
towards the quick dissemination of information to the District Headquarters after the data is obtained from the 
satellites, so that human and material losses can be minimized. (Para 19.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 Noted. The development/semi operational model of the C band Airborne Synthetic Aperture Radar 
(ASAR) has been completed and is currently under exte nsive testing. A Near Real Time Processor for the 

ASAR data has also been developed. Under the Virtual Private Network (VPN), 27 nodes have already been 
set up as a part of first phase. In the next phase, in  association with Ministry of Home Affairs and National 
Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), further expansion of the network to multi-hazard prone district head 
quarters is being taken up. The information pertaining to satellite based disaster impact assessment is 
disseminated routinely to the concerned District Collectors. 
Comments 
 The Committee is of the view th at better co-ordination between th e Ministry of Home Affairs and 
the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), is  essential, for the successful implementation of 
the Disaster Management System (DMS), Proper ut ilization of the technical information regarding 
impending natural disasters received from satellites needs to be given prime importance. 
INDIAN INSTITUTE OF SPACE SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY 
8. Recommendation  
 The Committee commends the Department on the setting up of a specialized Institute to generate the 
quality manpower required for the Space Programme. The Co mmittee observes that only one institute with an 
intake of around 20 is proposed to be set up and ther efore recommends that the possibility of setting up of 
more such institutes in the rest of the country may be explored. The intake of students may also be increased 
gradually to cater to the growing requirements of space scientists. (Para 20.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department has noted the recommendations of the Committee. It is clarified that the annual intake 
approved for Indian Institute of Space Technology is 15 0 to 200. The Institute will start functioning from the 
academic year 2007-08 as planned. 
Comments 
 The Committee reiterates that the Department should explore the fe asibility of setting up of more 
specialized institutes serving specifically to the growing requireme nts of technically qualified manpower 
for expanding space programme.
