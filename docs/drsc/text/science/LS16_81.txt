2.The Committee took up consideration of the draft Eightieth, Eighty-first, Eighty-second and Eighty-third reports on
 Preservation of Gir Forests and Protection of Wild-Life (Ministry of Environment & Forests), Action Taken by the
 Department of Science & technology on the recommendations contained in the 72 nd Report of the Committee on
 Demands for Grants (2000-2001), Action Taken by the Department of Scientific & Industrial Research on the
 recommendations contained in the 73rd Report of the Committee on Demands for Grants(2000-2001) and Action Taken
 by the Department ofBiotechnology on the recommendations contained in the 74 thReport of the Committee on
 Demands for Grants(2000-2001) respectively and adopted the same.
3.The Committee decided to lay/present the above Reports in the week commencing 11
th December, 2000.For this
 purpose, the Committee authorized its Chairman to present the 80 th, 81st , 82 ndand 83rd Report in Rajya Sabha and in
 his absence, the Committee authorized Smt. Savita Sharda, Shri Sukhdev Singh Libra and Shri Birbhadra Singh to
 present the Reports.The Committee authorized Prof. R.R. Pramanik to lay 80 th , 81 st, 82 nd and 83 rd Reports in Lok
 Sabha and in his absence the Committee authorized Dr.(Smt.) Beatrix D’ Souza, Shri Lakshman Sethand Shri
 Venkatesh Naikto lay the Reports. 
 
4.The Committee also discussed tentatively to undertakea study visit to different institutes/bodies/centers etc., within the
 administrative control of the Ministries/Departments falling under the purview of the Committee with a view to
 acquaint itself with their working andhold discussions with certain institutions/ individuals/ organisations on the subject
 matter of Biological Diversity Bill-2000, tentatively from 10
th to 20thJanuary, 2001 to Raipur, Bastar in Chattisgarh;
 Jabalpur, Gwalior, Bhopal, Panchmarhi in Madhya Pradesh and Nagpur in Maharashtra. The Committee authorised
 Chairman of the Committee to approach Hon’ble Chairman for seeking necessary permission in this regard.
5.The Committee then adjourned at 10.00 a.m.
