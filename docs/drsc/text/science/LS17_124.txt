FUND FOR THE IMPROVEMENT OF S&T IN FRASTRUCTURE IN UNIVERSITIES AND
OTHER HIGHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS (FIST) 
Recommendation 
            The Committee notes with constr aint that the quality of in frastructure available in ou r
Universities and research laborator ies for pursuing scientific resear ch is not upto the mark. Lack o f
interest on the part of students to go for science education is indeed alarming.  The Department is taking
steps to encourage young scientists  to pursue serious sc ientific work.  The Committee notes with
concern the decline of the quality of  our research papers.  There is an  urgent need to take concerted
efforts to arrest this trend to give new dynamism and momentum to Indian science. 
(Para 10)
Action taken by the Department 
            The Scheme ‘Fund for Improvement of S&T In frastructure in Univ ersities and Highe r
Educational Institutions’ was initiated in the year 2000 to address these concerns.  The scheme has so far
identified and supported about 460 Departments at various universitie s and related higher educational
institutions in the country.  The supports are mainly provided for st rengthening of infrastructure and
equipments, acquisition of comput ation networking facilities, mode rnisation of library, providing good
quality teaching material, enha ncement of research facili ties in this departments.   While majority of the
Departments supported so far are now  in the process of acquiring thes e facilities, a few departments
have already acquired the same.   Towards monitoring and reviewing of  the progress, these departments
have now submitted the Technical Progress Report, which are being evaluated along with the visit of the
sites by the area Experts. 
In 2003-04, the fresh proposals re ceived from the prospective Departments/Centres of the 
Universities and related acad emic institutions are be ing processed for consideration of support in the
current financial year. 
Comments 
The Committee feels that the Department shoul d monitor and supervise the Technical Progress
Report to evaluate the performanc e of the schemes being implemente d to prevent the misuse of the
funds made available under the schemes.

a clear pointer towards adopting greater vigilance for protecting our biodivers ity.  The Committee
recommends that the Department ma y augment its own efforts in this  regard and give due publicity to
such activities for the benefit of scientists, resear chers and the general public.   After all to save ou r
traditional knowledge we will not only depend on scie ntific community but also on women, rural fol k
and people and individuals. 
(Para 25)
Action taken by the Department 
            The Department will augment its efforts in creating awareness about Intellectual Property Rights
(IPR) among scientists and researcher s.  Attempts are also being ma de to create awareness in schools
and general public.

 JOINT TECHNOLOGY PROGRAMME 
Recommendation 
            While the Committee notes with sa tisfaction that the Department is engaged in producing and

given on the issue of producing clean  and eco-friendly technologies fo r sustainable development of ou r
country.  Increasingly, we have to follow the path  of eco-friendly industrial revolution.  For ou r 
sustainable development eco-friendl y industrial revolution seems to be  a necessity.  Therefore, the
Committee recommends that the Department must  concentrate its attent ion towards producing such
technologies which will be consistent with ecology and industrial development. 
(Para 28)
Action taken by the Department 
The recommendation is noted and this Departme nt would give special focus on producing clean
and eco-friendly technologies for sustainable development. It may be pertinent to mention here that DST 
has already initiated activities in th e line of clean and eco-friendly tec hnologies in the area of alternate 
fuels for substitution of diesel by the non-edible oil from karanj seed s, promotion of pollution control
technologies for cupola, rice shelling and brick kiln  industry, clean disposal of arsenic and fluoride
sludge, plastic waste disposal using plasma pyrolysis technology, use of micro hydel for powe r

systems development for eco-friendly industrial development. 
Comments 
The Committee feels that the technologies developed should be disseminated to industry and end
users at the earliest for the wider acceptance. 
TECHNOLOGY FOR BAMBOO PRODUCTS 
Recommendation 
            The committee was informed that while some of  the national centres of excellence in India
possess rich expertise in bamboo technology, the knowledge flow to the industry has not come up to the
desired level. As such it is high time to bring the technology sourcing avenues and industries fo r
technology development, transfer and commercialization. Thus the aim is to undertake a programme at
the national level which could impa rt a significant boost to the usage of bamboo, promote specialized
products for commercialization and would generate good employment opportunities. 
(Para 33)
Action taken by the Department 
To bring together technology sourcing avenues and industrie s for technology development,
transfer and commercialization, and to provide a si gnificant boost to the usage of bamboo and its value
added products, a major initiative has been ta ken in the form of Na tional Mission on Bamboo
Applications (NMBA). Th e task of implem enting the Mission has been en trusted to the TIFAC, an
autonomous organisation under Depa rtment of Science & Technolo gy. The Mission has been devolved
to provide a new impetus and direction to the bamboo sector by seeking to:

and cultivation (d) processing of edible bamboo shoots (e) development and commercialization of value
added products. 
The Mission has also taken up a programme of development of indigenous machinery fo r

b) slivers for woven applications have been established and evalua ted, and are now available through
local manufacturers. 
The Mission, in collaboration with the Indian Plywood Industries Research and Training Institute
(IPIRTI), Bangalore has taken up an exercise to develop validat e and document process technology fo r
manufacture of bamboo composite boa rds, from sympodial bamboo of commercially significant species
available in India to be able to offer techno-economic packages of support to prospective entrepreneurs.

THE PHARMACEUTICALS RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT FUND (PRDSF) 
Recommendation 
            The Committee commends the Department for re orienting its strategy to produce molecules fo r 
production of drugs so that it can meet the challenges of product patent arising out of the enforcement of
intellectual property rights from December, 2004.  Wh ile the Committee notes that the Department has
earmarked Rs.150 crores as corpus  fund for pharmaceutical research and development, it recommends
that the benefits derived from the funds provided by the Governme nt of India should be utilized
correctly.  The Committee was told that many multin ational corporations coul d help in pharmaceutical
research and development.  The Committee noted with anguish and pain that most of the pharmaceutical
formulation developed under the exemptions given by the Government of I ndia is taken by the
multinational corporations to their head offices abroad and patented th ere and marketed as international
brands and at international rates.  The Committee was agonized to know that such formulations are
produced by Indian scientists and with the help of Indian tax paye rs’ money and yet the benefits are 
reaped by the multinational drug companies who sell the products at a price wh ich most of the Indians
cannot afford.  The Committee, therefore, strongl y recommends that such technology or formulation
produced by Indian scientists must be patented in India before these are transferred or taken abroad.  The
Committee further recommends th at patenting of such formulat ions in India should be made
compulsory.  The Committee notes  the alarming trend of multina tional companies manufacturing even
Ayurvedic products, branding them and selling them with a high co mmercial pitch.  The Committee
recommends that all out efforts must be made to  reverse this trend and safeguard our traditional
knowledge from appropriation by foreign agencies. 
(Para 48)
Action taken by the Department 
“Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Research” (D&P Programme) was mounted by DST in 1994-1995 for 
promoting collaborative R&D in the Drugs and Pharmace utical sector between industry and institutions.
The programme supports rese arch in all systems of medicine, be it the trad itional Indian Medicinal
System (IMS) or the modern one . Till date the department (DST) has funded 45 Projects, 38
collaborative research projects alongwith 7 Nationa l Facilities. The overall commitment for these 45
programmes is of the or der of Rs.77.00 crores. (Government shar e of Rs. 32.00 crores and industry’s 
share of Rs.45.00 crores). Out of the various projects financially supported under the scheme 4 product
patents and 12 process patents have been filed so far by the institution and industry.
