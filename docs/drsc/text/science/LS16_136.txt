Recommendation 
  
         The Committee appreciates the Departme nt for carrying out research  in many frontier areas o f
science and technology.  It is true that science is one of the areas, which can bring leadership and meet
the kind of objectives that India ha s set for itself.  However, the Co mmittee is of the opinion that the
Department should make efforts fo r transfer of technologies to in dustry and also strive for wide r
dissemination of technologies to raise funds from  Industry by having a greater interface between
Industry & R&D laboratories.                                                                                                                
(Para 27) 
  
Action Taken by the Department 
  
The suggestions made by the Committee are well taken. CSIR has been proactive in reaching to
the industry. CSIR is the only orga nization which involves industry fr om early stage of development
with a view to build an organic linkage with the industry. Recognizi ng the diversity of activities and
complexity of marketing R & D outputs, CSIR has adopted and put in place a decentralized approach
for marketing the entire knowledgebase generated at  different locations. The laboratories maintain
database of industry relevant to th eir sphere of activities. The laborat ories also make special efforts to
constantly maintain interactio ns with the industries to di sseminate the knowledgebase through
conventional as well as non-conventional approaches, including the print and electronic media. In each
of the laboratory a dedicated group inter- alia acts as a window for the marketing of the entire
knowledgebase of the laboratory. 
  
Comments 
         To realize the full potential of  the efforts involved in  the research and development in the CSI R 
laboratories, the Committee hopes th at a close contact with the i ndustry may be maintained. The
Committee recommends that a special cell may be set up to coordinate and pool the knowledge gained
in the CSIR laboratories and thereaf ter disseminate it through  direct interactions  with the industry o r
through the print and electronic media.
