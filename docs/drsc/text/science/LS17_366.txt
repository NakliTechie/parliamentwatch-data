 REPORT 
 
The Action Taken Report (ATR) of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing 
Committee on Science and Technology, Environment, Forests and Climate Change  deals with 
the action taken by the Government on the recommendatio ns/observations of the contained in its 
„Three Hundred Fifty Eighth Report‟ on „Demands for Grants (2022 -23) of the Department of 
Atomic Energy‟. The Report was presented to Rajya  Sabha and laid on the Table of Lok Sabha 
on the 15th March, 2022. 
2. Action Taken Note s (ATNs) have been received from Department of Atomic Energy in 
respect of all the twelve recommendations/observations contained in the „Three Hundred Fifty  
Eighth Report‟. The replies  of the Department of Atomic Energy  have been  categorised as  
follows:- 
Chapter  I:  Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the 
Government: Para Nos.:- 2.3, 2.11, 3.8, 4.2, 6.3, 6.4, 6.5 and 6.6  
Total recommendations – 08 accepted. 
The Committee is pleased to note that the Department has accepted its eight 
recommendations/observations as mentioned in Chapter-I of the Report.  
Chapter II:    Recommendations/Observations whic h the Committee does not desire to 
pursue in view of the Government’s replies: 
 Para Nos.:- 2.4, 2.5, 2.6 and 5.2   
Total recommendations –  04 not to be pursued further. 
                         The Committee is convinced with the explanations furnished by the    
Department and, therefore, does not want to pursue the recommendations 
further. 
Chapter III: Recommendations/Observations in respect of which replies of the 
Government have not been accepted by the Committee: Nil               
Chapter IV:  Recommendations/Observations in respect of which final replies of the 
Government are still awaited: Nil 
3. The details of the ATNs have been analyzed in the succeeding Chapters.

DELEGATION OF ENHANCED POWERS FOR RE -APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS TO 
SECRETARY, DEPARTMENT OF ATOMIC ENERGY 
 
Recommendations/Observations 
1.8 During the meeting, the Department apprised the Committee that at present there are 
restrictions for re -appropriating funds from “Major Works” to any other primary units like 
“Minor Works”, “Repairs & Maintenance”, “Tools & Plants”, etc.  It was further brought to the 
notice of the Committee that presently the powers of re -appropriation for “Administrative 
Expenses” (viz. under the primary units salaries, wages, travel expenses, etc.) is limited to 25% or 
Rs.1 crore, whichever is less.  The Department, accordingly, proposed that full powers may be 
given to the Secretary, Department of Atomic Energy, for re -appropriation of above expenses 
within the same grant which will enable the Department to utilize the budgetary g rant to the 
optimum level.                                                                                                             (Para 4.2 ibid) 
 
Action Taken  
1.9 DAE has noted the observations of the Committee.                             
Recommendations/Observations 
1.10 The Committee appreciates the efforts being made by the  Tata Memorial Centre (TMC) to 
reach out to the underserved areas of the country so as to ensure that adequate cancer care 
facilities are made available in these areas. It is also heartening to note that the progress 
achieved by the TMC in this direction wo uld start showing concrete results in times to come.                               
The Committee feels that the efforts of the TMC further need to be supplemented financially for 
which availability of adequate funds holds the key to success.  Therefore, it  is imperative to make 
available the required funds to the TMC through the Department of Atomic Energy for this cause.                                  
The Committee is happy that in BE 2022 -23, the Central Government has allocated Rs.700.00 
crore for cancer care, education and research. The Committee, accordingly, hopes that the TMC 
would make best use of the allocated funds and, if required, the Department would seek additional 
funds as well in order to realise the objectives envisioned for the year 2022-23.      (Para 6.3 ibid) 
Action Taken  
1.11 The funds provided for 2022 -23 would be optimally utilized by TMC. If required, 
additional funds will be sought by DAE at RE 2022 -23 stage, considering the expenditure trend 
and firm commitments, for the remaining period of FY 2022 -23 to achieve its physical and 
financial targets. 
Recommendations/Observations 
1.12 The Committee firmly believes that merely having physical infrastructure in place alone 
would not yield the desired results and it is, therefore , of the utmost importance and great 
significance that these facilities are equipped with qualified and trained human resource as well.  
Therefore, availability of qualified and trained human resources is pivotal in the success of these 
facilities.                                                                                                                      (Para 6.4 ibid) 
Action Taken  
1.13 Human Resources are pivotal for the success of the cancer centres. Therefore , human 
resource requirements are submitted along with the DPR for all new projects. Recruitment is made 
in a phased manner based on the increase in quantum of patient load. Augmentation of HR may be 
required after a few years of functioning.  
Recommendations/Observations 
1.14 The Committee has, accordingly, been given to understand by the Department that making 
the „Homi Bhabha National Institute‟ an “Institute of National Importance” would enable the  

4 
 
 
 
existing TMC centres at various States to start super specialty courses in oncology which would 
further ensure the convergence of human resource with the development of these cancer centres. 
                                                                                                                                     (Para 6.5 ibid) 
Action Taken  
1.15 Postgraduate and sub -specialty courses in various fields of oncology would commence 
once regulatory approvals for conversion of HBNI into „ Institute of National Important ‟ (INI) is 
received.   
Recommendations/Observations 
1.16 The Committee in order to address the issue of availability of skilled workforce to operate 
these facilities, finds it appropriate that necessary policy interventions are brought out by TMC 
through the Department of Atomi c Energy to declare the „Homi Bhabha National Institute‟ as an 
“Institute of National Importance”.  The Committee, therefore, recommends the Department to 
make necessary efforts in the matter.                                                                          (Para 6.6 ibid) 
Action Taken  
1.17 After following due procedure, a Note for the Cabinet regarding “ Converting Homi 
Bhabha National Institute (HBNI) into an Institute of National Importance (INI) through an Act of 
Parliament” has been submitted for placing before the Cabinet.

proposal will be submitted to Project Appraisal Committee (PAC) for its approval.
