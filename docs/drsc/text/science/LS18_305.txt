REPORT
The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Scientific and
Industrial Research on the recommendations/observations contained in the Two Hundred Ninety-seventh
Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science and Technology,
Environment and Forests on the Demands for Grants (2017-18) of the Department of Scientific and
Industrial Research, (Ministry of Science and Technology) presented to both the Houses of Parliament
on 31st March, 2017. There were thirty-two recommendations/observations in the Report of the Committee.
Action Taken note on the recommendations of the Committee were received from the Department on
20th July and 11th December, 2017.
The Committee's recommendations, action taken note thereon and comments of the Committee on
the Action Taken by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research, are set out in the following
paragraphs:—
FINANCIAL  ACHIEVEMENTS  OF  THE  DEPARTMENT
Recommendation
The Committee observes that financial performance of the Department has been satisfactory in the
past few years. However, the Committee notes that the Department has utilized 
` 3203.45 crore (upto
31.01.2017) against the allocated amount of ` 4062.80 crore only during 2016-17 which shows that
proper and timely planning for utilization of the allocated fund was not done. In view of this, the
Committee recommends that the Department must have proper and robust monitoring mechanism for
utilizing the allocated funds so that optimum utilization of allocated funds may be ensured .(Para 2.2)
Action Taken
Utilization of allocated funds till 31.01.2017 was 78.84%. An amount of ` 4051.67 crore was
utilized during the full F.Y . 2016-17, which amounts to 99.73% utilization of allocated funds.
The department notes the observation of the Committee for optimum utilization of funds and shall
strive for even better utilization next year.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply.
REQUEST AND JUSTIFICATION FOR ADDITIONAL BUDGETARY ALLOCATION FOR
FINANCIAL YEAR 2017-18
Recommendation
In view of the above submissions of the Department, the Committee feels that request for the
enhancement in the budgetary allocation to the tune of

SCHEMES  OF  THE DEPARTMENT OF SCIENTIFIC AND INDUSTRIAL RESEARCH
Recommendation
The Committee notes that there has been gross under-utilization of allocated resources not only
during 2016-17 but in the past too, in all the four Schemes viz. Promoting Innovations in Individuals,
Start-ups and MSMEs (PRISM), Patent Acquisition and Collaborative Research and Technology
Development (P ACE), Building Industrial Research and Development (BIRD) and Access to Knowledge
for Creation of Innovative Capacity and Dissemination (A2K) of the Department. The reasons for under-
utilization of allocated funds have also not been given by the Department. Because of under-utilizations,
allocation at BE stage for 2017-18 in some of the said schemes have been reduced substantially. The
Committee, therefore, strongly recommends that the Department should strengthen its administrative and
monitoring mechanism for utilizing the allocated resources right from beginning and evaluation be done
in each quarter. (Para 4.11)
Action Taken
The reasons for under-utilization of allocated funds in the DSIR schemes on PRISM, PACE, BIRD
and A2k+ have been: delay in formulation and approval of guidelines and standard operating procedures
of the schemes; and slow pace of expenditure by the project implementing agencies in spite of rigorous
follow-up by the department.
The department takes note of the recommendations of the Committee for strengthening the monitoring
mechanism for optimum utilization of the allocated resources during 2017-18. Subsequent to obtaining
approval for continuation of the schemes for 2017-20, the department shall monitor the utilization of
allocated resources by means of a Committee comprising of representatives of Group Heads of the
schemes and Financial Adviser.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply and hopes that the steps initiated by the Department would
yield the desired results.
PROGRAMME  FOR  DEVELOPMENT OF  TECHNOLOGIES FOR COMMERCIALISATION
(PDTC)
Recommendation
The Committee notes that NRDC plays a significant role in transfer of technologies emanating from
R&D laboratories and its operation covers entire spectrum of technologies. However, the Committee

3
expresses its deep concern over under-utilization of allocated amount during 2016-17. The Committee
hopes that proper planning would be done for optimum utilization of allocated amount during 2017-18.
The Committee further recommends that NRDC should have close co-ordination and constant interactions
with CSIR-labs in order to promote, develop and commercialize the technology emanating from various
labs so that it could reach the masses of the country. (Para 5.6)
Action Taken
NRDC was allocated an amount of ` 2.75 crore by DSIR under the scheme on "Programme for
Development of Technologies for Commercialization (PDTC)" during 2016-17. It has utilized ` 2.35
crore, which amounts to 85.45% utilization of the allocated funds. The department notes the observation
of the Committee for optimum utilization of funds by NRDC during 2017-18 and shall closely follow-
up with NRDC in this regard.
Further, NRDC has renewed the umbrella MoU with CSIR as well as signed MoUs with a number
of CSIR labs to commercialize their technologies for reaching out to the masses.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply.

COUNCIL OF SCIENTIFIC AND INDUSTRIAL RESEARCH (CSIR)
Recommendation
The Committee notes and appreciates the efforts of CSIR in publishing large number of research
papers in International SCI journals every year. However, the Committee observes that good work done
by the CSIR in the form of research papers do not translate into sustainable technologies for use of the
masses. Hence, the Committee recommends that Department should make all out efforts so that research
and inventions may be translated into sustainable technologies. The Committee further recommends that
the scientific journals/papers/publications etc. should come out on time and with high standards.
(Para 8.6)

The Department is thankful for the appreciation indicated by the Committee. The Department likes
to inform the Committee that the translation of published papers into technologies takes time.

publish scientific journals/papers etc. on time and with high standards.

NATIONAL  S&T HUMAN RESOURCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME
Recommendation
The Committee welcomes the enhancement in allocations for this Scheme from 
`300 cr. in 2016-
17 to `325 cr. in BE 2017-18. However, the Committee takes serious note that during 2016-17, an
amount of ` 225 cr. only (upto 31.01.2017) was spent against the allocated RE of ` 300 cr. which clearly
states that physical targets could not be achieved fully and many of the deserving students might have
been denied the opportunities to get the fellowships/ Scholarships. The Committee, therefore, recommends
that in future, the Department should be more careful in utilizing the allocated amount. (Para 15.8)
Action Taken
Noted for action in future. However, the expenditure under the Scheme depends on the submission
of Fellowship grant-in-aid claim bill along with Utilization Certificate (UC) and Statement of Expenditure
(SE) by the grantee institutions. The grantee institutions will be requested to submit the fellowship grant-
in-aid claim bill in time to enable CSIR to fully utilize the allocated funds. Further the efforts have been
made to increase the number of fellowships. The number of JRF-NET award has been enhanced to more
than 4000 (4038) through the NET conducted in June and December 2016 compared to 2369 awarded

11
during 2015-16. Similarly, the number of SRF is increased to 518 in 2016-17 compared to 218 during
previous financial year.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply.
Recommendation
The Committee also feels that to catch young students and nurture, groom and develop them into
budding scientists, it is imperative that a concerted and co-ordinated strategy is made on a long- term
basis. Since, CSIR has vast chain of scientific labs and the biggest institution as employer of scientists;
it has to actively play a major role in this direction. Just by awarding some fellowships/ Scholarships,
this mammoth task is not going to be accomplished. Therefore, the Committee recommends that the
Department must come out with some concrete proposals to trap, harness and retain human resources in
the field of science. (Para 15.9)
Action Taken
To harness and train the human resources in the field of science, CSIR has modified the eligibility
criteria for its CSIR-UGC National Eligibility Test (NET) for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) wherein
graduates in science and engineering can also appear in the NET and may take the benefit of fellowship
for pursuing doctoral research in the field of science. CSIR has a JRF-GATE scheme for GATE qualified
engineering and GPAT qualified pharmaceutical graduates to pursue research through a suitable PhD
programme in AcSIR or in any other institution. Also, CSIR awards Senior Research Fellowships in
Science, Engineering and Medicinal and Pharmaceutical Sciences through interview mode to young
talented students who have two years research experience. Such steps will help in accomplishing the task
of catching the young students and nurture, groom and develop them into the budding scientists. Further,
21% of total research fellows (about 7000) supported by CSIR are pursuing their research work in CSIR
laboratories.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply and hopes that CSIR would continue its efforts in this
direction in right earnest.

desired results and will go a long way in respect of social upliftment of down-trodden persons of the

CSIR'S ROYALTY AND EARNINGS OF DEPARTMENT BY TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER
Recommendation
The Committee observes that income of the Department from royalty component is very low as
compared to the huge investment of the Government on account of research/innovations/R&D etc.
vis-a-vis profit earned by the private companies/industries by making commercial use of technologies
developed by the Department. In view of this, the Committee recommends that MoU should be signed
with the companies/industries for fixing royalty component while making technology transfer/inventions
for allowing its product for commercial utilization so that Department could be financially self reliant
in every aspect and invest in R&D. (Para 20.4)
Action Taken
The Department thankfully notes the observations of the Committee. MoUs signed with companies/
industries at the time of sanctioning technology development and commercialization projects do fix the
royalty percentage to be paid by industries but the amount of royalty realized depends upon the actual
commercialization and the earnings made by companies, which varies as per the prevailing market and
technology scenario. CSIR has also put in place new guidelines for technology transfer Utilization of
Knowledgebase-2017. The department endeavors to maintain a balance between its objectives of promotional
role for technological up-gradation of industry and to ensure that more and more royalty accrues to
Government, which can be ploughed back into further R&D.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply.

AW ARENESS AND OUTREACH PROGRAMME OF THE DEPARTMENT
Recommendation
The Committee during its meeting held on 15th February, 2017 had also observed that people of
the country including elected representatives are not aware of the excellent work in the field of Science
being done by the Department or much about the Department itself which may be beneficial for a very
big segment of the society like farmers, fishermen, students, research scholars etc. The Committee,
therefore, recommends that the Department should prepare a comprehensive awareness programme with
a calendar of events using social media platforms, radio, television etc. meant for researchers, academia,
farmers, industries, students and general public but most importantly for all the elected representatives
right from district level i.e. Members of Zila Panchayats, Legislative Assemblies and Councils and
Members of Parliament. The Committee strongly feels that elected representatives can play an important
role in making their constituents aware of scientific development and ultimately developing scientific
temperament in the public at large. (Para 22.2)
Action Taken
The Department notes the observations of the Committee. It is submitted that an exhibition on
'Scientific achievements and technologies ready to be deployed" by all the Scientific Ministries/Departments
was organized by DSIR/CSIR at the Parliament House Annexe during the Monsoon Session, 2017 of the
Parliament. The exhibition, organized at the behest of 'Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science &
Technology, Environment & Forests' was visited by a large number of MPs and Ministers, who appreciated
the innovative products/processes and systems showcased by various scientific ministries.
Comments
The Committee notes the reply and would like the Department to take measures to initiate
comprehensive awareness programmes for other stakeholders mentioned in the recommendations
as well.
