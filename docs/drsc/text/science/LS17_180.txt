REPORT 
 The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Scientific and Industrial 
Research on the recommendations contained in the One Hundred Seventy-first Report of the Department- 
related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science & Technology, Environment & Forests on the Demands 
for Grants (2007-08) of the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research, which was presented to both the 
Houses of Parliament on 26thApril, 2007, There were fifteen recommendations in the One Hundred Seventy-
first Report of the Committee. Action Taken Notes on the recommendations of the Committee were received 
from the Department on 25th July, 2007. 
 The Committee’s recommendations, Action Taken thereon and comments of the Committee on the 
Action Taken by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research are set out in the succeeding paragraphs: 
- 
1.  Recommendation  
 Budgetary Allocation 
 The Committee recommends that the Department should make efforts to spend funds which have been 
allocated and should take due care to make proposal which could be practically implemented within the time 
frame set up by the Department.            (Para 2.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The views of the Committee have been noted and all Departments will follow the same in letter and spirit 
while formulating the proposals during 2008-09. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the re ply and would like the Department to ensure compliance. 
2.  Recommendation  
 Technology Promotion, Development and Utilisation Programme (TPDU)  
 The Committee is of the opinion that demonstrati on and dissemination of new technologies is as 
important as their development and the Committee expects the Department to leave no stone unturned to 
popularize such technologies, as the ultimate test of their success is market/popular acceptability.          
(Para 3.1.1.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The views of the Committee are well taken. The re search projects assisted under the TDDP and the 
outcome of such projects including the list of new technologies are published in the Annual Report and 
website of the Department. The new technologies developed are transferred to NRDC after specified period for 
commercialization through advertisement and contact with industries. The commercialization of new 
technologies has to be however, within the provision of IPR Act. 

Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like that gradually there is greater self-reliance through 
communication transactions. 
3.  Recommendation  
 Technopreneur Promotion Programme (TePP)  
 The Committee is happy to know that the Department has increased its support to individual innovators 
and has made efforts to expand the reach of the programme so as to cover more innovators. Such 
encouragements are an important first steps in spreading the culture of innovation and out-of-the-box thinking. 
(Para 3.2.2.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 Encouraged by the observations of the Committee, Te PP has further increased outer limits of support to 
independent innovator to Rs. 15 lakh in Phase I and Rs . 45 lakh in Phase II. Further, TePP outreach centres 
funded by DSIR have been increased to 10. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like to be apprised of so me outstanding innovations and 
there impact in civil society in economic and social terms. 
4.  Recommendation  
 Technology Man agement Programme 
 The Committee is of the opinion that technological upgradation of cottage industries and other such 
industries falling in the unorganized sector has to be taken seriously. This will not only improve the cost 
effectiveness of our economy but will also raise income s of the people in these sectors touching lives of 
millions as the majority of workforce belong to the unor ganized sector. But for this to happen the technologies 
have to be small and affordable so that they are business friendly.                 (Para 3.3.6)  
Action Taken by the Government 
 Among the important initiatives taken under the Technology Management Programme, are Analytical, 
Technology status and Development studies. These study reports provide information to Industry, policy 
makers, entrepreneurs, academic and research institutes in the areas of their concern relating to technologies in 
which effective action can be taken to mitigate problems and enhance efficiency. 
 Cognizant that there are many region specific resour ces in different States in the country that have 
spawned industries, which could benefit through further research and analysis in respect of value addition, 
technology upgradation and information dissemination these reports have been taken up to specifically address 
this need. Further, it is well recognized that identification of technology gaps in existing industries in respect 
of important products and processes, including cottage, tiny and small industries in the unorganized sector, is 
an important requirement that needs to be continually ad dressed. There are also a nu mber of areas where there 
is need for indigenous development of technology and for value addition, including rural based technologies, 
in respect of which information collection and analysis is required. 

 Around 20 such studies have been completed in the last five years. Apart from these studies addressing 
specific region based resources, more than 180 technology  status studies of specific products or processes had 
earlier been completed until the late 1990s. Efforts to disseminate the findings of these reports are consciously 
made, to stimulate action, mitigate gaps and facilitate decision-making. 
 These reports cover sector-specific status of techno logy in the country, international trends, gaps in 
technology, research facilities and other such information, in respect of specific identified products and 
processes. 
 Further efforts would be taken to boost this initiative and newer areas and regions calling for attention 
that would benefit such communities at large would be identified and studied in keeping with this 
recommendation, and the findings would be shared across all beneficiaries and stakeholders. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply. However, it is of  the view that studies completed until the late 
1990s and also those during the last five years shou ld now be implemented i.e. transfer of feasible 
technologies to small entrepreneurs and cottage industries. 
5.  Recommendation 
 International Technology Transfer Programme  
 The Committee is of the view that the Department should make all efforts to encourage the start-up 
companies and should monitor their growth trajectories so as to improve and encourage the culture of 
entrepreneurship in the country.                     (Para 3.4.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department has prepared a proposal for setting up a Fund for Accelerating Start-Ups on Technology 
(FAST). The details of the proposal are being worked out for seeking the approval of the Government. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like to know the progress made. 
6.  Recommendation 
 Consultancy Promotion Programme  
 The Committee welcomes the consultancy programme s for small and medium enterprises run by the 
Department. It is hopeful that the Department will do all the needful to ensure the quality of such services so 
that they remain substantial and are not reduced to mere formalities.         (Para 4.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Consultancy Programme would support more programmes in 11
th Plan for setting up Design and 
Engineering services centres and consultancy clinics in important industry clusters to assist small and medium 
enterprises. It is envisaged that such projects even after successful completion would function on self 
sustaining basis and would provide quality services for SMEs. 

Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like the Department to monitor the progress made. 
7.  Recommendation 
 National Research Development Corporation  
 The Committee is of the opinion that commercializing of indigenous technologies and promotion and 
commercialization of inventions is as important as inve ntions themselves if not more. This is only possible 
with dissemination of information to the recipients so that a market for such technologies could be created. 
Development and promotion of rural technology is yet anot her priority area as our farm sector with its own set 
of peculiarities need indigenous technologies suitable to it.         (Para 6.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 As regards commercialization of technologies, 39  new processes were assigned to the NRDC for 
commercialization and it has executed 43 license agreements during the year 2006-07. To promote inventions, 
besides publishing Awishkar and Invention Intelligence on regular basis, NRDC has announced 5 cash awards 
amounting Rs. 5.00 lakh and one WIPO Gold Medal; provided assistance for filing 86 patent applications; 
organized 26 seminars and. training programmes; c onducted 35 patents searches; delivered 43 lectures on 
awareness regarding IPR; provided techno commercial support to 40 promising inventions and financially 
supported one party through ‘Angel Funding’.  
 Further, the Corporation, under its programme “Promotion of Rural and House hold technologies” has 
participated in 5 rural exhibitions to disseminate appropriate rural technologies to the masses; provided support 
to 8 Rural Technology Development and Training (RTDT) centres; initiated the cluster development 
programme - Dairy and Sericulture- to create awareness among the clusters regarding the latest technologies 
available. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply. It would like the De partment to make more efforts to disseminate 
such technologies and increase their popularity. It  would also like the Depa rtment to do follow up 
studies on such technologies so as to track their successes and failures. 
8.  Recommendation  
 Council of Scientific and Industrial Research 
 National Laboratories  
 The Committee is of the opinion that the Department should do proper groundwork before making a 
demand for allocation so that it can escape the embarrassing situation of not being able to spend money of its 
projects it initially demanded. The Committee also believes that all possible steps should be taken to prevent 
spillovers from one plan period to another. It should also try to complete its programmes within the time- 
frame it has set for itself.           (Para 9.8.1) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department has taken note of the advice of the Hon’ble Committee and all efforts would be made to 
complete activities in a time-bound manner. 

Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and desires better coordination in implementation of budgetary 
allocation. 
9.  Recommendation  
 The Committee appreciates the decision of the Department to break its various programmes being run by 
National Laboratories under following sub-divisions: S upra Institutional Projects, Network Projects, Inter-
Agency projects and Facilities. The Committee is of the opinion that such a division will be helpful in 
developing core-competencies and professional focus.       (Para 9.8.2) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department has noted the appreciation. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply. 
10.  Recommendation  
 The Committee fully agrees that the present day process of knowledge generation and diffusion is 
characterized by a shift from research based stand alone scientific disciplines to trans-disciplinary and inter - 
institutional networking. The Committee is hopeful that the Department will try novel ways to develop 
synergies in its research efforts to reach latest solutions in the ever evolving research field.   (Para 9.8.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department has following plan: 
 First is to conceptualize, plan and work, in network mode, on R&D of relevance both nationally and 
globally - and to align it with public, private, strategic or social needs as the case may be. There would be a 
strong paradigmatic link between network and PPP modes. 
 Second is to forge viable, defined and scientifically challenging R&D projects in Supra institutional mode 
to make each laboratory a cohesive and close knit unit. This would help align and reinforce the core 
competency of the laboratories. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply. It is of the opinion that the plan  formulated by the Department 
should be meticulously implemente d so as to make break throughs in frontier areas of scientific 
research. 
11.  Recommendation  
 National S&T Human Resource Development  
 The Committee recognizes the need of monetary incentiv es to attract young talent to research field, In a 
world where information technology and management streams offer lucrative career options, 
scholarships/fellowships have to keep in tune with the times so as to not only attract new talent but also to hold 

the existing one so that the attrition rate are kept within manageable limits.                  (Para 
10.4) 
Action Taken by the Government  
 The Department notes the concern of the Committee to attract and at the same time retain the talented 
young persons and it will make all efforts in this direction including introducing new scholarships. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would lik e to be apprised on the progress made. 
12.  Recommendation  
 Intellectual Property a nd Technology Management 
 The Committee appreciates the idea of pro-active and planned patenting. The Committee is of the opinion 
that in a globalised environment where competition comes in all possible hues and from different parts of the 
globe, protecting our hard earned fruits cannot be over-emphasized. The Committee desires that the 
Department should do all the needful to protect our intellectual interests.      (Para 11.4) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 During the last three years, CSIR has put special emphasis on the global protection of those inventions 
which are commercially and strategically important for the organization and the country. 
 In order to meet above objective, all the proposals required for patenting are thoroughly scrutinized and 
necessary suggestions are made to laboratories to provid e/generate required data for the above said purposes. 
Similarly, special emphasis has also been laid on the projects arising out of public-private partnership and 
protection of IP generated from such coordinated efforts. 
Comments 
 The Committee would like to be apprised on specific outcomes as a result of these initiatives. 
13.  Recommendation  
 New Millennium Indian Technology Leadership Initiative (NMITLI)  
 The Committee notes with satisfaction that during the Tenth Plan NMITLI has created a brand image and 
is viewed today as a benchmark of P ublic Private Partnership (PPP) schemes.  It is also happy about the fact 
that this scheme is benefiting both the Department and its industrial partners. The Committee feels that such 
schemes can serve as a model for future projects as good research can only be successful in the long run only if 
it turns out to be a commercial success as well.        (Para 13.3) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The feeling of the Committee to be a model for future projects is well taken and organization will strive 
to work in this direction. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply and would like to  know how the interests of the scientists are 
protected in the scheme. 

14.  Recommendation  
 Setting up of Institute of  Translational Research  
 The Committee is of the opinion that setting up of the new institute will help to focus better on high-end 
clinical research which requires inputs from innumerab le fields, as it involved problems of human existence 
which require a holistic approach the implication of which also involved issues which are not exclusively 
s c i e n t i f i c .                ( P a r a  1 4 . 3 )   
Action Taken by the Government 
 The proposal for setting up such institute will be taken up for consideration by the Competent authority 
during the XI Plan when Committee’s views would be fully incorporated. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply. 
15.  Recommendation  
 After considering the Demands for Grants of the De partment of Scientific and Industrial Research and 
assessing the progress of various important progra mmes and projects administered by it, the Committee 
recommends that the budgetary allocatio n of Rs. 1902 crores under Demand No. 82 (Department of Scientific 
and Industrial Research) as sought by the Department for the year 2007-08 may be approved.     (Para 15) 
Action Taken by the Government 
 The Department is thankful for the Committee for guidance and recommending the outlay of Rs. 1902 
crore to the Government of India. 
Comments 
 The Committee notes the reply.
