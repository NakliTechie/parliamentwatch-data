REPORT 
 
The Action Taken Report (ATR) of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing 
Committee on Science and Technology, Environment, Forests and Climate Change  deals with the 
action taken by the Government on the recommendations/observations of the contained in its 
„Three Hundred Sixty First  Report' on „Demands for Grants (202 2-23) of the Department  of 
Science and Technology‟. The Report was presented to Rajya Sabha and laid on the Table of Lok 
Sabha on the 15th March, 2022.  
2.  Action Taken Note s (ATNs) have  been received from Department of Science and 
Technology in respect of all the sixteenth recommendations/observations contained in the „Three 
Hundred Sixty First Report‟. The replies of the Department of Science and Technology  have been 
categorized as follows:-  
Chapter I:       Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the 
   Government:   
 Para Nos. 2.4, 2.11, 2.12, 4.5, 4.10 and 6.5  
             Total recommendations – 06 accepted  
The Committee is pleased to note that the Departme nt has accepted its six 
recommendations/observations as mentioned in Chapter-I of the Report.                            
Chapter II: Recommendations/Observations which the Committee does not desire to  
pursue in view of the Government’s replies: 
 Para Nos. 2.3, 2.5, 2.6, 4.9, 5.3 and 5.4  
           Total recommendations – 06 Not to be pursued further 
   The Committee is convinced with the explanations furnished by the Ministry and , 
therefore, does not want to pursue the recommendations further.

have not been accepted by the Committee: Nil 
Chapter IV: Recommendations/Observations in respect of which final replies of the

Para Nos. 3.5, 3.6, 3.7, and 5.5 
           Total recommendations – 04 to be pursued further

Recommendations/Observations 
2.8 The Committee is constrained to observe that while Rs.75 crore was allocated to the 
Department in R.E. 2021 -22 under TDB, it has been able to spend only Rs.23.04 crore, which is 
only about 30 percent. The Committee, therefore, strongly recommends that TDB should scale up

Action Taken 
2.9 It may be noted that Technology Development Board  (TDB) is mandated to provide 
financial assistance in the form of loan or equity and/or in exceptional cases, grant to industrial 
concerns and other agencies attempting commercial  application of indigenous technology or 
adapting imported technology for wider domestic application. 
 
2.10 The financial assistance of TDB is released to borrower company in installments that are 
linked to associated milestones as per agreement executed w ith the industrial concern. Thus 
utilization of allocated funds is dependent on disbursement/s which may spread over project life 
cycle, however, demand of funds is always done at total TDBs commitment of the project/s.   
2.11 The Budget Estimate (BE) for FY 2021-22 was prepared based on the committed liabilities 
of previous year as on 31st March, 2021 and requirements of new project during Current Financial 
Year (CFY). The details of fund requirement and allotment for FY 2021-22 are as follows:- 
 
Budget Estimate: Rs 50 Crore 
Revised Estimate: Rs 155 Crore 
Fund Allocation: Rs. 125 Crore (Rs. 50 Crore received on 30.03.2022) 
2.12 The total committed liabilities as on 31.03.2021  However, due to multiple waves of this 
pandemic most of the ongoing projects were delayed and borrower companies could not achieve 
the targeted milestones as expected during FY 21-22. This resulted in   rescheduling / deferment of 
disbursements (cash outgo).  
 
2.13 Notwithstanding this pandemic, 06 new agreements have been signed with v arious 
industrial concerns in FY 2021 -22 with TDB‟s commitment Rs. 130.77 Cr ore. The first 
installments against these projects have already been released. Remaining shall be released when 
the associated milestones are achieved project wise.

2.15 Further, the Board, after joining of the new Secretary, has exte nsively enhanced its

EXTRAMURAL RESEARCH SUPPORT PROVIDED BY THE DEPARTMENT 
 
Recommendations/Observations 
2.17 The Committee observes that in the States of Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, 
Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Tamil Nadu; the total number of projects fund ed by the Department in 
the years 2019 -20, 2020 -21 and 2021 -22 are 741, 812 and 591, respectively. The Committee 
further observes that the share of these six States in the number of total projects funded by the 
Department during these years is more than 50  per cent of the total projects funded by the 
Department in those years. Similarly, the amount of funding released to these States is more than 
50 per cent of the total funding released by the Department.                              (Para 5.3 ibid) 
 
Action Taken 
2.18 The figures in the table referred are the details of year -wise funds received by the 
Academia/R&D institutions located within the jurisdiction of different states. Department of 
Science and Technology provides research and development grants p urely on competitive mode to 
the Institutions across the country. Proposals are invited from potential researchers/institutions 
across the country through open calls and the proposals received are evaluated based on their 
merits. Except for establishing th e State S&T Councils and the State Climate Change Cells, no 
other scheme/programme offers grants state -wise. It may be noted that ~50% of the S&T 
institutions (including ~25% of institutions of national importance and the Universities) in the 
country are l ocated in these five states, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, 
Delhi and Tamil Nadu have.  The higher ratio of S&T institutions along with the institutions of 
national importance are largely contributing to research & development activ ities and therefore, 
these five states are able to take away almost 50% of the total extra mural grant offered by DST.   
 
Recommendations/Observations 
2.19 The Committee is constrained to note that in the States of Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, 
Jharkhand, Bihar and Rajasthan, the number of projects funded by the Department are 
approximately 11 per cent of the total projects sanctioned. The Committee is perturbed to observe 
that the efforts of the Department of Science and Technology are falling short in pro moting 
scientific research and technology development in the North-Eastern Region as well.  
              (Para 5.4 ibid)   
 
 

9 
 
 
Action Taken 
2.10 The States of Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar and Rajasthan together 
accommodate to only ~7% of the total S&T institutions (including ~13% of institutions of national 
importance and ~13% of the Universities) of India. Further, the States in the NE region share only 
3% of the total S&T institutions in the country. Since, the extra -mural research grant offered to the 
institutions is through competitive mode at national level and the distribution of the institutions is 
not equitable among the states, State‟s with strong S&T institution -base are able to receive the 
majority of the EMR grant. Although D epartment of Science and Technology has been offering 
equal opportunities to all the researchers/institutions across the country, some of the 
researchers/institutions are able compete strongly to grab the EMR grant offered by Department of 
Science and Technology. Department of Science and Technology is well concerned about the 
difficulties and challenges of the NE region and have been putting concerted efforts through some 
of the programmes like Climate Change Programme, Science & Society Programme, State S&T 
Council programme, etc. to provide special attention for the researchers/institutions from the NE 
region.

NATIONAL MISSION ON INTERDISCIPLINARY CYBER PHYSICAL SYSTEMS (NM-
ICPS) 
Recommendations/Observations 
4.1  The Committee observes that National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber Physical System 
(NM-ICPS) is aimed at promoting development and translation of futuristic technologies 
especially in the areas of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Machine Learning, 
Augmented reality, etc. These technologies are crucial to keep the country at the forefront of state -
