GEO STATIONARY SATELLITE SERVICES 
Recommendation 
The Committee feels that the major thrust of  the INSAT programme pl an should be towards
augmenting the space segment capacity of the INSAT sy stem with fourth generation INSAT-4 series o f 
satellites to meet the increasing demands for transponders in vari ous sectors including government and
social sectors.  The Depa rtment should therefore interact with the users and servic e providers in both
government and non-governme nt sectors, and then endeavour to  achieve space segment capacity o f 
transponders in INSAT system.  The Department should also e ndeavour to complete the ongoing
INSAT-3E satellite followed by next generation INSAT-4 series (INSAT-4A to G) during the 10 th plan 
period. 
(Para 23)
Action Taken by the Department 
The augmentation of INSA T system has been planned taking into account the expected demand
for transponders for broadcasting, telecommunications, etc., based on extensive interaction with users in 
both Government and Non-Government  sectors. A Satcom users meet  with the participation of both 
public and private agencies was also organised to assess the transponder requirements during 10 th plan 
period and accordingly the fourth generation INSAT-4 satellites have been planned.   Further, the 
INSAT Co-ordination Committee (ICC) compri sing of secretaries of  User Departments 
(Telecommunications, Broadcasting, Meteorology, et c.,) provides policy guidelines, operational co-
ordination and inputs for long term planning of INSAT system.  
INSAT-3A satellite is scheduled to be launched by end 2002 followed by launch of INSAT-3E 
in early 2003.  The next generation INSAT- 4 series have been planned for the 10 th Plan period. INSAT-
4A & 4B configurations have been finalised. These satellites will a ugment INSAT space segment
capacity to meet the increasing demand for transponders in various sectors including Government and
social sectors. 
Comments 
            The Committee notes the reply. 
GEO STATIONARY SATELLITE SERVICES 
(GSLV / INAT-4) 
  
Recommendation 
The indigenous GSLV Mk  II capable of launching 2000 kg INSA T type of satellites should be
fully utilised for launching INSAT-4 satellites.  The Department should examine the feasibility of using
low cost experimental GSAT satell ites for the development flights of GSLV.  Besides, an Advanced
Communication Technology Satellite (ACTS) should be planned wi th a view to develop and
demonstrate advanced communication techniques and technologies for future generation INSATs. 
Page 4 of 13Hundred and Eighth Report of Committee of Science & Technologies
7/16/2008http://10.21.217.2/book2/reports/science/108threport.htm

(Para 23)
Action Taken by the Department 
The Department has planned GSAT series of satellites as low cost experimental satellites to be
launched onboard the developmental flights of GSLV.  The first satellite, GSAT-1 has been successfully 
launched onboard GSLV D1 on 18 th April 2001.  Currently, the GSAT-2 satellite is advanced stages o f 
integration and is scheduled for launch onboard GS LV D2 during first quart er of 2003.  GSAT-3 & 
GSAT-4 satellites  are also planned for launch onboard GSLV in the time  frame of 2003-05.  Through 
these developmental flights, the GSLV is expected to be ready for operational fl ight capable o f
launching 2T weight satellite  into Geostationary Transfer Orbit (G TO), which will be fully utilised fo r
launching INSAT-4 satellites in the weight class of 2T.  
Detailed studies on the conf iguration of an Advanced Communication Technology Satellite
(ACTS), planned for launch in the early part of 11th plan period, have been initiated.  
Comments 
            The Committee notes the reply with satisfaction.   
  
GEO STATIONARY SATELLITE SERVICES 
(ADVANCED    METEOROLOGICAL SATELLITE) 
  
Recommendation 
The Committee is of the view that an advanced meteorologi cal satellite, should be planned to
provide valuable inputs for climatic / numeric weather prediction model studies related to vertical profile
of temperature & humidity.   It would enhance the quality of the Meteorological se rvices aimed at 
accurate weather forecasts. 
(Para 24)
Action Taken by the Department 
An advanced meteorological satellite called IN SAT-3D has been planned by the Department o f 
Space. INSAT-3D will consist of a 19-channel sounder and 6-cha nnel imager. The sounder is an 
instrument, which is capable of giving vertical profiles of te mperature and humid ity and help in
climatic / numeric weathe r prediction studies.  The work on IN SAT-3D satellite has already been 
initiated.  
  
Comments 
            While the Committee notes the reply of the Department with satisfaction, it reiterates the need to
provide accurate and reliable pr ediction of the monsoon by the concerned agencies to enable the
Administration to cope with or plan  for the situations of excess rainfa ll resulting in floods or failure o f
monsoon resulting in drought.

satellite communication.  However, it recommends that the establis hment of a tele-medicine networ k 
connecting district health care hos pitals to s pecialised health centers in cities su pported b y INSAT

The Committee feels that considering the po tential of the satellite based network fo r

The Observations/Recommendations of the committee are noted.   
One of the recent applicatio ns of space technology initiate d by ISRO is in the field o f
telemedicine to provide expert me dical services to the rural and remote areas. Under the telemedicine
project, hospitals/health centres in remote locations are linked via INSAT satellites with super speciality

Tagore Hospital at Calcutta.  Guwahati Medical College Hospital and the District Hospital, Leh is being 
connected to The All India Institute of Medical Sciences, New Delhi. Indira Gandhi Hospital,  
Kavaratti , Lakhsadweep is  being connected to Amritha Institute of Medical Sciences, Kochi.  In Orissa,
three medical college hospitals at Cuttack, Burla and Behrampur are pl anned to be connected to Sanjay
Gandhi Post Graduate Institute of  Medical Sciences, Lu cknow. Besides this, the discussions are in
progress with  North-Eastern  States to provide tele-medicine connectivity covering more hospitals.   
Based on the positive feedback obtained from  the  pilot project “Jhabua Development 
Communication Project” (J DCP), it has been planned to esta blish a country-wid e satellite based 
GRAMSAT communication network fo r developmental purposes incl uding distance education and
training.   Towards this, GRAMSAT programmes have been initiated in  the States of Madhya Pradesh,
Gujarat, Orissa, Karnataka, Andhr a Pradesh and Goa.   It is al so proposed to develop a satellite
dedicated for Education (E DUSAT) at all levels covering a large part of the country including North-
East region and the consultations wi th the potential users are in adva nced stages.  Towards developing
new communication services, the wo rk on a Technology Demonstration System (TDS) for the satellite
based aircraft navigation has been initiated jointly with Airpor ts Authority of I ndia.  The Mobile
Satellite Services have been provided with the INSAT system on an experimental basis and the INSAT-
3C satellite, launched in  January 2002  carries a  Mobile Satellite Services (MSS) payload.  The
development of an improved digita l version of the Cyclone Warning Dissemination system (emergency
communication) has also been takenup.

mainly as a spatial data repository with specialized  information services on natural resources.  The
Committee feels that the Department should ensu re that Earth Observat ions data are used fo r
developmental programmes by various user agencies which ma y include rural roads connectivity
analysis; drinking water mission, water shed development program me, urban / city master plan
preparation, coastal zone regulati on and other developmental program me, urban / city master plan

a long-term, multidisciplinary, ope rational oceanographic database for prediction of the ocean state and

DISASTER MANAGEMENT SUPPORT (DMS) 
Recommendation 
The Committee observes that th e Disaster Management Support (DMS) should be a key area o f
space applications.  Therefore, th e DOS should evolve a space base d system to support the disaste r
management services in the c ountry on a reliable and timely ba sis.  These include mapping and
monitoring support for disaster management, prepar ation of flood hazard zonation maps based on close-
contour information, development of reliable and fail-safe emer gency communication system for 
disaster management sup port and undertaking pilot pr ojects with user agencies  in the areas of drought
monitoring and forest fire detection. 
(Para 33)
Action Taken by the Department 
The Observations/recommendations of the committee are noted.  
The overall objective of the Disa ster Management support progra mme of ISRO is to suitably
integrate the space inputs and services to the national sy stem to make it more effective and efficient.  In
this direction, based on the expe rience gained through several pilot scale studies and the real-time 
experience of disaster management support provided in the past,  the following will be the thrust areas:

SPACE TRANSPORTATION 
Recommendation 
In the area of Launch Ve hicle Development, the Committee desires that  DOS should strive to
complete the development flights and operationalise the GSLV (w ith indigenous cryogenic upper stage)
for launching 2T INSAT cl ass of satellites.  Considering the trend towards going for bigger and heavie r
spacecraft for satellite communicati ons, the Department should initia te development of an advanced
GSLV  
Mk III capable of launching 4T INSA T class of satellite.  Besides, in  tune with the long-term vision o f 
low cost access to space, advanced  R&D in Reusable Launch Vehicl es (RLV) should be pursued.  To
this end, smaller-scale technology de monstrator in the areas of air breathing propulsion technology an d
space capsule recovery missions could be planned. 
(Para 38)
Action Taken by the Department 
  
The capability of GSLV  is being enhanced  from 1540 kg. in its first flight to 1800 kg. for the
second flight.  With the induction of indigenous cr yogenic upper stage, the capability would further be
increased to 2000 kg. for launching INSAT satellites of this weight class. 
Government approval has already been obtaine d for GSLV MK III pr oject to realise the 
capability for launching  4 Tonne class Satellites into Geo-synchronous  Transfer Orbit.   The first 
development flight is targeted during early years of 11th plan period. 
Keeping long-term vision in mi nd, configuration and mission st udies are already under progress
for initiating R&D in the area of reusable launch ve hicles.  Based on this, a reusable launch vehicle
demonstrator is proposed to be conceived. 
A separate project has been initiated for dem onstrating Space Capsule Recovery on one of the
PSLV flights during 2004-05 time fr ame. Also advanced technol ogies in the areas of Air- breathing 
Propulsion through experimental and investigative studies are initiated. 
Comments 
            The Committee notes the reply.

SPACE SCIENCES 
Recommendation 
The Committee observes that Space  Science & Environment rese arch has been  an important
component of the Indian  Space Pr ogramme.  The Committee feels that the thrust of the Space Science
Programme should be towards inve stigations in the new, emerging and challenging areas of Space
Science research including Planetary Science / exploration and Micro gravity Science.  A dedicated and
state-of-art Indian Multi-wavele ngth Astronomy satellite could be of immense  value for detaile d
scientific investigations on celesti al X-ray sources.  The committee is also of the view that something
needs to  be done for the study of tropical weather system for climate research area. 
(Para 43)
Action Taken by the Department 
Several exciting experiments and mi ssions have been planned during 10 th plan in the area o f 
Space Science and environment.    The GSAT-2 satellite, planned fo r launch in 2003, carries a Solar X-
ray Spectrometer  for study of sola r flares and a Coherent Radio B eacon Experiment  for low latitude
ionoshperic studies.  The development of the advanced scientific instrument for the multi-wavelength X-
ray astronomy satellite “ASTROSAT”  has been initiated.  An I ndo-French Joint mission “Megha 
Tropiques” has been taken up for th e study of tropical weather systems.   It is planned to conduct the 
scientific experiments for middle atmopsheric research using Indian Sounding rockets.  
Comments 
            The Committee notes the reply. 
  
LUNAR MISSION  
Recommendation 
The Committee would like the Department of Space  to take an objective and dispassionate view
on the Lunar Mission. 
The Committee looks forward to the Report of the Moon Missi on Task Force se t up by ISRO. 
However, it feels that the proposed mission to moon is ex pected to provide an im petus to the
Page 9 of 13Hundred and Eighth Report of Committee of Science & Technologies
7/16/2008http://10.21.217.2/book2/reports/science/108threport.htm

advancement of science in India and a challenge to  technologists.  This can also serve as a test
bed for future missions that coul d be undertaken by Indi a to explore the outer  world in the new
millennium, thus providing chal lenging opportunities to the younger generation scientists of the
country.  The Committee   also   understands   that the mission is expected  to result in the development
of new technologies that c ould have various spin-off applications, which could be of direct relevance to
the common man.  Also, the moon is going to be the ta rget of international attention in coming decades
(as a take off point for planetary e xplorations), the Committee is theref ore of the view that India should
possess the capability of reac hing the moon which will as sert its right and presen ce there.  Hence, the
Committee recommends that India s hould go step by step, from orbiters to landers , and then to crafts

The observations of the committ ee has been brought to the atte ntion of the Moon Mission tas k
force. The Task Force has recently  submitted its report.  The task force has identified the potential
scientific objectives of the mission, the payload options, the spacecraft configuration, the launch vehicle
requirements and th e mission related aspects. The recomm endations of the committee are being
reviewed by a Peer group of elite scientists in the country for finalising the further course  of action.  
Comments 
While the Committee notes the re ply, it would like to be appr ised of the decisions furthe r
progress at the time of consideration of Demands for Grants next year. 
  
INTELLECUTAL  PROPERTY  RIGHTS 
Recommendation 
The Committee commends the efforts made by DOS in the field of IPR movements.  However, it
feels that DOS should set a formidable  goal of achieving more patents in areas of its interest.  Also, the
Department should create a Patent Facilitating Cell which may assist in completing of all formalities fo r
filing of patents. 
(Para 49)
Action Taken by the Department            
The    Observations/recommendations   of    the    committee   are   noted.   
In line with the  r ecommendations made, IPR monitoring & Evaluation Committees have been 
formed at all ISRO Ce ntres under the overall guidance of a high level Standing committee on IP R
comprising of senior members drawn from various ISRO Centres.  Besides encouraging and assisting in
patenting activities, both these co mmittees have a mandate to create  greater awareness on IPR issues
amongst  DOS/ISRO  engineers / scientists, institute suitable patent alert mechanisms etc.  As part of its
drive to increase patenting effort s, nine workshops were held at all ISRO Centres, which have been
successful in creating an increased awareness on the various aspects of IPR activities including filing o f
patent applications,  internationa l laws, new patent re gime, copyright, tradem ark, need for patent
searches etc.  Faculty for such wo rkshops consisted of experts from the various patent  offices, reputed
patent attorneys, practicing IPR consultants and  legal  experts  from  the   National  Law  School  of 
India   University.    A   comprehensive publ ication on “Guide to Inte llectual Property Rights fo r 
DOS/ISRO personnel” was also brou ght out and widely circulated  for the benefit of DOS/ISRO
scientists/engineers. 
A direct consequence of the various initiatives undertaken has been the increased filing of patent

applications by DOS/ISRO.  Today, ISRO has more than 125 patents (both in India and abroad).

Comments 
            The Committee notes the position with satisfaction. 
  
INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION 
Recommendation 
The Committee feels that India has achieved c onsiderable success in the areas of communication
and remote sensing satellites, la unch vehicles and applications of  space technology.  The DOS should
therefore explore more avenues of  cooperation with major space agen cies and commercial tie-ups with 
private operators with a view to share country’s experience with developing countries and also to market
products and services from other countries.  In a step towards this direction, PSLV can be considered for
launch of small and medium class of satellites into different orbits on cooperation and commercial basis. 
(Para 53)
Action Taken by the Department            
The Observations / recommendations of the committee are noted.   
Antrix / ISRO have been in cont inuous contact with various  cust omers.  This coordinated effort
resulted in launching two micro sa tellites, one each for Republic of  Korea and one for Germany during
1999.  Two more microsatellites, one each from Belgium and Germ any were launched during the yea r
2001.  Efforts are continuing for launching small and medium class satellites on PSLV.  The GSLV  also
will be available on a commercial basis for the International customers from the year 2004 onwards. 
The DOS / ISRO    alongwith Antrix  Corporation is taking    care   to project the achievements  
and   capabilities   of DOS / ISRO   th rough different forums to  promote cooperation and
commercialisation. 
Comments 
            The Committee appreciates the efforts made by the Departme nt in promotion of Space
Cooperation and commercialization.
