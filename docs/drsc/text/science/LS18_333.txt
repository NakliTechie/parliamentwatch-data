REPORT 
The Action Taken Report (ATR) of the Committee deals with the action taken by the 
Government on the recommendations/observations contained in Three Hundred Thirty Second Report 
of the Committee on 'Demands for Grants (2020 -2021) of the Ministry of E arth Sciences (Demand 
No.23)'.  The Report was presented to Rajya Sabha and laid on the Table of Lok Sabha on the 6 th 
March, 2020. 
2.  Action Taken Note (ATN) was received from the Ministry of Earth Sciences in respect of all 
the thirteen recommendations/observations contained in the Three Hundred Thirty Second Report. This 
has been categorized as follows:- 
Chapter I: Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the Government:  
Para:- 2.4, 2.8, 4.6, 5.9, 5.10 and 6.3.   
(Total – 6) 
Chapter II: Recommendations/Observations which the Committee does not desire to pursue in view 
of the Government‟s replies:  
                   Para:- 3.4, 3.8, 3.12, 3.16, 4.2, 4.3 and 5.8 
                        (Total – 7) 
The Committee is convinced with the repl ies furnished by the Ministry and, therefore, does not 
want to pursue above-mentioned recommendations further. 
Chapter III: Recommendations/Observations in respect of which replies of the Government have not 
been accepted by the Committee:  
     Para:-  (Total - Nil-)  
  Chapter IV: Recommendations/Observations in respect of which final replies of  the Government are 
still awaited:   
Para:- (Total - Nil)  
3. The details of the ATR have been discussed in the respective Chapters in the succeeding pages.

RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED BY THE 
GOVERNMENT 
BUDGETARY DETAILS FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2019-20 AND ALLOCATION FOR 
2020-21  
Recommendation/Observation  
1.1 The Committee notes that the Ministry requires a bare minimum additi onal amount of 
Rs.245.90 crore during 2020 -21 to sustain its continuing schemes.  The additional amount is required 
for acquisition of Polar Research Vessel and procurement of machinery and equipments.  The 
Committee also observes that the Ministry’s quest  for acquisition of Polar Research Vessel has been 
pending since a very long time.  The Committee, therefore, recommends that the Ministry of Finance 
should positively consider the requirement of Ministry of Earth Sciences for allocation of an additional 
amount of Rs.245 crore during the current financial year in order to enable it to sustain its key 
programmes.                                                                                                                                (Para 2.4)  
Action Taken  
1.2 The Ministry of Earth Sciences would like to thank the Committee for recommending the 
allocation of an additional amount of 245 Crore during the current financial year to enable it to sustain 
its key programmes. 
 
Recommendation/Observation  
1.3 The Committee is in agreement with the Ministry that there is an urgent requirement to 
undertake deep ocean research by India.  As stated by the Secretary and acknowledged by the 
Committee, the monsoon is a critical factor for the livelihood and sustenance of crores of Indians, and 
it is determined not just by the land and atmospheric factors, but also the ocean.  The Ministry has 
been trying to get funds allocated to the Mission for the past five years.  Since the process for the 
initiation of the Mission h as been set in motion after the in -principle approval by the Ministry of 
Finance, and the Ministry of Earth Sciences has already spent Rs.19 crore out of their own budget, the 
Committee is of the considered view that the Ministry should now be provided the  requisite financial 
allocation for the Deep Ocean Mission during the current financial year and in future as well.  
                                                                                                                                                     (Para 2.8)                                                                                      
Action Taken  
1.4 The Ministry of Earth Sciences would like to thank the Committee for recommending the 
financial allocation for the Deep Ocean Missio n. The Deep Ocean Mission has now been approved by 
the Empowered Technology Group of the Govt . of India. The process for the EFC appraisal will now 
be initiated. 
 
AUTONOMOUS BODIES 
Recommendation/Observation  
1.5 The Committee is of the view that our long coast line is the lifeline of coastal communities and 
therefore sustainable management and restoration of the coastal zone and shorelines is essential.  The 
success of the demonstration studies carried out shows that such projects of the National Institute of 
Ocean Technology must be encouraged in all states along the coastline .  The Committee recommends 
that the Government should pay adequate attention to the needs of NIOT and equip NIOT with the 
promised manpower at the earliest to enable it to carry out its mandated activities.        (Para 4.6)
     

3 
 
Action Taken  
1.6 The Ministry of Earth Sciences would like to thank the Committee for recommendation to equip 
NIOT with the promised manpower. The Proposal submitted for creation of 90 posts has been 
forwarded for Dept of Expenditure.  Department of Expenditure has sought for certain clarifications 
and has been suitably clarified by NIOT and resubmitted to MoES for obtaining approval from 
Department of Expenditure.  With regard to creation of 35 permanent post s, based on the 
recommendation made by the Committee, the proposal is being submitted by NIOT to MoES for 
further processing. 
WEATHER FORECASTS/RAINFALL VARIABILITY AND CHANGES 
Recommendation/Observation  
1.7 Although the Ministry has stated that it may be  able to make predictions 24 hours before such 
events, the Committee is of the view that there is a need for the country to know the forecast about 
rains, heavy rains and cloudbursts, sufficiently in advance, in order to be able to take any pre -emptive 
action in the matter.                 (Para 5.9) 
 
Action Taken  
1.8 MoES is planning to undertake the development of Multi -Hazard Early Warning System 
(MHEWS) for Disaster Risk Management for Impact based early warnings of natural disasters through 
establishment of a Decision Support system (DSS) based on multiple meteorological disasters namely, 
Tropical cyclones, heavy rainfall, urban floods, Fog, Air pollution emergencies, Heat and Cold waves, 
thunderstorms, lightning, windstorms and flash floods. Ease of l iving would greatlyimprove 
forfishermen, coastal rural & sub-urban people, people in dense forests and hilly terrains. Coastal urban 
cities will achieve disaster risk reduction zonation. 
 
WEATHER FORECASTS/RAINFALL VARIABILITY AND CHANGES 
Recommendation/Observation  
1.9 The Committee is of the view that the Ministry of Earth Sciences, being the nodal Ministry of 
the Government of India in this field, must prioritise study of the monsoon. The Committee strongly 
recommends that the Ministry must make signific ant investments to expand the network of radars, 
High-Performance Computing systems etc. for predicting rainfall, cloudbursts, and natural disasters.  
The Committee further recommends that the Ministry must be allocated adequate additional funds 
towards setting up of observational facilities to improve the predictability of monsoon at the national 
level on a mission  mode, including studying the link between the monsoon and the ocean through deep 
ocean research.                                                (Para 5.10)          
 
Action Taken  
1.10 Ministry recognizes the importance of monsoon research and expansion of observational 
network to improve the monsoon forecasts. At present, DWR network of  MoES comprises of 27 
DWRs (including 3 DWRs from ISRO and 1 from IITM). During next five years, 48 additional DWRs, 
(as described in item 5.9) including 4 DWRs for East coast, are planned to be inducted in the existing 
network leading to effective Nowcasting and timely as well as precise warnings for severe weat her 
events like Thunderstorms, Heavy Rainfall, Downburst, Gale winds, Hail, etc. Two DWRs in the NW 
Himalayan region are in advanced stage of commissioning 
 1.11  An Atmospheric Research Testbed, a unique facility in Tropics and will be launched in 2021 
with first phase of instrumentation.This open field observatory planned to be spread in 100 acres of 
land (50 km away from Bhopal) is proposed for better understanding on processes governing monsoon 

4 
 
convection and land -atmosphere interactions over the core mo nsoon region using the state -of-the-art 
observational systems such as Radars, Wind Profilers, etc.  
 
 1.12  The Ministry had constituted a six -member committee headed by Prof N. Balakrishnan, IISc, 
Bangalore to augment the HPC facility at MoES institutes. The committee has given its 
recommendations for purchase of HPC in a phased manner. Accordingly MoES is bringing out a 
strategic document for procurement of 40 Petaflops of computing facility during the next Finance 
Cycle (2021-2026)

the data and information generated through a well -thought out policy, which does not compromise the

policy will help the Ministry to generate additional resources for augmenting important programmes 
and also help the Ministry, to some extent, to tide over the financial crunch the Ministry faces every 
year in financing some of its important projects/schemes.                                                          (Para 6.3)

ATMOSPHERE & CLIMATE RESARCH -MODELLING OBSERVING SYSTEMS & 
SERVICES (ACROSS) 
Recommendation/ Observation 
2.1 The Committee urges the Ministry, which has been able to spend only 71% of the allocated 
amount under the ACROSS scheme upto 7th February 2020, to make optimal utilisation of the allocated 
funds by the end of the financial year.                                                                                         (Para 3.4)
        
Action Taken 
2.2 The total expenditure under ACROSS scheme as on 31st March 2020 is 86% wrt to BE 2019-20 
and 93% wrt to RE 2019-20.  Due to lockdown on account of COVID, a few orders for procurement of 
equipment could not be realized and late submission of bills from vendors leading to no payment 
prevented full 100% expenditure under ACROSS.   
 
SEISMOLOGY AND GEOSCIENCES (SAGE) 
Recommendation/ Observation 
2.3 The Committee observes the declining trend of utilisation of funds under the SAGE scheme.  
The reasons offered by the Ministry relate to delay  in projects. In 2019 -20, although the allocation 
under the head was increased at the RE stage, the Ministry has so far been able to utilise little less than 
50% of the allocation made under the head.  Therefore, it is unclear how the Ministry will spend t he 
unspent 50% of the allocation in the remaining part of the financial year. The Committee urges the 
Ministry to take corrective action in this matter.                                                                         (Para 3.8)
          
Action Taken 
2.4 About50% of the allocation in the remaining part of 2019 -20 was allocated for requirement of 
additional funds for International Geological Congress (IGC) scheduled in March 2020 and 
microzonation work. However, in wake of COVID -19 date of IGC 2020 wa s postponed to November 
2020 and alsomicrozonation studies could not be initiated due to lockdown. Subsequently, date of 36th 
IGC was again postponed  in wake of COVID -19 pandemic from  November 2020  to August 
2021. Also, payment towards LC for procurement o f seismic systems could not be made in  March 
2020 due to COVID -19 outbreak and lockdown. Release for IGC shall be made later in due course in 
FY 2020-21. Moreover, payment towards LC has been done. 
 
OCEAN SERVICES, MODELLING, APPLICATION, RESOURCES AND TEC HNOLOGY 
(OSMART) 
Recommendation/Observation 
2.5 The Committee notes that the reasons for the reduction of funds under OSMART scheme are 
once again related to administrative delays. The Committee urges the Ministry to avoid such delays in 
the future so as t o be able to justify increased allocation of funds for its plans and upcoming 
programmes, that are important.                                                                                          (Para 3.12) 
                                     
 

7 
 
 
Action Taken 
2.6 As recommended by the committee the Ministry has taken measures to avoid such delays. 
 
RESEARCH, EDUCATION, OUTREACH AND TRAINING (REACHOUT) 
Recommendation/Observation 
2.7 The Committee notes that the existing monitoring mechanism of the Ministr y has not ensured 
overall effective utilisation of funds under the various schemes of the Ministry, which resulted in 
reduction of allocations at RE stage in these schemes. The Committee recommends that the Ministry 
needs to review its financial performanc e under the various schemes and programmes, analyse the 
reasons for underutilisation, identify the administrative bottlenecks and take corrective measures to 
ensure that funds allocated under various schemes are not underutilised in future.               (Para 3.16)
                 
Action Taken 
2.8 The Ministry thank the committee for its advice.  
2.9 Expenditure under each scheme is being reviewed on a bi -monthly basis to strictly follow the 
guidelines on “Expenditure Control” issued by Ministry of Finance vide its OM No. 12(13) -
B(W&M)/2020 dated 8 th April 2020 and OM No. 12(15) -B(W&M)/2020 dated 23 June 2020. In the 
first quarter of FY 2020-21 ending 30 June 2020, Ministry incurred the full expenditure of 15% of BE, 
as per the instruction of MoF.  
 
AUTONOMOUS BODIES 
Recommendation/Observation 
2.10 The Committee notes that the financial performance of all the autonomous institutions under 
the Ministry has been satisfactory during the years 2017 -18 and 2018 -19.  As regards 2019 -20, the 
Committee observed that the allocation of all the autonomous bodies had been reduced at RE stage, 
except NIOT, Chennai.  The reasons furnished for this reduction are deferment of maintenance work on 
existing building and upgradation of existing IT infrastructure, delay in start o f contractual work of 
lab, postponement of some civil work etc.  The Committee feels that the reasons furnished are mostly 
procedural and administrative, and could have been avoided. The Committee recommends that the 
Ministry should take up the matter with  all the concerned agencies and ensure that such 
underutilisation of funds is avoided in the future.                                   (Para 4.2) 
 
Action Taken 
2.11 The Ministry thanks the committee for its advice. During 2019 -20, allocation under Assistan ce 
to Autonomous bodies at BE stage was Rs. 178 crore and as on 30.9.2019 fund utilization was Rs. 
102.29 which was 57.49% of BE.  However due to overall cut in the budget at the RE stage by Ministry 
of Finance, the autonomous institutes under the Ministry  deferred the work related to maintenance, 
upgradation of IT infrastructure and some civil work. NIOT was given additional grants to meet the 
deficit in Leave encashment and gratuity to NIOT employees. 
Recommendation/Observation 
2.12 However, the Committee  notes that out of the increased allocation of Rs.50.78 crore, NIOT 
could spend only Rs.20.49 crore upto 7 th February 2020, which is 40 percent of the allocated amount.  
The Committee recommends that the Ministry impress upon NIOT, Chennai to take necessar y action to 
ensure that the funds are utilised within the current financial year for the purpose for which additional 
funds were granted at RE stage.                                               (Para 4.3) 

8 
 
Action Taken 
2.13 Against the allocation of Rs.50.78 crores (RE) towards Manpower and Operational Expenses of 
NIOT (Assistance to Autonomous Bodies), the expenditure incurred by NIOT as on 31 st March 2020 is 
54.50 crores with an excess spent of Rs.3.85 crores.   
 
WEATHER FORECASTS/RAINFALL VARIABILITY AND CHANGES 
Recommendation/Observation 
2.14 The Committee underlines the importance of the monsoon for India and its people, and finds 
that a 30 percent allocation of the Ministry’s budget to study the monsoon is a low figure. Further, the 
Committee is of th e considered view that given the alarming nature of changing rainfall patterns in 
various parts of the country, which adversely affects the local population, and especially the farmers 
and their crops; there is an urgent need to provide advance information  about such events to all 
stakeholders, especially to farmers and fisher folks in the country.                                            (Para 5.8)
          
Action Taken 
2.15 The Ministry thank the Committee for its support.  Prediction of monsoons requir es intense 
observations of land, sea and atmosphere, assimilation of observations, running high resolution models.  
At present reliable forecasts at districts and block level are generated at least 5 days in advance. In 
addition, an extended range forecasting system (for forecasts for next four weeks) is also implemented. 
These forecasts are shared with the farming community in the country. For fishermen, ocean state 
forecasts and potential fishing zone advisories also issued for next three days. The Minist ry will try to 
increase the allocation for Monsoon research in future.
