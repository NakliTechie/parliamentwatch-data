REPORT 
The Action Taken Report (ATR) of the Committee deals with the action taken by the 
Government on the recommendations/observations contained in the Three Hundred Forty-Seventh 
Report of the Committee on “ Demands for Grants (2021 -22) of the Ministry of Earth Scienc es”.  
The Report was presented in  Rajya Sabha and laid on the Table of Lok Sabha on the 8 th March, 
2021. 
2. Action Taken Note s (ATN) have  been received from Ministry of Earth Sciences  (MoES) 
in respect of the recommendations/observations con tained in the Three Hundred Forty - Seventh 
Report. This has been categorized as follows:- 
Chapter I: Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the 
Government: Para:- 2.3, 2.11, 2.12, 2.14, 3.4, 3.5, 4.3, 4.5, 5.2 , 5.3 and 5.5 (of 
the Three Hundred Forty-Seventh Report) 
 Total recommendations – 11 Accepted 
The Committee is pleased to note that the Ministry has accepted its 
recommendations/observations as mentioned in Chapter-I of the Report. 
Chapter II: Recommendations/Observations which the Committee does not desire to pursue 
in view of the Government’s replies: Para Nos. 2.4 and 2.10 (ibid)     
Total recommendations – 02.  Not to be pursued further. 
The Committee is convinced with the explanations furnished by the Ministry 
and, therefore, does not want to pursue the recommendations further.  
Chapter III: Recommendations/Observations in respect of which replies of the Government 
have not been accepted by the Committee: Para Nos.- 2.9(ibid)     
Total recommendations – 01 Not convincing reply. 
  The Committee expresses its concern over the non -implementation of its 
recommendation contained in Chapter -III and desires that the Ministry should 
furnish convincing action taken steps in respect of those recommendations.  
Chapter IV:  Recommendations/Observations in respect of which final replies of the 
Government are still awaited: Para Nos.- Nil 
3. The details of the ATR have been analyzed in the succeeding Chapters.

CHAPTER-I 
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED BY THE 
GOVERNMENT 
BUDGETARY DETAILS FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2020 -21 AND ALLOCATION 
FOR 2021-22 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.1 The Committee notes that the Ministry, under its Capital Budget, was allocated Rs.74.90 
crore in RE 2020 -21 against the budgeta ry allocation of Rs.172 crore in BE 2020 -21.  The 
Committee further notes that the Ministry has been given Rs.163 crore in BE 2021 -22 which is 
Rs. 57.79 crore more than the actual expenditure of Rs.105.21 crore during the year 2019 -20 in 
the same category. The Committee urges the Department to complete all its programme s/projects 
envisaged for the year 2021 -22, and fully utilise the allocation of Rs.172 crore in BE 2020 -21, 
especially since the trend of the last several years even before COVID -19 pandemic has been of 
actual expenditure being lower than the revised estimates.                 (Para 2.3of the 347th Report) 
Action Taken 
1.2 Noted for compliance.  Due to the austerity measures , due to Covid pandemic, the overall 
budget of the Ministry was reduced by Rs. 770 crore from Rs. 2070 crore to Rs.1300 crore at RE 
stage.  Accordingly, at RE stage allocation u nder Capital was reduced to Rs. 74.90 crore against 
allocation o f Rs. 172 crore under Capital in BE 2020 -21.  The Ministry has incurred an 
expenditure of Rs. 69.70 crore (93.1% of RE) during 2020 -21 under Capital.  The Ministry could 
not fully utilize Rs. 74.90 crore (RE 2020-21) as Central Public Work Department  (CPWD) was 
unable to fully utilize the funds authorised to them to carry out Major works assigned to them by 
India Meteorological Department  (IMD) and Centre for Marine Living Resources & Ecology 
(CMLRE) and slow progress of boundary wall construction by the contractor at Mandapam for 
National Centre for Coastal Research (NCCR) Field Centre. 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.3 The Committee further opines that the Ministry should prepare a blueprint highlighting 
the avenues that can be explored by the Ministry to generate revenue during the next 5 years, to 
reduce the pressure on the budget of the Ministry, and accomplish its critical expansion plans.  
                                 (Para 2.11ibid)                                              
Action Taken 
1.4 Noted for compliance. The Ministry will come out with a blueprint highlighting the 
avenues that can be explored to generate revenue during the next 5 years. 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.5 The Committee recommends that the Ministry scale up holding various seminars, training 
sessions, etc. online, wherever feasible, to help the Ministry to save its financial resources, 
whereas the money saved could be better utilised for its R&D activities or other equally important 
priorities.                                                                                                                    (Para 2.12 ibid) 
Action Taken 
1.6 Noted for compliance. 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.7 The Committee recommends that the Ministry should take immediate corrective remedial 
action in the matter and impresses upon all the related government/private bodies to initiate the 
process of clearing their dues at the earliest.                                                        (Para 2.14 ibid)

Action Taken 
1.8 The matter of over dues of payment has been taken up with the top level of Airports 
Authority of India (AAI) and D.O. letters dated 15.04.2020 and 16.12.2020 have been written by 
DGM, India Meteorological Department ( IMD) to the Chairman, AAI.  To further remove the 
lagging in the recovery of dues from AAI in future, the IMD is in process of signing a new MOU 
with AAI.  In this MOU, a new mechanism has been proposed whereby IMD will charge 4.5% of 
Route Navigation Facility Charges ( RNFC) of AAI.   Further, it is being insisted that before 
signing the New MOU, AAI has to clear all dues of IMD.   It is also mentioned here that in 
compliance with audit observation, IMD has also requested AAI to include the penalty clause in 
the MOU for delay i n payment towards the cost of Aviation Met services being provided to AAI 
by IMD. It is also mentioned in the new MOU that the bill will be raised on a monthly basis by 
the IMD. 
UMBRELLA SCHEMES OF MINISTRY OF EARTH SCIENCES 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.9 The above data suggests that the overall financial performance of the umbrella schemes of 
the Ministry has been satisfactory.  However, under the Seismology and Geosciences (SAGE)  
scheme, the Ministry has spent only 65% of its allocated fund upto January, 2021 during the year 
2020-21.  The Committee urges the Ministry to optimally utilise the unspent funds during the 
remaining part of the Financial Year 2020-21.                                                          (Para 3.4 ibid)                                                                                                                                                                                         
Action Taken 
1.10 Against Rs.39 crore allocated at RE stage under SAGE during 2020 -21, an amount of Rs. 
38.74 crore has been spent during the year. 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.11 The Committee also notes that though the overall budgetary allocation under the umbrella 
Schemes of the Ministry in BE 2021 -22 is little less than the allocation of BE 2020 -21, it is 
hopeful that the Ministry will make earnest efforts to ensure that the funds allocated in BE 2021 -
22 will be optimally utilised.   The Committee also hopes that t he Ministry, with the help of its 
periodic reviews of the financial and physical performance of these Schemes, will strive hard to 
achieve the targets set for the year 2021-22.                              (Para 3.5 ibid)                                                      
Action Taken 
1.12 The Ministry thanks the Committee and has noted the advice for compliance.   Half yearly 
reviews are regularly undertaken under the Chairmanship of Secretary to review the financial and  
physical performance of each of the schemes.   In addition, periodic reviews are done every month 
to ensure judicious use of the budget to ensure un -interrupted service for operational activities of 
the Ministry. The Ministry has incurred an expenditure o f ₹259 crore which is 13.65% of BE as 
on 10th June 2021.  
AUTONOMOUS BODIES 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.13 The Committee finds the overall fund utilisation by the autonomous institutions during the 
years 2018-19, 2019 -20 and 2020 -21 to be satisfactory which has been to the tune of 99.31%, 
98.90% and 84.86% respectively. The Committee, however, feels that the financial performance of 
National Centre for Earth Science Studies (NCESS) could have been better during the year 2020 -
21, whereas it utilised only 71.20% of the allocated amount upto 31.01.2021.         (Para 4.3 ibid)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
Action Taken 
1.14 The expenditure in 2020 -21 (71.2% as per the table) till 31.01.2021 was arrived at based 
on expenditure of Rs. 8.90 crore which is likely to be a mid -year figure. The expe nditure of the 
full year is ₹12.50 crore which is 100% of the RE of Rs.12.50 crore.

Recommendation/ Observation 
1.15 The Committee has noted with concern the non -utilisation of grants amounting to Rs. 
173.67 crore received by National Institute of Ocean Technology ( NIOT) from the Ministry and

DEEP OCEAN MISSION 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.17 The Committee notes the various components and proposed activities envisaged by the 
Ministry under its Deep Ocean Mission and the funds required to realise the intended benefits of 
the Mission.  The Committee acknowledges that this Mission will not only st rengthen country’s 
position in the Indian Ocean region, but envisages exploration of minerals, energy and marine 
diversity of the underwater world, a vast part of which still remains unexplored.  Since this 
Mission is an     inter-ministerial Mission where various other Ministries and Departments of the 
Government of India such as  Indian Space Research Organisation  (ISRO), Defence Research & 
Development Organisation ( DRDO), Council of Scientific & Industrial Research ( CSIR), 
Department of  Biotechnology (DBT), etc. also on board with the Ministry of Earth Sciences.  The 
Committee urges all these wings of the Government to work in tandem to make this Mission a 
success.    The Ministry has indicated a requirement of ₹400 crore for the Deep Ocean Mission 
(DOM).  It is of the utmost importance that the Mission is adequately funded by the Government 
with timely availability of the funds to facilitate the Mission in realising its intended goals in a 
systematic and time bound manner.                                                                            (Para 5.2 ibid)  
Action Taken 
1.18 The Ministry thanks the Committee for its support to the Deep Ocean Mission. The Deep 
Ocean Mission, India‟s ambitious plan to explore and harness deep -oceanic resources was 
approved by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs  on 16/06/2021.  The program w ill be 
implemented at an estimated cost of  ₹4,077 crore over five years in a phased manner.   MoES 
shall coordinate with the institutes mentioned above and also with academic organisations and 
industry partners for the efficient timely execution of the Mission. 
Recommendation/ Observation 
1.19 The Committee, therefore, recommends that the Ministry of Finance should provide the 
requisite funds at the RE stage to the Ministry so that the Mission is successful.      (Para 5.3 ibid)                                                                                           
Action Taken 
1.20 The Ministry will seek the required funds through the 2 nd Supplementary Demands for 
Grants 2021-22. 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.21 The Committee acknowledges both the strategic importance of India’s presence in the 
Arctic, the Antarctic, and the Southern Indian Ocean, and the tangible impact of scientific 
research carried out there for the nation, especially on climate change and the Indian monsoon.  
The Committee also notes how China has invested both resources and scientific manpower in 
these regions.  The Committee therefore recommends that the Ministry prepare a realistic plan for 
its expansion in the next 5 years, including capita l expenditure for the acquisition of polar 
research vessel and aircraft, and scientific manpower in the Arctic, Antarctic and the South 

5 
 
Indian Ocean.  The Committee urges the Ministry to impress upon the Ministry of Finance to 
provide requisite funds for the same in a timely manner.                                           (Para 5.5 ibid) 
Action Taken 
1.22 Ministry thanks the Committee for acknowledging the strategic importance of India‟s 
presence in the Polar Region and impact of scientific research carried out there.  The realistic plan 
for expansion in the next 5 years is including in the Expenditure Finance Committee (EFC) memo 
for continuing of Polar and Cryosphere Research (PACER) scheme.  It also includes the proposal 
of creation of scientific m anpower, both permanent and temporary, for Polar Science. Since the 
proposed EFC is for continuing program, the capital expenditure for acquisition of Polar Research 
Vessel (PRV) and renovation/reconstruction of Maitri Station will be taken up as a sep arate EFC 
for Cabinet approval.  MoES will pursue with the Ministry of Finance to provide requisite funds 
for the same.

RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH THE COMMITTEE DOES NOT 
DESIRE TO PURSUE IN VIEW OF THE GOVERNMENT'S REPLIES 
BUDGETARY DETAILS FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR 2020 -21 AND ALLOCATION 
FOR 2021-22 
Recommendation/Observation 
2.1 The Committee observes that though the Department utilise d Rs. 1,121.86 crore till 
January, 2021 against Rs. 1,300 crore in RE 2020 -21 which is 86.29% utilisation, however, the 
utilisation of funds in terms of BE 2020 -21 allocation of Rs. 2070 crore has only been to the tune 
of 54.19%.                                                                                           (Para 2.4 of the 347th Report) 
Action Taken 
2.2 The reduced utilization of funds to the tune of 54.19%  in terms of BE 2020 -21 allocation 
of Rs. 2070 crore was due to the reduction in the overall budget of the Ministry by Rs. 770 crore 
from Rs. 2070 crore to Rs. 1300 crore at RE stage , in view of the austerity measures due to 
Covid-19 pandemic.  MoES managed the available resources to operate its critical services. 
Recommendation/ Observation 
2.3 The Ministry of Earth Sciences should impress upon  the Ministry of Finance and sensitise 
about its absolute bare minimum requirement of additional funds in order to sustain its key 
programmes.                                                                                                              (Para 2.10 ibid)  
Action Taken 
2.4 The Ministry thanks the Committee and has noted the advice for compliance.  A letter was 
sent by Secretary MoES to Secretary , Expenditure on 15th April 2021, appraising the requirement 
of additional funds with full justif ication. However, the proposal was not accepted by Ministry of 
Finance (MoF).
