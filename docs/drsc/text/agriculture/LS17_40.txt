parity amongst regions can be brought about. Efforts to make these regions more financially 
included are also stressed so that the improved credit 
offtake can be affected along with 
higher allocation of targets.
 

 
To address regional disparities in the flow of priority sector credit, higher weightage have 
been assigned to incremental priority sector credit in ‘identified districts’ where priority secto
r

-

Sheet Exposure (CEOBE), whichever is

Scheduled Commercial Banks to Agriculture.

10% between 2020
-

-


 
Government of India 
implements an interest subvention scheme under which short term 
crop loans up to  Rs. 3.00 lakh are provided to farmers at a reduced interest rate  of 7% p.a. 
The scheme provides interest subvention of 2 % per annum to Banks on use of their own 
resources.

the loan, thereby reducing the effective rate of interest to 4%.
