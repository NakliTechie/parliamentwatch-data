The Committee are constrained to note that the Department has surrendered

PRIVATE INVESTMENT IN AGRICULTURE

The Committee are aware of the fact that Private Investment in Agriculture 
Sector is the need of

the private investment is for in

processing, animal health and farm machinery. These Private R&D Organizations

Government to safeguard the interests of vulnerable farmers while allowing private 
investment in this Sector.
