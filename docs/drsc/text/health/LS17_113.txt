Recommendation
3.1 The Committee observes that constraints of funds should not come in the way of the medical
as well as health research. The Committee would like the Department to formulate a Comprehensive
Action Plan, while projecting its funds requirement before the Ministry of Finance. The Committee
believes that the Department of Health Research has not been effective in persuading the Ministry
of Finance for seeking required funds and therefore, the additional funds are received   at fag end
of the Financial Year . The Committee recommends that the Department should be more proactive
in holding pre- budget discussion and forwarding the proposals for Supplementary Grant in order
to ensure that funds are released well in advance at RE stage. (Para 2.3)
Action Taken
3.2 The recommendation of the Hon’ble Committee has been noted for compliance. Necessary
action would be taken by the Department to more effectively project its requirements before the
Ministry of Finance during pre-budgetary discussions on finalization of the Revised Estimates for
2018-19.
Further Recommendation
3.3 The Committee believes that the Department of Health Research had effectively forwarded
its requirements of funds at the stage of Revised Estimate for 2018-19 to complete the
schemes of DHR and ICMR as per the set targets. However, the Committee would like to
be apprised of the funds received at RE stage and the physical achievement of ongoing/
already sanctioned projects against the set targets. The Committee also believes that DHR
would also hold budget discussion  2019-20 with Ministry of Finance and effectively place  its
projected demand as per Comprehensive Action Plan, as noted by DHR in Para 2.3 of  107th
Report for compliance. In this regard, the Committee would like to be apprised of the
ingredients of Comprehensive Action Plan of DHR and the requirement of funds to accomplish
the set objectives/targets.
Recommendations
3.4 The Committee notes while on the one hand the Department claims that here is shortfall of
funds for implementation of the projects/schemes of the Department on the other hand, there is
under-utilization of funds with respect to certain schemes/projects like Development of Tools to
prevent Outbreaks of epidemics (33.20%); Human Resource Development for Health Research
(53.15%), Grant-in-aid Scheme for inter-sectoral convergence and Coordination for Promotion &
Guidance on Health Research (55.43%),  Research Governance–HTA (6.83%), International Cooperation
(14.00%), Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) 69.68%), Secretariat Expenditure  (60.71).
The Committee, therefore, recommends that the Department should seriously monitor the funds
allocated at BE stage as well as RE stage in each quarter with a view to ensure optimum

IV INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT FOR HEALTH RESEARCH SETTING-UP NATION
WIDE NETWORK OF LABORATORIES FOR MANAGING EPIDEMICS AND NATIONAL
CALAMITIES.
Recommendation
3.32 The Committee notes that against the target of setting up 25 VRDLs in the year 2017-18, only
10 new VRDLs have been established. On being asked about the shortfall, the Department clarified
that establishment of 1 regional level VRDL is under process. Funds would be released to 4
approved VRDLs after completion of necessary nodal formalities, proposals for 4 new VRDLs have
been received from State Governments of Maharashtra and Gujarat and visit to 2 new sites have
been undertaken. The Committee would like the Department to seek more funds, if needed at RE
stage, keeping in view the importance of such laboratories. The Committee also recommends that
the Department may take initiatives for setting up VRDLs in the States of Goa and D elhi
specially. (Para 4.7)
Action Taken
3.33 12 new VRDLs (1 Regional level; 1 State level and 9 Medical College level VRDLs) have been
established during 2017-18. Another three VRDLs have been sanctioned in April-May, 2018, bringing
the cumulative number of VRDLs at 83 across the country.
3.34 As regards Delhi, a proposal for establishment of VRDL at Lady Harding Medical College has
been received and is under process. Efforts are being made to seek the proposal from Goa. The
allocation in BE 2018-19 for this scheme is ` 70.00 crores and additional funds, if required, will
be sought at the RE stage.
Further Recommendation
3.35 The Committee observes with concern that out of the 25 VRDLs proposed to be set up
in 2017-18 only 12 VRDLs have been established which is less than 50% of targets set. A
track record of this purpose would stymie the setting up of VRDLs in future that  would
result in backlog of setting up a VRDLs in future. The Committee, therefore implores upon
the Department to achieve the target set for setting up VRDLs upto 2019-20 in the country
so that adequate infrastructure and capacities are created for timely identification of known
viruses/agents of emerging epidemics and develop new diagnostic Kitts. The Committee further
recommends the ICMR to undertake fresh research for identification of emerging and new
genetically active/modified viruses and accordingly, disseminate information for imparting training
to health professional/practitioners in treating new diseases across the country.
