REPORT
The Report of the Committee deals with the action taken by the Government on the Ecommendations
contained in the Ninety-Fourth Report of the Committee on Demands for Grants (Demand No. 43) of
the Department of Health Research for the year 2016-17.
2. Action Taken Notes (ATNs) have been received from the Government in respect of the
recommendations contained in the 94th Report. They have been categorized as follows:
(i) Recommendations/Observations in respect of which replies of the Government have been
accepted by the Committee: 2.9, 2.15, 2.18, 3.7, 3.11, 5.3, 9.6, 12.2
TOTAL - 8 (Chapter I)
(ii) Recommendations/Observations which the Committee does not desire to pursue in view of
the Government's replies: 2.10, 2.12, 2.16, 3.15, 4.7, 7.5, 8.3, 9.5, 11.4
TOTAL -9 (Chapter II)
(iii) Recommendations/Observations in respect of which replies of the Government have not
been accepted by the Committee: 3.12, 3.14, 3.19, 6.5, 10.4
TOTAL - 5 (Chapter III)
(iv) Recommendations/Observations in respect of which final replies of the Government are still
awaited:
TOTAL - Nil (Chapter IV)
3. The details of the ATNs are discussed in various Chapters in the succeeding pages.

RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERV ATIONS IN RESPECT OF WHICH REPLIES OF

II. BUDGETARY ALLOCATION
Recommendation/Observation
1.1 The Committee finds that against total Twelfth Plan outlay of ` 10,029.00 crores, actual total
allocation at R.E. stage for the four years of the Twelfth Plan is only ` 2316.60 crores (Table No.l) and
the actual expenditure up to 21st March, 2016 is ` 2257.15 crores. The Committee observes that the
Department had projected a requirement of `  1689.43 crores but the actual allocation for 2016-17 is
` 750.00 crores (Table No. 3). This shortfall would affect the proposed targets of Schemes like Human
Resources Development for Health, Establishment of VRDLs, MRUs and MRHRUs for 2016-17. The
Committee was apprised of these difficulties which would be faced in completion of these schemes
during the meeting of the Committee with the Secretary, Department of Health Research on 21st March,
2016. On being asked as to what was the bare minimum requirements of additional financial resources
that would be essential for carrying out the important activities of the Department, the Secretary,
Department of Health Research informed that unless `  300.00 crores additional resources are provided,
even the pending projects would be in a jeopardy. The Committee observes that the Department of Health
Research is mandated to promote basic, applied and clinical research related to medical health and
bio-medical performance in cutting edge areas, starving of the on-going and proposed basic, applied and
clinical research projects in the medical and public health research would prove detrimental to generation
of new knowledge in the areas of health research. The Committee, therefore, lends its Parliamentary
support for enhancement of funds to the tune of ` 300.00 crores to the Department of Health Research
for financial year 2016-17. This recommendation of the Committee may be immediately brought to the
notice of Ministry of Finance. The Committee desires to be apprised of response of the Ministry of
Finance in this regard. (Para 2.9)
Action Taken
1.2 Proposal for additional budget requirement of ` 545.00 crores has been submitted to Ministry of
Finance under the First Batch of Supplementary Demand for Grant, with detailed justification and
scheme-wise requirements. Proposal for additional requirement of funds would again be taken up at pre-
budgetary discussions and RE stage.
Recommendation/Observation
1.3 The Committee observes that there has been a major reduction at RE (2015-16) stage in respect
of funds provided for establishment of multi-disciplinary Research Units (MRUs) and Grant-in-aid for
Inter-Sectoral Convergence and Promotion and Guidance on Health Research. The Committee has also
been informed that establishment of MRUs depends on proposals of the respective State Governments.
The Committee finds that the funds provided at RE (2015-16) stage have nearly been exhausted. The
Committee therefore recommends that the Ministry of Finance should consider enhancing the funds

previous year. (Para 2.15)
Action Taken

under the First Batch of Supplementary Demand for Grant, include requirement of ` 6.00 crores for the
scheme of Establishment of Multi-disciplinary Units (MRUs) and another ` 6.00 for Grant-in-aid for
Inter-Sectoral Convergence & Promotion and Guidance on Health Research. Proposal for enhancement
of budgetary allocation will also be impressed upon at the RE stage.

that the Department should consider this aspect. (Para 2.18)

recommendations for more attention and investment in the field are acknowledged and noted for compliance.

RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH THE COMMITTEE DOES NOT DESIRE
TO PURSUE IN VIEW OF THE GOVERNMENT'S REPLIES
II. BUDGETARY ALLOCATION
Recommendation/Observation
2.1 The Committee also expresses its dissatisfaction on the big mismatch between the projected
requirement and allocated funds (Table No.3). The Committee notes that some major programmes/
schemes are likely to be affected as a result of the shortfall of ` 939.43 crores in Plan allocation for
2016-17. The Committee desires that constraints of funds should not come in the way of the medical
as well as health research. The Committee recommends that the Department prepare a comprehensive
action plan while presenting its fund requirement before the Ministry of Finance which may be bifurcated
into projects for which funds are desirable and projects for which funds are essential and therefore non-
negotiable. Considering the fact that scientific knowledge generated through health and bio-medical
research is used to develop drugs, diagnostic vaccines and devices that ultimately find place in the health
care delivery systems of the country as also the fact that health research has a vital role to play in
discovery and development of indigenous health products, the Committee recommends that the allocation
for the Department for 2016-17 be suitably enhanced at RE stage. The Committee also impresses upon
the Department to pay focussed attention to more efficient deployment of the available resources.
(Para 2.10)
Action Taken
2.2 The action plan for categorising the projects as per priority including on-going projects in the field
of health research is being prepared by ICMR, which will clearly demarcate the projects for which funds
are essential and non-negotiable and the projects which will be taken up subject to availability of additional
funds. The requirement of additional funds as per the two categories on the basis of priority in the areas
of public health concern shall be projected before the Department of Expenditure during discussions at
RE stage.
ICMR has prioritized its research activities to ensure efficient deployment of available resources,
with focus on (i) Environmental Health and Climate Change impact on Health; (ii) Research on emerging
viral disease e.g. Ebola, Dengue, Chikungunya; as well as Non-communicable Diseases like Cancer and
Diabetes; (iii) improving maternal and child health and combating malnutrition, and strengthening health
research capacity of institutes in the country.
Recommendation/Observation
2.3 The Committee has in the earlier part of this Report already recommended enhancement of Plan
allocations for the Schemes of Department. As regards the non-plan allocations remaining stagnant during
2015-16 and 2016-17, the Committee observes that the Ministry of Finance while making non-plan
6

7
allocation for the Department for 2016-17 should have taken inflation into account and made adequate
non-plan allocation for 2016-17 accordingly. The Committee, therefore, recommends that the Ministry
of Finance should address the inadequacy of non-plan allocation to the Department for 2016-17 after
factoring in inflation. (Para 2.12)
Action Taken
2.4 Proposal for additional budget requirement of ` 545.00 crores has been submitted to Ministry of
Finance under the First Batch of Supplementary Demand for Grant, include the Non Plan requirement
of ` 91.00 crores. Proposal for enhancement of budgetary allocation will also be impressed upon at the
RE stage.
Recommendation/Observation
2.5 In reply to a query regarding the number of utilization certificates pending and the amount involved
therein for the research projects/schemes funded by the Department and the efforts made in this regard,
the Committee has informed that pendency of UCs in the year 2013-14 was 22 involving an amount of
` 27.50 crore under MRUs. However, in the year 2014-15, the number of pending UCs was reduced
to 3 involving a sum of `  1.61 crore. The pending UCs under the ICMR in the year 2014-15 was
amounting to `  87.00 lakhs. Explaining the efforts made by the Department for expediting settlement

visits carried out and desk review meetings held. So far 45 field visits have been undertaken. The
Committee notes the efforts of the Department in settlement of pending UCs and hopes that pendency

(Para 2.16)
Action Taken

UCs against other schemes of Ministry of Health & Family Welfare to facilitate transfer of funds under
the schemes of Department of Health Research.

problems of the area.
2.19 Research proposals involving high maternal mortality and high infant mortality in the area will be
given priority.

CHAPTER-III
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERV ATIONS IN RESPECT OF WHICH REPLIES OF THE
GOVERNMENT HA VE NOT BEEN ACCEPTED BY THE COMMITTEE
III. INDIAN COUNCIL OF MEDICAL RESEARCH (ICMR)
Recommendation/Observation
3.1 The Committee observes that the high priority research initiatives like T.B., Cancer, anaemia and
nutrition which are affecting people from all strata of the society need more attention and therefore more
funds are required. The Committee recommends that the Department should ensure that the research
initiatives be prioritised as essential on which no compromise can be allowed. The Committee also
recommends that the Department should also formulate a list of diseases which can be eradicated
completely like Hepatitis, Kala Azar, Filarisis, Leprosy Measles, etc. and impress upon the Ministry of
Finance for allocating more funds to ICMR. (Para 3.12)
Action Taken
3.2 ICMR is actively involved in the field of Health Research and taken following initiatives:
Establishment of India TB Research Consortium: to address Tuberculosis (TB) in Mission
Mode that aims bringing together all agencies to address overarching scientific questions.
Triple drug therapy for filariasis elimination.
Novel non-invasive method for diagnosis of visceral leishmaniasis by testing of sputum samples.
ICMR Institute of Cytology and Preventive Oncology (ICPO) a Knowledge Hub in India for
"Smokeless Tobacco Use" by WHO. ICMR has also developed a Cancer Web Portal for cancer
awareness.
Launch of Mobile App on 'Dietary Guidelines for Indians: The App is based on the
Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDAs) for Indians.
Fortified Food to Fight Malnutrition:  ICMR has come up with a micronutrient mix that is being
considered by Government for its flagship programmes
ICMR is working with Ministry of Health and Family Welfare Disease Control Programme to give
inputs for elimination of Leprosy, Filariasis, Kala-azar and Measles.
Further Recommendation
3.3 The Committee takes note of the initiatives taken by ICMR for promotion of Health
Research. However, no mention has been made in the action taken note about formulating
research projects for complete eradication of diseases like Hepatitis, Kala-azar, Filariasis etc.
within a specified time-frame, as recommended by the Committee. The Committee feels that
pure biomedical research without any accompanying gain in the shape of solutions to health
10

11
issues confronting the country within a specific time-frame will not serve the national interests.
The Committee, therefore, desires that the Department should chalk out a realistic, time-bound
roadmap for eradication of the above diseases and project financial requirements accordingly.
Recommendation/Observation
3.4 The Committee observes that in a resource constrained country like India, judicious utilisation of
funds is of paramount importance. Equally important is the fact that the attention should be focussed
on health outcomes rather than health outlays. The Committee would therefore like the Department to
get all ongoing research projects, being funded wholly or partially with Government money evaluated by
an independent agency and place the findings in the public domain. (Para 3.14)
Action Taken
3.5 The mechanism for evaluation of overall research outputs has been put in place by the ICMR
through an external agency. The agency will carry out the analysis, which would include the impact of
ICMR projects as well as stakeholders perspective.
Further Recommendation
3.6 The Committee notes that a mechanism for evaluation of overall research outputs has
been put in place by ICMR through an external agency which will analyse the impact of the
projects of ICMR. However, details about this external agency have not been shared with the
Committee. The Committee would like the Department to put the findings of this external
agency in the public domain so as to inform the general public about the projects of ICMR and
also make ICMR accountable for judicious utilisation of funds.
Recommendation/Observation
3.7 The Committee takes note of a strategic vision document of ICMR, i.e. one year, three year and
five years in which the Department focuses on developing new drugs, traditional medicines and taking
initiatives on Nutrition, TB and Clean Ganga with the collaboration of DBT, CSIR. The studies should
be completed at the earliest and desired outcomes should be achieved within the time frame fixed.
Particularly study on medicines for dengue should be done on priority. (Para 3.19)
Action Taken
3.8 The Strategic Vision Document-2030 has already been prepared and submitted to NITI Aayog.
Studies on medicines for dengue are being carried out by ICMR Institutes in collaboration with AYUSH.
A multi- institutional collaborative project on Exploring the micro-biome of Ganga has been submitted to
the Ministry of Water Resource, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation for funding.
Further Recommendation
3.9 The Committee would like to draw the attention of the Department to manifold increase
in cases of dengue and Chikungunya in the country. Severe outbreak of these diseases in the

Recommendation/Observation
3.10 The Committee is disappointed to note that against RE (2015-16) of ` 80.00 lakhs, the actual

in Health Research is of vital importance for the country. Exchange of ideas and sharing of
knowledge would enhance and boost our country's research enabling us to tackle various medical
and health challenges faced by the people. The Committee believes that an action plan for every

X. ESTABLISHMENT OF MODEL RURAL HEALTH RESEARCH UNITS (MRHRUs) IN THE
STATES
Recommendation/Observation
3.13 The Committee observes that there is a wide gap between the available specialized health care
technology and the technology being developed vis-a-vis their utilization in the State health systems and
there is a need to calibrate the same according to local State needs. The Committee finds that in the 12th
Plan Period, only 15 MRHRUs have been approved. Out of 15, 12 MRHRUs have already been sanctioned
and proposals of three are in pipeline. The Committee recommends that the Department should devise
a mechanism to ensure that set targets are accomplished within timeline. The Committee desires that the
proposal of the Department to have larger projects may be considered and decision taken at the earliest.
(Para 10.4)
Action Taken
3.14 Efforts are being made to sanction the remaining 3 MRHRUs during 2016-17. A Task Force
Project entitled "Improving Health and Nutritional Status of vulnerable Segment of Population by
Implementing Multi-component Health and Nutritional Education Intervention as Sustainable Model of
Intervention" has been initiated by 8 MRHRUs, namely, Assam, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan,
Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Punjab. Another project entitled: Establishing Health Surveillance
in Rural Areas of DHR MRHRUs across India" is proposed to be initiated by all the 12 MRHRUs.
Further Recommendation
3.15 The Committee notes the inordinate delay in the sanctioning of the remaining three
MRHRUs. Projects undertaken by MRHRUs will only be successful if it is benefiting large
number of population in different States. Hence, the Committee feels that the remaining three
MRHRUs need to be made functional without any further loss of funds so that health interventions
through research in the States could be given impetus. The Committee would also like to
reiterate that selection of projects should be based on the relevant information of the region and
as per the needs of the local population.
