REPORT
DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH RESEARCH
I. INTRODUCTION
1.1 The Department of Health Research has been created in the Ministry by amending Allocation
of Business Rules. The President of India had notified creation of the Department of Health
Research under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare on 17.09.2007.
1.2 The allocation of work to the newly created Department of Health Research is as under:—
• Promotion and co-ordination of basic, applied and clinical research including clinical
trials and operational research in areas related to medical, health biomedical and medical
profession and education through development of infrastructure, manpower and skills in
cutting edge areas and management of related information thereto;
• Promote and provide guidance of research governance issues, including ethical issues in
medical and health research;
• Inter-sectoral coordination and promotion of public private partnership in medical,
biomedical and health research related areas;
• Advanced training in research areas concerning medicine and health, including grant of
fellowships for such training in India and abroad; International co-operation in medical
and health research, including work related to international conferences in related areas
in India and abroad;
• Technical support for dealing with epidemics and natural calamities;
• Investigation of outbreaks due to new and exodic agents and
• development of tools for prevention;
• Matters relating to scientific societies and associations, charitable and religious
endowments in medicine and health research areas;
• Coordination between organizations and institutes under the Central and State
Governments in areas related to the subjects entrusted to the Department and for
promotion of special studies in medicine and health; and
• Indian Council of Medical Research.
1.3 The Secretary, in his presentation apprised the Committee that the Department is at the
inception stage, and will become fully operational after selection of an incumbent to the post of
a Secretary-cum-DG, ICMR. The Search Committee has been constituted under the Chairmanship
of the Cabinet Secretary for selecting a Director General of ICMR who would also be the
Secretary, Health Research.
1.4 The Committee feels that appointment of a full-fledged Director General, ICMR-
cum-Secretary, Department of Health Research is very important, for the Department,
being the nodal agency for promotion of Health Research, should be headed by a scientist
of repute and perpetuation of bureaucratic hold over the Department would defeat the very
purpose for which the Department of Health Research has been created. The Committee,
accordingly, urges upon the Department to finalise appointment of DG, ICMR-cum-
Secretary, Health Research at the earliest.

2
1.5 On a specific query about the appropriateness of medical education also being brought
under the purview of the Department of Health Research, the Committee has been given to
understand that Health Research and Medical Education have for long been two distinct areas on
account of functional and administrative reasons. The Committee is, however, not inclined to
agree with the contention of the Department. The Committee would like to point out that
both agricultural education and agricultural research have been placed under one
Department i.e. the Department of Agricultural Research and Education in the Ministry of
Agriculture. On the same analogy there should not be any difficulty in placing both medical
education and health research under the same Department. The Committee strongly feels
that this arrangement would be necessary for the sake of better coordination between the
different agencies involved in research work and medical educational institutions not only
providing clinical material for research but also engaged directly/indirectly in such projects.
Duplication of work undertaken by the different institutions/agencies in the field of medical
education and health research can also be made easily avoidable. The Committee is of the
view that medical education and health research cannot be placed in two distinct areas.
Instead both are complementary to each other in many ways. The Committee also feels that
with the merger of two separate Departments of Health and Family Welfare, the jurisdiction
of the Department has been expanded manifold. Besides, many Central Government
Hospitals, Premier Medical educational institutions and autonomous bodies, a very large
number of Central and centrally sponsored schemes are being administered/supervised by
the Department of Health and Family Welfare.
1.6 The Committee has been observing that with the setting up of more and more medical/
para-medical colleges in the country, vital area of medical education was not being taken care of
properly. It is a well-known fact that there is acute shortage of health care manpower of every
category in the country. The Committee is of the view that both for better management and proper
focus, subject of medical education needs to be brought together with health research. After being
part of the main Department for decades, Health Research has been assigned a distinct role with
the overall mandate of assigning health research its rightful place. The Committee feels that no
negative development would be there, if the subject of medical education also becomes part of the
Department of Health Research.
1.7 The Committee, therefore, recommends that both the medical education and Health
Research should be kept under one Department i.e. the Department of Health Education
and Research on the lines of the Department of Agricultural Research and Education for
better coordination and also for avoiding duplication of work amongst various institutions/
agencies engaged in Medical Education and Research.
II. BUDGETARY ALLOCATION
2.1 Total budgetary allocation made in BE 2008-09 in respect of the Department of
Health Research is Rs.531.75 crore; Rs.420 crore have been sanctioned on the plan side and
Rs.111.75 crore is the non-plan component against the projected demand of the Department for
Rs.1613.00 crores on the plan side and Rs.111.75 crores on the non-plan side.
2.2 On being asked whether the reduced allocation on the plan side would hamper
research projects of ICMR, the Financial Adviser (FA), ICMR, while deposing before the
Committee, clarified that the Eleventh Plan projections made by the ICMR was to the tune of
Rs.5000.00 crores against which Rs.4496.00 crores have been approved, which was well within
the range that had been projected. When asked to comment on the projected demand of the
Department for Rs.1613.00 crore for 2008-09, it was informed that the Department of Health
Research had made projections of Rs.1613.00 crore for 2008-09 because the Department wanted

3
to do major infrastructure upgradation and also start new activities in one go for which
Rs.600.00 crores had been projected and now that only Rs. 420.00 crores have been allocated for
2008-09, the Department would reschedule its activities and reorient the outflow of funds for the
remaining four years of the Eleventh Plan.
2.3 The Committee is not convinced with the clarification given by the representatives
of the Department. The Committee is unable to understand that in which manner the
Department will manage the expenditure on various activities out of meager allocation of
Rs.420 crores against the projected demand of Rs.1613 crores. The Committee has all
reasons to apprehend that the proposed major infrastructure upgradation as well as the new
activities proposed to be started by the Department are going to be affected badly. The
Committee desires that constraints of funds should not come in the way of the
infrastructure upgradation as well as the proposed new activities. The Committee, therefore,
recommends that the Department should not hesitate in asking for an enhanced allocation
at R.E. stage so that the Research and Development activities are not affected for want of
funds.
2.4 In response to the question about the budgetary allocation in respect of ICMR in
2007-08, the Department informed that the allocation for Plan and Non-Plan was
Rs.144.15 crores and Rs.90.00 crores, respectively. The budgetary allocation for ICMR during
2008-09 is Rs.356 crores, which is a substantial increase over the previous year.
2.5 The Committee observes that the allocation for 2008-09 under Plan will be fully
utilized by the Indian Council of Medical Research. The actual expenditure under Non-Plan
is likely to exceed the sanctioned Budget Grant. The additional expenditure will be met from
the Council’s Miscellaneous Receipts.
III. INDIAN COUNCIL OF MEDICAL RESEARCH (ICMR)
3.1 The Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), the apex body for the planning,
organization, implementation and coordination of the medical research in the country promotes
biomedical research through a network of its 21 permanent Institutes and 6 Regional Medical
Research Centres located throughout the country and also through grants-in-aid given to the
projects undertaken by the non-ICMR institutes.
3.2 ICMR promotes biomedical research in the country through intramural and extramural
research. During the year under report, the Council’s continued research and development on
critical areas of research such as management of communicable diseases, fertility control, maternal
and child health, nutritional deficiencies and disorders, solving environmental and occupational
health problems, research on non-communicable diseases such as cancer, cardiovascular diseases,
haematological disorders, mental health, geriatric problems and research on traditional medicine.
3.3 The Committee notes that management of communicable diseases, fertility control,
maternal and child health care, nutritional deficiencies and disorders, environmental and
occupational health problems, non-communicable diseases like cancer, AIDS etc. have been
a serious cause of concern in the health sector. Some of the above problems have posed a
serious threat to a larger section of population of the country. This is the right time that
the ICMR should gear itself for a greater role in undertaking and coordinating the health
research undertaken by the different institutes/agencies as also it should take all out
efforts for minimizing the casualties as a result of the diseases referred to above
particularly due to the non-communicable diseases, nutritional deficiencies, maternal and
child mortality etc. The Committee, therefore, recommends that the ICMR should not leave
any stone unturned in the matter of management and control of the above diseases.

utilized by the Indian Council of Medical Research. The actual expenditure under Non Plan
is likely to exceed the sanctioned Budget Grant. The additional expenditure will be met from
the Council’s Miscellaneous Receipts. (Para 2.5)
III. INDIAN COUNCIL OF MEDICAL RESEARCH (ICMR)
The Committee notes that management of communicable diseases, fertility control,
maternal and child health care, nutritional deficiencies and disorders, environmental and
occupational health problems, non-communicable diseases like cancer, AIDS etc. have been
a serious cause of concern in the health sector. Some of the above problems have posed a
serious threat to a larger section of population of the country. This is the right time that
the ICMR should gear itself for a greater role in undertaking and coordinating the health
research undertaken by the different institutes/agencies as also it should take all out
efforts for minimizing the casualties as a result of the diseases referred to above
particularly due to the non-communicable diseases, nutritional deficiencies, maternal and
child mortality etc. The Committee, therefore, recommends that the ICMR should not
leave any stone unturned in the matter of management and control of the above diseases.
(Para 3.3)
The Committee is hopeful that the set targets would be achieved by ICMR and there
would be optimum and judicious utilization of funds allocated for the above mentioned
purposes. The Committee recommends that the additional funds, if any, required
for achieving the set targets should be made available to the Council at the R.E. stage.
(Para 3.5)
The Committee appreciates the achievements of the ICMR in the field of health
research. However, the Committee feels that this is an area of immense possibilities. A lot
remains to be done to fully tap the health research sector. The Committee would like to
have an idea about the ongoing research projects under ICMR, alongwith their present
status, funds allocated/utilised. The Committee would also like to be apprised about the
number of persons including doctors from different medical colleges, working on research
projects who have approached ICMR for financial support during the last three years
alongwith details of those who have been denied financial support and the grounds therefor.
(Para 3.7)
IV. PROMOTION, CO-ORDINATION AND DEVELOPMENT OF BASIC APPLIED AND
CLINICAL RESEARCH
The Committee feels that promotion and co-ordination of basic, applied and clinical
research is one of the very important activities of the Department. The Committee
impresses upon the Department that any financial/procedural constraints should not
hamper, in any way, this activity of the Department. The Council should make all possible
efforts for better monitoring and co-ordination of health research. The Committee would
also like to be apprised of the break-up of the funds released and utilized and the
achievements made under this Scheme. The additional funds, if any, required for this
activity should be made available at R.E. stage. (Para 4.3)

12
V. PROMOTION AND GUIDANCE ON RESEARCH GOVERNANCE ISSUES
The Committee notes that though an amount of Rs. 20.00 crores has been
allocated for establishment of Tripartite Research Policy and implementation/co-
ordination mechanism, the modalities are yet to be worked out. The Committee urges
upon the Department to expedite the finalisation of the modalities so that the allocation
made for various activities under this scheme could be utilized optimally and judiciously.
(Para 5.2)
VI. INTER-SECTORIAL CO-ORDINATION IN MEDICAL, BIOMEDICAL AND HEALTH
RESEARCH
The Committee hopes that all the activities falling under the Inter-sectorial
co-ordination in Medical, Biomedical and Health Research would be well taken care of by
the Department. The Department should ensure that any administrative/procedural
formalities will not affect, in any way, proper and optimum utilization of funds allocated for
the purpose. The Committee would also like to be apprised of the activities undertaken
under this Scheme during 2008-09 and the extent of their success vis-a-vis their envisaged
targets. (Para 6.2)
VII. ADV ANCED TRAINING IN RESEARCH IN MEDICINE AND HEALTH
The Committee notes that some very important activities are envisaged to be
undertaken under the above Scheme of Advanced Training in Research in Medicine and
Health. However, only Rs.5.50 crores have been earmarked for the purpose. The Committee
impresses upon the Department to ensure proper implementation of all the activities
referred to above. The Committee apprehends that this meager allocation of funds to the
tune of Rs.5.50 crores would not be sufficient to meet the requirement of this scheme
and in that case the Department should go for enhancement of the funds at R.E. stage.
(Para 7.2)
VIII. INTERNATIONAL CO-OPERATION IN MEDICAL AND HEALTH RESEARCH
The Committee observes that International cooperation in medical and Health
Research is a very important activity to be undertaken by the Department. Exchange of
information and research work at international level will boost health care services of the
country as also help in tackling health related problems. The Committee, therefore,
recommends that the Department should not loose any opportunity in this direction. The
Committee would like to be intimated about the activities undertaken as well as the break-
up of funds spent thereon under this Scheme during 2008-09. (Para 8.2)
IX. NORTH-EASTERN AREAS AND SIKKIM
The Committee observes that as per the policy of the Government of India 10% of
the budgetary allocations have been earmarked for NE Region and Sikkim. The Committee
supports this policy thrust. However, the Committee has noted from its past experiences
that utilization of funds earmarked for the N.E. Region and Sikkim leaves much to be
desired. The Committee, therefore, recommends that the funds allocated for taking care of
health services/research in North-eastern Region and Sikkim should be utilized properly and
optimally. The Committee would also like to be enlightened as to what specific measures
have been taken by the Department to ensure optimum and judicious utilization of allocated
funds. (Para 9.2)

13
X. COORDINATION IN THE FIELD OF HEALTH RESEARCH WITH GOVERNMENTS,
ORGANISATIONS AND INSTITUTIONS
The scheme seeks to coordinate between organizations and institutes under the
Central/State Governments in the areas of promotion of special studies in medicines and
health for this purpose the Department will have to coordinate with a number of
Departments like Department of Science and Technology, Department of Bio-technology,
Department of Agricultural Research and Education, Department of Space, Department of
Environment and Forests, Disaster Management Authority etc. The Committee feels that
coordination with various Government Departments/agencies in the field of health research
is the most important activity entrusted to the Department. A number of Government
Departments/agencies are engaged in research activities in the field of health care and for
getting better results and health care facilities, proper coordination and exchange of
information with the Government Departments/agencies is a must. The Department will
have to make all out efforts for effective coordination and monitoring with all the
Government Departments/agencies engaged in the field of health research. The Committee
would like to be informed of the efforts made by the Department in this regard as also the
outcome of such efforts. (Para 10.1)
XI. MISCELLANEOUS
The Committee would like to be apprised about the reaction of ICMR on the above-
mentioned observations of CAG and follow-up action thereon. (Para 11.3)
