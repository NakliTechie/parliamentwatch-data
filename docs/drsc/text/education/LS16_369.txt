INTRODUCTION 
 
 I, the Chairman of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on 
Education, Women, Children, Youth and Sports having been authorized by the Committee 
to present the report on the subject “Review of functioning and performance of Sports 
Authority of India (SAI) and Khelo India.”  
2. The Committee held a meeting i.e. on 6th June, 2025 with the Secretary, Department of 
Sports, Secretary, Sports Authority of India (SAI) and other officials of the Department of 
Sports and SAI on the subject. 
 
3. While drafting the Report, the Committee relied on the following documents. 
(i) Detailed background note on the on the subject “Review of functioning and performance 
of Sports Authority of India (SAI) and Khelo India; and 
(ii) Annual Report of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports 2023-2024. 
(iii) Deposition of Secretary& other officials before the Committee on 06.06.2025 
(iv) Reply to the questionnaire on the subject by the Department of Sports. 
 
 
4. For the facility of reference and convenience, the recommendations of the 
Committee have been presented in bold letters in the body of the Report. 
 
5. The Committee considered the draft Report and adopted the same in its meeting held 
on the 18th August, 2025.
