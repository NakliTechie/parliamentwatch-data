An Overview 
The Department of Youth Affairs & Sports is comparatively a new Depa ·tment which deals with 
the vital subject :>f youth and promotion of sports. Though both these subjects fall under the P!!rview 
of the States, yet Government of India has a very prominent role to play not only in terms of 
operating its own ~chemes but also for coordinating with State Governments and other agcnc1es. The 
Committee found that on youth side, though there are a large number of schemes, but bulk of the

(NYt<S). National Scrvic.! Scheme (along ,.ith NCC) is the most important scheme fc; voiuntary

2. On the Sports side, it is observed that the Department has to coordinate with the State

sportspersons at the Olympic Games has been undistinguished and it is necessary that all efforts arc 
made oy various concerned agencies to raise the requisite/International standards. In this connectiOn , 
the Committee welcomes the reported consensus of the State Governments about the introduction of 
Sports and Physical Education as a compulsory subject from sehoul level onwards.

47. Apart from the Sports Authority of India development and promotion of sports is undertaken by 
vajous other or~anisations and bodies. National Sports Federations are such bodies which are getting 
financial assistance for furthering various sports disciplines. For this purpose, sports federations 
undertake such activities as conducting national championships, organising coaching camps, purchasing 
sports equipment, participating in International Tournaments, etc. During the year 1993-94 a total of 
Rs.l480.00 lakhs for plan and Rs80.00 lakhs for non-plan expenditure (which include an additional 
allocation of Rs.1080.00 lakhs for the preparation of Indian Contingent for Asian games)' was given to 
sports :ederations. But, the poor performance of teams in international level games has shown that all is 
not going well with the sports federations. Therefore, the Committee recomends that while doling out such 
huge amounts norms and conditions fixed therefor should be strictly adhered to. There should also be some 
mechanism for monitoring to find out whether the amount is being properly utilised or not. There must also 
be a supervisory body to see that the sports federations functions smoothly. 
48. The Secretary, Department of Youth Affairs & Sports during the course of his oral evidence 
tenderd before the Committee stated that since sports is a State subject no proper check upon sports 
federations could be kept. He further added that plans are also being formulated to ensure that these 
federations are run properly. The Committee strongly recommends that all-out effort should be made by 
Government to tone up the functioning and performance of the Sports Federations.
