REPORT
           
The Manipur University Bill 2005 seeks to convert the existing Manipur University into a Central University with
a view to ensure its accelerated development and minimize the imbalance in educational facilities in the North Eastern
Region.
 
2.         The Manipur University was established at Imphal in June 1980, under the Manipur University Act, 1980 enacted
by the Manipur Legislative Assembly.  Prior to the establishment of this University, the Centre of Post Graduate Studies
of the Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), established at Imphal in the year 1972, was catering to the educational needs
of the students of Manipur at the postgraduate level and the undergraduate colleges, including some professional
colleges, in the State were affiliated to Guwahati University.
 
3.         The Committee was informed that with the coming into force of the Manipur University Act, 1980, Guwahati
University ceased to exercise jurisdiction in the State of Manipur and the erstwhile Centre of Post Graduate Studies of
JNU was merged with the newly established Manipur University.
 
4.         The Committee was further informed that the Manipur University has at present 23 post-graduate teaching
departments under three Schools of Studies.  It also has 73 affiliated colleges including a medical college, three law
colleges, six teacher-training colleges, three technical/engineering colleges and a college of physical education and
sports.  The number of students presently enrolled in the teaching departments and affiliated colleges of the University is
about 2,000 and 30,000 respectively.
 
5.         According to the Department of Secondary and Higher Education, as the Manipur University started without any
foundation grant and could not get adequate plan assistance from the Government in the succeeding years, it has not been
in a position to create the required infrastructure and to diversify its courses  to the  extent required  so far.  It is
surrounded by central universities of the North-Eastern Region at Kohima, Silchar, Tezpur, Aizawl and Shillong.  This
has caused a huge imbalance in educational facilities within the region, and a consequent migration of teachers, students
and researchers in search of better infrastructural and academic facilities in other states.  Not only this, many years of
insurgency in different parts of the State resulted in the exodus of a large number of students from the State to places like
Delhi.

6.         Against this backdrop and the fact that the State Government, with its limited resources, was not in a position to
provide adequate funding for the development of the Manipur University, there had been a persistent demand for making
it as a central university.  Besides repeated requests from the State Government, the Manipur Legislative Assembly had
also passed unanimous resolutions to this effect on the 9th July, 1999 and more recently on the 13th March, 2003.
 
7.         Accordingly, the Manipur University Bill 2005, has been introduced in the Rajya Sabha for the establishment and
incorporation of Manipur University as a central university and for repealing of the existing Manipur University Act,
1980, enacted by the Manipur Legislative Assembly.  The Bill is broadly based on the pattern of the University of
Mizoram, the most recent of the North-Eastern Universities.
 
8.         The salient features of the aforesaid Bill are as under:
(i)                  The Bill seeks to establish and incorporate the existing Manipur University, a state university of
Manipur, under the proposed Central Act by the same name of 'Manipur University' with perpetual
succession and common seal, and to provide for the matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.
 
(ii)                The University would be a teaching and affiliating university with its Headquarters at Imphal and
territorial jurisdiction extending to the whole of the State of Manipur.
(iii)               It would function broadly on the pattern of the existing central universities, particularly of those of
the North-Eastern region.
(iv)              All colleges, Institutions, Schools and Departments affiliated to, or admitted to the privileges of, or
maintained by, the existing Manipur University shall stand affiliated to, or admitted to the privilege of, or
maintained by, the University.
(v)                The existing Institute of Technology, Imphal shall also be maintained by the University.
(vi)              The annual accounts of the University shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of
India and shall be laid before both Houses of Parliament along with the audit report and the annual report
of the University.
(vii)             During the transitional period, the appointment of the first Officers and the constitution of the first
Authorities of the University would be made by the Visitor for specified terms.
 
9.         The Committee, heard the Secretary, Department of Secondary and Higher Education and took up clause-by-
clause consideration of the Bill.   It suggested the following amendments additions to various clauses of the Bill.
 
Clause 2 (m)
 
10.       The Committee notes that the definitions for `college' and `institution' provided in 2(e) and 2(m) leave some
scope for confusion, particularly in view of different categories of colleges existing/likely to exist under the Manipur
University.   For example, `constituent colleges' and 'affiliated colleges' have not been defined.   The Bill, Committee
finds, does not provide explicitly for converting an affiliated college into a constituent college. Such a demand in future,
in fact, cannot be ruled out, if a college or an institution feels that it has required standards/facilities for the purpose. 
The Committee, therefore, is of the view that there is need to amplify the definitions of the terms `constituent college',
``affiliated college' and 'institution' with a view to removing possible ambiguities in these regards.   The Committee,
therefore, recommends that necessary amendments should be brought in the relevant provision of the Bill for this
purpose. 
 
Clause 9(3)
 
11.1     Clause 9(3), page 5, provided that the Visitor shall have the right to cause an inspection to be made, by such
person or persons as he may direct, of the University, its buildings, laboratories and equipment, and of any college or
institution maintained by the University or admitted to its privileges; and also of the examinations, teaching and other
work conducted or done by the University and to cause an inquiry to be made in like manner in respect of any matter
connected with the administration or finances of the University, and the said Colleges and Institutions.

11.2     The Committee feels that libraries are important and integral constituents of the University system and need to
be included in the above provision for inspection.  The Committee, therefore, recommends that in Clause 9(3) in line 33
after the word "buildings," the word 'libraries' should also be added.
 
New clause for providing Chief Rector
 
12.1     The Committee is of the view that Governors should have a role in those Central Universities which are located in
border States like Manipur. The Governor being an important link between the Centre and the State, may also be made a
part of the University, even though it is mere ceremonial.
 
12.2     The Committee, therefore, recommends that the following clause be added in the Bill after clause 9 and the entire
clauses from 10 to 46 may be renumbered accordingly:-
 
            "10.  The Governor of the State of Manipur shall be the Chief Rector of the University".
 
12.3     All the consequential changes may also be carried out in the Bill.
 
Clause 10
 
13.1     Clause 10, page 6, provides that the following shall be the officers of the University:-
(1)               the Chancellor;
(2)               the Vice-Chancellor;
(3)               the Pro-Vice Chancellor;
(4)               the Deans of Schools;
(5)               the Registrar;
(6)               the Finance Officer;
(7)               the Librarian and
(8)               such other officers as may be declared by the Statutes to be officers of the University.
 
13.2     The Committee feels that holding of examinations and awarding degrees is one of the most important functions of
a university.  If Librarian can be made as an officer, the Controller of Examinations, being an equally important
functionary of any university, should also be included as one of the officers of the university in the Act itself.  Moreover,
the post of 'Controller of Examinations' has already been provided in the existing Manipur University Act 1980, passed
by the State Legislature.
 
13.3     The Committee, accordingly, recommends that the Controller of Examinations may be added as an Officer of the
University in Clause 10 of the Bill at Page 6.
 
Clause 12(3)
 
14.1     Clause 12(3), page 7, provides that the Vice-Chancellor may, if he is of the opinion that immediate action is
necessary on any matter, exercise any power conferred on any authority of the University by or under this Act and shall
report to such authority the action taken by him on such matter.
 
14.2     The Committee while accepting the rationale behind providing emergency powers to the Vice-Chancellor to deal
with urgent matters, feels that the Vice-Chancellor should be required to report such actions taken by him to the
appropriate authorities at the earliest available opportunity.  The Committee, therefore, recommends that in Clause 12(3),
in line 8, the words 'at its next meeting' should be added after the word 'authority'.
 
14.3     The second proviso to clause 2(3) provides for making an 'appeal' by a person aggrieved by the emergent actions
of the VC to the EC.   The Committee feels the word 'appeal' may have different legal connotations leading sometimes to
avoidable situations.   The Committee, therefore, recommends substituting the word 'appeal' with 'representation' in line
12 of the second proviso to clause 12(3).

Clause 20 (1)
 
15.1     Clause 20 (1) provides that the constitution of the Court and the term of office of its members shall be prescribed
by the Statutes.      The Committee, however, notes that the Statutes appended to the Bill do not provide for the
constitution of the Court as well as the term of office of its members.
 
15.2     The Committee feels that the Court being the apex body of the university, provision for its constitution and term
of office of its members be provided in the first statutes itself.
           
Clauses 21(2), 22(2) and 23(2)
 
16.1     Clause 21(2) provides that the constitution of the Executive Council, the term of office of its members and its
powers and functions shall be prescribed by the Statutes.
 
16.2     Clause 22(2) provides that the constitution of the Academic Council, the term of office of its members and its
powers and functions shall be prescribed by the Statutes.
 
16.3     Clause 23(2) provides that the constitution of the College Development Council, the term of office of its members
and its powers and functions shall be prescribed by the Statutes.
 
16.4     The Committee notes that the Executive Council, Academic Council and College Development Council are the
important decision-making bodies of the University.  The Statutes appended to the Bill contain provisions only about the
powers and functions of these bodies but are silent about their constitution and term of office.
 
16.5     The Committee, therefore, recommends that the provisions for the constitution and term of office of the
Executive Council, Academic Council and the College Development Council, also be included within corpus of the first
Statutes itself

SCHEDULE
 
Clause 5(1) & (2) of the Statutes
 
17.1     Clause 5(1) of the Statutes provides for the appointment of Dean of a School by the Vice-Chancellor from
amongst the professors of the school for a period of three years, who shall be eligible for re-appointment.  The
Committee finds that the Statutes is silent about any criteria for selecting the professors for appointment as Dean.  The
Committee, therefore, recommends that the seniority as well as rotation should be included as criteria for this purpose.
           
17.2     The first proviso to clause 5 (1) of the Statutes provides that a Dean would cease to hold office at the age of 60.  
The Committee notes that the retirement age of Professors is sixty-two.  It feels that the Deans being professors, should
also retire at the age of sixty-two.
 
17.3     Clause 5(2) of the Statutes authorises the Vice-Chancellor to appoint "any person" as the Dean when the office of
the Dean is vacant or is unable to perform duties.  The Committee notes that this is a blanket power given to the Vice-
Chancellor and it should be limited only to the senior most Professors.
 
17.4     In view of the above, the Committee recommends that clause 5(1) of the statutes, its two provisos and clause 5(2)
may be deleted and the following may be substituted.
 
5(1) Every Dean of school shall be appointed by the Vice-Chancellor from amongst the Professors in the School
by rotation in the order of seniority for a period of three years;
 
Provided that in case there is only one Professor or no Professor in a School, the Dean shall be appointed, for the
time being, from amongst the Professors, if any, and the Readers in the School by rotation in the order of seniority;

Provided that a Dean on attaining the age of sixty-two years shall cease to hold office as such;
 
5(2) When the office of the Dean is vacant or when the Dean is, by reason of illness, absence or any other cause,
unable to perform duties of his office, the duties of the office shall be performed by the senior-most Professor or Reader,

Clause 6(3) of the Statutes
 
18.1     First Proviso to Clause 6(3) at page 17, of the Statutes provides that the Registrar shall retire on attaining the age
of sixty years.
           
18.2     The Committee learnt that retirement age of Registrar in other Central Universities was 62 years.  Sometimes, a
senior Professor might also be appointed as Registrar of the University, who would retire at the age of 62 years.  It might,
the Committee feels, put them at disadvantage.  The Committee, therefore, is of the view that the retirement age of the
Registrar should be made sixty-two.  The Committee, accordingly, recommends that in Proviso to clause 6(3) line 44 of
the Statutes, the words 'sixty years' should be substituted by 'sixty-two years'.  Consequently, the 'sixty years' in the
succeeding proviso should also be made as sixty-two years.
 
Clause 6(6) of the Statutes
 
19.       Clause 6(6) provides that the Registrar shall be ex officio  Secretary to the Executive Council, the Academic
Council and the College Development Council, but shall not be deemed to be a member of any of these authorities.  The
Committee has learnt that in the existing administrative set up of the Manipur University under the State Act, there is a
Director of the College Development Council. The Committee, feels that it would be convenient as well as appropriate if
he functions as the ex-officio Secretary to the College Development Council and not the Registrar as proposed by the new
Bill.  The Committee, therefore, recommends that the words "and the College Development Council" at page 18, line 20
may be deleted from clause (6)(6).
 
Clause 9(5) of the Statutes
 
20.1     Clause 9(5) of the Statute prescribes that twelve members would constitute quorum for a meeting of the Court.
 
20.2     The Committee notes that generally the quorums are in odd number, otherwise it might lead to deadlock in
decision-making by the Court.  The Committee, therefore, recommends that in line 12 of page 20 (statute 9(5)), the word 
'twelve' may be substituted by 'eleven' or thirteen as the case may be.
 
Clause 16(1) of the Statutes
 
21.1     Clause 16(1) of the Statutes prescribes that the College  of Technology , Imphal shall be maintained by the
University.
 
21.2     The Committee has learnt that the name of the College of Technology, Imphal has been changed to the Manipur
Institute of Technology.  The Committee, recommends that the name of the Institution should accordingly be corrected in
the clause.
           
Clause 19(1) of the Statutes
 
22.1     Clause 19(1) of the statutes at page 24, provides that notwithstanding anything contained in Statute 18, the
Executive Council may invite a person of high academic distinction and professional attainments to accept a post of
Professor or Reader or any other academic post in the University, as the case may be, on such terms and conditions as it
deems fit and on the person agreeing to do so, appoint him to the post.
 
22.2     The Committee notes that this sub clause empowers the university to appoint persons of eminence in the

Miscellaneous
Students Council
 
24.       The Committee notes that the Mizoram University Act provides for constitution of a Students Council, whereas
Manipur University Bill is silent about it.  The Committee is of the view that to accommodate the views of the student
community in academic as well as welfare matters, Students Council can be formed with suitable powers of
consultation.  The selection of students to the Council may be purely on the basis of merit. It, therefore, recommends that
the following may be added as Statutes at an appropriate place:
(1) There shall be constituted in the University, a Students' Council for every academic year, consisting of -
(i)                  the Dean of Students' Welfare who shall be the Chairman of the Students' Council:
(ii)                all students who have won prizes in the previous  academic year in the filed of studies, fine arts,
sports and extension work;
(iii)               twenty students to be nominated by the Academic council on the basis of merit in studies, sports,
activities and all-round development of personality;
 
Provided that any student of the University shall have the right to bring up any matter concerning the University
before the Students' Council if so permitted by the Chairman, and he shall have the right to participate in the discussions
at any meeting when the matter is taken up for consideration.
 
(2) The functions of the Students' Council shall be to make suggestions to the appropriate authorities of the
University in regard to the programmes of studies, students' welfare and other matters of importance, in regard to the
working of the University in general and such suggestions shall be made on the basis of consensus of opinion.
(3)        The Students' Council shall meet at least once in an academic year preferably in the beginning of that
year.
 
Alumni Association
 
25.       The alumni of a university represent its proud achievements.  A regular interaction with them will help in the over
all growth of the university. The experiences and the feed back received from the past students can be an asset to the
university.  The Committee, therefore, feels that the necessary provision for an "Alumni Association" may be
incorporated in the Bill itself as has been done in the case of Mizoram  University.  The Committee recommends that the
following may be added in the statute:-
 
            (1) There shall be an Alumni Association for the University.
(2) The subscription for membership of the Alumni Association shall be prescribed by the Ordinances.
            (3) No member of the Alumni Association shall be entitled to vote or stand for election unless he has been a
member of the Association for at least one year prior to the date of the election and is a degree holder of the University of
at least five years standing:
            Provided that the condition relating to the completion on one year's membership shall not apply in the case of the
first election.

26.1     The representative of the Ministry who appeared before the Committee quoted the incident of Banaras Hindu

2.         In the absence of Chairman, Smt. Chandra Kala Pandey  chaired the day's proceedings.
The Chairperson informed the Committee that the Chairman, Rajya Sabha in consultation with the Speaker, Lok
Sabha has referred  ‘The Commissions for Protection of Child Rights Bill, 2005’ as introduced and pending in the Lok
Sabha, to the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Human Resource Development for
examination and report within three months. The Committee decided to hear the Secretary, Department of Women and
Child Development on the Bill in its next meeting. 
3.         The Committee discussed the latest guidelines issued in respect of study visits by the  Parliamentary
Committees. The Members expressed their reservations and anguish over the wordings of the guidelines. The
Committee decided not go ahead with its proposed visit to  Chandigarh, Srinagar and Leh.
4.         The Committee ,thereafter,  took up clause-by clause consideration of the Manipur University Bill, 2005
alongwith the representatives of Department of Secondary and Higher Education. Members, then sought clarification
on several provisions of the Bill, to which the representatives of the Ministry replied.. The Committee suggested  the
following amendments/deletions in certain clauses of the Bill.
 
Clause 2(m)
The definitions for 'college' and 'institution' provided in 2(e) and 2(m) leave some scope for confusion,
particularly in view of different categories of colleges existing/likely to exist under the Manipur University.
 
Clause 9(3)
In Clause 9(3) in line 33 after the word "buildings," the word 'libraries' should also be added.
 
New clause for providing Chief Rector
           
The following clause be added in the Bill after clause 9 and the entire clauses from 10 to 46 may be renumbered
accordingly:-
"10. The Governor of the State of Manipur shall be the Chief Rector of the University".
All the consequential changes may also be carried out in the Bill.
 
Clause 10
The Controller of Examinations may be added as an Officer of the University in Clause 10 of the Bill at Page 6. 
 
Clause 12(3)
 
In Clause 12(3), inline 8, the words 'at its next meeting' should be added after the word 'authority' Substitute the
word 'appeal' with 'representation' in line 12 of the second proviso to clause 12(3).
 
Clause 20 (1)
The Committee feels that the Court being the apex body of the university, provision for its constitution and term
of office of its, members be provided in the first statutes itself.
 
Clauses 21(2), 22(2), and 23(2)
The provisions for the constitution and term of office of the Executive Council, Academic Council and the
College Development Council, also be included within corpus of the first Statutes itself.
 
Clause 5(1) & (2) of the statutes
Clause 5(1) of the statutes, its two provisos and clause 5(2) may be deleted and the following may be
substituted:-
            5(1) Every Dean of school shall be appointed by the Vice-Chancellor from amongst the Professors in the School
by rotation in the order of seniority for a period of three years;
            Provided that in case there is only one Professor or no Professor in a School, the Dean shall be appointed, for
the time being, from amongst the Professors, if any, and the Readers in the School by rotation in the order of seniority;

Students Council
            The following may be added as statutes at an appropriate place:
(1) There shall be constituted in the University, a Students' Council for every academic year, consisting of -
 
(iv)              the Dean of Students' Welfare who shall be the Chairman of the Students' Council:
(v)                all students who have won prizes in the previous  academic year in the field of studies, fine arts,
sports and extension work;
(vi)              twenty students to be nominated by the Academic Council on the basis of merit in studies, sports,
activities and all-round development of personality;
 
Provided that any student of the University shall have the right to bring up any matter concerning the University
before the Students' Council if so permitted by the Chairman, and he shall have the right to participate in the discussions
at any meeting when the matter is taken up for consideration.
 
(2)        The functions of the Students' Council shall be to make suggestions to the appropriate authorities of the
University in regard to the programmes of studies, students' welfare and other matters of importance, in regard to the
working of the University in general and such suggestions shall be made on the basis of consensus of opinion.
(3)        The Students' Council shall meet at least once in an academic year preferably in the beginning of that year.
 
Alumni Association

(1) There shall be an Alumni Association for the University.

(3) No member of the Alumni Association shall be entitled to vote or stand for election unless he has been a member of

five year standing:
