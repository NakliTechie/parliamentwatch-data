PREFACE 
 
I, the Chairman of the Department-Related Parliamentary Standing 
Committee on Education, Women, Children, Youth and Sports, having been 
authorised by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, do hereby present 
this Three Hundred and Fifty Fourth Report of the Committee on " Review of 
Rashtriya Yuva Sashaktikaran Karyakaram (RYSK)". 
 
2. The committee wishes to place on record its gratitude to the representatives of 
the Departments of Youth Affairs, Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports for 
furnishing necessary information/documents and rendering valuable assistance to 
the Committee in its deliberations.   
 
3. For the facility of reference and convenience, the recommendations of the 
Committee have been presented in bold letters in the body of the Report. 
 
4. The Committee considered the Draft Report and adopted the same in its 
meeting held on the 7th August, 2023.

17. The Chairman welcomed the Secretary, Department of Youth Affairs and other Officers to the 
meeting of the Committee and asked them to highlight the primary objectives of the Rashtriya Yuva 
Sashaktikaran Karyakram Scheme (NYSK), along-with any bottlenecks being faced and issues that 
required intervention, the status of the sub-schemes and how they were progressing especially in terms of 
implementation etc. 
18. The Secretary, Department of Youth Affairs during the presentation on the Rashtriya Yuva 
Sashaktikaran Karyakram, which is an umbrella Scheme of the Department, explained the Committee 
that the main objective of the Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan is to encourage the spirit of volunteering in 
the community to involve the youth in nation building activities and to promote synergies among 
different departments.

19. The Secretary, further stated that Rashtriya Yuva Sashaktikaran Karyakram, is an umbrella 
scheme for different programmes like Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan, the National Youth Corps, the 
National Programme for Youth and Adolescent Development, the National Young Leaders Programme, 
International Cooperation, Youth Hostels and Assistance to Scouting and Guiding Organizations.  The 
Secretary also briefed the Committee about the services of Rashtriya Yuva Sashaktikaran Karyakram 
rendered to the nation during the Covid-19 pandemic. 
20. The Secretary then stated about other major programmes being conducted by the Department of 
Youth Affairs and implemented by NYSK such as Clean India Programme, the National Unity Day, the 
Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, the Catch the Rain, the "Namami Gange Programme, Skilling and Handholding, 
Atmanirbhar Bharat, Orientation of Youth, Personal Contact and Facilitation Programmes, Education in 
Basic Vocations, Digital Facilitation, Combating Covid-19. The Secretary also apprised that Department 
of Youth Affairs has conducted many programmes in collaboration and with financial support from other 
Ministries, while some programmes were implemented through Grants-in-Aid of Department of Youth 
Affairs and some from other Ministries like the rain project, 13th Tribal Youth Exchange Programme and 
Namami Gange Project awareness on Investor Education and Protection.  The Committee was also 
apprised of other programmes implemented by NYKS without any financial support such as observance 
of International Day of Yoga, Poshan Maah, Beti Bachao Beti Padhao, Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat, 
Celebration of Constitution Day, Eradication of TB and Swastha Balak Balika Spardha etc. 
 21. The Secretary also enlightened the Committee of the fact that the Department of Youth Affairs 
organized International Youth Exchange Programmes.  It was also informed that Department facilitates 
participation in International Youth Forums like BRICS Youth Summit, Youth 20 Summit, World Youth 
Forums and the Commonwealth Youth Programme.  With the coming of India's G20 Presidency, which is 
scheduled to begin from 1st December, we would also be organizing the Youth-20 Summit, which has 
begun on 17th September, 2022 and it will continue till 16th September, 2023. The Committee was briefed 
about the Youth Hostel in the country and Scouting and Guiding Schemes.   
22. The Members raised various queries and expressed their views on the subject. Some of the queries 
raised by the Members were responded to by the representatives. They also mentioned that Department of 
Youth Affairs would send responses to the queries raised by the Members.
