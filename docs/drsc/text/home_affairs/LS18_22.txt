REPORT 
I, the Chairman of the Committee on Home Affairs, having been authorised by the 
Committee, present this Twenty-second Report . of the Committee relating to the 
Pondicherry (Administration) Amendment am·, 1995. The Bill seeks to insert a new section 
in the Pondicherry {Administration) Act, 1962 (49 of 1962), to make the provisions of 
Chapter VI of Part VI of the Constitution applicable to the High Court at Madras in relation 
to the exercise of its jurisdiction over the Union Territory of Pondicherry with effect from 
the 6th November, 1962. · 
2. In pursuance of the rules relating to the Department-Related Parliamentary Standing 
Committees, the Chairman, Rajya Sabha referred•• the Bill, as introduced in the Rajya 
Sabha on the 25th April, 1995 and pending therein, to the Committee, for examination and 
report. 
3. The Committee considered the ~ill in its meeting held on the 13th June, 1995 and also 
heard the representative of the Ministry of Home Affairs. 
4. The Committee while considering the Bill took note of the following documents 
furnished by the Ministry:-
(i) Background Note on "the Bill; 
(ii) The Pondieherry {Administration) Act, 1962 {49 fo 1962); and . . 
(iii) Relevant extracts from the Constitution. 
5. The Committee considered and adopted the Report in its meeting held on the 19th 
July, 1995. 
6. In its note fumished to the Committee, the Ministry explained the bactcground and the 
necessity for amendiag the Pondicherry {Administration) Act, 1962, as under:~ 
(i) Section 9 of Qte 'Act' provides tha~ as ft:om the sixth day of November, 1962, the 
jurisdiction of the 'High Court at Madras shall extend to the Union Territory of 
Pondicherry. 
(ii) Section 10 of the 'Act' provides inter-alia that the Madras High Court shall have, 
in respect of the Union Territory of Pondi~herry, all such jurisdiction, as under the 
law in force immediately before the appointed day (i.e. the 16th day of August, 1962) 
was exercisable in respect of French Establishment by the Cour de Cassation, the 
Cour Superieur d' Arbitrage and the Conseil d' Etat of France. However, the 'Act' 
does not expressly provide that the provisions of Chapter VI of Part VI of the 
Constitution shall be applicable .to the. High Court at Madras with respect to its 
administrative control over , the subordinate· coUrts in the Union Territory of 
Pondicherry. 
(iii') The necessity to amend the 'Act' arose as a result of Madras High Court 
judgement, and its subsequent endorsement by the Supreme Court, on certain writ 
petitions challenging the appointment of certain judicial officers in the Union Territory 
of Pondicherry. The· High Court observed that though its jurisdiction had been 
extended to the Union Territory of Pondicherry, its administrative control over the 
Judicial Wing of the Union Territory had not been given effect to. The High Court 
•Published in the Gazette on India, Extraordinary Part·ll, Section 2, dated the 25th April, 1995.

further observed that although the powers under the provisions of Chapter VI of Part 
VI of the Constitution had always been available and exercisable by the Madras High 
Court in relation to the Union Territory of Po.ndicherry, it was high time that it should' 
expressly assume its administrative conkol over the Judicial Wing of the Union 
Territory. The High Court also directed the Government of Union Territory of 
Pondicherry to amend the relevant legislation and the Pondicherry Judicial Semce 
Rules. The Supreme Court al~ directed to U'pdate the rules. 
7. The Committee, having regard to the submission made by the representative of the 
Ministry and the documents furnished to it on the subject, is of the view that there is a need 
to amend the Pondicherry (Administration) Act, 1962 (49. of 1%2) to achieve the objectives 
set out in the Statement of Objects and Reasons given in the Bill and also to give effect to 
the directions of the Madras High Court, which were also upheld by the Supreme Court.

"3. It is hereby declared that,-
(a) no appointment, posting, promotion or transfer of any person already in 
the judicial service in the Union Territory of Pondicherry , or otherwise, made at 
any time and before the coming into force of this section otherwise than in 
accordance with the provisions of article 233 or article 234 or article 23S'shall be 
deemed to be illegal or void or ever to have become illegal or void by reason 
only of the fact that such appointment, posting, promotion or transfer was not 
made in accordance with the said provisions; 
(b) no jurisdiction exercised, no judgement, decree, sentence or order passed 
or made, and no other act or proceeding done or taken, before the coming into 
force of this seotion, by, or before any person appointed, posted, promoted or 
transferred as a judge in the judicial service in the Union Territory of 
Pondicherry otherwise than in accordance with the provisions of article 233 or 
article 234 or article 235 shall be deemed to be illegal or invalid or ever to have 
become illegal or invalid by reason only of the fact that such appointment , 
posting, promotion or transfer was not made in accordance with the said 
provisions." 
9. Consequent upon the insertion of clause 3, sub-clause (2) of clause 1 of the Bill may be 
substituted as under:-
"(2) Section 3 shall come into force at once and section 2 shall be deemed to have 
come into force on the 6th day of November, 1962." 
10. The Committee recommends that the Bill be· passed with the above changes.
