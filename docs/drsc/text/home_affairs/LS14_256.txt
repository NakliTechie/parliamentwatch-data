Affairs, having been authorized by the Committee to submit the Report on its behalf, do hereby

CHAPTER I 
 
Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the Government 
 
1.1 Utilization of Funds 
 
Recommendation/Observation 
 
1.1.1 During the deliberations of the Committee, the Secretary, DoNER informed the Committee 
that the utilization as on 27.02.2025 is ₹ 3352 Crore i.e. almost 85% of utilization. The Committee 
hopes that the Ministry may utilize the full budget by the end of March, 2025. 
 
(Para 2.2.7 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.1.2 The total expenditure during 2024–25 stood at ₹3,452.99 crore, representing 86.20% of the 
Revised Estimates (RE). This marks a significant increase of 74.68% over the  
₹1,976.76 crore spent in 2023–24. Notably, the 2024–25 expenditure is the highest ever recorded by 
MDoNER, surpassing the previous peak of ₹2,669.72 crore in 2019–20 by 29.34%. MDoNER will 
make all possible efforts to further improve the utilization of allocated funds during 2025-26. 
 
1.2 Outlays for Financial Year 2025-26 
 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.2.1 The Committee notes that due to classification in Head of Account MH 4552 under various 
schemes, the major part of the budget outlay has been reflected under the Capital Section. Hence, the 
total Revenue outlay decreased to ₹ 1,882.76 crores in BE 2025-26 from ₹ 2,040.68 crores in BE 
2024-25.The Committee also notes that under the head ‘Schemes of NEC’, there is a shortfall of ₹ 
178.66 crores in BE 2025-26 compared to the projected demand of ₹ 909.30 crores. The Committee, 
therefore, recommends that the Ministry coordinate with NEC to prioritize the expenditure out of the 
present budgetary allocation and seek more funds at the RE 2025-26 stage to meet the shortfalls. The 
Committee also recommends that the requirement of additional funds under the Capital Section may 
also be realistically assessed at the RE stage and additional funds, if required, may be sought.  
(Para 2.4.4 ibid) 
Action Taken 
1.2.2 The Committee’s recommendations have been noted for compliance. 
1.2.3 The requirement of additional funds shall be taken up by MDoNER with the Ministry of 
Finance at the RE stage in FY 2025-26 based on various factors such as progress in expenditure, 
reduction of outstanding UCs, unspent balances, projections and demands made by the concerned 
Divisions etc. and other related considerations. 
 
Recommendation/Observation 
 
1.2.4 The Committee notes that during 2022-23 and 2023-24, percentage of funds utilization is not 
satisfactory which proves the slow pace of work on the proposed projects. The Committee 

3 
 
appreciates the fact that during 2024-25 almost 85% fund has been utilized till 27.02.2025 and 
hopefully 100% utilization by the end of this Financial Year.  
(Para 2.4.5 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.2.5 There were multiple reasons for underutilization of funds during 2022-23 and 2023-24. The 
fund flow was hampered due to issues faced in transition to Central Nodal Agency (CNA) system, 
which was adopted from 01.04.2022. Unspent balances with the States/ Implementing agencies were 
also a factor as the CNA system does not allow fresh releases until the previously released amount is 
utilized. Allocations for Prime Minister’s Development Initiative for North-East Region (PM-
DevINE), which was announced in Union Budget 2022-23, largely unutilized during 2022-23 and 
2023-24 as the operational guidelines could be notified only in August 2023. Utilization of allocated 
funds was also hampered due to delay in project implementation by State Government/ 
Implementation Agencies because of short working season on account of inclement weather 
conditions; difficulties in transportation of material due to difficult terrain; land acquisition issues; 
local law and order issues etc. and also because of delay in submission of demand/ claims by the 
State Governments. 
1.2.6 The total expenditure during 2024–25 stood at ₹3,452.99 crore, which is 86.20% of the 
Revised Estimates (RE). MDoNER will make all possible efforts to further improve the utilization of 
allocated funds. 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.2.7 The Committee would like to be apprised about the budgetary allocation to the NER from 
2005-2014 and 2014-2024 alongwith a comparative study of the socio-economic as well as 
infrastructural development between the period from 2005-14 and 2014-24. 
(Para 2.4.6 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.2.8 Details of budgetary allocations for MDoNER, from 2004-05 to 2013-14 and 2014-15 to 
2024-25, are at Annexure-I. 
1.2.9 The budgetary allocation to the North Eastern Region (NER) from 2005-2014 and during 
2014-2024 (FY- wise) is enclosed at Annexure –II. 
 
1.2.10 Further, it is also submitted that the socio-economic and infrastructural development in the 
NER has been a priority for the Government. Over the years, substantial investments have been 
made to improve connectivity, education, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities in the region. Key 
infrastructure projects include the expansion of road, rail, and air connectivity. Despite challenges 
posed by difficult terrain and dispersed population, the concerted efforts of the Centre and State 
Governments have resulted in measurable progress across various sectors in the NER. 
1.2.11 During 2005-2014, Growth in the NER is as follows: 
 In 2005, the Special Accelerated Road Development Programme for North East (SARDP-
NE) was launched to improve 8,737 km of roads. The first phase of approved work was of 
2,304 km. 

4 
 
 Major railway projects were taken up, including electrification of the 800 km Barauni–
Katihar–Guwahati route, construction of a rail-cum-road bridge over the Brahmaputra at 
Bogibeel, and gauge conversion of the Lumding–Silchar and Rangia–Murkongselek lines. 
New railway lines for the state capitals of Assam, Tripura, Manipur, Nagaland, Mizoram and 
Arunachal Pradesh, besides opening up the possibility of a rail link at a later date due to 
Chittagong Port from Sabroom were also planned. 
 Several schemes for development of airports in the NER had been taken up at Agartala, 
Barapani, Dibrugarh, Dimapur, Guwahati, Silchar, Imphal, Jorhat and Rupsi. 
 Foundation stoneslaidfor the 3,000 MW Dibang Multipurpose project- the country’s largest 
hydroelectric project and a 750 MW gas-based thermal power plant in Tripura. 
 The Government approved the 600 MW Kameng hydroelectric power project and resumed 
work on the 2,000 MW Subansiri Lower hydroelectric project. 
 90% assistance under Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan was given to States as compared to 65% for 
other States. 
 NEIGRIHMS, Shillong was made operational and Indian Institute of Management was set up 
at Shillong. 
 North Eastern Region Livelihood Project (NERLP) launched in 2012 focused on women-
led SHGs, rural enterprise development, and micro-finance. 
 During 2014-2024, Growth in the North Eastern Region (NER) is as follows: 
 North Eastern Region Power System Improvement Project (NERPSIP) was approved in 2014 
with World Bank assistance. 
 Expansion of National Highways: The length of National Highways in the NER has seen a 
significant rise, increasing from 10,905 km in 2014 to 16,207 km by 2024. 
 Sela Tunnel: Constructed at a cost of ₹825 crore, the Sela Tunnel provides all-weather 
connectivity between Tezpur and Tawang. 
 Railway Infrastructure Growth: NER has added 1,909 km of railway tracks, with 19 major 
railway projects worth ₹81,941 crore currently underway. Additionally, 972 km of railway 
track has been converted to broad gauge. 
 Key Railway Projects Since 2014: 
o Agartala–Sabroom New Line (112 km) at ₹3,904.61 crore 
o Commissioning of the Bogibeel Rail-cum-Road Bridge (2018) 
o Completion of Agartala (Tripura) – Akhaura (Bangladesh) Railway Link at the cost 
of ₹1,100 crore 
 Connectivity to State Capitals: Three State Capitals- Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura and 
Mizoram are connected by broadgauge rail. The railway network project to connect Imphal 
(Manipur) is in the advance stage of completion. 
 Aviation Sector Boost: The number of airports in the region has grown from 9 in 2014 to 17 
in 2024.

 Waterways Expansion: With an investment of ₹1,040 crore, the number of national 
waterways in the NER has expanded from 1 to 20. Notable milestones include: 
o MV Lal Bahadur Shastri’s successful cargo voyage from Patna to Pandu via

o Launch of MV Ganga Vilas, the world’s longest river cruise, operating from Varanasi 
to Bogibeel via Bangladesh (2023) 
 Hydropower Projects: 
o Kameng Hydro Power Project (600 MW) in Arunachal Pradesh made operational  
o Dibang Multipurpose Hydroelectric Project (2,880 MW) under development in

 Medical Infrastructure: The first AIIMS in the NER was established in Guwahati in 2017. 
Assam will have South Asia’s largest Cancer hospital network with 14 hospitals. 
 
1.3 Schemes of North East Council  
 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.3.1 The Committee acknowledges that North Eastern Council is one the important agencies for 
overall inclusive growth of North East Region. The Committee hopes that the increased allocation of 
₹ 822.00 Cr. in B.E. 2025-26 will provide a much needed support to the agency for carrying out 
socio-economic development, regional planning and welfare activities in the region. 
(Para 3.1.8 ibid) 
Action Taken 
1.3.2 MDoNER in association with North East Council (NEC) will make every effort towards the 
full utilization of the budget allocated for FY for 2025-26. 
 
Recommendation/Observation 
1.3.3 The Committee suggests that the North Eastern Council and the Ministry of DoNER must 
take necessary steps to enhance the infrastructure capacity and technological upgradation in the 
North East Region. The Committee notes that under North Eastern Council, 61% of budgetary 
allocation has been utilized till Dec, 2024. The Committee hopes that the Ministry will make 
necessary efforts to clear liabilities of all the ongoing projects in the Financial Year 2025-26 itself so 
that they may not spill over to the next financial year. 
(Para 3.1.9 ibid) 
 
Action Taken 
1.3.4 Plan is being formulated to enhance the infrastructure capacity and technological 
upgradation in the NER. Under Schemes of NEC, the utilization of funds is 99.55% & under 
NESIDS Roads, the utilization is 99.98% as on 31.03.2025. NEC will clear all liabilities in FY 
2025-26. 
 
Recommendation/Observation

nationally and regionally, targeting key areas such as behavioural change, legal and institutional

enabling infrastructure to afford to women speedy justice and support in any crisis. Projects under 
this fund are being executed by various Ministries and Departments including the Ministry of Home 
Affairs, Ministry of Railways, Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, Department of Justice, 
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Ministry of Tourism, Ministry of Panchayati 
Raj, Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs, Ministry of External Affairs and several State 
Governments across the country.

2.13.14  Some of the key initiatives under the fund have been enumerated below:

backbone of India's emergency safety infrastructure for women-available 24/7 in any location,
