REPORT OF THE COMMITIEE 
I, the Chairman of the Department Related Standing Committee on Home Affairs, having been 
authorised by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, present this Third Report of the 
Committee. 
2. In pursuance of the rules relating to the Department Related Parliamentary Standing 
Committees, the Chairman, Rajya Sabha, in consultation with the Speaker, Lok Sabha referred* the 
Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Amendment Bill**, 1991, as introduced in the Lok 
Sabha on the 20th December, 1991 and pending in that House, to the Committee for examination and 
report. 
3. The Committee, in its meeting held on the 16th August, 1993 discussed the provisions of the Bill 
and took up clause-by-clause consideration thereof. 
4. The Committee, in its meeting held on the 18th August, 1993 considered and adopted the draft 
Report. 
5. The Bill proposes to provide that where official residence is not provided to a Supreme Court 
Judge, he may be paid house rent allowance of Rs. 3000 per month which should be exempt from 
income tax. This provision is proposi!d to be given effect from the 9th May, 1986, when the number 
of Judges of the Supreme Court was increased and the official residence could not be provided 
immediately to some Judges. Accordingly, clauses 2 and 3 of the Bill were adopted by the Committee 
without any change. 
6. Clause 1, the Enacting Formula and the Title were adopted with some changes which are of a 
consequential or drafting nature. 
7. The Committee recommends that the Bill, as reported, be passed.
