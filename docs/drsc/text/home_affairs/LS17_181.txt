INTRODUCTION
I, the Chairman of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Home
Affairs, having been authorized by the Committee to submit the Report on its behalf, do hereby
present this One Hundred Eighty First Report on Action Taken by Government on the
recommendations/observations contained in the 180 th Report on Administration of Union
Territories (Daman & Diu, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Chandigarh).
2. The One Hundred and Eightieth Report of the Department-related Parliamentary
Standing Committee on Home Affairs was presented to Rajya Sabha and laid on the Table of
Lok Sabha on 19 th February, 2014. After presentation of the Report, the copies thereof were
sent to the Ministry of Home Affairs requesting them to furnish Action Taken Notes on the
recommendations of the Committee contained in the Report. The Ministry furnished the Action
Taken Notes on the said report to the Committee on 22 nd October, 2014.
3. The Committee first considered the draft Statement on Action Taken Notes furnished
by the Ministry of Home Affairs on the recommendations of the Committee contained in its
180th report in its sitting held on 20th November, 2014. The Committee, thereafter, considered
Action Taken Notes and the draft 181 st Acticon Taken Report on the recommendations/
observations contained in the 180 th Report on Administration of Union Territories (Daman &
Diu, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Chandigarh) and adopted the same in its meeting held on 4 th
December, 2014.

Recommendation
1.1 The construction of ring road in Silvassa Town area is necessary as there has been fast
increase in population growth in recent period. The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
should accord early clearance for proposed construction of Ring Road. Besides, the UT
Administration should also make endeavour to improve existing road network as well as
construction of new roads for improving connectivity which will help fast flow of goods and
services. (Para 1.2.4  of 180 th Report )
Action Taken
1.1.1 The SFC proposal for construction of 4.3 Km. Ring Road (Phase-I) in Silvassa
amounting to `47.99 crore was submitted to the Ministry of Road Transport & Highway vide
letter dated 26.8.2013 and regularly followed up vide reminders dated 4.9.2013, 13.1.2014.
1.1.2 The UT Administration had then forwarded the proposal for construction of Ring Road
for Silvassa town amounting to Rs.48.58 Crore to MHA on 7.5.2014 in pursuance to its O.M.

their consideration and approval.

1.1.4 The Committee notes with concern that in spite of reminders, no action was
taken. Now that the power of Administrator has been increased, early action should be
taken to approve the proposal. Construction of the Ring Road should start with three
months.

Level Milk Co-operative Societies will also be organized in all remaining villages of the territory
and benefit of IDDP and establishment of Small Scale Dairy Unit will be provided to every
village of the Union Territory as per the feasibility of the scheme.

Recommendation
Stimuli for Industrial Development:
1.10 The Committee is aware of the fact that despite India being an attractive investment
destination, entire exercise of the procedure and legal formalities make it tedious and
confusing. The Committee is of the view that long legal formalities and procedural delays make
frustrating impact on the investors. The Committee recommends that UT Administration should
introduce single window composite application form for all the procedural clearances such as
acquiring land, granting of construction permission, pollution control procedures, issuing
occupancy certificates, power allocation, environment and forest clearances and other licences/
registration which would make this region investment friendly and boost industrial growth in
the UT. The Committee also recommends that suggestions/views of representatives of Industry
and trade associations should also be taken into account while framing policies and laws
pertaining to industries as they are the people who know what are the bottlenecks coming in
their way. The Committee also recommends that the Ministry of Home Affairs should positively
consider the demands of representatives of trade and industry as subsidies given would provide
stimuli for further industrialization. (Para 1.4.9 ibid )

8
Action Taken
1.10.1 The U.T. Administration is committed to providing an investment friendly and
conducive environment, including through expeditious clearances, for industrial promotion in
the territory. A ‘Single window Invertors Friendly Time Bound’ (SWIFT) system is already
operational in the territory since 1996, which serves as a single counter for receipt of various
application related to industrial clearance and for delivery of the approval letter.
1.10.2 The Administration has also launched the Samay Sudhini Seva in May, 2013, wherein
time bound delivery of various public services including various approvals required to be
granted to industries, is monitored. A total of 62 public services (including some to industries)
are covered presently and is proposed to be extended to other services in a phased manner
over a period of time.
1.10.3 The recommendation of Committee for introducing single window composite application
form has been noted.
1.10.4 The U.T. Administration does consider the suggestion/views of representatives of
industry and trade association while framing policies and laws pertaining to Industries. As
already stated above, a proposal for V AT incentives/exemptions to new industries and provision
of other incentives to retain the existing industrial units in the UT as well as to encourage new
investment industrial sector is under consideration.

governmental services helps fast and targeted delivery while checking leakage. The Committee

opening 10 new e-Sampark kiosks during the year 2013-14. e-Sampark kiosks are unique

concept is that the infrastructural as well as operations costs are significantly low and
these kiosks can be set up in areas quickly where space is a constraint. A new fully

network of e-Sampark Centres, thus adding to existing bouquet of services offered by the

certain key services in a time bound manner. Under e-District, services of the various
Departments of Chandigarh Administration would come under the ambit of e-Sampark
Centres. These include services of departments like Deputy Commissioner (DC) Office,
Registration and Licensing Authority (RLA), Education, Election, Food and Civil Supplies

Recommendation
Misuse charges at R500/- per sq.ft. per month
2.4 The revised rates carry the sanctity of law as on date to serve as a deterrent to
unauthorized conversion of activities. The UT Administration should undertake downward
revision of the charges on priority basis and notify the same at the earliest.
(Para 3.5.12 ibid )

32
Action Taken
2.4.1 The levy to misuse charges is as per the provisions contained in section 15 of the
Capital of Punjab (Development and Regulation) Act 1952, which reads as under :-
(i) Penalty for breach of rules:  Except as otherwise provided for in this Act, any
contravention of any of the rules framed there under shall be punishable with
fine which may extend to five hundred rupees, in case of a continuing
contravention, with an additional fine, which may extend to twenty rupees, for
each day during which such contravention continues after the first conviction;
and the Court while passing any sentence on conviction of any person for the
contravention of any rule, may direct that any property or part thereof in
respect of which the rule has been contravened, shall be forfeited to the
(Central Government).
(ii) The misuse charges fixed way back in 1952 had lost their deterrent effect and
accordingly the rates were revised in the year 2007 to R500/- square feet per
month as under :-
(iii)  Procedure in case of building violations and/or misuse of site/building
(a) When a building violation(s) and/or misuse of a site or building under
rule 9, is reported or comes to the notice of the Estate Officer then,
without prejudice to any action taken under Section 8-A of the Act, a
notice of period not less than 15 days shall be served on the allottee/
lessee(s) and on the occupier(s) of the site or building requiring that the
allottee/lessee(s) and the occupier(s) shall within a period of two
months, remove the said building violation(s) and/or misuse and pay
monthly charges @ R500/- per sq. ft. of area under building violation(s)
and/or misuse, which shall be paid jointly and severally by the transferee
and the occupier of the site or building for every month or part thereof
the building violation(s) and/or misuse occurs.
(b) The decision of the UT Administration to revise the misuse charges to
R500/- by making an amendment in the Rules had come up before the
Hon’ble High Court of Punjab and Haryana at Chandigarh. Even though
the main Act viz.  Capital of Punjab (Development & Regulation) Act
1952 fixed the misuse charges way back at R20/- only, the Hon’ble
High Court has not set aside the amendments in the Rules made by the
UT Administration fixing the misuse charges at R500/-per sq. ft. per
month on the area under building violation.
(c) The High Court had also directed that necessary action should be taken
to amend the Act. The UT Administration is examining the matter to
make a proposal to the MHA for undertaking the requisite amendment in
the main Act of 1952. As the Capital of Punjab (Development &
Regulation) Act 1952 was enacted long back, it was also intended to

33
consider making comprehensive changes in the Act in some of the areas
in view of the changed scenario and to suit the current requirement of
the Administration.
(d) In fact the Second Administrative Reforms Commission has also
recommended in its 15 th Report that the UT Administration should revisit
the Capital of Punjab (Development & Regulation) Act and the Punjab
New Capital (Periphery control) Act, 1952 to align them with the
changed circumstances. As the matter is complex, it is taking some time
to make the required reference to the Ministry of Home Affairs.
(e) The UT is constituting a committee to draft the changes required to be
made in the law in regard to the above and a proposal shall be submitted
to MHA, GOI in the near future.

3.9 The Committee recommends that the issue of regularization of daily wages persons

Government sector as daily wage workers/casual workers in the prime time of their life feel
cheated when the matter of regularization of their jobs crops up. By that time, neither they are
regularized nor are they taken in private sector as other more young aspirants are eying jobs

therefore strongly recommends that the issue of regularization of daily wage persons working

daily wages as approved. (Para 2.4.36 ibid )

think tank should be involved in this matter. Besides, there should a permanent body for its

the lake. (Para 3.7.53 ibid )
