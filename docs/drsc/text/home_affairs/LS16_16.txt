17. The Committee has been informed that the turnover and the profits of the Kendriya 
Bhanda r have increased over the years. Its actual performance is also above the norms fixed 
by NCCF. ln this context, the comm ittee has note.d that with a capital base of Rs. 76.80

for the 'Best Consumer Co-operative Society' for the year 1993·94. 
18. Notwithstanding the above performance and achievements of the Kendriya Bhandar, 
the Committee feels that there is lot of scope for improvement in its functioning. The 
Committee is not inclined to agree with the Ministry's view that the Kendriya Bhandars are 
run on professionaJ lines. It is generally seen that notice boards displaying the prices of 
essential goods are not properly maintained outside these stores. The names of items and 
their prices are written with chalks which is often either erased or not legible. Upkeep of 
most of the stores. especially display of goods. clean liness, maintenance of stores availability 
of goods is also far from satisfactory, despite the inspections carried out by senior officers 
from time to time. The quality of goods in the stores, particularly grocery items, is not uplo 
the desired level. lnspite of the inspections and procedure for quality control several 
wnsumer items sold through the stores have been found to be unfit for consumption. fn 
some cases, the behaviour of the sales staff is found wanting. Choice of goods in most of the 
stores is limited. Furthem1ore, the stores do not observe uniform timings and days of 
working. While some stores work on all days of the week (with one weekly off), some stores 
observe Centr al Government working days. There is no arrangement for carry bags in the 
stores. The Committee. therefore, strongly feels that all these aspects need to be seriously 
looked into to make such stores consumer-friendly and run on real professional lines. 
19. The Committee has bee n informed that the existing numeric machines · in the 
Kendriya Bhandars are being replaced with the new Alpha Numeric Machines which also 
print the name of the Items purchased and their prices on the cash memo. 45 of the stores 
have been provided with these machines so far. The existing machines arc being replaced 
according tO a phased programme. The Committee in this regard recommends that Alpha 
Numeric Machines should be installed in all the 84 stores of the Kendriya Bhandar within this 
financial year. This should not be difficult to achieve in view of the sound financial position of 
the Kendriya Bhandar.
