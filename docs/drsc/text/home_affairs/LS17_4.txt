REPORT OF THE COMMITIEE 
I, the Chairman of the Department-Related Standing Committee on Home Affairs, 
having been authorised by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, present this 
Fourth Report of the Committee. 
2. In pursuance of the rules relating to the Department-Related Parliamentary Standing 
Committees , the Chairman, Rajya Sabha, in consultation with the Speaker, Lok Sabh a, 
referred• the High Court and Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Amendment 
Bill, •• 1992 as introduced in the Lok Sabha on the 1st December, 1992 and pending in that 
House, to the Committee for examination and report. 
3. The Committee, in its meeting held on the 16th August, 1993 discussed the provi sions 
of the Bill and took up clause-by-clause consideration thereof. 
4. The Committee, in its meeting held on the 18th August , 1993 considered and adopted 
the draft Report. 
5. The Bill propose to exempt from the liability to pay income-tax on the entire value of 
leave travel concessions made available to Judges of the Supreme Court and the High 
Courts, instead of the existing limit of two journeys performed by them in a b lock of four 
calender years, to be given effect from the 1st April , 1986 when the numb er of journeys 
entitled to the Judges was enhenced. Accordingly, clauses 2 and 3 of the Bill were a dopted 
by the Committee without any change. 
6. Clause 1, the Enacting Formula and the Title were adopted with some changes which 
are of a consequential or drafting nature. 
7. The Committee recommends that the Bill, as reported , be passed.
