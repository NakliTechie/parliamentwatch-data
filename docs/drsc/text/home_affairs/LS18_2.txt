REPORT OF THE COMMITTEE 
I, ·the Chairman of the Department-related Standing Committee on Home Affairs, having been 
authorised by the Committee to present the Report on its behalf, present this Second Report of the 
Committee. 
2. In pursuance of the rules relating to the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committees, 
the Chairman, Rajya Sabha, in consultation with the Speaker, Lok Sabha, !*referred the Governors 
(Emoluments, Allowances and Privileges) 2•• Amendment Bill, 1992, as introduced in the Lok Sabha 
on the 3rd March, 1992, and pending in that House, to the Committee for examination and report. 
3. The Committee, in its meet ing held on the lOth August, 1993 discussed the provisions of the Bill 
and took up clause-by-claus~ consideration thereof. 
4. The Committee, at its meeting held on the 16th August, 1993" consider ed and adopted the draft 
Report. 
5. In view of the decisions taken by the Government of India to the effect that the pension 
equivalent of gratuity may not be deducted while fixing the pay of re-employed pensioners , and that 
the pension equivalent of gratuity will not be taken into account while fixing the initial pay of retired 
Judges of the Supreme Court and the High Courts appointed to Committees or on Commissions, it 
was felt that the benefit of not deducting pension equivalent of gratuity from the emolument s payable 
to Governors, may be allowed. According to the amendments proposed in the Bill, Governors who 
had received retirement gratuity in respect of previous service would be benefited . Accordingly, 
clause 2 of the Bill was adopted by the Committee without any change. 
6. Clause 1, the Enacting Formula and the Title were adopted with some changes which are of a 
consequential or drafting nature. 
7. The Committee recommends that the Bill, as reported , be passed .
