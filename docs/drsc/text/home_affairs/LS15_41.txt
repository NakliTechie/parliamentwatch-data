REPORT

1, the Chairman of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on
Home Affairs, having been authorised by the Committee to present the Report on its
behalf, present this Forty-first Report of the Committee, relating to the High Court and
Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Amendment Bill, 1998.*

2. In pursuance of the Rules relating to Department-related Parliamentary Standing
Committees, the Chairman, Rajya Sabha in consultation with the Speaker, Lok Sabha
referred** the High Court and Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Amendment
Bill, 1998 as introduced in the Lok Sabha on the 28th May, 1998 and pending therein, to the
Committee for examination and report by the 3rd July, 1998.

3. The Statement of Objects and Reasons of the Bill states that the salaries of the
Judges of the Supreme Court and the Judges of the High Courts were last revised with effect
from 1५ April, 1986 under the Constitution (Fifty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1986 as
indicated below:—

Chief Justice of India Rs. 10,000 per month
Judges of the Supreme Court Rs. 9,000 per month
Chief Justice of a High Court Rs. 9,000 per month
Judges of High Courts Rs. 8,000 per month

3.1 The Statement further states that the Fifth Central Pay Commission had recom-
mended the revision in the salaries and allowances of the Central Government employees
including the members of All India Services and the Government accepted the majority of
the recommendations. The notification dated the 30th September, 1997 amending the
existing pay rules, namely, the Central Civil Services (Revised Pay), Rules, 1997 had also
been issued and the revised pay rules deemed to have come into force on the Ist day of
January, 1996, The Statement further adds that having considered all aspects of the matter,
a necessity was felt to increase the salaries of the Judges with effect from
Ist January 1996 as detailed below:—

Chief Justice of India Rs. 33,000 per month
Judges of the Supreme Court Rs. 30,000 per month
Chief Justice of a High Court Rs. 30,000 per month
Judges of High Courts Rs. 26,000 per month

3.2. The Statement also states that since the Parliament was not in session, the
President promulgated the High Court and Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service)
Amendment Ordinance, 1998 on the 24th April, 1998 to give effect to the increase in the
salaries of the Judges. The Bill seeks to replace the said Ordinance.

4. The financial Memorandum of the Bill states that the additional expenditure on
account of revision in salaries in case of Judges of the Supreme Court, Delhi High Court and
6% (approximately) for Punjab and Haryana High Court will be charged on the
Consolidated Fund of India. The additional expenditure in respect of the Judges of other
High Courts will be charged on the Consolidated Fund of respective States. The sanctioned
strength of Judges of the Supreme Court, the Delhi High Court and the Punjab and Haryana
High Court is at present 26 and 31 (including 6 additional Judges) and 37 (including 7
additional Judges), respectively. On an average there will be an increase of Rs. 10,000 per
month per Judge. Thus, additional expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of India on
account of revision of salaries would be about Rs. 71 lakhs per annum. No other
expenditure, either recurring or non-recurring, is likely to be incurred.

5. In its meeting held on the 25th June, 1998, the Committee heard the Additional
Secretary, Department of Justice, Ministry of Law, Justice and Company Affairs on various
aspects of the Bill. ‘fhe Committee was informed that the Bill seeks to replace the
Ordinance promulgated on the 24th April, 1998 which is to expire after six weeks of
reassembly of the Parliament session. The Parliament's session commenced on the 27th May,
1998 and the six week period would be over on 8th July, 1998 and as such the Bill is to be
passed by both the Houses of Parliament before that date.

5.1 The Committee noted that the recommendations of the Fifth Central Pay
Commission were accepted by the Government in September, 1997 and it failed to
understand why the Government could not bring a Bill to amend the relevant Acts to effect
the enhancement in the salaries of the Judges of the Supreme Court and the High Courts.
The Committee did not appreciate the recourse taken by the Department in promulgating an
Ordinance which if not converted into an Act by 8th July, 1998, would result in an
anamolous situation in asmuchas the salaries of the Judges would revert back to the pre-
revised scales until the current session of Parliament was prorogued cn or after August 14,
1998 and another Ordinance promulgated on the subject. Such a situation could be very
awkward and embarassing for Parliament in view of the provisions of Articles 125 and 221
of the Constitution in terms of which the allowances etc. of the Judges of the Supreme Court
and High Courts shall not be varied to their disadvantage after their appointment.

5.2 Members sought clarifications from the representatives of the Department of Justice
on the various provisions of the Bill which were replied to.

5.3 The Chairman of the Committee informed that he had received a copy of a
representation dated 3rd June, 1998 from the All India Judges Association regarding the
revision of salaries of the Judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts. The Committee
was apprised of the issues raised therein. The said representation has been forwarded to the
Department of Justice for their consideration. In this connection the Committee urges upon
the Government to seriously consider the suggestions made in the representation expediti-
ously. The Committee recommends that the Government should have a second look at the
salaries and allowances of the Judges of the Supreme Court and the High Courts and take
decisions in that regard at the eartiest.

6. The Committee considered and adopted this Report in its sitting held on the 30th
June, 1998.

7. The Committee agreed to the amendments proposed to be effected by Clauses 2, 3
and 4 of the Bill in the High Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1954 and adopted
them without any change.


3

8. The Committee also agreed to the amendments proposed to be effected in the
Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1958 by Clauses 5, 6 and 7 of the Bill
and adopted them without any change.

9. Clauses 8 and 9 of the Bill were adopted without any amendment.
10. Clause 1, the Enacting Formula and the Title were adopted.
11. The Committee recommends that the Bill as reported by it, be passed.
