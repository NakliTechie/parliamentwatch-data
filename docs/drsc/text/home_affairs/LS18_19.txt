REPORT 
I, the Chairman, Committee on Home Affairs, having bean authorised by the 
Committee, preSent this Nineteenth Report on the Representation of the People (Amend­
ment) Bill*, 1995. The Bill seeks to further amend the representation of the People Act, 
1950. 
2. In pursuan.ce of the rules relating to the Department-related Parliamentary Standing 
Committees, the Chairman, Rajya Sabha, referred** the bill, as introduced in the Lok 
Sabha on the 20th March, 1995 and pending therein, to the Committee for examination and 
report with the direction that the Committee may submit its report at the earliest so that the 
Bill could be passed during the current session of Parliament. 
3. Accordingly, the Committee met on the 31st May, 1995 to consider the Bill and 
adopt its report thereon. 
4. As per the Statement of Objects and Reasons given in the Bill amendment to the 
entries relating to the State of Kamataka in the Fourth Schedule to the Representation of 
the People Act, 1950, has been necessitated consequent upon the coming into force of the 
Constitution (Seventy-third Amendment) Act, 1992 and the Constitution (Seventy-fourth 
Amendment) Act , 1992, and the amendment made in the law by the State of Kamataka 
relating to the local bodies. Under the amended law, the local bodies, namely, City 
Municipal Corporations, City Municipal Councils, Town Municipal Councils, Town Pancha­
yats, Zilla Panchayats, Taluk Panchayats, Gram Panchayats have been created. These local 
bodies will substitute the existing local bodies namely, Municipalities, Mandai Panchayats, 
Cantonment Board, Notified Area Committees and Zilla Parishads, except the Cantonment 
Boards for the purpose of election to the Legislative Council in Kamataka. 
5. In view of the above, the Committee is of the view that there is a need to amend the 
entries relating to the State of Kamataka in the Fourth Schedule to the Representation of 
the People Act, 1950. The Committee agrees with the proposed amendment and recom­
mends that the Bill be passed.
