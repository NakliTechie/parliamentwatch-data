enterpr ise into Non-Ferrous Mrning and Metallu rgical Industries and steps being taken to 
promote foreign investment. the Ministry of Mines stated tltat the Indian Mining Sector was 
opened up in stages. to private. including foreign investment, as part of the structural adju'>lntcnt 
programme of the Indian economy. This has been done in tune with the gen~ral economic 
libcralisation and reforms introduced by the Government in various sectors. The Nauonal

statute, was amended in 1994, by which foreign direct investment (FDI) in the mineral sector was

today is on par with global standards in this regard . 
3.3 The Central Government has const ituted the foreign Investment Promotion Board (FlPB) 
for clearance of applications for the foreign direct investment proposals. Since January 199\ the 
Government had been ,allowing FDI upto 50% in mining (except in respect of mining gold, silver 
and precious stones) and 74% in metallurb'Y and minera l processing through the automatic route. 
For FDI above these limits, the proposals had to come before the FIPB which used to consider 
them on a case to case basis. The FDI policy has been further liberalised w.e.f. ll 'h February , 
2000 to accelerate foreign investment in the mineral sector There will henceforth be no difference 
in tbe caps on foreign equity holdings to be allowed at ihe stages of exploration and that of 
mining:-
(a) For all minerals with which the Ministry of Mines is concerned other than diamonds 
& precious stones , foreign equ ity holding up to J 00% is allowed on the automatic 
route, for both explorat ion and mining. 
(b) In case of diamonds & precious stones, foreign equity up to 74% is allowed on the 
automatic route for both explora tion and mining operations. For proposals seeking 
higher than 74% foreign equity, the cases will have to come to FJPB for clearance. 
(c) Foreign equity upto 100% is allowed on the automatic route for processing of 
minerals and metallurgy . 
(d) There is no requirement of NOC from an exist ing joint venture partner for fresh 
investments . The prospective investo r will only have to give a declaration that he has 
no existingjoint venture for the same area and/or the partic~ar mineral. 
3.4 So far, through the FIPB route the Govt. has approved 67 proposals for foreign direct
