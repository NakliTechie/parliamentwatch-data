7.         The Secretary, thereafter elaborated on the role of the Department of Public Enterprises. He also
stated that the role as such had not abated even after creation of a separate Department of Disinvestment.
Thereafter, he dwelt  at  length on how BHEL, the main profit making PSU out of 48 public enterprises,
under the administrative charge of the Ministry had emerged as a potential public undertaking and was
contributing to the growth of the economy. He explained in detail the burgeoning losses, payment of
statutory dues to the employees, BIFR/revival status, technological obsolesce in public enterprises, need for
protection of indigenous industry by imposing import tariff and the status of automotive industry.
 
8.         Members raised their queries on various issues arising out of discussion.
 
9.         The Chairman asked the Secretary to furnish notes on issues which came up for discussion in the
meeting and could not be replied properly to the Secretariat at the earliest.
 
10.       Committee met at 5.00 p.m. again to hear the Chairman, SCOPE. The Chairman, SCOPE made a
brief presentation about the role and activities of SCOPE in respect of Public Enterprises. Members sought
clarification about the status of SCOPE. The Committee desired from the Chairman, SCOPE  a note stating
the steps taken by them in the pre-budget period for the PSEs and after the budget what further steps are
necessary.
 
12.       At the end of deliberation, members and staff present joined in a brief ceremony to bid farewell to the
Chairman whose term was expiring on 2
nd April, 2000. Members narrated their experiences with the
Chairman and extolled his contributions to the Committee. In the end, members wished him a happy life and
wished him to comeback soon as a remember again.
 
A verbatim record of the proceedings was kept. 
 
13.       Committee then adjourned at 5.40 P.M.
