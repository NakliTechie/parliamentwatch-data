      CHAPTER – I 
  
  
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS, WHICH HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED BY THE 
GOVERNMENT 
  
  
  
RECOMMENDATION 
  
          Last but not the least, under the present scen ario of stiff competition from private and global companies, th e
DPE should strengthen the MoU system specially where is sues involving inter-ministerial/ inter PSU co-ordinatio n
are concerned. The DPE should play a pro-active role in making the PSUs competitive and strengthening them fo r
sustained growth. 
  
COMMENTS 
  
            Efforts are made during the MOU negotiat ion meetings to achieve the inter-ministerial and inter-PSU 
coordination. The incorporation of PSU-s pecific efficiency parameters in the MOUs is an attempt to improve the 
profitability and competitive strength of the PSUs. Theref ore, the MOU system already plays, an active role in 
strengthening the PSEs to become more competitive. These efforts will be further continued.  
  
  
RECOMMENDATION 
  
The MoU system has undeniably made a positive contribu tion to improve the performance of the PSEs.  The 
management of PSEs were appreciative of the fact that the MoU system has, for the first time brought about clarity o f 
the goals to be achieved on the basis of precise targets laid down in the MoU ensuring accountability on an objective 
basis.  They were also enthusiastic of the larger delegati on of powers available to the MoU signing Companies.  The 
managements of the PSEs could act freely to a great extent with clear-cut objec tives and goals.  Especially in thei r 
day-to-day operations, the interference by the Ministries had reduced to a considerable extent. This has provided an 
opportunity to the managements to take quick decisions and furthermore, evaluation of their performance is now 
being done in a very objective and transparent basis. 
  
  
COMMENTS 
  
The observations of the Standing Committee on the work ing of the MOU system are taken note of and efforts 
will be made to further strengthen it.  
  
   
  
RECOMMENDATION 
  
The Committee also feels that through the MoU system, an effort has been made to synthesise different 
objectives.  Under the MoU system the parameters selected  as performance indicators ar e a good mix of financial, 
dynamic and efficiency indicators.  Thus, it is not only the financial parameters  which are given importance but other 
dynamic and efficiency parameters  whic h are very essential for the future growth of the PSEs, and which are also 
incorporated as performance indicators.  Thus, under the present system an attemp t has been made to strike a balance 
between financial and non-financial parameters.  The Comm ittee is of the firm view that the MoU system has 
produced reasonable results as it is unders tood that during the negotiation meetings an effort is made to set targets, 
which represent an improvement over the level of performance  in the previous year, and the system has significantly 
contributed to a progressive increase in the performance of PSEs.  The Committee, ther efore, recommends that the 
MoU system should continue on a long-term basis. 
  
COMMENTS

            The observations of the Standing Committee on the importance of MOU system has been taken note of and it 
has been decided to continue the system on a long term basis.         
   
  
RECOMMENDATION 
  
Recently, the Public Sector Enterprises have been expos ed to domestic and international competition.  Most 
of them were operating in a totally protective environmen t.  With the policy of liberalisation and globalisation, the 
PSEs have to compete in the market for obtaining orders bot h in products and services.  For a PSE to be competitive 
it is essential to have an edge over its competitors, both in cost and quality.  In such a situation, the Committee feels 
that  up gradation of technology including modernisation, up gradation of skills of em ployees and rationalisation o f 
manpower etc. are key factors to generate necessary comp etitive strength to PSEs.  The Committee, therefore, 
recommends that the MoU system should be used as an effective instrument to bring about the change in the working 
environment of the PSEs by introducing specific strategies through it to make them more competitive. 
  
COMMENTS 
  
            To a large extent technology upgradati on, modernisation, upgradation of skills of employees and 
rationalisation of manpower etc. generally form part of the dynamic parameters, which are incorporated in the MOUs. 
However, more emphasis will be laid on these issues while  issuing the guidelines for formulation of draft MOUs fo r 
next year.  
  
   
  
RECOMMENDATION 
  
On the question of reduction of budgetary support to Depa rtment of Public Enterprises and its consequent 
winding up in pursuance of recommendation of Expenditure  Reforms Commission (EFC), it has not considered the 
essentially vital role played by the  Department of P ublic Enterprises in making the Memoranda of Understanding 
which the Government of India enters with its Public Sector  Units on the annual basis.  The Department is merely not 
facilitating but enabling the MoU to be an important tool for performance assessment mutates mutandis to that o f 
Profit Center Concepts of large Conglomerates in devel oped countries.  The MoU is an  effective and objective tool 
today only because of it being facilitate d by a non-stakeholder that is Department  of Public Enterprises.  Hence, the 
Committee strongly feels that the Department of  Public Enterprises should be strengthened with budgetary support.   
  
COMMENTS 
  
The   objective of MOU system was to improve the performance of PSEs  and to intruduce a mechanism to 
evaluate the performance of the managements in an objective and transparent manner and it was in this interest, the 
job of implementing the MOU system was entrusted to a nodal Department. The need to strengthen the Department 
with budgetary support is taken note of and will be reviewed while formulating the budget proposals. 
  
   
RECOMMENDATION 
  
The Committee observes that the strength of the MoU system is that the targets are finalised and the 
performance of the PSE is evaluated in an objective and transparent manner that is ensured by the DPE and ATF.  
Thus, in order to maintain and further instil objectivity, th e Committee feels that DPE should play a more active role 
in the process and the MoU Division should be strengthene d so that they can carry out bench marking studies, 
performance related studies and other associated work so that the targets given to the PSEs are more realistic. 
  
COMMENTS 
  
               The MOU Division has the benefit of getting the advice of the ATF which is a body of experts an d
academicians eminent in their respective fields. Functi ons like conduction benchmark studies, performance relate d
studies can be sourced out to prominent professional bodi es. The need for strengthening MOU Division and playing a
more active role in the process has been take note of.

RECOMMENDATION 
  
             The Committee is, however, constrained to note that the MoU Division of the Department of  Public 
Enterprises has been proposed to be abolished.  The Secret ary, Ministry of Heavy Indus try and Public Enterprises, 
during the course of his oral evidence before the Committee in connection with the Demands for Grants, informed the 
Committee that the Expenditure Reforms Commission has recommended that the Department of Public Enterprises 
may be down-sized .  It was proposed that  the  MoU Syst em  may be transferred to the respective Administrative
Ministries of the concerned PSEs. The Committee, however, does not agree with this.   The Committee feels that the 
Department of Public Enterprises provides a neutral forum for finalising MoUs  which is essential to make it an 
objective systems.  It is also ensured that the entire MoU ex ercise is carried out in an objective, transparent and broad 
based manner.  The Committee, therefore, recommends that instead of transferring the MoU System to the concerned 
Administrative Departments, it may be retained with the De partment of Public Enterprise s. In fact the MoU Division 
needs to be further strengthened for an effective implementation of the MoU System.  
  
COMMENTS 
  
In order to ensure the objectivity of the system the job of implementing the MOU system was entrusted to a 
nodal Department, if the task of  preparing/finalising the MOUs is left to the respective concerned Administrative 
Ministries such a situation is bound to defeat the very purpose of having the MOU system. It has accordingly been 
decided not to transfer the MOU related work to the Admini strative Ministries and it will continue to the with DPE 
whose nodal and neutral status will be maintained. As regards the need for strengthening the MOU Division the 
recommendation of the Committee has been noted.  
  
CHAPTER-II 
  
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATION WHICH THE COMMITTEE  
 DOES NOT DESIRE TO PURSUE IN VIEW OF THE GOVERNMENTS’ REPLIES  
  
  
  
RECOMMENDATION 
  
The Committee is constrained to note that the MoU prep aration has generally been an annual target setting 
exercise.  The Committee feels that the MoU system should focus on strategic issues rather than be a mere annual 
target setting exercise.  For that purpose the Administ rative Ministries should provi de adequate input/support to 
enable PSEs to develop future strategies in association wi th the Ministries.  The parameters under the MoU need to 
be devised depending upon the strategy for its future growth in the particular area of its operations.  The performance 
of the PSEs needs to be reviewed on the basis of parameters so devised.  This would give a clear picture as to how a 
particular PSE has been able to make its products/services  cost effective and how they compare with their counte r 
parts in the private sector.  Such an approach would certa inly make the PSEs not only competitive but also provide an 
opportunity to become global players.  Therefore, the Committee suggests that the approach under the MoU system 
needs to be reviewed in line with the existing competitive economic environment. 
  
COMMENTS 
  
The Ad-hoc Task Force is already seized of this issue and during the MOU ne gotiation meetings, great 
importance is attached to the strategic issues. Further efforts in this regard will continue to bring to the notice of the 
PSEs and Administrative Ministries, the crucial importance of the strategi c issues and impressing upon them to 
develop appropriate strategies for improvement of the performance of the PSEs.  
  
   
RECOMMENDATION

The Sub Committee during the course of its study of MO Us of many Public Sector Undertakings like Bhara t

view, it will be difficult for a Cell in the DPE to handle the export  related issues and problems of the PSEs operating 
in various disciplines.

There have been persistent efforts by various Depart ments with the Finance Ministry to correct the duty 
structure.  With the liberalisation, the duties on the finished goods have come down faster than the duties on the raw

required.

            The rationalisation of the duty structure is a continuous process and this issue is deliberated upon before the 
formulation of the Central Budget each year in consultation with the Administrative Minist ries concerned as well as 
the Industrial Associations. This reco mmendation of the Standing Committee will be passed on to the Ministry o f 
Finance for their consideration.

RECOMMENDATION 
  
Though the parameters such as preparation and implem entation of corporate plan  and devising a strategic 
plan or vision plan, etc. are being me ntioned in the MoU documents, the targets set in the MoUs do not often 
correspond to the corporate vision/plans drawn up by the PSEs.  The Committee, therefore, stresses that there should

            Most of the PSEs have now formulated co rporate plans, as this has been impressed upon them during the 
MOU negotiation meetings. This recommendation of  the Standing Committee has been noted and the 
PSEs/Administrative Ministries  will be requested through the guidelines that are proposed to be issued for the 
formulation of MOUs for the next year to try and establish the necessary linkages between the corporate plans and the 
MOU targets.  
  
COMMITTEE'S OBSERVATIONS  
  
            DPE  may ensure implementation of this by a ll Ministries. This is necessary because if the targets of MOUs 
do not correspond with corporate vision/plans, the MOUs preparation  will be a futile exercise.   
  
   
RECOMMENDATION 
  
As PSEs have to operate in a free market, they cannot continue to depend upon Government’s assistance on 
matters such as obtaining orders, etc.  In the present situ ation, negotiating for orders is  a managerial function and, 
therefore, the Government’s  intervention should be very sel ective, if at all.  For inst ance, a number of the PSEs are 
working in the same area but they are under the control of different administrative minist ries.  In some cases, these 
PSEs operate as competitors to each other.  Similarly, PSEs are also involved in research and development projects 
and some of the PSEs are spending quite a significant  MoU units  on R&D activities.  Unfortunately, these PSEs are 
working in isolation and there is no coordination to provide synergy in R& D, marketing, etc. to enhance thei r 
competitive edge over their counter partners in the private sectors.  Since these PSEs are functioning in watertight 
compartments, they are not able to reap the maximum benefits of their investments on R&D and up gradation o f 
technology.  Separate and isolated efforts on the part of the PSEs in such areas only result in addition to their costs.  
Therefore, there is tremendous scope in bringing together  PSEs operating in the same discipline, but under different 
Ministries, to optimise inve stments in marketing, R&D etc. in a collect ive and coordinated manner.  The Committee, 
therefore, feels that the Department of Public Enterp rises operating through the MoU system appears to be an 
appropriate forum where the issues involving inter-ministries /inter-PSEs coordination can be taken up more 
effectively and DPE as a nodal Department should play a mo re pro-active role in making the PSEs competitive and 
strengthening them for sustained growth. 
  
COMMENTS 
  
            The possibility of such synergy exists  particularly in the area of R&D, Technology up gradation and 
marketing. Sub optimal investment by individual PSEs often does not generate  productive results. On the other hand, 
a collaborative effort in the field of R&D and marketing by pooling the resources of different enterprises can produce 
useful results. In the MOU negotiation meetings, it is generally attempted to bring together PSEs in the same 
discipline and explore such possibilities.  
  
COMMITTEE'S OBSERVATIONS
