REPORT
 
            The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Public Enterprises (Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public
Enterprises) on the recommendations contained in the 82 nd Report of the Department related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Industry on MoU
System in Strategic Sector PSEs, which was presented to Rajya Sabha and laid on the Table of Lok Sabha on the 10th December, 2002.
 
            Action Taken Notes have been received from the Department of Public Enterprises in respect of the recommendations contained in the Report.
They have been categorized as follows:-
 
i)
                    Recommendations/Observations which will be conveyed to the concerned PSE and the Administrative Ministry for appropriate action.
(Chapter-I). Para nos.:   4.18.1, 4.18.2, 4.18.4, 6.13.2, 6.13.5, 6.13.10, 5.8.7, 5.8.8.
 
ii)
                  Recommendations/Observations which will be conveyed to the Administrative ministry to include suitable parameter in MoU in the next
year or will be kept in View before finalizing next year’s MoU. (Chapter-II). Para nos.:4.18.3, 5.8.1, 5.8.2, 5.8.5, 6.13.4, 6.13.8, 6.13.9.
 
iii)                 Recommendations/Observations which have been noted by the Department. (Chapter-III). Paras nos.4.18.5, 4.18.6, 5.8.4, 5.8.6, 6.13.3,
6.13.6, 6.13.7.
 
iv)                Recommendations/Observation on which Department have not prepared any action. Para nos. 5.8.3
 
The details of the ATR are reproduced in various chapters in the succeeding pages.

CHAPTER – I
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH WILL BE CONVEYED TO THE CONCERNED PSE AND THE
ADMINISTRATIVE MINISTRY FOR APPROPRIATE ACTION
 
BHARAT EARTH MOVERS LIMITED
 
 
1.1            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE
 
            The Committee was pleased to note that BEML had been showing profits since its inception. However, the Committee was concerned to note that
there has been a constant decline in the profits over the past several years. The Committee feels that BEML has not made adequate efforts to expand its
market base. The Committee recommends that BEML should resort to aggressive marketing and explore new areas beyond their traditional customers to
expand their market base. In this context the Committee feels that BEML should develop value added products so as to garner a larger share of the market.
The Committee is of the view that with the infrastructure sector looking up, there are tremendous opportunities for BEML. The Committee also feels that
BEML should also explore the possibilities of providing maintenance service for high cost machines.
 
(para no. 4.18.1)
Reply of the Government
 
The recommendations of the Committee will be conveyed to the PSE as well as the Administrative Ministry for taking appropriate action with the
advice to formulate appropriate strategy.
 
 
1.2            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE
 
            The Committee was deeply concerned to note that BEML has not been able to get adequate work orders for its railway coach-manufacturing unit at
Bangalore. The Committee is of the opinion that BEML should approach organizations like the Delhi Metro Corporation to obtain work orders, and the
issue should be taken up with the Railway authorities at the highest level.
(para no. 4.18.2)
 
REPLY OF THE GOVERNMENT
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be communicated to the PSE and the Railway Board for taking immediate appropriate action. 
 
1.3            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE
 
            The Committee felt that since the MOU is a mutually agreed document on performance parameters, a focus shift in the contribution of Government
for this type of PSE is also imperative.  The performance parameters of the Department of Defence Production and Supply in the MOU should lay
emphasis on more tangible factors like facilitating more orders from Railways for their products, export promotion for their remote controlled armoured
vehicles, extension of use of export offices of other PSUs like Bharat Electronics etc in other countries for promotion of BEML products etc.
 
(para no. 4.18.4)
 
Reply of the Government
 
While in the emerging economic environment obtaining orders is primarily a managerial function, however, the Administrative Ministry can
always play a more pro-active role in this regard.  The recommendation of the Committee relating to promotion of exports from the PSE will be brought to
the notice of the Administrative Ministry with the request to support the efforts of the PSE in promotion of its exports.
 
 
NUCLEAR POWER CORPORATION LIMITED
 
1.6            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE

defence, contribution to Indian Space Research Programme, Disaster Management and Relief efforts.
(para no.6.13.2)
 
Reply of the Government
 
The recommendations of the Committee will be brought to the notice of the PSE as well as the Administrative Ministry for taking due note while
drafting the MOU document for the year 2003-04.

Committee informed about the action taken by the PSE concerned Ministry on the recommendations made by the Committee.

requested to prepare its strategic plan by incorporating their strategy of marketing and diversification.
 
 
2.3            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE
 
            The Committee also observed that having regard to the vital sector in which the unit is operating,  the structure of the MOU should be more
appropriately geared to this factor.  It was observed that the focus on financial performance being reiterated in the MOU should be altered to shift the focus
on more important factors of national interest. Hence the MOU should accord higher weightage to performance parameters relating to R&D, contribution
to defence, contribution to Indian Space Research Programme, Disaster Management and Relief efforts etc.
(para no. 5.8.2)
 
 
Reply of the Government
 
In case of HAL the financial parameters are accorded the weightage of 40%, which was 60% earlier.  The main objective of giving higher
weightage to the financial parameter is to ensure the financial viability of the PSEs so that this crucial aspect, which is essential for their existence, is not
lost sight of. The recommendation of the Committee is noted and efforts will be made to accord comparatively higher weightage to the parameters relating
to R&D, Contribution to Defence, Disaster Management and Relief Efforts, etc.
 
 
 
2.4            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE

infrastructure sector. It is therefore felt that the weightage for HRD should also be higher.
(para no.  5.8.5)
Reply of the Government
 
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be kept in view while finalizing the MOU for the next year.

CHAPTER –  IV
HINDUSTAN AERONAUTICS LIMITED
 
4.1            OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE
 
            The Committee felt that since the MOU is a mutually agreed document on performance parameters, a focus shift in the contribution of Government
for this type of PSU is also imperative.  The performance parameters of the Department of Defence Production and Supply in the MOU should emphasis
on more tangible factors like facilitating more orders from non Defence areas for their products, export promotion activities for their products, extension of
use of export offices of other PSUs like BharatElectronics etc in other countries for promotion of HAL products and services etc. In addition it was also
felt by the Committee that Government should also extend its assistance in ensuring HAL’s participation in UN Projects, including sale of its helicopters
for UN Peace Keeping Missions, UN activities in other relief and rehabilitation programmes in war torn, disasteraffected areas.  The Government should
also enable HAL in ensuring their participation in such activities of other multilateral agencies. In addition, during a demonstration of Helicopter testing
the Committee appreciated the testing competence of the HAL.  The Committee felt that these facilities and competencies are of international standards
and the government should strive hard to enable the export of these facilities and services to other countries.
(para no. 5.8.3)
 
Reply of the Government
 
It is true that MOU is a mutually agreed document and ideally the responsibilities of  both the parties should be clearly indicated in document itself.
While finalizing the MOU targets during the MOU negotiation meetings the managements of the PSEs are required to clearly indicate the assistance
required from the Government to achieve those targets.  In fact, there is a separate section in the MOU which clearly specifies the responsibility of the
Government in order to achieve the set targets and the evaluation of the performance of the PSE at the end of the year is carried out by taking into account
the extent of fulfillment of obligations on the part of the Government.  In case, the Government fail to fulfill these obligations the management of the PSE
is not allowed to suffer on account of it. Otherwise also, in the changing economic environment it is the role of the management to strengthen the
operations of the PSE and the Administrative Ministry of course, can play a more pro-active role to ensure the growth the PSE on a long-term basis.
