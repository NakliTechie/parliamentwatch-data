REPORT 
  
  
 The Action Taken Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Ministry 
of Heavy Industries and Public Enterpris es (Department of Heavy Industry) on the 
recommendations contained in the Committee ’s Two hundred and eighth  Report on the  
Demands For Grants (2008-09) pertaining to Department of Heavy Industry.   
  
2. Action Taken Notes have been received from the Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public 
Enterprises (Department of Heavy Industry)   in respect of the   recommendations contained in 
the Report.   These have been categorized as follows: 
  
Chapter -I- The recommendations/observations, which have been accepted by the Ministry.  
  
 The Committee is pleased to observe that the Ministry has accepted its recommendations 
as mentioned in the Chapter – I of the Report.   
Chapter -II- The recommendation/observations which the Committee does not desire to pursue 
in view of the Government's replies.  
  
 In respect of these recommendations the Committee is convinced with the logic and 
explanations advanced  by the Ministry and therefore does not want to pursue them for the 
present.  
Chapter -III- The recommendations/observations in respect of which the Committee has either 
not accepted replies of the Ministry or has asked the Ministry to review its reply. 
 The Committee expresses its concern over the non -implementation of recommendations 
contained in Chapter – III and desir es that the Ministry should furnish logical explanations  
therefor. 
Chapter -IV- The recommendations/observations in respect of which final replies of the Ministry 
have not been received.  
  
     In respect  of recommendations placed in Chapter -IV, the Min istry has  either   
furnished the interim replies or couched its language in  vague terms like  noted for 
compliance/guidance, therefore, the Committee desires  that the Ministry should furnish Action 
Taken Notes in  respects of recommendations categorized    in Chapter -IV  in detail and to the 
point, as per the intention and spirit of the recommendations of the Committee. 
    
3. The details of the ATR is being discussed in the succeeding pages.

Para 70 
 
Action Taken Note  
 
In terms of Article 266(3) of the Constitution of India, no money out of the Consolidated 
Fund of In dia shall be appropriated except in accordance with law and for the purposes and in 
the manner provided in the Constitution.  Accordingly any expenditure met out of Consolidated 
Fund of India needs the prior approval of the Parliament.  Though the expendit ure relating to 
investment in BHEL through issue of Bonus shares and the corresponding Capital receipts are 
notional for true and fair depiction in Government account both the receipts and expenditure 
need to be reflected in the Union Government amounts tr ansparently.  Approval of the 
Parliament was, thus, sought through Supplementary Demands for Grants. 
 
The receipts like dividend and interest are not capital receipts.  SBE ’s indicate the 
position of Govt. expenditure Ministry/Department-wise. 
 


 17

receipts through issue of Bonus shares in the statement of Budget Estimates.

Recommendation/Observation 
 
 The Committee has observed with reservation the delay in making reference o f BRPSE’s

Secretaries of the Ministry of Finance, Department of Public Enterprises and that it relies 
significantly on the inputs of administrative Departments, the Committ ee finds the practice of

as superfluous and time consuming. The views of the Government on the BRPSE ’s 
recommendations are further ratified by the BIFR. The Co mmittee objects to such a practice that 
leads to further critical delays. On the one hand BRPSE is not provided with adequate budget to 
make its own independent assessment and is made to rely upon the administrative Ministry; on 
the other hand its recommen dations are further referred to a collateral body of Secretaries.  In 
view of the Committee such a practice only undermines the recommendations of the BRPSE.    
Para 18 
Action Taken Note  
 
The recommendations of BRPSE are advisory in nature and the revival proposals are considered 
by the representatives of various Ministry/Departments etc. at formulation stage as such their 
views cannot be taken as final comments/concurrence of the Ministry to the proposal. The Inter -
Ministerial consultation is pre -requisite and mandatory before revival packages are finally 
approved by the Government. Moreover, the revival plan of the BIFR referred PSEs, also 
requires the ratification/approval of the BIFR which is a statutory body.   
 
 
Further Recommendation  
 
The line of argu ment put forth in the action taken note does not take into account 
the logic given in the recommendation. The idea in the recommendation is to avoid delay in 
taking action on the recommendations of BRPSE which includes Secretaries of Ministry of 
Finance an d Department of Public Enterprises. The Committee  would appreciate if same 
thinking is developed to evolve a suitable mechanism and  strongly feels that the entire 
process of revival plan has to be made less bureaucratic and more sincere and brisk. 
Hence, the Committee reiterates its recommendation at Para 18.
