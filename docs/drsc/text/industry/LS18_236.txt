Recommendations/observations 
 
 The  Committee  also  feels  that  in  an  increasi ngly  dynamic  and competitive  
market  system  land,  labour  and  capital  will  play  a  little  lesser  role  in defining the 
business concerns of an enterprise.  In fact, innov ation will be central to the success of 
modern day business ventures.  It is gratifying for  the Committee to note that in  the  
MoU  system  due  weightage  is  given  to  researc h,  development  and  innovation. 
During the course of the interaction of the Committ ee Members with the Officials of the 
Rourkela  Steel  Plant  it  was  found  that the  p lant  did  not  allocate  enough  resources  
for the  research  and  development.    The  manage ment  of  the  Steel  Plant  confessed  
that  the research  and  development  did  not  rec eive  priority  attention.    It  is  rather  
unfortunate that a public sector steel plant which spearheaded steel production in our 
country after independence  is  lagging  behind  in   research  and  development.    The  
Committee, therefore,  recommends  that  PSEs  must   earmark  specific  and  adequate  
fund  for pursuing research, development and innova tion which are central to their 
success in the increasingly  commercial  and  busin ess  environment.    The 
administrative Ministries of all the PSEs must moni tor the investment made by the 
concerned PSEs for this purpose. The  Committee  wo uld  like  to  get  a  report  in  this  
respect  from  the  administrative Ministries.

Recommendations/observations 
 
 The Committee feels that our energy security depend s on our efforts  to  explore 
and exploit the oil resources available within the country.  Therefore, the oil companies 
need to upgrade their technology for augmenting the  oil supply.  It is in this context that 
Committee recommends that more power must be given to the oil companies to exercise 
the choice for greater investment.

capital expenditure, investment in joint ventures/ Subsidiaries.  
 
 These include Investment in JVs/Subsidiaries up to  Rs. 5000 Crore for 
Maharatana and up to Rs.1000 Crore for Navratana pe r single project to be considered on

Recommendations/observations  
 The Committee feels that the above observation of ONGC reflects the concerns of 
not only ONGC but most of the oil companies.  The C ommittee, therefore, is of the view 
that these points raised by the ONGC may be kept in mind while signing MoUs .         
Para-20 
 
Action Taken Note  
 The suggestions regarding fixation of targets thro ugh bilateral negotiation 
between oil companies and Administrative Ministry, and a modest growth over the 
previous year has been considered in MoU 2012-13. I t can be seen that in the case of 
ONGC, targets fixed for Gross Sales and Net Profit for 2012-13 (Excellent level) is Rs 
84548 Crore and Rs 21571 Crore respectively as agai nst the actual Gross Sales of Rs 
76130 Crore and Net Profit of Rs 25123 Crore in 2011-12. 
 
 
Recommendations/observations  
 The Committee is of the opinion that the administra tive Ministries need to be 
more  vigilant  in  ensuring  that  targets  are  realistically  fixed  taking  into  account  the 
Technological capability  of  PSEs.    The  Committee  takes  note  of  the  observation  of  
the Department  of  Public  Enterprises  that  quit e  often  the  PSEs  under promise  and 
overachieve.    Such  kind  of  approach  does  not   help  the  PSEs  concerned  to  give  
their best.  It is, therefore, recommended that the  administrative Ministry, the Department 
of Public  Enterprises  and  the  Task  Force  must   jointly  ensure  that  the  PSEs  set  
their targets  as  per  their  potential.    Failur e  to  do  so  will  lead  to  their  failure  in  
the  highly competitive business environment .                                 
Para-24 
 
Action Taken Note  
 This issue has been taken care of in the MoU guide line for 2012-13 issued on 
dated 31 st  Oct 2011 vide para no: 2.2.3 that provides that wh ere cases of under pitching 
and over performance are found, DPE/Task Force will have the liberty of calling CMD to 
explain plausible reasons. DPE may then take up the  issue with the high power 
committee on MoU and suggest for an imposition of p enalty marks on CPSEs’ MoU 
score as adjudged by the Task Force. MoU targets of  2012-13 have accordingly been 
fixed realistically.

the suboptimal performance of Board of Directors wh ich adversely impact efficiency and

Para-27 
 
Action Taken Note  
 The Department of Public Enterprises shares the co ncern expressed by the 
Department Related Parliament Standing Committee on  Industry regarding filling up of 
vacant posts of non-official Directors on the Board s of Central Public Sector Enterprises 
(CPSEs). Accordingly the above recommendation has b een accepted by the Department

Recommendations/observations 
 
 The Committee while examining some organizations f ound that these had spent 
crore of rupees to facilitate medal winners of the Commonwealth Games. They showed 
such expanses under the category of Corporate Socia l Responsibility which is part of the 
MoU system. When the Committee asked the representative of the Department of Public  
Enterprises to clarify on the matter he maintained that that such activities do not come 
under the CSR scheme and, therefore, the concerned PSEs would not get any points 
under MoU performance.  The DPE representative furt her stated that promotion of sports 
form part of the CSR activity.  However, he maintai ned that expenses incurred for the 
purpose of felicitating award winning sports person s would not come under the category 
of promotion of sports. The committee took a strong  view on the matter and expressed 
opinion that CPSEs should not divert money meant fo r CSR activities for such 
programmes which involved felicitation of sports pe rsons.  The funds allocated for CSR 
should be spent in such a way which creates a long- term asset and such infrastructure 
which will be of lasting value for society. 
Para-29 
 
Action Taken Note  
 In the CSR Guidelines for Central Public Sector En terprises (CPSEs) issued by 
Department of Public Enterprises (DPE) in April, 2010, it is clearly mentioned that  

5 
 
investment in CSR should be project based.  In view  of this, any activity undertaken for 
promotion of sports and games should be project bas ed. The Committee has rightly 
observed that CPSEs should not divert money meant f or CSR activity for programmes 
not undertaken under the project mode. 
 
 
 
Recommendations/observations  
 The Committee also took a serious note of this iss ue and observed that the 
Department of Public Enterprises Should issue some clarificatory guidelines to all CPSEs 
which will enlighten them as to how the funds under CSR should be spent. 
Para-30 
 
Action Taken Note  
 The CSR guidelines issued by DPE in April, 2010 ha ve specified clearly how the 
funds under CSR should be spent by the CPSEs.  Furt her, the DPE regularly organizes 
workshops and meetings of CPSE representatives with  the MoU Task Force Member for 
stressing again and again the provisions contained in the Guidelines and for removing 
doubts, if any, in implementation of the CSR projects. 
 
 
Recommendations/observations  
 The Committee observes that the MoU system which i nitially focused attention 
on accountability and autonomy of PSEs eventually s tressed on their performance 
appraisal.  The Department of Public Enterprises ma intained that a lot more has to be 
done by PSEs to stress on both financial and non-fi nancial parameters.  The Committee 
welcomes the measures taken by DPE to make MoU syst em more broad based by 
including three Ps which in expanded form means profit, people and planet.                        
Para-31 
 
Action Taken Note  
 DPE has issued comprehensive guidelines on CSR, R& D and Sustainable 
Development. These are mandatory elements of MoU for CPSEs and specific weights are 
assigned to these in MoU. These initiatives have be en well accepted by CPSEs in the 
MoUs signed for the year 2012-13. By introducing th ese recent measures, MoU has 
become broad-based forward looking.

Recommendations/observations  
 It is the age when the business enterprises have t o be run on the basis of modern 
principles which are different from the norms gover ning Government departments.  
Therefore, the Committee feels that the MoU system should be such which would enable 
the PSEs and its management to take advantage of th e opportunities that are now 
available in the market for maximizing their profits.  While doing so, the PSEs concerned 
must avoid the problems and threats which might pose danger to its commercial viability.  
The non-financial parameters in the MoU are often d escribed as soft bellies.  It is 
important, therefore, to give adequate weightage to  both financial and non-financial 
parameters .                                 
  Para-32 
Action Taken Note  
 In the MoU Guidelines for the year 2012-13, the fi nancial and non-financial 
parameters have weightage of 50% equally with an exception for Sick & loss making and 
Section 25 companies where the ratio is 40:60. 
 
 The non-financial parameter is based on the princi ples of Specific, measurable, 
attainable, result oriented & Tangible (SMART) and includes Dynamic Parameters, 
Sector-specific& Enterprise-specific Parameters, Co rporate Social Responsibility (CSR), 
Research & Development (R&D), Sustainable Developme nt (SD), Human Resource 
Management (HRM) and Compliance of Corporate Governance (CG).   
 
 
Recommendations/observations  
 The time has now come for the corporation bodies n ot only to use their strategic 
assets but also take advantage of the opportunities  available on a pro active basis.  A laid 
back approach will not help in furthering the cause  of business. The Committee, 
therefore, feels that MoU system must go beyond fin ancial performance and give 
importance to the issue of productivity improvement.  
  Para-33 
 
Action Taken Note  
 DPE has issued guidelines on the following issues with specific weightages in 
MoU: 
 (a) Adoption of innovative practices (b) Committed  capital Investments (CAPEX) 
by CPSE and (c) Listing of eligible CPSEs. These st rategic issues will go a long way in 
enhancing productivity of CPSEs.
