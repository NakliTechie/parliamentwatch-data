REPORT
 
 
            The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Public Enterprises (Ministry of
Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises) on the recommendations contained in the 83 rd  Report of the Department related
Parliamentary Standing Committee on Industry on MoU System in Allied Sector PSEs, which was presented to Rajya Sabha
and laid on the Table of Lok Sabha on the 10th December,  2002.
 
            Action Taken Notes have been received from the Department of Public Enterprises in respect of the recommendations
contained in the Report. They have been categorized as follows:-
 
(i)         Recommendations/Observations which will be conveyed to the concerned PSE and the  Administrative
Ministry for appropriate action and for inclusion of suitable parameter in MOU in the next year.
 
The details of the ATR are reproduced in various chapters in the succeeding pages.
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH WILL BE CONVEYED TO THE CONCERNED PSE AND
THE ADMINISTRATIVE MINISTRY FOR APPROPRIATE ACTION AND FOR INCLUSION OF SUITABLE
PARAMETER IN MOU IN THE NEXT YEAR
 
 
EXPORT CREDIT GURANTEE CORPORATION
 
1.1            RECOMMENDATION  (PARA 6.1)
 
 
            The Committee is of the view that ECGC should expand the range of its activities to cater to medium and small-scale
exporters. The Committee recommends that ECGC should endeavor towards specialization based on customer segmentation
based on customer size, which has become a necessity in the present circumstances.
 
ACTION TAKEN
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be brought to the notice of the management of the ECGC and the
Administrative Ministry for their consideration.

1.2            RECOMMENDATION (PARA 6.2)
 
The Committee after examining the performance of ECGC within the MOU parameters is of the opinion that
weightage should be given to the role played by ECGC in providing impetus to exports in the small-scale sector. The
Committee feels that the SSI sector makes a marked presence in the export market and comprises a part 45 percent of the
India`s exports. The Committee strongly recommends that ECGC should introduce some special scheme to promote exports
from the SSI sector.
 
ACTION TAKEN
 
The recommendation of the Committee is noted and the matter will be taken up with the PSE as well as with the
Administrative Ministry.
1.3            RECOMMENDATION (PARA 6.3)
 
            The Committee during the course of the deliberations felt that the customer requirements are changing and demand
for cost effective and efficient service on global standards is growing. The Committee strongly recommends that ECGC
should take immediate steps to become a strong customer oriented organization.
 
ACTION TAKEN
 
The PSE will be advised to take due note of the recommendation of the Committee  while formulating its long-term
policy.
 
1.4            RECOMMENDATION (PARA 6.4)
 
The Committee is of the opinion that the total exposure in terms of maximum liability underwritten by ECGC  is
disproportionate to the present capital base of ECGC. The Committee recommends that ECGC should undertake a cost
benefit analysis and a risk factor analysis so as to ensure that the liabilities do not harm the future prospects of ECGC.
 
ACTION TAKEN
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be brought to the notice of Administrative Ministry and the PSE for
taking necessary action in this regard.
 
1.5            RECOMMENDATION (PARA 6.5)
 
             The Committee also feels ECGC should start diversifying into new services like Export Insurance, Force Maseure
Insurance for exports etc. and also gear up to competition from Private Insurance Companies in these services.
 
ACTION TAKEN

NATIONAL FILM DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION
 
1.6            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.1)
 
            The Committee was of the opinion that in today’s world the impact of mass media is tremendous, however
commercial considerations should not be the sole criteria for production of films. The Committee strongly feels that films,
which are socially relevant must get preference in NFDC.
 
ACTION TAKEN
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be conveyed to the NFDC and to the Administrative Ministry for
necessary action.
 
1.7            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.2)
 
            The Committee during the course of deliberations was of the opinion that NFDC should play a more active role
towards the popularization of Indian Cinema at the international level. The Committee recommends that NFDC should
provide support to both Indian films and Indian Directors to enable them to get international exposure and success.
 
ACTION  TAKEN
 
The recommendation will be communicated to the Ministry and the PSE for taking action at their level.
1.8            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.3)
 
            The Committee is of the considered opinion that the film festivals being organized by NFDC are mainly held in major
metropolitan cities. The Committee recommends that film festivals should be held in smaller towns so as to propagate and
promote Indian cinema and to provide wider audience.
 
Reply of the Government
 
The recommendation will be brought to the notice of the Administrative Ministry and the PSE for taking further
necessary action.
 
1.9            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.4)
 
            The Committee feels that NFDC should help in promoting regional films so as to enable them to get all India
viewership. The Committee recommends that NFDC should help in the distribution/exhibition of regional films by providing
Hindi/English subtitles.

ACTION TAKEN
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be conveyed to the PSE for necessary action.
 
1.10            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.5)
 
            The Committee is of the opinion that Indian Cinema cannot remain isolated from the global cinema and hence steps
must be taken to bring good quality foreign cinema to India. The Committee recommends that NFDC should bring in good
quality foreign cinema to India.
 
ACTION TAKEN
The recommendation is being brought to the notice of NFDC.
 
1.11            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.6)
 
            The Committee was informed that resource constraint has been a major factor hampering the growth of NFDC. The
Committee feels that with the grant of Industry status to the film industry financial institutions can finance the production of
quality films The Committee strongly recommends that government should take up the matter with the financial institutions
so that more loans are extended to NFDC.
(Para 12.6)
 
ACTION TAKEN
The recommendation of the Committee will be communicated to the Administrative Ministry and NFDC for taking up
the matter with the financial institutions.
 
1.12            RECOMMENDATION (PARA  12.7)
 
           The Committee was informed of very little participation of NFDC in the social thematic films made by WHO,
UNESCO, UNICEF etc. Having regard to our comparative advantages as well as core competence in filming, the Committee
recommends that MOU should give separate weightage to NFDC’s participation in this regard. Also, Government should also
extend all endeavor in promoting their participation in UNICEF, UNESCO, WHO for social theme films.
 
ACTION TAKEN
 
The recommendation of the Committee will be communicated to the PSE and due consideration will be given while
finalizing the MOU of the PSE for the next year.

The Committee was also informed that NFDC’s had severe bad debts and  outstanding payments from states of North

Further observations/recommendations of the Committee
 
The Committee is of the view  that the responsibility of  Department of Public Enterprises  does not end by conveying
