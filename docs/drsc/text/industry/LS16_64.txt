HINDUSTAN ZINC LIMITED (HZL)

1. Hindustan Zinc Limited (HZL), one of the leading producers of lead and zine, was
incorporated in January 1968 as a public sector Company after the take over of the erstwhile
Metal Corporation of India Limited, to develop mining and smelting capacities to meet the
domestic demand of zinc and lead metals.. The authotrised share vapital of HZL is Rs. 500 crore
and the paid up capital is Rs. 422.53 crore, The Government holds 75.92% of the equity as
24.08% of its equity was disinvested during the period 1991-93. Government has decided to
further disinvest 26% equity capital of HZL to a Strategic partner with an appropriate role in
management. During the year 2000-2001 the company achieved 94,7% capacity utilization in
respect of zinc metal production The company has $3% market share in respect of zinc smelter.

7.0 MARKETING STRATEGY OF HZL AND SALES PERFORMANCE

7 To improve market share of HZL, it has recently expanded its zinc smelting capacity by
17,000 tpa. HZL also contemplates putting up of 100,000 tpa Greenfield Zinc Smelter besides
further increase in its Brown field zinc capacity in consonance with the zinc demand growth in
the near future.

72 The existing Zinc and Lead production capacities in the Country do not fully meet the
demand of these metals. The gap in demand and indigenous production of Zinc and Lead metals



are filled through imports. a last three years, the demand-supply deficit in zinc ranges
between 23 to 26% and that of lead between 32 and 520;0 and the entire quantity was met by
imports.

73 In the beginning of IXth Plan, the Working Group constituted by Govt. of India projected
that demand of zinc and lead are likely to grow @ 6% and 7% respectively during [Xth and Xth
Plan periods. In the interim review of the IXth Plan, the actual growth of zinc and lead metals are
by and large at par with the projections made by the Working Group. It is also envisaged that
even after putting up of Greenfield zinc smelter of 100,000 tonnes per annum capacity, the gap
between demand and supply will continue to exist, to be met through imports.

74 Under the situation, the Sub- Committee was infornied the predominant market strategy
of HZL is two fold:

(i) to have appropriate pricing policy to ensure that most of the indigenous
customers should prefer to HZL products rather than opting for imports.

(ii) to provide best customer service and to take products at the door steps of the
customers which will help them to maintain minimum inventory at their end and
not to face any transportation problem.

7.5 These factors will continue to play key role in the next five years. Therefore, keeping
these aspects into consideration the marketing strategy of HZL is as follows:

(i) Just in time supply’ has been developed to serve the needs of the customers at a
short notice.

(ii) Customers Service Support System has been developed by which, customer gets
all the information and prompt services. This also helps in maintaining high
market share. To serve the customers effectively, a network of stock points has
been established, which are situated very near to customers’ factory and delivery
can be given on the same day.

(iii) By this, the customers will save on inventory carrying cost and due to cost
effective method, HZL is able to sustain major customers.

(iv) | Zine and lead metals can be freely imported into the country under OGL.
Therefore, it becomes imperative to fix the prices in such a manner that,
indigenous customers may continue to get the material at a competitive price.

(vy) To achieve this. HZL has to follow a competitive parity pricing policy. Importers
are one of the major competitors. As such. LME price fluctuation will be the
benchmark and the prices are revised according to the market condition.

(vi) As per the need of the market and financial conditions of the customer, HZL
also offers various credit terms (Secured Credit) and discount to value customers
so that, they get material at the same price as from any other sources.

(vii) sales payments are collected in time and HZL ensures no loss to the
organization.

9.0 MODERNISATION AND EXPANSION PROGRAMME

9.1 The HZL management stated that during the financial year 2000-2001 the existing Zinc
production capacity has been increased by 10,000 TPA at Debari Zine Smelter and the existing
Zinc production capacity was also increased by 7,000 TPA at Vizag Zinc Smelter consequent to
which the total installed capacity of the company has become 1,69,000 tonnes of zinc production.
The management also stated that the Nickel Technology Proving Plant for recovery of Nickel
from low grade lateritic chromite overburden dumps at the Sukinda Valley in Orissa successfully
completed the campaigns in February 2001 to firm up to operational parameters. The results
were encouraging and the process was found technically viable and know-how package was
prepared. The work to convert this into a business/investment proposition is under-way. HZL
management further informed that HZL has been continuously upgrading its technology for


=| its operations and growth, thereby firmly positioning itself as market leader in the
country. Other expansion and diversification schemes under contemplation and in progress are
Greenfield Zing Smelter Project at Kapasan, Nickel Technology Proving Plant, Di-ammonium
Phosphate (DAP) Plant, and Sindesar Khurd Lead Zinc Prospect.

9.2 The Committee notes that existing zinc production capacity has increased by 10,000
tpa at Debari Zinc Smelter and Zine capacity has also been increased by 10,000 tpa at Vizag
Smelter , The Sub-Committee hopefully recommends that all out efforts should be made for
installed capacity utilisation.

93 The Committee hopes that the Nickel Technology proving plant for recovery of Nickel

10.0 CAPITAL RESTRUCTURING PROGRAMME

10.1 As regards the ratio of profit in comparison to capital investment in HZL the management
stated that the Company earned net profit before tax Rs 282.22 crores and profit after tax
Rs.169.22 crores on sales turnover of Rs.1613.05 crores during the year 2000-2001. The capital
employed as on 31.3,2001 was Rs.1010.01 crores. The return on capital employed was 26.33%.
When the Sub-Committee wanted to know as to what steps may be taken with regard to capital
restructuring for improved physical and financial performance of HZL, the management stated
that HZL has become a Zero Debt Company since October, 1999. Further, the Government has
already initiated action to divest its 26% stake to a Strategic partner,

10.2 When the Sub-Committee wanted to know the status of further disinvestment of 26% of
HZL equity the management stated that the Government of India has decided to disinvest 26%
equity to a Strategic Partner. Two rounds of due diligence by the qualified interested parties have
been completed. Those parties who participated in the due diligence are M/s Bhushan Steels Ltd.,
M/s Indo gulf, M/s Sterlite Industries Ltd., M/s Binani, M/s Metdist, M/s Glencore and M/s
Allied Deals, UK.

10.3 The Committee is happy to note that the return on capital employed on HZL is to the
tune of 26.33%. The fact that company is a zero debt company gives satisfaction to the

. Committee. The Company should continue to maintain this status.

10.4 The Committee has been given to understand that HZL is at the advanced stage of
further divestment to the extent of 26% to a strategic partner. The Committee hopes that due
care would be given for evaluation of assets of HZL at current market value and adequate
transparency would be maintained. However, the process of restructuring should not be
procrastinated . In case there is going to be more than 6 months in restructuring, necessary
steps should be taken to solve the all pending issues and problems, Delay in restructuring may
delay implementation of many schemes.

11.0 RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

11,1 Pertaining to the outcome of projects on R&D in HZL and efforts made in reducing the
cost of production and increasing the physical and financial performance of the company, the
management stated that the company attaches great importance to continuous upgradation of
technology to reduce production cost and recovery of metal values from wastes and residues
through its Research and Development activities as well as in collaboration with other national



and overseas R&D institutes of repute. The Sub-Committee was apprised of some major R&D
projects which are as under:-

(i) The studies of extraction of Gold from Samples of Jagpura Gold Prospect are
aimed at developing a suitable flow scheme by a combination of gravity
separation, floatation, roasting and cyanide leaching to minimize treatment cost
and achieve high recoveries. The initial results are encouraging and further work
is in progress.

(ii) For development of Bio-leaching Technology for recovery of metals from Lean
Ores and Tailing laboratory tests were carried out for enrichment of zinc in the
solution obtained after bio leaching of Rampura-Agucha beneficiution plant
tailings. The Committee eonstituted by S&T, which recommended further
testing of the scheme ysing a continuous bioreactor, reviewed the test results.
The test work will be carried out in the current year.

(iii) A Pilot plant to treat 500 Kg/day of Poly Metallic nodules to recover copper,
nickel and cobalt is under construction at Central Research and Development
Laboratory at Debari (Rajasthan). This plant will be operated during 2001-2002
for proving technology for utilization of this resource,

11.2 The Sub-Committee was also apprised of the fact that in order to improve the
performance of the company efforts on the Exploration and R&D are required which inter-alia
include (i) Jagpura Gold Prospect (ii) Kayar Lead-Zinc Prospect, Ajmer (iii) Ajmer Regional
Exploration. Development of Bio-Leaching Technology for recovery of metals from Lean Ores
and Tailings, Poilot Plant to treat 500 Kg/per day of Poly Metallic Nodules to recover Copper,
Nickel and Cobalt.

11.3 The Committee is of the view that adequate investment on R&D generates capability
to innovate HZL, management may consider to make sufficient investment on Research and
* Development.

11.4 The Committee hopes that-the investment made on the Jagpura Gold project, Bio-
leaching technology for recovery of metals from leach Ores and pilot plant for treating 500
kg/day of poly metallic nodules to recover copper, nickel and cobalt would prove helpful in
exploring new vistas and increasing profitability.

12.0 RATIONALISATION OF MANPOWER

12.1. Pertaining to manpower strength in HZL the management informed that the company has
surplus manpower in its Mining and Smelting Units owing to cither depleting resources or due to
