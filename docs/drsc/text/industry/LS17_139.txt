RECOMMENDATION 
  
1.1        FUNCTIONAL STRUCTURE OF SAIL – AN INTRODUCTION 
  
The Committee while noting the func tional structure of SAIL is of  the view that each and every
organ of SAIL would perform their respective task so as to achieve the over all objectives and mission
of the company. The Committee hopes that each pa rt/division of the SAIL functions in complete
equilibrium to achieve its corporate goal. 
                                                                                                                        (Para 1.6) 
Action Taken 
  
1.1.1    The Board of Directors of  the Steel Authority of India Li mited (SAIL) has approved a vision
statement for SAIL.  Further, th e Board approves every year an Op eration Budget for SAIL at the
beginning of the financial year detailing therein the physical parameters and financial performance to be
achieved during the year.  Beside s, Capital Expenditure budget is also approved by the Board, which
inter alia, includes the schemes aiming at improvement  in quality and productivi ty.  A detailed micro-
level planning is done at various/units  level to achieve these.  As suc h, all the organs of SAIL including
the management and the Board of  Directors work towards achiev ing the objectives and mission o f
SAIL.  In order to improve the f unctioning of the Board a nd also to comply with the Securities and
Exchange Board of India (SEBI) requirements, the Board has been re-constituted, which now comprise 
more than 50% of independent Directors.

1.7     Reasons For Cost Over-Run 
  
The Committee notes that reasons for cost over-run ar e mainly increase in volume of work, inclusion of 
new items, price escalation, exchange rate variation, increase in duties and taxes and increase in interest
payments due to change in mode of  financing. The Committee is constrained to express its anguish over
the fact that the modernisation of Durgapur Steel Plant was approved in Feb., 1989 by Govt. at an
estimated cost of Rs. 2668 crore with FE component of Rs. 685 crore ( Base date: 3rd quarter 1988). The 
revised cost estimate of DSP modernisation worked out to Rs. 5074.71 crore (FE: 1375.42 crore) on
base date of 2nd quarter 1999. As per Govt’s approval, the project was to be completed by March, 1993.
The modernisation was completed in March 1998 with an overall delay of 60 months.  
(Para 2.11.4)
