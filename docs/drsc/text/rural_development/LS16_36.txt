1.1
 
The 
Government of India, as part of poverty reduction strategy, launched the 
Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak
 
Yojana (PMGSY) on 25

Sponsored Scheme to assist the States, though Rural Roads are in the State List under 
the Constitution. The primary objective of PMGSY is to provide connectivity by way of a 
single All

d cross
-

-

areas. In respect of ‘Special Category States’ (Arunac

Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir 
and Uttarakhand), the Desert areas, the Tribal (Schedule V) areas and selected Tribal 
and Backward districts as identified by the Ministry of Home
 
Affairs/erstwhile Planning 
Commission, the objective is to connect eligible unconnected habitations as per Core
-
Network with a population of 250 persons and above (Census 2001). In critical LWE 
affected blocks (as identified by MHA), special dispensation

bring execution of the Programme to the desired quality
 
standards, a three tier quality 
management mechanism has been institutionalized under PMGSY. First
-
tier of this 
mechanism is in
-
house quality control at Programme Implementation Unit (PIU) level.  
Objective of this tier is process control through mandator
y tests on material at field 
laboratory and workmanship.  Second
-
tier is structured as an independent quality

regular inspection of works has been envisaged for ensuring 
better quality.  Under the

-

inspection of road works at random, not only to monitor quality but also to provide 
guidance by senior professionals to the field functionaries.

are sent for action to the State Governments and Action Taken Reports (ATRs) are 
monitored at NRRDA.
