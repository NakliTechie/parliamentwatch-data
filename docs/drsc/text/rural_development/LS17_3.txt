Under the erstwhile Integrated Watershed Management Programme (IWMP), 
8214 watershed development projects were sanctioned in 28 States (except Goa) 
during
 
the period 2009
-

-

-

-

& Ganga Rejuvenation and NITI Aayog. The 
protocol 
inter alia
 
envisages
 
to ensure(i) the due completion of unfinished works (if any), 
(ii) maintenance, (iii) security and (iv) sustainability of the watershed development 
projects. It also includes (v)an apt, quick and low
-
cost/cost
-
effective end
-
line evaluation 
of the project 
or a group of projects within the approved cost norm for M&E component. 
Before the projects are formally treated as closed by the Department of land resources, 
the completion and closure protocol has to be duly adopted by the states in respect of 
the proje
cts administratively reported to have been completed.
