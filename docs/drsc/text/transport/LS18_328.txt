CHAPTER-I 
 
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS WHICH HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED BY 
THE MINISTRY 
 
NATIONAL LIBRARY, KOLKATA 
Recommendation/Observation 
1. The Committee fails to comprehend how the National Library could be expected to 
function to its maximum potential when the top -level post of Director-General of the Library 
has been lying vacant for several years on the run. The Committee recommends that the post 
of the Director -General in the National Library should not be kept vacant for such a lon g 
time and steps be taken to fill it up immediately. The Committee strongly recommends that 
urgent action needs to be taken for filling up the human resource requirements of the 
National Library. 
(Para 13)  
 Reply of Ministry  
1.1 The vacancy has been advertised in Employment News for the week 29 th January - 4th 
February, 2022 for filling up the post of DG, NL. All the necessary actions are being initiated 
in this regard. 
 
CENTRAL SECRETARIAT LIBRARY, DELHI 
Recommendation/Observation 
2. The Committee notes that Central Secretariat Library, a premier library  
situated in the heart of the capital, is a big attraction for politicians, bureaucrats and  
scholars. In view of its locational advantage, the Committee is of the view that CSL  
should undertake the r ole of advising decision makers on matters of library information 
services. The Committee also desires that CSL should play a lead role to coordinate with 
national and international agencies, associations, Non -Governmental 
Organizations (NGOs), publishers and other stakeholders for the development of  
library services in the country and for deriving benefit from huge collections of  
Government reports and gazettes. 
(Para 17)  
Reply of Ministry  
 
2.1 This Ministry vide Letter dated 06.04.2022 has directed CSL to take necessary action 
as recommended by PSC.

CHALLENGES 
 Legislation 
Recommendation/Observation 
3. The Committee is, therefore, of the opinion that the Ministry should bring  
pressure on state governments to enact library legislation in the states where it has not been 
enacted covering all aspects like structure of buildings, staff requirement,  
preservation of valuable manuscripts etc. for their proper growth. Libraries in  
Southern States may be emulated in this regard. 
(Para 37)  
Reply of Ministry  
3.1 This Ministry vide D.O Letter dated 13.04.2022 and subsequent reminders dated 
04.07.2022 &  31.08.2022 requested all States/UTs Authorities to take necessary steps for 
implementation of the recommendations of the Committee. Response received in this regard 
from 05 States namely Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tripura, Sikkim and Chandigarh may be seen 
at Annexure- C. 
 Financial Resources 
Recommendation/Observation 
4. The Committee, therefore, recommends that the State Governments may be  
guided to frame rules/amendments in the Act in a manner that money collected for the 
purpose is utilized properly and spent only for the libraries. It should not go to other  
State accounts and be used for other purposes. 
(Para 42)  
Reply of Ministry 
4.1 Information on the money collected and expenditure incurred is being obtained from 
State/ UT Authorities. An interim reply with the data received from 9 States has already 
been provided to the DRPSC (Annexure-A). Other States are also being pursued constantly.

RAJA RAMMOHUN ROY LIBRARY FOUNDATION, KOLKATA (RRRLF) 
(Autonomous Org.) 
Recommendation/Observation 
2. The Committee, therefore, recommends that central government, state  
governments and RRRLF should come forward and take initiative for proper  
development of public library system in India. They should devise a master plan to  
revive India's public libraries to cater to needs of special groups like children,  
differently-abled, elderly people and ethnic minorities and  achieve the goal of  
information for all. 
 
(Para 21)  
 Reply of Ministry  
 
2.1 It is pertinent to mention that Library is a State Subject as per 7th Schedule of the 
Constitution. Therefore, development of public library system comes under the State 
jurisdiction. RRRLF only runs some Matching and Non -Matching schemes to provide 
financial assistance to the Libraries based on the proposals received from the State/ UT 
authorities. However, as recommended by the PSC, RRRLF has been asked to prepare a 
blueprint to take up the matter with State Governments for betterment of the Public Libraries.

DELHI PUBLIC LIBRARY, DELHI (Autonomous Org.) 
Recommendation/Observation 
3. However Committee notes that membership of Delhi Public Library has witnessed 
decrease in recent times, one of the reasons being Covid -19 pandemic. It has come down to 
1.5 lakh membership from what it was prior to corona period i.e. 1.85 lakh. Being a model 
library created in 1951 in Asia with UNESCO’s technical, financial assistance, it sh ould lead 
other libraries in country and extend all possible help in their growth. Delhi Public Library 
should make all out efforts to increase its membership/viewership in the interest of general 
public. 
(Para 23)  
Reply of Ministry  
3.1 Library being a State subject and DPL having jurisdiction within Delhi only, there 
may not be any scope for DPL to reach out to other Libraries in the country.  
 
3.2 The Committee has rightly observed that there has been a perceptible decline in the 
membership of Delhi Public Library (DPL) during last two years. This was mainly due to the 
COVID situation and also absence of reading habit among the society. However, Delhi 
Public Library has been striving to increase its membership by conducting outreach activities, 
approaching schools/ colleges/ RWAs, distributing membership forms and waiving of 
membership fee. Apart from this, the DPL has successfully launched “free membership 
drive” during December, 2021 and this is going on and will be undertaken in the months to 
come. 
 
 
NATIONAL MISSION ON LIBRARIES 
Recommendation/Observation 
4. The Committee is of the view that public libraries have been neglected for a long  
time now and any change would need substantial time and effort from all directions - 
State, community, private sector and non -governmental organisations. The Committee  
recommends that public library system in India should be modified while keeping in  
mind the latest developments in the field of Information and Communication Technology 
(ICT) so as to provide minimum  network based services for the urban and  
rural populace. Libraries should be developed as Community Information Centre  
models and computerization of libraries should be taken up fast to upgrade them. 
(Para 33) 
Reply of Ministry 
4.1 Ministry of Culture has already developed Indian Culture Portal where knowledge 
and cultural resources of various organizations of Ministry of Culture are now available in 
public domain on a single platform. This project is a part of the Prime Minister‟s D igital 

6 
 
India initiative to showcase information about the rich tangible and intangible cultural 
heritage of India both at home and abroad. India is one of the most diverse countries in the 
world. We have a rich cultural heritage but unfortunately we are ju st able to showcase 10 
percent of it to the world. This portal hosts documents, images, audio -video files and other 
data from archives, museums, academies and libraries across the nation. It has a total of about 
2.5 lakh digital artefacts, with metadata. I t also has more than 9 lakh bibliographic entries. 
The content is presented in 19 categories, such as Rare Books, E -Books, Archives, Paintings, 
and Union Catalogues etc.  It also has 9 categories of content, such as Stories, Snippets, 
Historical Cities, and Forts. The portal is currently available in English and Hindi. The portal 
can be accessed through an App called Indian Culture, available on both Android phones and 
iPhone. 
 
CHALLENGES 
 Organizational Structure 
Recommendation/Observation 
5. The Committee feels that there is a need for uniformity in this area. The  
Committee, therefore, recommends that there is an urgent need to set up a Committee to draft 
National Policy on library and information system. A Committee on National policy on 
library and inform ation system was set up on October 7, 1985 under the Chairmanship of 
Prof. D.P. Chattopadhyaya and its Report was submitted on 31 st May, 1986. It is more than 
three decades and the recommendations made then may have outlived its utility. So there is 
an urgent need to look into it. The Committee, therefore, recommends that a Committee for 
drafting a National Policy on Library and Information System may be set up by the Ministry 
of Culture at the earliest. 
(Para 40)  
Reply of Ministry 
5.1 It may be stated that, in the year 1985, a Committee on National Policy on Library 
and Information System (NAPLIS) was formed under the Chairmanship of Prof. D.P. 
Chattopadhyaya. The final report of the Committee was submitted on 30.5.1986. From the 
ToR of  the Committee, it is found that, the document on National Policy on Library and 
Information System was initially prepared by RRRLF and the said Committee, on the basis 
of its own discussion and public reactions, finalized the report.  
 
5.2 The objective o f this policy was to foster, promote and sustain the organizations, 
availability and use of information; to take steps for mobilizing and upgrading the existing 
Library and Information system and services and initiating new programmes relevant to our 
national needs, taking advantage of the latest advances of the technology; and to encourage 
and initiate programmes for the training of library & information personnel. 
 

7 
 
5.3 Apart from the above, in the 1997, an expert group was formed in CSL for 
strengthening of the Library system. Report of this expert committee is enclosed herewith as 
Annexure –B. 
 
5.4 But, it may be kept in mind that all these documents are too old and there have been 
immense technological advancement after that. The objectives of NAPLIS are  fulfilled in 
National Mission on Libraries, especially through NVLI (or ICP), to much extent. However, 
to have uniformity in Library System in PAN India, Central Secretariat Library (CSL) and 
Raja Rammohun Roy Library Foundation (RRRLF) has been directed to frame a blueprint for 
implementation of the said recommendation of Committee and furnish to this Ministry.

 School Education 
Recommendation/Observation 
7. As library is a growing organism, not physically but virtually, new roles, 
challenges and opportunities for libraries are coming up now. The Committee  
recommends that facilities may be provided for online/virtual learning for school  
children. The Ministry should come up with guidelines for large scale digitization of  
various books, journals, manuscripts and many other library resources for catching  
attention of children, especially in the light of Corona virus pandemic. A great  
opportunity exists today to increase the reading habit of general public, academicians,  
students, scholars and researchers in different fields by making various knowledge  
resources available through a single platform of virtual library. Technical expertise to  
make reading resources available with Central Libraries should be shared with the  
state libra ries. If need be, Public -Private Partnership (PPP) model for libraries can be  
explored. 
(Para 46)  
8. The Committee also recommends that in order to keep the interest of school  
children growing, textbooks can be uploaded on the internet. Like the e National 
Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) books, State Council of Educational 
Research and Training (SCERT) books may also be made available on the  
internet. Many school going children are not having textbooks or they cannot afford  
textbooks. Further, copyrights of some of expensive books can be bought from the  
authors and digital version of these textbooks can be uploaded and put on dedicated  
sites. Students from all over the country will benefit from it. 
(Para 47) 
9. By making such res ources available to the citizens of this country in different Indian 
languages, the concept of - One Nation – One Comprehensive learning platform may be 

10 
 
realized, and technologically enhance the user's experience in these Libraries, earlier 
understood in common parlance as footfall in libraries. 
(Para 48) 
 
Reply of Ministry  
 
9.1 The National Digital Library of India, a project under Ministry of Education is already 
in existence. This portal  has objective to collect and collate metadata and provide full text 
index from several national and international digital libraries, as well as other relevant 
sources. It is a digital repository containing textbooks, articles, videos, audio books, lectures, 
simulations, fiction and all other kinds of learning media. T his provides free of cost access to 
many books in the Indian languages and English. Text books of NCERT and 20 other school 
boards, question papers, video lectures, materials for exam preparation, solutions to questions 
of JEE Main and JEE Advanced and several other study materials are available in this portal. 
9.2 Hence, separate action from this Ministry may not be required.

NATIONAL LIBRARY, KOLKATA 
Recommendation/Observation 
1. The Committee finds that there is no proper preservation of books/manuscripts in the 
National library in printed and digital format. The library is also not receiving copies of 
printed as well as digital publications published in the country. The Committee finds it 
extremely important that the Ministry of Culture and National Library, Kolkata come up with 
a mechanism for collection , preservation of material and digital publications and making 
them accessible not only to general public but also to posterity since the objective of the 
National Library being a permane nt depository of all reading material produced in India 
cannot be achieved. It is understood that a project has been submitted to develop Repository 
of Indian publications ensuring almost 100% of delivery under Delivery of books Act,1954 
amended subsequent ly in 1956 and 2005. The project has been approved by Ministry of 
Culture. The Committee would like to know how the Ministry is ensuring this. 
(Para 11)  
Reply of Ministry  
 
1.1 This Ministry is not aware of any such project. Thus, National Library has been 
requested to furnish the details in this regard.  
 
Further Recommendation/Observation 
1.2 The Committee desires to be apprised of the details furnished by the National 
Library with regard to the project to develop Repository of Indian Publications. The 
Committee would also like to know the mechanism presently in place for preservation 
of books/manuscripts in the National Library in both printed and digital formats since 
having a robust mechanism is a prerequisite for meeting the objective of the Nati onal 
Library being a permanent repository of all reading material produced in India. 
 
KHUDA BAKHSH ORIENTAL PUBLIC LIBRARY, PATNA, BIHAR (Autonomous Org.) 
Recommendation/Observation 
2. The Committee noted that there was no regular Director in the library for the period 
2014 to April 2019 and there was also no board in 2018. Board has been reconstituted in the 
year 2020.The Committee is dismayed to note that under the administrative contr ol of 
Ministry of Culture, the said library had been running without a permanent Director for a 
few years which must affect the functioning and decision -making process adversely. The 

14 
 
Committee recommends that Ministry of Culture should shed this apathetic attitude in the 
interest of Libraries and general public. 
(Para 27)

Recommendation/Observation 
4. The Committee recommends that the Ministry may carry out a special exercise

System and Services throughout the country with the active cooperation of the State/ U.T. 
Library Authorities. RRRLF provides financial assistance for development of public libr aries 
through its various Matching and Non -Matching Schemes towards different areas of 
development in public libraries, i.e. modernization, extension of library building, procuring 
furniture etc. This Ministry has written to all the Chief Secretaries / Pri ncipal Secretaries of 
all the State for better participation in these schemes. They have also been requested to take 
up necessary advocacy programmes among the prominent libraries in their respective 
State/U.T. for robust development of public library serv ices. Similar pursuance may be 
continued in future too.

recommendation with respect to Central Libraries in the country. The reply furnished

improvement in the Central Libraries across the country. The Committee therefore 
desires to be apprised in this regard.

Recommendation/Observation 
5. The Committee also recommends tha t public libraries in India should focus on
