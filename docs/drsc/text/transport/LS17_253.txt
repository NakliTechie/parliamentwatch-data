8. The Committee observes that the 12th Five Year Plan outlay for the Ministry of Shipping development

9. At the commencement of the 12th Plan in 2012, the indicative GBS of Ministry of Shipping was

was 3188.92 crore, which is 97.85 % of the total GBS allocations of the Ministry during the 12th plan
period. Thus, the Ministry had utilized almost the complete plan funds provided to it as per the budgetary
allocations. It may be appreciated that Ministry of Finance undertakes the budgetary allocations across

PORTS SECTOR
12. The position with reference to the targets envisaged in the 12th Plan for the Ports Wing is as
under:

(a) Traffic and Capacity Augmentation: The 12thPlan projected total capacity of the port
sector to be 2,289.04 million tonnes (1,229.24 MT - Major Ports and 1,059.80 MT.- non-
Major Ports) and over all traffic of the ports to be 1,758.26 MT (943.06 MT. - Major Ports
and 815.20 MT. -Non Major Ports) by 2016-17. The total installed capacity of the Indian
Ports at the end of the 12th Plan was 1897.11 MT (1065.83MT -Major Ports, 831.28 MT
- Non Major Ports) as on 31st March 2017 compared to 1245.30 MT (696.53 MT Major
Ports, 548.77 MT Non Major Ports) at the end of 11th plan. The traffic handled by the
Ports at the end of the 12th Plan has been 1135.76 MT (647.76 MT Major Ports, 488 MT
Non Major Ports) as on 31st March 2017 compared to 913.32 MT (560.14 MT Major
Ports, 353.18 MT Non Major Ports) at the end of the 11th plan. The year wise details

(b) Dredging: The 12th plan envisaged to increase the drafts to at least 14 meters in all ports
by the end of 12th plan period and achieve 17 meters in Hub-Ports. 10 of the Major Ports
have already achieved a draft of 14 meters or more.
(c) Productivity and Dwell Time: As emphasized in the 12th Plan, successful attempts have
already been initiated for closely working with Customs Department to reduce the dwell
time for containers in Ports and CFSs. Also, Direct Port Delivery facility, under the accredited
client programme has been extended by JNPT.
(d) Containerization and Hinterland Connectivity.: The share of Containerized traffic during
2016-17 was 19.23%. The container handling capacity of Major Ports increased from
138.88 Million Tonnes during 2011-12 to 183.81 Million Tonnes during 2016-17 with a
CAGR of 5.92%. Due focus was given in Twelfth Plan for increasing the share of
containerized cargo in ports with a view to capturing a higher share of international trade.
A number of projects for rail/road connectivity were taken up and monitored closely both
for Major and non-Major Ports.
(e) Private Sector Participation: In the 12th Plan, the need for working out PPP model for
infrastructure Port development Project was stressed. Active steps have been taken to fully
utilize the potential, resources and professionalism of the private sector. These include
standardization of biding documents, transparent bidding process for selection of PPP

4
operators etc. Further, 100% FDI is permitted under automatic route in the port sector.
These steps have resulted in several port PPP projects coming up in the 12 Major Ports.
At present, a total of 38 PPP projects are operational and 20 more PPP projects are under
implementation/construction.
(f) Development of Non Major Ports: The share of capacity creation at the Indian Ports is
constantly increasing. It has increased from 39% during 2011-12 to 43% during 2016-17.
The increase is due to number of private ports coming up particularly in the States of
Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra etc.
(g) Inland Water Transport Sector : The 12th Plan had stipulated increase in modal share
from 0.5% to 1-1.5% and increase in BTKM from 5 BTKM to 15 BTKM. During 2012-
13 to 2016-17, there has been a continuous increase in the volume of traffic carried through
the IWT mode. It has increased from 23.68 Millions Tones in 2012-13 to 55.20 Million
Tones in 2016-17. Similarly, in terms of BTKM also IWT has registered an upward trend
from 2012 onwards. It has doubled in the five year period to reach 5.063 BTKM in 2016-
17, compared to 2.488 BTKM in 2012-13.
13. IWAI has undertaken various projects /initiatives, which will substantially increase the volume of
cargo movement through IWT and its modal share in the coming years.
