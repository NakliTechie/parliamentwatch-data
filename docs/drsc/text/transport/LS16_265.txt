ROAD DEVELOPMENT
Recommendation/Observation
9. The Committee recommends that the Ministry should take initiative to encourage private
participation in various road projects. (Para 30)
Reply of the Ministry
9.1 Private investment in road infrastructure through PPP route was on decline because the
traditional Build Operate Transfer (BOT) model had high traffic and capital risk for the private
player which made it unattractive. As a result, the Government has implemented the Hybrid Annuity
Model (HAM) wherein 40% of the upfront investment is borne by the Government and the private
partner gets guaranteed annuities over a 15 year period. Toll is collected by the Government which
thus takes over the entire traffic risk. Government, on the other hand is able to stagger its capital
investment over a longer 15 year period as opposed to the traditional EPC project. The Ministry/
NHAI has been awarding projects under Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM)/Built-operate-Transfer (BOT)
and has awarded 15% length in 2015-16; 17% length in 2016-17; 21% length in 2017-18 under
HAM/BOT mode out of the total awarded length.
9.2 NHAI has also launched Masala Bonds which is rupee denominated bonds issued in a foreign
country. These have been able to attract foreign investment and address currency fluctuation risk
which otherwise required additional hedging costs.
9.3 Further, as already mentioned in reply to para 15 ante, CCEA approved Toll, Operate and
Transfer (TOT) Model in August, 2016 for monetisation of developed NH stretches; it is targeted
to raise funds to the tune of about ` 34,000 crore under TOT concessions upto 2021-22. Target
of ` 20,000 crore has been set for realization of funds under TOT during 2018-19; this includes
first bu ndle of TOT concessions awarded for 9 nos. of NH stretches of 680 km. length for

10
Bid Concession Fee of ` 9,681.50 crore, for which fund is expected to be realised during
2018-19.
Further Recommendation/Observation
9.4 The Committee is happy to note that the share of private participation in road projects
is steadily increasing every year. The Committee desires that the Ministry continues to find
ways to encourage such participation in the future as this mode of investment is very
important for the creation and maintenance of road projects in a vast country like India.
Recommendation/Observation
10. The Committee recommends that issues regarding land acquisition and Environment/ Forest/
Wildlife Clearance should be planned well in advance to avoid delay if taken up at a later stage
as one cannot predict the minor details involved in each project. (Para 34)
Reply of the Ministry
10.1 The Projects are awarded after adequate progress is achieved in terms of Land Acquisition,
obtaining statutory clearances, etc. The process for which is initiated after finalisation of alignment
and submission of final feasibility report by the Consultant.
10.2 The process of land acquisition and securing clearances are initiated during the process of
project preparation. The processes have been streamlined to a great extent. Regarding Environment
and Forest Clearances, MoEF&CC has delegated powers to their Regional Offices to cut short the
delay. Also, permission for felling of the tree is given by the forest department after Stage-I
clearance only without waiting for Stage-II clearance subject to further deposition of amount of
Net Present V alue (NPV) and Compensatory Afforestation (CA) as decided by the Forest Department.
10.3 Multiple initiatives have been identified and undertaken to streamline and expedite land acquisition
process and simplify procedures. Firstly, the entire process of preparation and issuance of land
acquisition notifications has been moved to an online system known as Bhoomi Rashi. Under this,
the draft notification is prepared by the CALA (Competent Authority Land Acquisition) and the
Project Director (PD) and all the approvals, at regional level and at central level such as at NHAI
headquarters or at Ministry, are obtained through the online workflows. This has ensured swift
processing, eliminated discrepancies such as those between multiple versions of notification, and
has ensured transparency of where approvals are pending. It has thus been possible to reduce the
entire process of preparation and approval from an earlier approximate 3 months to 7-10 days.
10.4 Further, to expedite land acquisition at the end of CALA, DPR consultant has now been made
responsible for assisting in preparation and approval of land acquisition notifications, including
providing necessary support to CALA to assist in on-ground surveys.

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways initiated mega road development programme
in North East known as ¯Special accelerated Road Development Programme in North East (SARDP-
NE)  and Arunachal Pradesh Package. The scope of the programme has been enlarged from time
to time, since September, 2005. As on date Government has given approval for 2/4 laning of 6,418
km of various categories of roads under Phase ‘A’ and Arunachal Package of SARDP-NE in entire
North East at an estimated investment of about ` 33,750 crore. Apart from the above Government
has also given approval to preparation of detailed project report for 3,723 km of roads under
Phase ‘B’.
2. Major objective of the Schemes are:-
 Upgrade National Highways connecting State Capitals to 2/4 lane
 To provide connectivity of all 88 District Headquarter towns of NER by at least 2-lane
road;
 Improve roads of strategic importance in border area.
 Improve connectivity to neighboring countries
 Total 4,099 km under SARDP-NE Phase-A, 2,319km under Arunachal Package has been
proposed for improvement.
 3,723 km under SARDP-NE Phase-B has been approved for DPR preparation.

Projects in total length of about 5,345km length has been sanctioned under SARDP-NE. Out
of this 2,754 km length has been completed so far.

Arunachal Pradesh and Assam, has been awarded on Hybrid BOT (Annuity) mode consisting of 4
packages. 3 works have been completed/substantially completed ( viz. Bridge across Dibang river,
Dhola Sadia Bridge and 2L Nechipu – Hoj). Implementation of one work, viz. Potin – Pangin has
been delayed.
