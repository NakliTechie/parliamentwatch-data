Assessment of the Demands for Grants (2015-16) of the Ministry 
 
RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS OF THE COMMITTEE 
5.     The Committee notes that the present Budget Estima tes shows an increase of just 
Rs.10 crores in comparison to the Revised Estimate last year i.e.,  (2014-15). The Committee 
is also surprised to note that the Ministry had to face a sharp budgetary cut at RE stage in the 
last financial year. The Committee failed to get a convincing reason for this cut. Given the 
importance of the mandate of this Ministry i.e,  to preserve, protect and promote our vast 
cultural heritage, both tangible and intangible, sp read across the length and breadth of the 
country, the Committee is at a loss to see that Pla n allocation to this Ministry has come down 
from Rs.1835 crore in BE 2014-15 to Rs.1500 crore i n RE 2014-15 and to Rs.1455 crore in 
BE of 2015-16. Such allocation are highly insuffici ent and the Committee recommends that 
Plan allocation for the Department be increased adequately at RE stage.       
          (Recommendation at Para No.13 of the 219 
th  Report) 

REPLY OF THE GOVERNMENT 
6.  As against total budgetary provision of Rs. 251 1.00 crore at BE 2014-15 which was 
reduced to Rs. 2159.00 crore at RE 2014-15, the bud get for 2015-16 for Ministry of Culture 
has been pegged at Rs. 2169.00 crore consisting of Rs. 1455.00 crore for Plan and Rs. 714.00 
crore under Non-Plan Heads. This budget is almost equal or about 0.46% more than budgetary 
provisions at RE 2014-15. Ministry of Finance has n ot intimated any reason for the reduction 
in Annual Plan allocation at RE stage during 2014-1 5. However, the Ministry will make 
efforts to seek an additional allocation at RE stage during the current financial year 2015-16.

17. The Committee is at a loss to note that allocat ions had been reduced in almost all 
sectors, which has adversely impacted Archaeology, Museums, Libraries and other important

financial year, with the Ministry of Finance.
