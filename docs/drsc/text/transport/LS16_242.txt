RECOMMENDATION/OBSERVATION OF THE COMMITTEE  
7. The Committee notes from this table two trends –  (i) there has been only 
marginal increments in the GBS to the Ministry ever y year and (ii) this GBS has been 
invariably reduced at RE stage every year. Taking a  realistic view, if expenditure is 
taken in terms of RE allocation, its percentage has  been satisfactory and as per the 
financial norms. It means that Ministry has shown its capability to spend the finds given 
to it every year. The Committee, however, fails to understand, how the Ministry of 
Finance could with such a good expenditure, allocat e funds to Ministry of Shipping 
with a marginal increase every year. In other words , may be, if it is given more funds 
which shipping sector needs badly, the Ministry cou ld have fruitfully used it. The 
Committee in view of the potential and capabilities  of Shipping sector in our economy 
in days to come, recommends that Ministry of Financ e must increase GBS to Ministry 
of Shipping every year substantially.  
 (Recommendation at Para No. 7 of 235 th  Report)
