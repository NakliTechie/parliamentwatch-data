The Communication Security (ComSec) scheme has been

i
.
 
SCOPE:
 
 
a
.
 
   
The sc
ope of certification shall cover all types of telecom

.
 
NCCS shall be responsible for developin
g and for release of 
(Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements (ITSARs)

c
.
 
   
The use of certified equipment, unless specifically

d
.
 
If the equipment is being imported for Research and

prior security certification may 
be exempted for limited numbers of equipment.
 
 
ii.  SCHEME
 
 
a
.
 
Department of Telecommunications aims to meet the 
following objectives in developing, operating and 
maintaining the ComSec scheme:
 
 

9
 
 

 
To develop country specific st
andards, processes and


 
To develop testing and certification eco
-





b
.
 
The

Requirements (ITSAR), Designation of Telecom Security

c
.
 
The I
TSAR shall be prepared based on country specific

d
.
 
The security testing against ITSAR shall be

e
.
 
Applicants intending to get their equipment certified will

evaluation, security certificate will be issued.
