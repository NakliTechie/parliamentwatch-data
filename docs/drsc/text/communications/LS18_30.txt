POSTAL OPERATIONS – Rural Business and Access to Postal Network 
    
(Recommendation Sl. No. 3) 
5. The Committee, in their Original Report, made the following  
Observation/Recommendation:– 
 “The Committee observe that the sub -scheme on Rural Business and Access to 
Postal Network under the Central Sector Scheme on Postal Operations was 
envisaged to comprehensively modernize and strengthen the rural postal network 
with the aim of providing equitable access to postal, financial and citizen -centric 
services, particularly in rural, hilly, tribal and underserved areas. The Committee 
further note that against a proposed allocation of Rs. 18.96 crore, the sub -scheme 
was allotted Rs. 15.00 crore at the BE Stage which was further reduced to Rs. 
14.96 crore at the RE Stage during financial year 2025 -26. Furthermore, during 
financial year 2025 -26, up to November 2025, the actual expenditure under this 
sub-scheme amounts to Rs. 9.36 crore which is about 62.56% of the RE Stage 
allocation during FY 2025 -26. However, for the same duration, the cumulative 
figures for physical targets achieved against those allotted amount to 65.96% i.e. 
Rs.13968.00 crore out of Rs. 21175.00 crore. The Committee note that target 
achievement for the activity on ‘Opening of Branch Post Offices (BOs) / Sub -Post 
Offices (SOs) by redeployment/relocation and improving rural SO by providing 
infra upgrade’ amounts to 23.30% i.e. 48 out of 206, for the scheme duration upto 
November 2025. The Committee also note that since there were no major 
impediments in the implementation of the sub -scheme, achievement of physical 
targets can be made more satisfactory. The Department may furnish the reasons 
for shortfall in achieving the physical targets set for financial year 2025-26. 
The Committee further note that against a proposed amount of Rs. 300.97 crore 
for the sub -scheme during financial year 2026 -27, the Department was allocated 
Rs. 60.00 crore at the BE Stage for that year. The Committee are also of the view 
that the Department would need to align the targets and implementation plans 

3 
 
under the sub-scheme in accordance with the budget cut for the financial 2026-27. 
Furthermore, the Committee observe that achievement of physical targets set 
under the sub -scheme for financial year 2026 -27 is expected to augment 
digitization of postal transactions for customers with an anticipated rise of almost 
10% in the number of postal transactions over the previous financial year among 
the post offices strengthened under this sub -scheme. Accordingly, the Committee 
feel that this sub -scheme not only positions the postal network as a platform for 
governance, commerce and inclusive growth but also bridges service gaps in rural 
India and reinforces the Universal Service Obligation of India Post. Therefore, the 
Committee recommend the Department to ensure that physical targets set under 
this sub-scheme for financial years 2025 -26 and 2026 -27 are fully achieved and 
do not necessitate curtailing of budget allocations at the RE Stage during financial 
year 2026-27. The Committee would like to be apprised of the progress made with 
respect to physical targets and the subsequent outcome achieved in this regard.” 
6. In their Action Taken Reply, the Department of Posts (Ministry of 
Communications) submitted as under:– 
“To monitor the performance of the sub scheme, "Rural Business and Access to 
Postal Network" during FY 2025-26, advisories were issued to all Circles. Further, 
the performance indicators under the scheme are reviewed on a quarterly and 
basis and updated on Output-Outcome Monitoring Framework (OOMF) dashboard 
of NITI Aayog.  
 
The Quarter-wise/ Annual OOMF report for FY 2025 -26 is enclosed as Annexure 
I.”

7. The Committee, in their Report recommend the Department to ensure that 
physical targets set under the sub -scheme Rural Business and Access to Postal 
Network for financial years 2025 -26 and 2026 -27 are fully achieved and do not 
necessitate curtailing of budget allocations at the RE Stage during financial year 
2026-27. The Committee also desired to be apprised of the progress made with 
respect to physical targets and the subsequent outcome achieved in this regard. 
The Department, in its reply stated that to monitor the performance of the 
sub scheme ‘Rural Business and Access to Postal Network’ during FY 2025 -26, 
advisories were issued to all Circles. Further, the performance indicators under 
the scheme are reviewed on a quarterly basis and updated on Output -Outcome 
Monitoring Framework (OOMF) dashboard of NITI Aayog. In the Action Taken 
reply, the Department has also annexed (i) Quarter -wise/ Annual OOMF report for 
FY 2025-26; and (ii) Output indicators with targets achievement details for         Q4 
2025-26 and FY 2025-26.  
The Committee note that in the ‘Augmentation in digitization of Postal 
Transactions for the convenience of Customers’, the Department has achieved 
19.49% increase against a target of 10% in the ‘number of transactions in Branch 
Post Offices’ with reference to the previous financial year. Similarly, the 
Department has exceeded the targets for FY 2025 -26 in respect of ‘Providing 
infrastructure to BOs (Branch Post Offices)’ and ‘Installation of letter boxes in 
rural post offices’. The Committee note that the achievement under the ‘Number of 
Rural Sub Post Offices and Branch Post Offices opened by way of relocation/ 
redeployment’ and ‘Installation of signages in rural post offices’ were less than the 
target set for FY 2025-26. The Committee emphasize that the opening of Rural Sub 
Post Offices and Branch Post Offices by relocation/ redeployment is vital as it may 
optimize resources by shifting services from over -served or high rent urban 
spaces to underserved, high -growth rural areas. This targeted strategy promotes 
universal accessibility, extends the reach of government financial inclusion and 
supports e -commerce and logistics. As regards installation of signage in Rural 
Branch Post Offices, the Committee understand that installing prominent signage 

6 
 
in Rural Branch Post Offices establishes brand visibility, builds public trust and 
ensures last -mile accessibility for G overnment welfare schemes and financial 
services thereby increasing business of Rural Branch Post Offices . The 
Committee, therefore, are of the view that the Department may try to mitigate any 
possible infrastructural hurdles for installation of signage in Rural Branch Post 
Offices. The Committee may be apprised of the action taken in this regard. 
 
POSTAL OPERATIONS – Mail and Parcel Network Optimization and International 
Relations 
(Recommendation Sl. No. 4) 
 
8. The Committee, in their Original Report, made the following  
Observation/Recommendation:– 
“The Committee note that the sub -scheme on Mail and Parcel Network 
Optimization under the Central Sector Scheme on Postal Operations was 
envisaged to streamline mail processing through hubs and vehicle fleets while 
introducing performance monitoring mechanisms. The sub -scheme focuses on 
standardizing operations; closing infrastructure gaps; IT upgradation of 
infrastructure; Road Transport Network (RTN) expansion and revamping of letter 
boxes as 24x7 digital collection points. The Committee further note that against a 
proposed allocation of Rs. 145.80 crore the sub -scheme was allotted Rs. 130.34 
crore at the BE Stage which was increased to Rs. 169.18 crore at the RE Stage 
during financial year 2025 -26. However, during financial year 2025 -26, up to 
November 2025, the actual expenditure under this sub -scheme was Rs. 62.86 
crore only which is about 43.84% of the RE Stage allocation during financial year 
2025-26. Reasons for such under-utilization may be apprised to the Committee. At 
the same time, the Committee are pleased to note that there was no shortfall 
observed in achieving the physical targets set during financial year 2025-26. 
Against a proposed amount of Rs. 188.18 crore for the sub -scheme during 
financial year 2026 -27, the Department was allocated Rs. 178.12 crore at the BE 
Stage for that year. The Committee note that the Department would need to align 
the targets and implementation plans under the sub -scheme in accordance with 
the budget cut for financial year 2026 -27. Furthermore, the Committee observe 
that achievement of physical targets set under the sub-scheme for Mail and Parcel 
Network Optimization and International Relations for financial year 2026 -27 is 
expected to strengthen articles handling capacity with an anticipated increase of 
almost 2% in the articles delivered on D+0 (i.e. delivered on the same day as their 

7 
 
receipt) from delivery offices; 15% increase in parcel handling capacity; 10% 
increase in the number of PPU (Parcel Packaging Units) transactions; 10% 
increase each in booking of shipments through online mode (Dak Niryat Kendras) 
value booked through online mode in comparison with the previous financial year. 
The Committee also observe that an increase in the delivery of articles on D+0 
from delivery offices indicates enhanced operational efficiency, enabling articles to 
be sorted out, processed and delivered on the same day. 
Taking cognizance of the enormity of the impact of this sub -scheme on the postal 
sector as well as on the national economy, the Committee call upon the 
Department to ensure that funds allotted towards the sub -scheme are optimally 
utilized and physical targets set for financial year 2026-27 are achieved in entirety. 
The Committee would like to be apprised of the progress made with respect to 
physical targets and the subsequent outcome achieved in this regard.” 
9. In their Action Taken Reply, the Department of Posts (Ministry of 
Communications)  submitted as under:– 
“i. Fund Utilization under the scheme are monitored continuously, and 
accordingly, directions are issued to the Circles from time to time for effective 
utilization of funds as per fund utilization plan. During FY 2025 -26, 99.6% of the 
fund allocated to the circles under the MNOP Scheme were utilized and all the 
physical targets were achieved. For FY 2026-27, the proposed new scheme is yet 
to be approved by the Ministry of Finance. However, all circles have already been 
instructed to prepare their work plans in alignment with the activities envisaged 
under the proposed scheme so as to ensure its prompt implementation 
immediately upon approval. 
ii. Efforts are made to improve article handling at delivery offices through Mail 
Monitoring Units (MMUs) at various levels. However, achieving the anticipated 
increase of 2% in articles delivered on D+0 requires upgradation  of delivery 
offices and is expected to be achieved after the approval of the proposed new 
activity by Expenditure Finance Committee. 
iii. It is informed that, with respect to the scheme components handled by the 
Parcel Directorate during FY 2025 -26, the Budget Estimates (BE) were fixed 
at₹91.34 crore, which were subsequently increased to ₹ 100.74 crore at  the 
Revised Estimates (RE) stage and it was ₹100.70 crore at the stage of Final 
Grant. Although, up to November 2025, the actual expenditure recorded under the 
Parcel Network Optimization Project (PNOP) was ₹ 50.07 crore, the total 

8 
 
utilization at the stage of Final Grant amounted to₹ 98.84 crore. Thus, utilization 
under the scheme components handled by Parcel Directorate was 98.15%. 
iv. In line with the co mmitment to achieve a 10% year -on-year increase in the 
booking of shipments and export value through the online mode under Dak Niryat 
Kendras, Department  shall make all possible efforts to ensure sustained growth 
through the online platform as compared to the previous financial year.” 
 
10. The Committee in their Report while taking note of the importance of the 
sub-scheme on Mail and Parcel Network Optimization, had called upon the 
Department to ensure that funds allotted towards the sub -scheme are optimally 
utilized and physical targets set for financial year 2026 -27 may be fully achieved. 
The Committee also desired to be apprised of the progress made with respect to 
physical targets and the subsequent outcome achieved in this regard. 
The Department, in its reply stated that Fund Utilization under the scheme 
are continuously monitored as per fund utilization plan and 99.6% of the fund 
allocated to the circles under the MNOP Scheme during FY 2025 -26 were utilized 
and all physical targets were achieved. The Department stated that for                FY 
2026-27, the proposed new scheme is yet to be approved by the Ministry of 
Finance. With respect to the scheme components handled by the Parcel 
Directorate, it is stated that during FY 2025 -26, utilization under the scheme 
components handled by Parcel Directorate was 98.15%. The Committee are 
apprised that with a commitment to achieve a 10% year -on-year increase in the 
booking of shipments and export value through online mode under Dak Niryat 
Kendras, the Department shall make all possible efforts through online platform to 
ensure sustained growth as compared to previous financial year.  
While appreciating the earnest effort of the Department in optimal utilization 
of fund, t he Committee would like to be apprised of the progress made by the 
Department to ensure year -on-year increase in the booking of shipments and 
export value through online mode to ensure accelerated growth.   
 
HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT 
 
(Recommendation Sl. No. 8) 
 
11. The Committee, in their Original Report, made the following  
Observation/Recommendation:– 
“The Committee note that the Scheme on Human Resource Management aims for 

9 
 
training of manpower and upkeep of training institutions. The Committee 
understand that against a proposed allocation of Rs. 38.87 crore the Scheme was 
allocated Rs. 38.87 crore at the BE Stage which was reduced to Rs. 34.97 crore 
at the RE Stage during financial year 2025 -26. Up to November 2025, during 
financial year 2025 -26, the actual expenditure under the Human Resource 
Management Scheme amounts to Rs. 17.32 crore which is about 49.52% of the 
RE Stage allocation during FY 2025 -26. The Committee note that while less than 
50% of the allocated funds have been utilized until the third quarter of the financial 
year, the Department has shown significant accomplishment of targets set under 
the Scheme during financial year 2025 -26, while facing no major impediments 
during implementation. 
Against the proposed amount of Rs. 64.99 crore during financial year 2026-27, the 
Department was allocated only Rs. 35.00 crore for the Scheme at the BE Stage. 
The Committee desire that the Department should take initiative to align physical 
targets and implementation plans under the Scheme in accordance with the 
budget cut during financial year 2026 -27. Achievement of physical targets set 
under the Human Resource Management Scheme during financial year 2026 -27 
is expected to upgrade training facilities for postal workers, focus on technological 
integration and holistic skill enhancement while conducting 3500 training 
programmes to train 1.25 lakh officials. Taking cognizance of the vast human 
capital of about 4.74 lakh people employed within the postal sector, the 
Committee are of the opinion that upskilling and upscaling of available human 
resource ought to be a priority of the Department of Posts. The Committee, 
therefore, recommend that the Department work towards focused implementation 
of the Scheme on Human Resource Management along with ensuring complete 
utilization of allocated funds during the upcoming financial year. The Committee 
would like to be apprised of progress made in this direction.”

Communications)  submitted as under:– 
“The observations of the Committee regarding fund utilization and alignment of 
physical targets under the Human Resource Management (HRM) Scheme have 
been noted.  It is submitted that implementation of the Scheme during FY 2025-26 
progressed as planned, and the major physical targets were achieved without any 
impediments.

ESTATES MANAGEMENT  
 
(Recommendation Sl. No. 9) 
 
14. The Committee, in their Original Report, made the following  
Observation/Recommendation:– 

12 
 
“The Committee note that the objectives under the Scheme on Estates 
Management include development of appropriate infrastructure, providing a 
congenial work environment and improvement of the delivery of postal services in 
a cost -effective way. The Committee further note that against a proposed 
allocation of Rs. 145.50 crore the Scheme was allotted Rs. 120.50 crore at the BE 
Stage which was increased to Rs. 124.66 crore at the RE Stage during financial

ii. Sale & Recharge of BSNL SIM Cards 
 
Department of Posts and Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL) have signed a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 17.09.2025, in New Delhi to expand BSNL’s 
mobile connectivity reach across India. 
 
Under this agreement, DoP will leverage its unparalleled postal network of over 1.64 lakh 
Post Offices for sale of BSNL SIM cards and mobile recharge services across the 
country. Through this collaboration, post offices will act as Points of Sale (PoS) for 
BSNL, Mobile SIM sale and for mobile recharges. BSNL will provide SIM stock and 
training, while DoP will onboard new customers for BSNL and facilitate transactions in a 
standardized and secure manner.

This initiative aims to make BSNL's telecom services more accessible and affordable, 
particularly for citizens in the remotest parts of the country who often struggle with limited 
connectivity. By enabling Post Offices as service points, the partnership seeks to bridge 
the digital divide, empower rural households with mobile services, and advance the 
broader goals of Digital India, financial inclusion, and socio -economic development. The 
proof of concept was implemented in Assam, where it has already shown significant 
success, demonstrating its potential as a scalable model for nationwide rollout. 
iii. Contact Point Verification of Informal Micro Enterprises 
On 31.12.2025, the Department of Posts (DoP), Ministry of Communications, 
Government of India, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Small 
Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) to undertake Contact Point Verification 
(CPV) for credit underwriting process on credit marketplace for Informal Micro 
Enterprises (IMEs) registered on the Udyam Assist Platform (UAP). The agreement 
establishes a formal institutional framework for collaboration between the two 
organizations to support verification, data integrity and credit enablement initiatives for 
micro enterprises. 
Under this MoU, the Department of Posts will leverage its extensive postal network of 
over 1.64 lakh post offices and trained field workforce to conduct on -ground Contact 
Point Verification of enterprises registered on the Udyam Assist Portal. The verification 
process will include confirmation of demographic details, business particulars and 
location-based validation, supported by geo -tagged evidence, in accordance with the 
CPV framework prescribed by SIDBI. 
This partnership marks an important step in leveraging India Post’s citizen-centric service 
delivery capabilities to support national priorities such as MSME development, financial 
inclusion and formalization of the informal economy.
