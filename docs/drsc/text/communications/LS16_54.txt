OBSERVATIONS/RECOMMENDATIONS WHICH HAVE BEEN
 
ACCEPTED BY THE GOVERNMENT
 
 
(Recommendation Sl. No.1)
 
 
 
The 
Committee observe that Song and Drama Division (S&DD), one of the media 
units
 
of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) was set up in 1954 with a 
wide mandate to propagate various Policies, Programmes and Schemes of the 
Government. They are engaged in interpersonal communication through live media 
utilizing various rang
e of performing arts such as Drama, Dance
-
Drama, Composite 
programme, Puppetry, Ballets, Operas, Folk and Traditional Recitals, Mythological 
Recital, Magic and other Local Folk & Traditional Forms with their network of 10 
Regional Centres and 9 Sub
-
Centres
 
across the country. The Committee also not that 
the programmes conducted by S&DD are mainly to disseminate information and 
generate awareness on Governments’ flagship schemes and thereby bring about 
behavioural changes among the masses. The Committee howe
ver, find the Division 
lacking in fulfilment of its objectives due to various constraints being faced by them 
which are broadly discussed in the succeeding paragraphs.
 
 
 
The Ministry has itself admitted that with the meagre budget allocations, it is no 
possible to fulfil the mandate given to the Division. Besides, due to inadequate staff 
strength, the Division is unable to effectively disseminate information to the entire 
country. The Committee feel that the people at the grass root level should not be k
ept 
deprived of the huge benefits that could accrue to them from the Government Policies, 
Schemes and Programmes meant for social welfare simply because of the lack of 
awareness to them due to ineffective functioning of this media unit. The Committee 
would
 
therefore strongly recommend the Ministry to devise all possible ways and 
means to reinvent this Division so that it plays a more meaningful role for bringing 
timely awareness to the public at large.
 
 
Reply of the Government
 
 
The Govt. of India, M/o I&B 
integrated its three media units namely, Directorate 
of Audio Visual Publicity (DAVP), Directorate of Field Publicity (DFP) and Song & 
Drama Division into a single entity named Bureau of Outreach and Communication 
(BOC) vide order number P
-
11011/4/2012
-
PPC
      
dated 8.12.2017.      The 
headquarters of BOC is in Delhi.   BOC will function through 23 Regional Outreach 
Bureaus (ROBs) at regional level and 148 Field Outreach Bureaus (FOBs) at field level.
 
Each Outreach Bureau combines and synergises the erstwh
ile functionalities of the 
three Media Units viz DAVP, DFP and S&DD.
 
 
 
BOC will have an integrated communication approach synergising the activities 
of DAVP, DFP and S&DD for better impact and wider outreach. Integration of the above 
Media Units is aimed a
t ensuring effective communication campaigns
 
across the 
country
.
