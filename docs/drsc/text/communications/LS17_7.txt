Subsequently, General Post Offices

Presidencies of Kolkata (177
4), Chennai (1786)

followed by the more comprehensive Indian Post
 
Office Act of 
1854. The Act reformed the entire
 
fabric
 
of the p
ostal system and its provisions 
granted the monopoly of carrying mail in the
 
British territories in India to the Indian 
Post
 
Offices. The present postal system in India
 
thus came into existence with the 
Indian Post
 
Office Act of 1854. In the same 
year, Railway
 
Mail Service was introduced 
as also the Sea
 
Mail Service from India to Great Britain and
 
China. Thereafter, the 
I
ndia Post Office Act of
 
1898 was pa

country.
