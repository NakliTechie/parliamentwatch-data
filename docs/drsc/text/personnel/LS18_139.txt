R E P O R T 
 This Action Taken Report of the Committee deals with the action taken by the Legis-
lative Department (Ministry of Law and Justice ) on the recommendations / observations of 
the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Personnel, Public Grievances, 
Law and Justice in its One Hundred Thirtieth (130th) Report on "Demands for Grants (202 3-
24)", which was presented to the Rajya Sabha on 15th March, 2023 and laid on the Table of 
the Lok Sabha on the 20th March, 2023. 
2. The Committee had made 16 recommendations/observations in its 130th Report. Re-
plies of the Department thereto have been categorized and scrutinized under three chapters on 
the basis of satisfaction of the Committee. These replies have been categorized as follows: 
Chapter – I:  Recommendations/observations (6) which have been accepte d by the D e-
partment, i.e. in respect of para s 2.22, 2.41, 3.5, 3.6, 3.7 and 3.14  of the 
Report. The Committee is pleased to note that the Department has a c-
cepted those recommendations. 
Chapter – II:  Recommendations/observations (3) which the Committee does not desire 
to pursue in view of the replies given by the Department, i.e. in respect of 
paras 2.19, 2.38 and 2.42  of the Report. The Committee is satisfied with 
the explanations advanced by the Department and does not wish to pur sue 
those as of now.  
Chapter – III:  Recommendations/observations (7) in respect of which Committee has not 
accepted the replies of the Department, i.e. with regard to paras 2.17, 2.18, 
2.27, 2.34, 2.36, 2.35 and 3.11  of the Report. The Committee does not  ac-
cept the reasons given by the Department and reiterates the same. 
3. The Committee desires that the Department should furnish pointed and detailed A c-
tion Taken Notes in respect of all the further Recommendations/Observations made by the 
Committee in this report. 
4. The details of the ATR have been discussed in the respective Chapters in the succee d-
ing pages. For reference purpose, para numbers of the original report (i.e. of 1 30th Report) 
have been mentioned at the end of each recommendation/observation.

Recommendation/Observation 
 
1.0 The Committee observes that allocations for „Elections (Reimbursement to State/UTs)‟ 
has been drastically reduced in BE (2023 -24) over the projections made to the Ministry of 
Finance, by about 90% under the head „Elections (Reimbursement to State/UTs)‟. The Co m-
mittee is not aware about the reasons for this maj or reduction. The Committee feels it may be 
due to inaccurate projection or inflation of funds required which is a failure of the Depar t-
ment/ECI, hence this huge reduction. The Committee is therefore of the view that while ma k-
ing projections to the Ministr y of Finance in future, the Department should ensure that its 
projections are rational and appropriate to meet its requirements. (Para 2.22) 
 
Action Taken 
 
1.1 Legislative Department is concerned with the Elections related expenditure including 
cases of settlement of Election Accounts of all States/UTs of India, which contains following 
steps: 
• For the purpose, Governments of States/UTs concerned, forward the valid Audit Ce r-
tificates obtained from the Accountant General (AG) offices concerned of the Stat e Govern-
ment, to Legislative Department, conveying the election expenditure incurred by that 
State/UT in a particular financial year, under the Major Head 2015- Elections.  
• Subsequently, all the records/ Audit Certificates are thoroughly scrutinized in t he De-
partment and the dues of Government of India‟ share for that particular financial year is se t-
tled and conveyed to the Government of State/UT concerned, along with the Election Co m-
mission of India. 
• Based on the settlement of dues of Government of Ind ia‟ share for a financial year in 
respect of States/UTs from where Audit Certificates have been received, projections of total 
dues is made at in R.E stage to Ministry of Finance (MOF).  
• After receipt of the amount in R.E. stage, the same is distributed among the State/UT 
concerned. 
1.2 On the basis of above procedure, in the year 2022 -23, Election accounts of 11 States 
(viz. Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Mah a-
rashtra, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tamil Nadu and West Be ngal) have been settled till 27.02.2022 
on the basis of Audit Certificates received from their respective AG Offices. Accordingly, a 
demand of Rs.3166.08 Crore was made in the second and final batch supplementary from 
Ministry of Finance. Subsequently, an amount of Rs.3166.08 crore has been received for 
Major Head-2015 (Elections) in the Second and Final Batch Supplementary.  Thereafter, 
the amount of Rs. 3166.08 crore has been released to the aforesaid 11 State Governments.

1.3 On the basis of above patte rn, projection for BE (2023 -24) was made to the Ministry 
of Finance under the Major Head-2015- „Elections (Reimbursement to State/UTs)‟. However, 
the amount has been drastically reduced by the Ministry of Finance. 
1.4 Further, as recommended by the Committee, in future the Legislative Department will 
ensure that its projections to the Ministry of Finance are rational and appropriate to meet its 
requirements. 
 
 
Recommendation/Observation 
 
1.5 The main mandate of the department is to draft laws which it is carrying out with due 
diligence and as per constitution requirements and legislative framework. However, the 
Committee thinks that currently, all the bills that are being drafted by the Department can be 
understood either by the intelligentsia of the count ry or the people having legal ac u-
men/knowledge. For a laymen to understand the bills which are presented to the Parliament is 
very difficult. The Committee therefore, would like to recommend that the bills should be 
drafted in such a simple language so tha t even a common man can also understand about the 
proposed objective and implications of the bill like the Digital Personal Data Protection Bill, 
2022 and Indian Telecommunication Bill, 2022 recently brought by the Government.. (Para 
2.41) 
 
Action Taken 
 
1.6 As regards the Committee‟s observation, Legislative-I Section of Legislative Depart-
ment (LD) has stated that they vide D.O. letter No. 11(10)/2017-L.I dated 24.1.2023 has re-
quested to all the Ministries/ Departments of the Government of India to ensure that the lan-
guage used in the drafts should be precise, plain, simple and unambiguous, etc. This Depart-
ment has also circulated instructions to all the draft persons of this Department to follow/ 
adopt the instructions/ guidelines relating to use of plain and simple language while drafting 
laws. 
1.7 Further, the process of drafting laws is continuous and ongoing one. The efforts to-
wards this end by the Legislative Department are more and more evident in the laws drafted 
by it in the recent past. The Legislative Department will continue in its efforts to draft laws in 
simple and plain language.

Recommendation/Observation 
2.0 The allocation to the Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan (VSP) has also increased marginally by 
7.15 percent over  RE(2022-23) and the expenditure incurred by this wing is 72% of 
RE(2022-23) as on 31st January, 2023. The Committee appreciates the department for proper 
utilization of funds. At the same time, the Committee hopes that more number of authorit a-
tive Hindi versions of judgements of Supreme Court and the High Courts will be done in the 
near future. (Para 2.19) 
Action Taken 
2.1 As regards the Committee‟s observation, Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan (VSP) has stated 
that it is a wing of Legislative Department, dedicated for promotion and propagation of Hindi 
in the legal field under the mandate of Constitution of India (Article 351), recommendations 
of Parliamentary Committee on Official Language and Orders of Hon‟ble President of India. 
In the year 1958, the Committee of Parliament on Official Languages recommended that ar-
rangements be made to bring out authorised Hindi version of all reportable judgements of the 
Supreme Court of India and various High Courts for the purpose of promotion and propaga-
tion of Hindi in the legal field and this work should be entrusted to a Central Office under the 
supervision of Law Ministry. Thereafter, on the recommendations of the Hindi Advisory 
Committee, a “Journal Wing” was set up in the Legislative Department in the year 1968 with 
the object of promoting Hindi in the legal field which was later on known as “VIDHI SA-
HITYA PRAKASHAN”. Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan has published Hindi text of about fifty 
thousand judgements passed by Hon‟ble Supreme Court, various High Courts and Privy 
Council. Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan has also published high standard 38 Law Text Books in 
Hindi on various legal topics. These books are under the copyright of Government of India.   
2.2 Apart from the publication of the above three Hindi Journals, Vidhi Sahitya Praka-
shan is also responsible for the following works, namely:– 
(a) Publication of high standard text books in Hindi in the field of law for the benefit of Law 
Students, Advocates, Judges and Members of Law Faculty; 
(b) Awarding various prizes for the best publication of Hindi Text Books on various subjects 
in the field of law; 
(c) Sale of Hindi Law Journals and Hindi Text Books on various legal subjects, Central Acts 
in diglot edition, Constitution of India, Election Manual and Legal Glossary etc.;  and 
(d) Holding conferences, seminars, workshops and book exhibitions in different parts of the 
country for propagation and popularisation of Hindi in the legal field;

ACTION TAKEN REPORT: 
2.3 Publication of Hindi Law Journals: During the period under report, Supreme Court 
Journal „Uchchatama Nyayalaya Nirnaya Patrika‟ has been uploaded on the site of Legisla-
tive  Department upto January, 2022, High Court Civil journal „Uchcha Nyayalaya Civil Nir-

tice i.e. http://legislative.gov.in/vidhi-sahitya. Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan shall make best efforts 
to publish more and more authoritative Hindi Text of the judgements passed by Hon‟ble Su-
preme Court of India and various High Courts. 
2.4 Digitization: Like any other department of the Government of India, Vidhi Sahitya 
Prakashan has also entered into the era of digitization of records. Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan 
has uploaded three Hindi Law Journals in PDF format since 2012 on the site of Legislative 
Department i.e. http://legislative.gov.in/vidhi-sahitya. Hard copies of the Central Acts and 
other Law Publications are also available for online sale on the site of Department of Reve-
nue i.e. https://bharatkosh.gov.in/ Product/Product/ on digital payment basis i.e. credit card, 
debit card and net banking etc. and the link is available on main Legislative Department front 
page. It is also part of „Ease of Doing Business‟. 
2.5 Apart from it, on the initiative of Hon‟ble Supreme Court of India and approval by the 
Competent Authority, all the judgements of Hon‟ble Supreme Court of India published by 
Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan in Hindi since 1968 till date have been uploaded by Hon‟ble Su-
preme Court of India on their e-SCR portal this year. As desired by Hon‟ble Supreme Court 
of India and approved by the Competent Authority, in future also, Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan 
will continue to provide Hindi text of the Judgements passed by Hon‟ble Supreme Court of 
India in English for uploading on their e-SCR portal. 
Exhibitions, Conferences, Seminars, and Sale of Law Books: 
2.6 Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan has been organizing conferences, seminars and exhibitions- 
cum-sale counters of Law Publciations. The most important publications availed to the public 
are Hindi Law Journals, Hindi Law Text Books, Constitution of India, Central Acts in diglot 
(Hindi-English) edition, Legal Glossary, Manual of Election Law  etc. Hard copies of these 
publications have been made available to the public for online sale on the site of Department 
of Revenue i.e.   https://bharatkosh.gov.in/Product/Product/ on digital payment basis i.e. by 
credit cards, debit cards and net banking etc. and the link is available on main Legislative 
Department page. It is a part of „Ease of Doing Business‟. A sale counter is also working in 
the office of Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan for sale of hard copies of the said publications. 
Recently, Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan organised exhibition-cum-sale of above said law publica-
tions in the District Court Campus, Ujjain and High Court (Indore Bench) Campus from 11th 
October, 2022 to 14th October, 2022 for promotion and propagation of Hindi in the legal 
field.  

pg. 14 
 
2.7 Editorial Board and Evaluation Committees: There are two Committees in Vidhi 
Sahitya Prakashan namely Editorial Board and Evaluation Committee. Members of these two 
Committees are appointed by Hon‟ble Minister of Law and Justice. Editorial Board advises to 
the Government on standards of the Hindi law journals and Hindi Tex Books published by 
Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan for promotion and propagation of Hindi in the legal field under the 
official language policy of the Government. Evaluation Committee is responsible for evalua-
tion of the books written by authors on different topics of law in Hindi and on the basis of 
their recommendations, awards are given on yearly and decade basis.

Recommendation/Observation 
3.0 The Committee infers that the Department has been able to spend only about 30% of 
the RE (2022 -23) upto 31st January, 2023. As per the instructions issued by the Ministry of 
Finance (MoF), expenditure in the last quarter has to be restricted to 33% ceiling  and in last 
month (March) expenditure has to be restricted to 15% of ceiling. The Expenditure made by 
the Department as a whole till 31st January, 2023 is very low and  therefore, the Committee 
strongly recommends the Department to utilize the funds alloc ated to it uniformly throughout 
the year.   (Para 2.17) 
Action Taken  
3.1 As regards the Committee‟s observation, Official Languages Wing (OLW) of Legislative D e-
partment (LD) has stated that they had been allocated Rs. 17.45 crore in Revised Estimate durin g fi-
nancial year 2022-23 and out of this an amount of Rs. 12.55 crore was incurred up to January 2023.  
This Wing had utilized 72% of the allocated budget up to January 2023 in financial year 2022 -23. 
This Wing had also incurred expenditure 14.04% in last quarter and 2.26% in last month i.e. March 
2023. Further, this Wing utilized 96.78% in all heads except sub -head salary. During financial year 
i.e. 2022-23, this Wing used 80.30% of allocated budget in salary head and this budget could not be 
used fully du e to vacant post. Therefore, this Wing had utilized the funds uniformly throughout the 
year.   
3.2 Further, Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan (VSP) of Legislative Department (LD) has stated 
that the Department Related Parliamentary Standing Committee has recommended  to utilize 
the funds allotted to VSP uniformly throughout the year. VSP has utilized the funds unifor m-
ly and efficiently. Department Related Parliamentary Standing Committee in Para 2.19 has 
appreciated the efforts of VSP for proper utilization of funds  
Further observation 
3.3 The Committee notes that the reply of the Department  pertains to the expend i-
ture incurred by the Official Language Wing  (OLW) and Vidhi Sahitya Prakashan 
(VSP). However, the Committee’s recommendation was on the overall allocation and 
expenditure . The Committee, therefore, reiterates its recommendation that the overall 
expenditure of the Department  has to be restricted to 33% ceiling in the last quarter 
and to 15% ceiling in the last month (March ) as per the ceilings prescribed by the Min-
istry of Finance (MoF) may be strictly adhered to. 
Recommendation/Observation

increased by 25.36 percent from the Financial Year (2021 -22) to (2022 -23), whereas it has 
increased marginally by 8.19 percent in the Financial Year (2023 -24). The Committee als o
