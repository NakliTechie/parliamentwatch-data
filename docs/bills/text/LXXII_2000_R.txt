STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Commission on Review of Administrative Laws which was set up by the Central
Government on the 8th May, 1998 has recommended, inter alia, repeal of certain enactments
relating to judicial administration. In pursuance of the recommendations of the Commission,
the Central Government, after consultation with the concerned State Governments, Union
Territory Administrations, High Courts and the Supreme Court of India, has decided to
repeal 17 enactments mentioned in the Schedule to the Bill as they have either outlived their
utility or have, ceased to serve any purpose and have become obsolete. In the circumstances,
it is considered appropriate to repeal these enactments.

2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
