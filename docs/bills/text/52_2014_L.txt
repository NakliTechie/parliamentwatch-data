BILL
further to amend the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 1997.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-fifth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1.  (1) This Act may be called the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (Amendment)
Act, 2014.
(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 28th day of May, 2014.
2. In the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 1997 (hereinafter referred to as the
principal Act), in  section 5,—
(i) for sub-section (8), the following sub-section shall be substituted, namely:—
“(8) The Chairperson and the whole-time members shall not, for a period
of two years from the date on which they cease to hold office as such, except
with the previous approval of the Central Government, accept—
(a) any employment either under the Central Government or under
any State Government; or
