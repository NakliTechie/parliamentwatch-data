further to amend the Divorce Act, 1869, the Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act, 1939, the
Special Marriage Act, 1954, the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 and the Hindu Adoptions
and Maintenance Act, 1956.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventieth  Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
CHAPTER  I
PRELIMINARY
1. (1) This Act may be called the Personal Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
CHAPTER II
AMENDMENT TO THE DIVORCE ACT, 1869
2. In the Divorce Act, 1869, in section 10, in sub-section ( 1), clause ( iv) shall be
omitted.

be omitted.

sub-section (2), clause (c) shall be omitted.
