STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The main' object of this Bill is to introduce the Metric System 
of Weights and Measures in postal transactions. In framing the 
proposals, the principle kept in view is that the metric weight units 
in terms of which postage would be levied should be simple for 
practical working and in consonance with decimal concepts.
