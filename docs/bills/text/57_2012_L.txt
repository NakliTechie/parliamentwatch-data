A
BILL
further to amend the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956.
 BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-third Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1)  This Act may  be called the Indian Medical Council (Amendment)
 Act, 2012.
(2) It shall come into force on the 15th day of May, 2012.
2.  In section 3A of  the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, in sub-section (2), for
the words “two years”, the words “three years” shall be substituted.
