BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-first Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Homoeopathy Central Council (Amendment)
Act, 2020.
(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 24th day of April, 2020.
2. In section 3A of the Homoeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, in sub-section (2), for
the words "within a period of two years", the words "within a period of three years" shall be
substituted.
