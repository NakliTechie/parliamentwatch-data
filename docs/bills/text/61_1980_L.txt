Constitution and the Proclama tion issued under article 356 of the Cons-

diture charged on the Consolidated Fund of the State of Maharashtj a and 
the grants made by the Lok Sabha for expenditure of the Government of 
Maharashtra for the financial year 1979-80. 
R. VENKATARAMAN.
