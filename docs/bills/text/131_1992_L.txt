STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Thig Bill is introduced in pursuance, of article 114(1) of the Consti-
tution of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the appro-
priation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required to
meet the supplementary expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund
of India of the Central Government, excluding Railways, for the financial
yeaT 1992-93, 1

from Shri Shantaram Potdukhe, Minister of State for Finance to the
Secretary-General, Lok Sabha.]

proposed Bill to authorise appropriation of certain further sums from
and out of the Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the finan-

and consideration of the Appropriation (No. 4) Bill, 1992 by Lok Sabha
