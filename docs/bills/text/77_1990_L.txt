Additional Duties of Excise (Goods of Special Importance) Act, 1957, 
to provide for payment to States of their share of additional duties of 
excise on sugar, tobacco, cotton fabrics, man-made fabrics and woollen

the entire net proceedtl, except the proceeds attributable to the Union
