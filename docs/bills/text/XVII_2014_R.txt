STATEMENT  OF  OBJECTS  AND  REASONS
Sub-sections (1) and (2) of Section 5 of the Central Vigilance Commission Act, 2003
respectively specify that the tenure of the Central Vigilance Commissioner or a Vigilance
Commissioner shall be a term of four years from the date on which he enters upon his office
or till he attains the age of sixty-five years, whichever is earlier. However, all the constitutional
authorities like Chief  Election Commissioner, or the Comptroller and Auditor General of India
has a tenure for a term of five years. There is anomaly in the case of the Central Vigilance
Commissioner or a Vigilance Commissioner.
Hence, there is an urgent need to remove this anomaly and make the tenure of the
Central Vigilance Commissioner or a Vigilance Commissioner for a term of five years.
The Bill seeks to achieve the above objective.
Hence, this Bill.
