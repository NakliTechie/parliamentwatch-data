STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the 
Constitution of India, read with article 115 thereof. to provide for 
the appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the 
moneys required to meet the expenditure incurred in excess of the 
appropriation charged on the Fund and the grants made by the 
Lok Sabha for expenditure of the Central Government on 
Railways for the financial year ended on the 31st March, 1952. 
NEW DELHI; 
The 5th March, 1956.
