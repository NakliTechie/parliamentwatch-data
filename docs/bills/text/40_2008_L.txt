A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2008-09.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty- ninth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation ( No. 2) Act, 2008.
2.  From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied sums
not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the  aggregate
[inclusive of the sums specified in column 3 of the Schedule to the Appropriation  (Vote on
Account) Act, 2008] to the sum of twenty-five lakhs ninety-two thousand three hundred and
ninety-one crores and seventy-four lakh rupees towards  defraying the several charges
which will come in course of payment during the financial year 2008-09 in respect of the
services  specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
