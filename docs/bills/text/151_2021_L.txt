1
THE NARCOTIC DRUGS AND PSYCHOTROPIC SUBSTANCES
(AMENDMENT) BILL, 2021
A
BILL
further to amend the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-second  Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(Amendment) Act, 2021.
(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of May, 2014.
2.  In section 27A of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, for
the words, brackets, letters and figure "clause ( viiia) of section 2", the words, brackets,
letters and figure "clause (viiib) of section 2" shall be substituted.

commencement.
