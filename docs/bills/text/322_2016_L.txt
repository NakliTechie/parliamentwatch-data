BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-eighth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the National Institutes of  Technology, Science Education
and Research (Amendment) Act, 2017.
 (2)  It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.

and Research  Act, 2007, after serial number 5 and the entries relating thereto, the following
