STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Section 4 of the Prevention of Disqualification (Parliament and 
Part C States Legislatures) Act, 1953 (I of 1954) as originally en-
acted granted protection from disqualification from membership of 
Parliament in respect of certain offices up to· the 30th April, 1954. 
In order to enable the Government to prepare comprehensive legis-
lation on the subject, Parliament, during the Budget Session this 
year, amended section 4 and extended the temporary protection up 
to the end of this year . 
. 'The question what exacUy is an 'office of profit' and the question 
in what circumstances such an office should not disqualify its holder 
from being a member of Parliament are both difficult questions for 
which ready answers cannot be found. A committee of Members 
of Parliament has also been considering this problem but has not 
yet reached definite conclusions. It has recommended that the 
temporary protection ·at present granted up to the 31st December, 
1954, may be extended for a further period of one year. 
The Bill is intended to give effect to this recommendation.
