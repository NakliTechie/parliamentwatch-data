further to amend the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950 for inclusion of

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-fourth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order (Amendment)
Act, 2023.
2. In the Schedule to the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, in
PART  XX.—Chhattisgarh,—
(i) for entry 5, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:—
“5. Bharia Bhumia, Bhuinhar Bhumia, Bhumiya, Bharia, Bhuinya, Bhuiyan,
Bhuyan, Paliha, Pando”;
