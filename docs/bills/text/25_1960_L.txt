This Bill is introduced in pursuance' of article 114 (1) of the Cons-
titution of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the

required to meet the expenditure incurred in e,xcess of the appropria-
tion charged on the Fund and the grants made by the Lok Sabha 
for expenditure of the Central Government on Railways for the 
financial year ended on the 31st day of March, 1958.
