STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
In order to curb circulation of black money in the form of deposits 
with banks, companies, co-operative societies and partnership firms by 
tax evaders either in their own names or in the names of others and 
to enable the income-tax authorities to identify the persons who have 
made such deposits, the Income-tax (Amendment) Ordinance, 1981 was 
promulgated by the President on the 11th July, 198!. 
2. The Ordinance inserted a new section 269T in the Income-tax 
Act, 196] to provide that no company, co-operative society ~r firm shall 
repay any time deposit otherwise than by an account payee cheque or 
an account payee bank draft or in the case of repayment by a bank by 
crediting the amount to the account of the payee with the bank where 
the amount of such deposit or the amount of such deposit with interest 
payable thereon is ten thousand rupees or more. The Ordinance' also 
mane the necessary amendments in the Income-tax Act to provide for 
punishment (fine equal to the amount of deposit and imprisonment for 
a period not exceeding two years) for contravention of the provision~ 
of this section. 
3. The Bill seeks to replace the Ordinance. It also seeks to make an 
amendment in the Income-tax Act to provide for the manner of repay-
ment of the amount of Special Bearer Bonds. The new section 269TT 
proposed to be inserted in the Income-tax Act for this purpose seeks to 
provide that the repayment at the time of the redemption of the Special 
Bearer Bonds should be made by an account payee cheque or an account 
payee bank draft only. This is in pursuance of an undertaking given 
by Government before the Supreme Court in a case in which the consti-
tutional validity of the Special Bearer Bonds Scheme was challenged. 
4 .. The Bill seeks to achieve the above objects.
