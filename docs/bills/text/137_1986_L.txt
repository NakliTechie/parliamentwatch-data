STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Central Excise duties are now levied at the rates specified in the
Schedule to the Central Excise Tariff Act, 1985 (5 of 1986). The Tariff
is based broadly om the system of classification derived from the Inter-
national Convention on the Harmonised Commodity Desvription and
Coding System with such contractions or modifications thereto as were
considered necessary. The Harmonised System is basically of 8 digit
code. The Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975 (51 of 1975) is also
based on the Harmonised System, For the sake of uniformity it would
be more convenient if the Customs Tariff and the Excise Tarif are
aligned to each other, However, full alignment may not be possible
because the seope of customs duties and central excise duties are dif-
ferent. It is felt that it would be beneficial to gradually align the
excise tariff with the customs tariff which would reduce classifications
disputes and also facilitate compilation of statistics. With this object,
Chapters 28 19 40 of the Schedule to the said Central Excise Tariff Act
have been identified where alignment of the Excise Tariff and Customs
Tariff is considered feasible at the 4 digit level.

2, The amendments proposed in the Bill thus seck to align Chapters
28 to 40 of the Central Excise Tariff Act with the corresponding Chapters
of the Custom Tariff Act at the 4 digit level with certain minor excep.
tons. The existing rates of duty are being preserved and the effective
tates of duty in certain cases would be provided through the exemption
notifications issued under the Central Excise Rules, 1944,

3. Opportunity has been availed of to increase the tariff rate of duty
in respect of Polypropyllene wastes.
4 The Bill seeks to achieve the above objects.
