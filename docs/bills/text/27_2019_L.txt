(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to provide for the withdrawal of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated Fund of
India for the services of a part of the financial year 2019-20.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventieth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1.  This Act may be called the Appropriation (V ote on Account) Act, 2019.
2.  From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India, there may be withdrawn sums
not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule, amounting in the aggregate
to the sum of thirty-four lakh seventeen thousand two hundred ninty-five crore and
thirty-eight lakh rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course
of payment during the financial year 2019-20.
3. The sums authorised to be withdrawn from and out of the Consolidated Fund
by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the Schedule
in relation to the said year.
