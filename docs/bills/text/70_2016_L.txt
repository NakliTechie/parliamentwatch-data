THE APPROPRIATION (VOTE ON ACCOUNT) BILL, 2016
(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to provide for the withdrawal of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated Fund of
India for the services of a part of the financial year 2016-17.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-seventh Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (V ote on Account) Act, 2016.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be withdrawn sums not
exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the sum
of eleven lakh forty-five thousand eight hundred seventy-eight crores and eight lakh rupees
towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment during the
financial year 2016-17.
3. The sums authorised to be withdrawn from and out of the Consolidated Fund by
this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the Schedule in
relation to the said year.
