STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Shelter is the basic necessity for sustaining life. Right to life and other g
tees for the well-being of the citizens enshrined in the Constitution are meaningless
the millions of persons who have no shelter. It is necessary that right to shelter

guaranteed and made a fundamental right.
Hence this Bill.

New DELHI;
June 21, 2001


FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

Clause 2 of the Bill provides that each family shall have the right to a dwelling
unit. The Bill, if enacted, will involve expenditure for providing houses to families who
are not having houses. Though housing is basically is a State subject, yet the Central
Government has to give grants to the State Governments for implementing the provisions
of the Bill. It is estimated that an annual recurring expenditure of about rupees ten
thousand crore is likely to be involved,

A non-recurring expenditure of rupees two thousand crore is also likely to be
involved.
