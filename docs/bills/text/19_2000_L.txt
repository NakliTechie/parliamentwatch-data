1



STATEMENT OF OBJECTSAND REASONS

Santhali is a very rich and popular language of Santhal tribes who reside in many
parts of the country especially in West Bengal, Bihar and Orissa. The language has its own
script and literature and has eminent scholars. The tribe is trying to get due recognition to
their language for a long time. To give justice and in view of their economic and social
background, it is imperative that their language should get due recognition without any
further delay. Hence it is proposed in the Bill that the Santhali language be included in the
list of Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.

‘The Bill seeks to achieve the above objective.
