STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Consequent on devaluation, Government considered it necessary 
to amend the Customs Act, 1962, 30 as to provide explicitly in sec-
tion 14 of the Act, which relates to valuation of goods for purposes 
of assessment, th{lt the price of imported goods should be deter-
mined with reference to the rates of exchange as in force on the 
relevant date referred to in section 15 of the Act. Such amend- , 
ment would ensure the determination of the value and the rate of 
duty for Customs purposes, i!1 respect of all goods imported before 
the date of devaluation but removed from Customs Bonded Ware-
houses on or after that date, at the new rates of exchange. It would 
place the importers who had warehoused their imported goods be-
fore the date of devaluation in the same position with regard to 
Customs duty vis-a-vis other importers of simil~r goocl$ who cleared 
them directly for home consumption after that date. The President 
a-ccordingly promulgated the Customs (Amendment) Ordinance, 
1966, on tl1e 7th July, 1966, to amend sections 14 and 15 of the Act 
for the above purpose. This Bill is intended to replace the 
Ordinance.
