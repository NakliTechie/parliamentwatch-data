STATEMENT  OF  OBJECTS  AND  REASONS
The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 provides for deterrent
punishment for abusing a mentally ill child below the age of eighteen years or when the
abuse is committed by a person in a position of trust or authority. The same legal protection
is needed for persons who are physically major but with mental capacity of a ‘minor’ or
mentally retarded or physically or visually handicapped, making it impossible for such
persons to defend themselves or to fend off ill-intended approaches.
The law must reach out to such persons and provide for their protection and for harsh
punishment for sexual assault or abuse of such persons.
Hence this Bill.
