BILL

to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from

of India as follows: —

hundred and forty-three crores and thirty lakh rupees towards defraying

the several charges which will come in course of payment during the

financial year 1990-91, in respect of the services specified in column 2 of
10 the Schedule.

3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the
services and purposes expressed in the Schedule in relation to the
said year,
