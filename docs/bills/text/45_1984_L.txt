STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
In pursuance of the recommendations of the Seventh Finance Com-
missi~ during each of tho inancial years from 1979·80 to 1983-84, the 
net proceeds of the Union duties of excise on electricity levied and collect-
ed in that year were paid out of the Consolidated FWld of India to each 
State excluding the State of Sikkim) in an amount equal to the collection 
in or attributable to that State. The perc~ntag~ in which the sums were 
to be distributed have been specified in the Union Duties of Exciso 
(ElectricitY) Distribution Act, 1980. 
2. The Eighth Finance CommissiQl1 in its interim report presented 
in November, 1983 has recommended that the existing arrangements in 
respect of the distribution of the Union duties of excise on electricity may 
continue provisionally during the financial year comrn~cing on the 1 st 
day of April, ] 984. It has further been recommended that since the law 
imposing duty on the generation of electricity has been extended to the 
State of Sikkim, that State shall also be entitled to a share in that duty. 
These provisional arrangement!; are, however, of interim nature and would 
be subject to re-adjustments as may be necessary on the basis of the final 
report of the Commission. 
3. The Bill seeks to give effect to the aforesaid recommendation of 
the Eighth Finance Commission.
