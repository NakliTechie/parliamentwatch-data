STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

In April/May, 1990, the Government of Jammu and Kashmir \
declared certain Organisations and Associations as unlawful under the
provisions of the Jammu and Kashmir Criminal Law Amendment Act,
1983 for the persistent activities of the members of these organisations
in inciting and instigating violence, secessionism and disruption of public
order and harmeny. |

3. On the advice of the Tribunal, the Government of Jammu and
Kashmir recommended, in October, 1990, the extending the statutory
time limit available to the Tribunal to give its findings in six months
period. This was agreed to by the Central Government and accordingly
the Governor of Jammu and Kashmir Promulgated an Ordinance on
17th December, 1990, amending State law. The Ordinance was later
replaced by an Act of Parliament entitled, “The Jammu and Kashmir
Criminal Law Amendment (Amending) Act, 1991” (10 of 1991).

4. The Government of Jammu and Kashmir on the basis of report
Teceived by it from the Tribunal, recommended further extension
of time by six months on the ground that it may not be possible to dence
scme of the references within even the extended period of limitation
which was expiring cn 17th June, 1991, as some triable issues had
arisen in some cases and Personal service of the notice could not be
effected in some others.

5, Since the State of Jammu and Kashmir is under President's Rule
and Parliament was not in session, the Governor promulgated the Jammu
and Kashmir Criminal Law (Amendment) Ordinance, 1991, on 16th
June, 1991, for extending the period of “one year” to “one year and six
months” to enable the Tribunal to complete its proceedings in relation
to such declarations,
