A
BILL
further to amend the Salaries and Allowances of Officers of Parliament Act, 1953.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Salaries and Allowances of Officers of Parliament
(Amendment) Act, 2008.
2. In section 3 of the Salaries and Allowances of Officers of Parliament Act, 1953, in
sub-section (1), for the words “forty thousand rupees”, the words “one lakh twenty-five
thousand rupees” shall be substituted and shall be deemed to have been substituted with
effect from the 1st day of January, 2006.
