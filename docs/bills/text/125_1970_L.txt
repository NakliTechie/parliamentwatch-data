STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the Constitu-
tion of India read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the appropria-
tion out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required to 
meet the expenditure incurred in excess of the appropriations charge{ 
on the Fund and the Grants made by the Lok Sabha for expenditure fI. 
the Central Government, excluding Railways, for the financial yefJ1 
ended on the 31st day of March, 1969.
