STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of articles 204(1) and 205 
of the Constitution and the Proclamation issued under article 356 of 
the Constitution in re$pect of the State of Rajasthan on the 13th 
March, 1967, to provide for the appropriation out of the Consolidated 
Fund of the State of Rajasthan of the moneys required to meet the 
supplementary expenditure charged on the Consolidared Fund of 
the State of Rajasthan and the grants made by the Lok Sabha for 
expenditure of the Government of Rajasthan for the financial year 
1966-67. 
MORARJI DESAI. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 207 
OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA

Shri Morarji Desai, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance

proposed Bidl to authorise payment and appropriation of certain

March, 1967, recommends under clauses (1) and (3) of article 207 
of the Constitution read with article 205 thereof and the Prodama-
tion dated the 13th March, 1967, issued under article 356, the intro-
duction of, the Rajasthan Appropriation Bill, 1967 in the Lok Sabha 
and a180 the consideration of the Bill.
