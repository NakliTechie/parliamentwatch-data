STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the Con-
stitution of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the 
appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys 
required to meet the expenditure incurred in excess of the appro-
priation charged on the Fund and the grants made by ihe Lot Sabha 
for expenditure of the Central Government on Railways for the finan-
cial year ended on the 31st day of March, 1959.
