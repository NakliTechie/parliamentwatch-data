In the Budget Spe~ch, an announcement was made of the decision of 
Government to abolish the levy or estate duty in respect of estates passing 
on deaths occurring on or after the lath day of March, 1985. This Bill is 
in pursuance o~ that assurance. 
2. The new section proposed in clause 2 of the Bi~ seeks to .provide

3. As regards levy of estate duty on agricultural ~and, it may be men-
