STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Instances of rape are on the rise. Women are not safe on roads. But in the recent past
several instances have come to notice when the father, brother or the relatives of women
themselves raped or helped in committing rape on women. They ought to protect their
womenfolk but they themselves rape or abet others to commit rape. In such cases, they
should be awarded with death punishment which should act as a deterrent.
The Bill, therefore, seeks to amend the Indian Penal Code, 1860.
Hence this Bill.
