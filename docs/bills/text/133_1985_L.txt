STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Employment of Children Act, 1938 regulates the employment Of 
children in certain industrial employments. Section 3 of the Act pro-
hibits the employment of children under 15 years and the employment, 
during night time, of children between 15 and 17 years in certain hazar-
duous occupations in RailwaY's and in ports. The section also prohibits 
the employment of children under 14 years in workshops in which any 
of the 'process mentioned in the Schedule to the Act are carried on. 
2. Section 4 of the Act which, inter alia, provides for the penalty for 
contraventions of the said section 3 makes no distinction between first 
and subsequent offences. Further, the penalty provided by the section 
is also not deterrent. It is accordingly proposed to provide that a per· 
son contravening the provisions of section 3 of the Act shall be punish-
able, in the case of a first offence, with imprisonment for a term which 
shall not be less than three months but which may extend to one year 
or with fine which shall not be less than five hundred rupees but whicn 
may extend to two thousand rupees or with both. and in the case of a 
c;econd or subsequent offence, with imprisonment for a term which shall 
not be less than six months but which may extend to two years. 
3, The Bill seeks to achieve the above objective.
