Bill No XVIII of 2000
THE SUGARCANE CONTROL (ADDITIONAL POWERS) REPEAL
BILL, 2000
A
BILL

To repeal the Sugarcane Control (Additional Powers) Act, 1962.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-first Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—

1. This Act may be called the Sugarcane Control (Additional Powers) Repeal
Act, 2000. .

2. The Sugarcane Control (Additional Powers) Act, 1962 is hereby repealed.

Short title.

Repeal of Act
39 of 1962.


STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Commission on Review of Administrative Laws headed by Shri P.C. Jain in its
report presented to the Government on 30-9-1998 identified the Sugarcane Control
(Additional Powers) Act, 1962 (39 of 1962) as one of the Acts which could be repealed.

2. The Sugarcane Control (Additional Powers) Act, 1962 was enacted to empower the
Central Government to amend the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1955 with retrospective effect.
The Sugarcane (Controt) Urder,1955 itself was repealed with the notification of the Sugarcane
(Control) Order, 1966. Since. the Sugarcane Control (Additional Powers) Act, 1962
empowered the Central Government to amend the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1955, which
stood repealed and replaced by the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966, there is no need to
preserve this enactment in the statute book. Its now proposed to repeal the said Act, which
has outlived its purpose.

3. The Bill seeks to achieve the above mentioned obyect.
