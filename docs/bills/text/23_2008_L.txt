BILL
further to amend the Sugar Development Fund Act, 1982 and the Sugar Cess Act, 1982.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Sugar Development Fund (Amendment) Act, 2008.
(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 5th day of February, 2008.
2. In the Sugar Development Fund Act, 1982, in section 4, in sub-section ( 1), after
clause (bbb), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:—
“(bbbb) for defraying expenditure for the purpose of financial assistance to
sugar factories towards interest on loans given in terms of any scheme approved by
the Central Government from time to time.”.
3. In the Sugar Cess Act, 1982, in section 3, in sub-section (1),—
(a) for the words “fifteen rupees”, the words “twenty-five rupees” shall be
substituted;
(b) the proviso shall be omitted.
