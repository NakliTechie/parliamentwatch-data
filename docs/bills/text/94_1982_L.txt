STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the Constitu-
tion of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the appropriation 
out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required to meet 
the supplementary expenditure on the grants made by the Lok Sabha for 
expenditure of the Central Government on Railways, for the financial 
year 1982-83. 
P.C.SETHI.
