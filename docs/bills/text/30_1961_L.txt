STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 204(1) of the 
Constitution of India, read with the Proclamation issued by the 
President on. the 25th February, 1961, in exercise of powers conferred 
on him by article 356 of the Constitution, to provide for the 
appropriations out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Orissa 
of the moneys required to meet the expenditure charged on the 
Consolidated Fund and the Grants made by the Lok Sabha for 
expenditure of the Government of Orissa for the financial year 
1961-62.
