STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of articles 204 (1) and 205 
of the Constitution and the Proclamation issued under article 356 
of the Constitution in respect of the State of Bihar on the 29th June, 
1968, to provide for the appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund 
of the State of Bihar of the moneys required to meet the supplemen-
tary expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund of the State of 
Bihar and the il'ants made by the Lok Sabha for expenditure of the 
. Government of Bihar for the financial year 1968-69. 
llORARJI DESAI. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 207 
OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. 5 (18)-B/68, dated the 12th December, 1968 
from Shri Morarji Desai, Deputy Prime Minister and Miniater of 
Finance to the Secretary, Lok Sabha.] 
The President, having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to authorise payment and appropriation of certain 
further sums from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of 
Bihar for the services of the year. ending on the 31st day of March, 
1969, recommends under clauses (1) and (3) of article 207 of the 
Constitution read with article 205 thereof and the Proclamation dated 
29th June, 1968 issued under article 356 of the Constitution the intro-
duction of the Bihar Appropriation (No.2) Bill, 1968 in the Lok 
Sabha and also the consideration of the Bill. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in the Lok Sabha after all the 
Supplementary Demands for Grants for 1968-69 have been voted.
