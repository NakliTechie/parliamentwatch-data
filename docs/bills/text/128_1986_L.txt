STATEMENT Oli' OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the Con-
stitution of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the appro-
priation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required 
to meet the supplementary expenditure on the grants made by the Lok 
Sabha for expenditure of the Central Governemnt on Railways for the 
financial year 1986-87.
