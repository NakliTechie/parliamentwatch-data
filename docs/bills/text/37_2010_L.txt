 BILL

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-first Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Indian Penal Code  (Amendment) Act, 2010.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint and different dates may be appointed for different States.
2. After section 335 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 and the Explanation thereunder,
the following section shall be inserted, namely:—
"335A. Whoever voluntarily causes grievous hurt to any man with intent to
emasculate that man, shall be punished with death or imprisonment for life and shall
also be liable to fine.”.

STATEMENT  OF  OBJECTS  AND  REASONS
In some parts of the country, offences against men are on the rise where the genitals of
men are cut off. Such a criminal act spoils the whole life of man as he is rendered a male less
of a man, that is to say, without any manliness.
Under the present penal provisions, such offences are tried as general crimes and
punishment provided for such acts is not proportionate to the gravity of the offence
committed.
Therefore, it is high time that a stringent law, which may act as a deterrent for such
perverted acts of male emasculation, is brought forward by amending the Indian Penal Code,
1860.
Hence this Bill.
