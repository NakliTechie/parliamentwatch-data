STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
In 94] a census was taken in the State of Jammu and Kashmir
as a part of the census operations for the whole of India. But in
495l a similar census was not taken there on account of administra-
tive difficulties. As these difficulties no longer exist, it is proposed,
with the concurrence of the Government of Jammu and Kashmir, to
undertake census operations in the State along with the rest of India
in 96l. The object of the Bill is to give effect to this proposal by
extending the Census Act, 948, to the State.
