STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Our Constitution ensures to the citizens the fundamental right to religious freedom.
However, there is no provision for preventing conversion by force or inducement in our
Constitution. The manner in which conversions are being promoted through money sent
from abroad on the pretext of education or charity is a danger to the unity and integrity of the
country. | जे |

Therefore, it is necessary to amend the Constitution with a view to making specific
provision regarding banning of conversion through force or inducement in any form. —

Hence the Bill.
