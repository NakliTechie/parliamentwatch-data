THE APPROPRIATION BILL, 2008
(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2007-08.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1.  This Act may be called the Appropriation  Act, 2008.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may  be paid and applied
sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate
to the sum of  one lakh forty thousand three hundred seventy-eight crores and ninety- one
lakh rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in the course of payment
during the financial year 2007-08 in respect of the services specified in column 2 of the
Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
