BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-fourth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order
(Second Amendment) Act, 2023.
2. In the Schedule to the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, in Part V.—
Himachal Pradesh, after entry 10, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:—
“11. Hattee of Trans Giri area of Sirmour district.”.
