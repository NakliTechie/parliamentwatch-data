STATEMENT  OF  OBJECTS  AND  REASONS
The people belonging to the 'Od Rajput' Community in the States of Uttarakhand and
Uttar Pradesh, who were displaced from Multan region of Pakistan at the time of partition,
have not yet been included in the list of scheduled castes of these States despite the fact that
the same caste has been included in the list of Scheduled Castes of other States like Haryana,
Punjab and Delhi. This disparity needs to be removed immediately.
Persons belonging to Od caste reside in many districts of Uttarakhand. These people
use surnames like Kudawali, Virpali, Kahaliya, Sarvan, Mangal, Mundai, Galagat, Bhagat,
Majoka, etc. Even  today, their economic, social and educational condition is pitiable. There
is a long pending demand to include Od caste in the list of Scheduled Castes. The Od Rajput
Utthan Samiti has been campaigning for long at various levels to include the Od caste in the
list of Scheduled Castes.
Similarly, Namasudra and Pod (Poundra) castes of  Bengali community displaced from
erstwhile East Pakistan have been accorded the status of Scheduled Castes in the State of
West Bengal but not in the State of Uttarakhand. These castes also need to be included in
Scheduled Castes in relation to the State of Uttarakhand so that development of these
communities can be ensured.
The Bill, therefore, seeks to amend Part XXIV.—Uttarakhand of the Schedule to the
Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950, with a view to include the above mentioned
castes in the list of Scheduled Castes in relation to the State of Uttarakhand.
Hence this Bill.

FINANCIAL  MEMORANDUM
Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to include persons belonging to Namasudra, Od, Pod, Poundra
community into the list of Scheduled Castes based on their social, educational and economic
backwardness. The Bill, therefore, if enacted, will involve expenditure from the Consolidated
Fund of India on account of benefits to be provided to the persons belonging to these
communities under continuing schemes meant for development of Scheduled Castes. It is
estimated that an annual recurring expenditure of about rupees fifty crore is likely to be
incurred from the Consolidated Fund of India.
A non-recurring expenditure of about rupees fifty crore is also likely to be incurred.
