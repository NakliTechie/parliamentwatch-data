STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

During the Eleventh and Twelfth Lok Sabhas, there were very less number of sittings
of each House of Parliament, with the result that the Government adopted the method of
issuing Ordinances frequently. Ina Parliamentary system of Democracy, the views of the
People are more important as the members express their views on all important legislations.
‘The reduction in the sittings of the Parliament has been opposed by the people. It not only
i "ges of Members of Parliament but also set an unhealthy trend in
4 democratic system. It is, therefore, proposed that Parliament shall sit for a minimum
Period of one hundred and fifty days in a calendar year in the interest of the country.

Hence this Bill.
