BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-first Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (Railways) No. 5   Act, 2010.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied sums
not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the
sum of one thousand twenty-four crores and sixty-one lakh rupees towards defraying the
several charges which will come in course of payment during the financial year 2010-11, in
respect of the services relating to Railways specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
