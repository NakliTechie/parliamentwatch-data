A 
BILL 
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the 
 Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2023-24. 
 BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-fifth Year of the Republic of India 
as follows:— 
 1.  This Act may be called the Appropriation Act, 2024. 
 2.  From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and 
applied sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting 
in the aggregate to the sum of two lakh two hundred ninety-nine crore and  fifty-four 
lakh only towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of 
payment during the financial year 2023 -24 in respect of the services specified in 
column 2 of the Schedule.
