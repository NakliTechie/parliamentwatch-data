[Copy of letter No. F. 4(54)-B(SE)/86. dated the 26th July, 1986 from

The President, having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to authorise appropriation of certain further sums from and

year ending on the 31st day of March, 1987, recommends the introduction 
of the Appropriation (No.4) Bill, 1986 in Lok Sabha and also recom-
mends to Lok Sabha the consideration of the Bill under article 117 (1) 
and (3) of the Constitution Tead with article 115 (2) thereof. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in Lok Sabha after all the Supplemen-
tary Demands for Grants for 1986-8'7 have been voted.
