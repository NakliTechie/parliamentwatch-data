STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Chattishgarhi is spoken by nearly two crore people living in the state of Chattishgrah
One of the main factors in the formation of the state was the language which has its own
grammar, dictionery and set of proverbs. It is the medium of expression of people's art,
literature and traditions. The "Pandawani", the Chattishgharhi Mahabharata, has assumed
international significance. The people of the state are known all over the country because of
Chattishgarhi. Akashwani and Doordarshan present everyday programmes in Chattishgarhi
and the Railways have adopted it for regular announcements.
The successive state Governments have unanimously passed resolutions for
recognition of Chattishgrahi, as official language of the state, to convey the benefits of
development to the last man and pave way for 100% literacy.
Inclusion of Chattishgrahi, will not only meet the long cherished aspirations of people
of a distinct area surrounded by Marathi, Telugu and Oriya speaking states in the southern
part, and predominantly Hindi belt in the north, but also contribute in enriching the assimilation
desired in Art. 351 of the Constitution.
Hence the bill.
