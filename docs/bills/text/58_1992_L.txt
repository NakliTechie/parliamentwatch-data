to authorise payment and appropriation of certain Further sums from and out of

Be it enacted by Parliament in the Forty-third Year of the Republic of
India as follows:—
4. This Act may be called the Manipur Appropriation Act, 1992.

2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Manipur there
may be paid and applied sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the
Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the sum of one hundred twelve crores,
thirty-three lakhs and sixty-two thousand rupees towards defraying the several
charges which will come in course of payment during the financial year 1991-92
in respect of the services specified in column 2 of the Schedule.

3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Conso-
jidated Fund of the State of Manipur by this Act shall be appropriated for the
- services and purposes expressed in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
