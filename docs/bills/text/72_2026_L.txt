A 
BILL 
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the 
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2025-26. 
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-seventh Year of the Republic of 
India as follows:— 
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation Act, 2026.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and
applied sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting 
in the aggregate to the sum of two lakh eighty-one thousand two hundred 
eighty-nine crore and twenty-six lakh rupees only towards defraying the several 
charges which will come in course of payment during the financial year 2025-26 in 
respect of the services specified in column 2 of the Schedule.

Appropriation. 3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the

purposes expressed in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
