STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Bill seeks to implement the constitutional. provisions under के
article 98 (2) to regulate py law the recruitment and the conditions
of service in the Rajya Sabha Secretariat. It also seeks to remove
certain existing anomalies in the pay scales and the conditions cf
service of various categories of posts as they exist under the rules
in force for the time being.

BHAI MAHAVIR.



FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

Clause 3 of the Bill deals with the set-up of the Rajya Sabha
Secretariat. The Secretariat will consist of officials of various eate-
gories as are enumerated in the First Schedule to the Bill.

Clause 13 deals with the payment of the City Compensatory
Allowance to various categories of the staff.

Clause 14 deals with the payment of house rent allowance to
those officials for whom no residence is provided.

According to the pay or scales of pay attached to each of the
post specified in column 1 of the Second Schedule to the Bill, recur-
ring annual expenditure, inclusive of allowances, honoraria, ete., is
estimated at Rs. 25,00,000 only.


MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

Clause 3 of the Bill empowers the Chairman to increase or dec-
rease the number of posts specified in the First Schedule to the Bill.

Clause 5 empowers the Rules (Service) Committee to make
rules in respect of matters connected with the recruitment and con-
ditions of Service of the staff in the Rajya Sabha Secretariat. The
delegation of legislative power is of a normal character.
