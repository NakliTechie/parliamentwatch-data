STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of section 29 (1) of the Govern-
ment of. Union Territories Act, 1963, read with section 31 thereof and 
the Order of the Vice-President acting as President made on the -11th 
May, 1977 under section 51 of that Act to provide for the appropriation 
out of the Consolidated Fund of the Union territory of Mizoram of the 
moneys required to meet the expenditure charged on the Consolidated 
}4'und and the grants made in advance by the Lok Sabha in respect of 
the estimated expenditure of the Government of Mizoram for a part of 
the financial year 1978-79. 
H. M. PATEL. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER THE GOVERNMENT 
OF UNION TERRITORIES ACT, 1963 
[Copy of letter No. F.3(123)-B(S)/77, dated the 10th Mareh, 1978 
from Shri H. M. Patel, Minister of Finance to the Secretary, Lok Sabha.] 
The President, having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to authorise payment and appropriation of certain SUIRS 
from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the Union territory of Mizoram 
for the services of a part of the financial year 1978-79 recommends, 
under sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 23 and section 31 of the 
Government of Union Territories Act, 1963, read with the Order made 
by him on the 11th May, 1977 under- section 51 of the said Act, the 
introduction of the Mizoram Appropriation (Vote on Account) Bill, 
1978, in and the consideration of the Bill by Lok Sabha. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in Lok Sabha immediately after the 
Demands for Grants 'on account' for expenditure of the Union territory 
of Mizoram for a part of the financial year 1978-79, have been voted.
