STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Under the' Constitution I (Application to Jammu and Kashmir) 
Order, 1954, issued on the 14th day of May, 1954, the Constitution of 
India has been applied to the State of Jammu and Kashmir subject 
to certain modifieations and exceptions. By reason of the Order, 
Parliament is now empowered inte,- alia to make laws relating to 
Central taxes for that State. The object of this Bill is to extend to 
that·State the taxation laws ·ml,force in the rest of India relating to 
income-tax and duties of customs and excise with the necessary 
modifications. 
Under an agreement enteI'ed. into between the Government of 
India and the Government of the State of Jammu and Kashmir, the 
State Government may continue to levy for a period of ten years the 
existing duty on motor spirit. Clause 3 of the Bill makes 
Jlecessary provision in this behalf.
