THE APPROPRIATION (No. 3) BILL, 2019
(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2019-20.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventieth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1.  This Act may be called the Appropriation (No. 3) Act, 2019.
2.  From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied sums
not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the
sum of  twenty-one thousand two hundred forty-six crore and sixteen lakh rupees towards
defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment during the financial year
2019-20 in respect of the services specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
