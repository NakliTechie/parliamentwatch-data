THE APPROPRIATION (No. 2) BILL, 2019
(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated
Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2019-20.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the SeventiethYear of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (No. 2) Act, 2019.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied
sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate
to the sum of ninety-eight lakh eighteen thousand nine hundred three crore and eighty-two
lakh rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment
during the financial year 2019-20 in respect of the services specified in column 2 of the
Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
4. References to Ministries or Departments in the Schedule are to such Ministries or
Departments as existing immediately before the 19th June, 2019 and shall, on or after that
date, be construed as references to the appropriate Ministries or Departments as reconstituted
from time to time.
