  THE APPROPRIATION (VOTE ON ACCOUNT) BILL, 2008
(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to provide for the withdrawal of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated Fund of
India for the services of a part of the financial year 2008-09.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (V ote on Account) Act, 2008.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be withdrawn sums not
exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the
sum of four lakh thirty-nine thousand two hundred nineteen crores and eighty-seven lakh
rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment during
the financial year 2008-09.
3. The sums authorised to be withdrawn from and out of the Consolidated Fund by
this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the Schedule in
relation to the said year.
4. Reference to the Ministries  or  Departments  in  the Schedule  are  to such Ministries
or Departments as existing immediately before 6th December, 2007 and shall on or after that
date be construed as references to the appropriate Ministries or Departments as
constituted from time to time.
