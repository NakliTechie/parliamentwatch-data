STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND .REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the 
Constitution of India read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the 
appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India Of the moneys 
required to meet the expenditure incurred. in excess of the appropria-
tions charged on the Fund and the grants made by the Lok Sabha for 
expenditure of the ICentral Government, exc1~g RailwayS, for the 
financial year ended on the 31st .day of March, 1980. .
