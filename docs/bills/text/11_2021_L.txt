STATEMENT  OF  OBJECTS  AND  REASONS
The aim of the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee
(MGNREGA) Scheme formulated under the MGNREGA  Act, 2005 is to provide
minimum one hundred days of employment in a financial year to a semi-skilled adult
member of each family by augmenting employment guarantee in rural areas of the
country. This law is termed by the Government as "The largest and the most ambitious
social security and public work programme of the world".
As per the Development Report 2014, the World Bank recognized this scheme
as "a starred example of rural development".  Apart from providing social security and
developing rural properties, this Scheme helps in safeguarding environment,
empowering rural women, reducing rural-urban migration and in encouraging social
equity. By effective emplementation of this Scheme, unskilled unemployed youth of
rural areas are being made self-dependant by way of providing them employment.
Many women in rural areas have been helped in securing social security along with
earning livelihood through this Scheme.
Even after seventy-two years of independence, the condition of farmers of our
country has not improved as expected. During the last few years, many welfare schemes
have been implemented in order to give relief to farmers and if in continuation to this,
agriculture sector is added to MGNREGA Scheme, it will be beneficial to both farmers
as well as labourers of the country. Besides, more opportunities of employment may
be created in rural areas. Both labourers and farmers are facing crisis at this juncture.
So, by conjoining these two sectors, the problem of both the sections may be resolved
simultaneously. The labourers will get more employment and money and the cost in
agricultural sector will get reduced.
The Bill seeks to achieve the above objectives.

FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM
Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to bring agricultural operations or related works under
the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee (MGNREGA) Scheme.
The Bill, therefore, if enacted, will involve expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of
India. It is estimated that a sum of rupees Three hundred crore would be involved as
recurring expenditure per annum.
No non-recurring expenditure is likely to be involved.
