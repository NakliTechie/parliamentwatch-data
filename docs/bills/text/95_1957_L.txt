STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Preventive Detention Act, 1950, is due to expire on the 31st 
December, 1957. Experience has shown that it is essential that the 
powers conferred by the Preventive Detention Act should be con-
tinued for a further period of three years. The Bill is meant to 
achieve this object.
