ceeds of the Union duties of excise, excluding cesses levied under special 
Acts and earmarked for special purpose is paid to the States and distri .. 
buted among them in the percentages whiCh the Seventh Finance Com-
mission had recommended. The Eighth Finance Commission in its in-

(i) subject to such re-adjustments as may be necessary on the 
basis of its final report, the existing arrangement& in respect of the 
distribution between the Union and the States of the net proceeds 
of Union duties of excise may continue provisionally during the 
financial year comme.ncing on the 1st day of April, 1984;

been extended to the State of Sikkim, that State would also be entitled
