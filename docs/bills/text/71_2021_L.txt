B
E it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-second Year of the Republic of India
as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Puducherry Appropriation Act, 2021.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of the Union territory of Puducherry
there may be paid and applied sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the
Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the sum of two hundred fifty-six crores,
four lakhs and ten thousand rupees towards defraying the several charges which will
come in course of payment during the financial year 2020-21, in respect of the services
specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
