A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2013-14
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-fourth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (No. 4 ) Act, 2013.
2.   From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India  there may be  paid and applied
sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate
to the sum of seven thousand four hundred ninety-nine crore and forty-two lakh rupees only
towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment during the
financial  year 2013-14, in respect of the services specified  in column 2 of the Schedule.
3.  The sums authorised to be paid and applied  from and out of the Consolidated  Fund
of  India  by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
