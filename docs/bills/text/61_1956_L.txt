STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
It is proposed to amend the Representation of the People Act, 
1950, in order that the displaced persons who migrated from Pakistan 
can exercise their right of franchise in the next general elections 
in accordance with the assurance given in Parliament when· the 
Citizenship Bill was under discussion. Under the provisions of the 
Representation of the People Act, 1950, a person's name cannot be 
included in the electoral roll of a constituency, unless he is a citizen 
of India and was ordinarily resident in that constituency on the 1st 
March, 1956. The drst step required to be taken, therefore, is to 
register the displaced persons as citizens of Ind:a. The Citizenship 
Rules have come into force on the 7th July, 1956, and arrangements 
have been made for the registration of peruons as citizens of India 
under section 5 (1) (a) of the Citizenship Act, 1955. Considering the 
number of such persons in the various States (and particularly in 
West Bengal where it runs into lakhs), the work of registration is 
not likely to be completed before the end of October, 1956. By that 
time, the electoral rolls for the year 1956 now under preparation are 
expected to be ready for final publication in most places. It will not, 
therefore, be possible, without some special provision, for the 
el~tion machinery to include in the electoral roll the names of dis-
placed persons who would be registered ascttizens of India under 
section 5 (1) (a) of the said Act. 
2. In these circumstances, the best course appears to be to amend 
the Representation of the People Act, 1950, making a special and 
temporary provision to achieve the object in view. It is proposed 
that every authority registering as citizens under section 5 (1) (a) 
of the Citizenship Act persons ordinarily resident in a constituency 
should by the 31st December, 1956, send to the electoral registration 
officer for the constituency a list of all persons so registered who, on 
the 1st March, 1956, were not less than 21 years ot &ge. On receiving 
the list, the electoral registration officer shall, after making due 
inquiries, direct the inclusion in the electoral roll of .the names of all 
persons included in the list who, in his opinion, are entitled to be 
registered in the electoral roll for the constituency. This special 
procedure will be available only to those displaced persons who are 
registered as citizens under section 5 (1) (a) of the Citizenship Act 
before 1st November, 1956, and who were ordinarily resident in 
India in some constituency or other on 1st March, 1956.
