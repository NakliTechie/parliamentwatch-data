STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Eleventh General Intemational Conference of Weights and 
Measu~s, held at Paris in October, 1960, in which India participat-
ed, has recOmmended certain changes in the definitions of 'metre' 
and 'second' as given in the Standards of Weights and Measures Act, 
1956. It is also' proposed to recogniM 'nautical mile' as a unit of 
length in navigation by sea or air on the basis of international 
practice. The Bill is intended to give effect to these changes.
