STATEMENT OF' 6BJECTS AND REASONS 
The' Ninth Finance Commission, in its first report dated the 29th July. 
ffi8 recommended for the financial year 1989-90, that-
(1) tM States' share in the proceeds of shareable excise duties 
should be forty-five per cent. 
(U) forty per cent. of the net proceeds of shareable excise duties 
should be distributed among the States in the percentages recommend .. 
ed by it; and 
(iii) the balance of five per ·cent. of the net proceeds of shareable 
excise duties should be distributed among the deficit States in the 
percentages recommended by it. 
2. In its second report dated the 18th December, 1989, the Ninth Finance 
Commission has recommended that a sum equivalent to forty-five per 
cent. of the annual net proceeds of shareable Union duties of excise is 
to be paid to the States. This is a departure from the recommendation 
made by it in the first report. 
3. The Government has accepted the recommendations of the Ninth 
FinanCe Commission in its second report for the financial years 1990-95 
and consequently decided to implement the same. 
4. This Bill is intended to give effect to the aforesaid recommendations 
of the Commission.
