STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
In our country, many private recognized institutions are imparting nursing education.
Since the Government is not able to meet the demands of the students to pursue nursing
education, the private institutions have mushroomed. But the students who qualify from
recognized private nursing colleges are neither eligible for registration in the Employment
Exchanges nor given Employment in Government hospitals.
Therefore, in order to remove this anomaly, it is proposed to make nurses who have
certificate, Diplomas or Degrees from recognized private nursing institutions can also become
eligible for employment in Government hospitals.
Hence this Bill.
