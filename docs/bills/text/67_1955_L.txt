STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The objects of the present amendments in the First Schedule to 
the Indian Tariff Act, 1934 (XXXII of 1934) are-
(i) to grant protection to certain types and specifications 
of piston assembly, pistons, piston rings. and gudgeon pins, 
(ii) to continue or discontinue protection to certain indus-
tries, and 
(iii) to impose an import duty on aluminium scrap, 
on the advice of the Tariff Commission.
