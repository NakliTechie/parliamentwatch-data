STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Under section 23 of the Advocates Act, 1961, the Attorney General 
of India, the Solicitor General of India and the Additional Solicitor 
General of India have in that order pre-audience over aU other advo-
cates. A second Additional Solicit:>r General has been appointed for 
the better discharge of the legal business of Government and on the 
same terms and conditions of service as the Additional Solicitor General 
and with the same functions. It is, therefore, proper that his right of 
pre-audience should be recognised by Statute. Accordingly, it is pro-
posed to amend the Advocates Act, so as to give him the right of pre-
audience immediately after the other three Law Officers of the Govern-
ment of India. 
The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
