STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

At present, the dearness allowance received by employees of Government and other
organisations are considered as income and subject to tax. Actually dearess allowance is
given to meet the increasing cost of living. This is calculated after taking into account
prices of all essential commodities at 4 particular time. Already there is a resentment that.
hundred percent. compensation is not given to employees by way of dearness allowance.
Apart from this, it is also taxable, As a result, low paid employees have come under the
tax net. It is but appropriate that dearness allowance shoulé be exempted from taxation.
