STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Haemophilia is a genetic blood disorder in which the patient bleeds spontaneously
and excessively even from trivial injuries. Repeated bleeds into joints and muscles leads to
progressive crippling and if into vital organs like the brain, leads to death. Haemophilia
can be considered a disability as it disables and restricts patients right from birth from
leading a full and active life within the range considered normal for a human being. Hence,
this Bill seeks to provide equal opportunities to those persons who are suffering from

Haemophilia disease by including this disease under the meaning of disability. |
