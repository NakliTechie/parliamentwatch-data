STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Government of West Bengal, based on unanimous Motion | in
Assembly dated-25.11.1997, have agreed to include the ‘Chain’ community in the
Scheduled Castes.

‘The issue has been taken up by the Government of West Bengal with the Ui
Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and also with the National Commissi
Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. This community, unless included in the
Scheduled Castes, would be socially, culturally and economically deprived of all
rights which are guaranteed to the Scheduled Castes in the Constitution of India. 1
members of this community feel highly neglected and isolated from the mainstreat
the Scheduled Castes in so far as their rights are concerned.

The Bill seeks to include ‘Chain’ community in the Scheduled Castes (Order)
of the Constitution.
