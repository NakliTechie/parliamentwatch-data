STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the Constitu-
tion of India. to provide for the apprapriation out of the Consolidated 
Fund of India of the moneys required to meet the expenditure char,ed 
on the Consolidated Fund of India and the grants made by Lok Sabha 
in respect of the estimated expenditure of the Central Government on 
Railways, far the financial year 1970·71.
