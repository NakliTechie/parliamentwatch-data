STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

‘The Betwa River Board Act, 1976 was enacted for the establishment
of a Board for the creation of a reservoir at Rajghat by construction of
a dam on the Betwa river and for the regulation of such reservoir.
‘The reservoir thus constructed was named as Rajghat Reservoir. How-
ever, there is a tradition of naming the reservoirs and projects after
great national leaders and having regard to the fact that Rajghat Dam
Project is located near Jhansi, it is felt that it would be appropriate to
rename the said reservoir after the great nationalist warrior Maharani
Laxmibai. The proposal has the concurrence of the State Governments
of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh. To rename the Rajghat
Reservoir as “Rani Laxmibai Sagar”. it is necessary to amend the Betwa
River Board Act, 1976.
