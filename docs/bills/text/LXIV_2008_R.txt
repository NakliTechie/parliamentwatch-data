THE CONSTITUTION (AMENDMENT)  BILL, 2008
A
BILL
further to amend the Constitution of India .
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Constitution (Amendment) Act,  2008.
(2) It shall come into force with immediate effect.
2. For the title of part-I and article 1 of the Constitution of India the following shall
be substituted, namely:—
Part-I
The Nation And Its Territory
1. Name and territory—(1) Bharat i.e. Hindusthan is a nation extending from the
Himalayas to the Ocean.
2. In Part I of the Constitution for the heading "THE UNION AND ITS TERRITORY"
the heading "THE NATION AND ITS TERRITORY" shall be substituted.
3. In article 1 of the Constitution, for clause 1, the following clause shall be
substituted, namely:—
"(1) Bharat i.e. Hindusthan is a nation extending from the Himalayas to the
Ocean."

STA TEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
After the country had gained freedom, it might have been worthwhile to name the
country as ‘India’ for a while, but ultimately it is our age-old name that alone can express
our distinctive feature in the comity of nations and can instill in our people, a sense of
self-respect. Probably that is why the names like Chennai, Mumbai, etc. have begun to be
adopted.
At present, emergence of unreasonable linguistic and regional egos have dealt a
serious blow to the national integrity and these factors have caused confrontation in
place of the fraternity which is mentioned in the Preamble.
It has been admitted in the Preamble to make Bharat a Republic, not the Union of
States. This Bill is, therefore, submitted to make the title of part-I and article 1 in consonance
with the Preamble.
Hence this Bill.

PART I
THE UNION AND ITS TERRITORY
1. (1) India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States.
(2) The States and the territories thereof shall be as specified in the First Schedule.
(3) The territory of India shall comprise—
(a) the territories of the States;
(b) the Union territories specified in the First Schedule; and
(c) such other territories as may be acquired.
