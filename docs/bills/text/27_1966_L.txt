STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of articles 204 (1) and 205 of 
the Constitution and the Proclamation issued under article 356 of the 
Constitution in respect of the State of Kerala on the 24th March, 
1965, as extendcJ by Parliament for a period of six months with 
effect from the 11th November, 1963, to provide for the appropriation 
out of the COTISCIlidated Fund of the State of Kerala of the moneys 
required to m~et the supplementary expenditure charged on the 
Consolidated Fund of the State of Kerala and the grants made by 
the Lok Sabha for expenditure of the Government of Kerala for 
the financial year 1965-66. 
SACHINDRA CHAUDHURI. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 207 
OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. F.5 (15) -B /65 dated the 24th February, 1966 
from Shri Cachindra Chaudhuri. Minister of Finance to the Secretary. 
Lok Sabha.] 
By virtue of the Proclamation dated the 24th March, 1965, issued 
under Article 356 of the Corutitution, as extended by the Parliament 
for a period of six months with effect from the 11th November. 1965, 
the President having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to authorise payment and appropriation of certain 
further sum>: from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State 
of Kerala for the services of the year ending on the 31st day of 
March, 1966 recommends the introduction of the Kerala Appropria-
tion Bill, 1966 in the Lok Sabhi and also recommends to the Sabha 
the consideration of the Bill under Article 207 (1) and (3) of the 
Constitv.tion read with Article 205 thereof. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in the Lok Sabha after all the Sup-
plementary Demands for Grants for 1965-66 have been voted.
