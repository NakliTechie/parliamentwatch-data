STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Despite great efforts made to ameliorate the sufferings of women, they continue to
remain socially and economically weak, They, therefore, deserve special consideration and
ities for improving the quality of their life. The percentage of women in employmentis
y low. There is job reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes and Backward
ses in order to make up leeway, But no such reservation exists for women, who consti-
tute a large segment of socially and educationally backward population, They are denied
social justice, हि

In order that women may enjoy the fruits of social justice, the job reservation facilities
should be extended to women as in the case of other weaker sections of the society,

There is also a move to reserve thirty-three per cent of seats in Lok Sabha and State
Assemblies in favour of women. It is but appropriate that reservation should be extended to
services also.

Hence this Bill
