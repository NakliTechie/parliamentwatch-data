STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Atrocities on women are increasing. It has often been observed that many Govern-
ment officers, after indulging in acts of eve teasing and other misconduct by taking
advantage of their post and official position and also for the lack of evidence too, go scot
free from the clutches of Law. An incident is in the news now-a-days in which Chief of
Police force of a State is alleged to have raped a girl player. The astonishing fact is that the
said officer became Chief of State Police after that incident.

At present, there is a provision of a sentence of ten years for such offences. Purpose of
this Bill is to make the sentence more rigorous by amending the Indian Penal Code.

Hence this Bill.
