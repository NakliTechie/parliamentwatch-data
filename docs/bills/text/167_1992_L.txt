STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 1991 provided for several
amendments in the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972, to make it more
comprehensive and effective, Section 26 of the Amendment Act provided
for insertion of a new Chapter IV A in the Act enabling the Central Gov-
ernment to set up a Central Zoo Authority for overseeing the func-
tioning and management of the zoos in the country. Under new sectior
38H, it was provided that the no z00 would be operated without being

to the Central Zoo Authority in the prescribed form on payment of
prescribed fees within a period of six months from the date of such com-
mencement. The aforesaid provisions were brought into force from the

4th February, 1992.
