STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) ot the Constitu-
tion of India, read with article 116 thereof, to provide for the appro-
priation from and out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the money. 
required to meet the expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund and 
the grants made in advance by the Lok Sabha in respect of the estimated 
expenditure of the Central Government, excluding Railways, for a part 
of th~' financi~ year 1984-85.

CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. F. 2(26-B(0) 184, dated the 8th March, 1984-
from Shri Pranab Kumar Mukherjee, Minister of Finance to the Secretary-
General, Lok Sabh9.] 
The President, having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to authorise payment and appropriation ot certain sums 
from and out of the Consolidated Fund of India for the services of a part 
of the financial year 1984-8~', recommends the introduction of the Appro-
priation (Vote on AccC1un~) Bill, 1984 in Lok Sabha and also recommends 
to Lok Sabha the consideration of the Bm under article 117 (1) and (3) 
of the Constitution of India read with article 116(2) thereof. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in Lok Sabha immediately after the 
Demands for Gran~s, 'on account' for expenditure of the Central Gov-
ernmt'nt (excluding Railways) for the year 1984-85 have been voted.
