This Bill is. introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the 
Constitution of India, read with article 116 thereof, to provide for 
the appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the 
moneys required to meet the expenditure charged on the Consoli-
dated Fund and the grants made in advance by the Lok Sabha in 
respect of the estimated expenditure of the Central Government, 
excluding Railways, for a part of the financial year 1957-58.
