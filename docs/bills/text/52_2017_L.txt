A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2016-17 for the
purposes of Railways.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-eighth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (Railways) No. 2 Act, 2017.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied
sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate
to the sum of four thousand seven hundred seventy-seven crore, twenty lakh and twenty
thousand rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment
during the financial year 2016-17 in respect of the services relating to Railways specified in
column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated
Fund of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in
the Schedule in relation to the said year.
