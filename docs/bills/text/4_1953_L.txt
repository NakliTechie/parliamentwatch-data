STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Section SA of the Indian Tariff Act, 1984 (XXXII of 1934) confers 
powers on the Central Govertunent to levy protective duties by notifica-
tion on the recommendation of the Tariff Commission to be followed by 
formal legislation. This aection will cease to be valid on the 28th March. 
1953. SlUlh powers are still required and the BUl seeks to remove the 
reatrictio~ placed on the life of the said section. It is also intended to 
make an amendment in the procedure which wUl permit of introducing a 
Bill in the session' of Parliament next following the date of issue of 8 
notification levying additional duties of customs on imported goods in 
accordance with the Tariff Commission's recommendation for the protec-
tion of an indigenous industry. . 
2. The Bill also seeks to grant protection to the Ball bearings industry 
on the advice of the Tariff Commission.
