B
E it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-fifth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Delhi Appropriation  Act, 2014.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of the National Capital Territory of Delhi,
there may be paid and applied further sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the
Schedule, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of three hundred sixty-three crores
five lakh and sixty-four thousand rupees towards defraying the several charges which will
come in  course of payment during the financial year 2013-14, in respect of the services
specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of the National Capital Territory of Delhi by this Act shall be appropriated for the services
and purposes expressed in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
