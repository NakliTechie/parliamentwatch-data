Act, 1934 (32 of 1934), in order to continue up to the 31st Decem-

force at present up to the 31st December, 1969. The opportunity 
has also been taken to effect a slight rationalisation of the existing 
rates of protective duty on (i) Silk yarn, including silk sewing 
thread, and silk waste [Items Nos. 46 (1), 47 and 47 (1)] and 
(ii) Silk fabrics (Item No. 48), by adopting a single rate of duty in 
each case instead of the varying rates in force at present, as the 
difl~rences have ceased to have any special significance. The modi-
