This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the Consti-
tution of India to provide for the appropriation out of the Consolidated 
Fund of India o'I the moneys required to meet the expenditure charged 
on the Consolidated Fund and the grants made by the Lok Sabha for 
expenditure of the Central Government, excluding Railways, for the 
financial year 1980-81. 
R. VENKATARAMAN.

Constitution, the introduction of the Appropriation (No.3) Bill, 1980 in
