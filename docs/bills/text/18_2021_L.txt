A
BILL
further to amend the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950 to modify the list of
Scheduled Castes in the State of Tamil Nadu.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-second Y ear of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order (Amendment)
Act, 2021.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
Short title and
commencement.
Bill No. 18-F of 2021

2
2. In the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950, in the Schedule, in
Part XVI.—Tamil Nadu,—
(a) for entry 17, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:—
"17. DevendrakulaVelalar [Devendrakulathan, Kadaiyan (excluding in the
coastal areas of Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Ramanathapuram, Pudukottai,
Thanjavur, Tiruvarur and Nagapattinam districts), Kalladi, Kudumban, Pallan,
Pannadi, Vathiriyan]";
(b) for entry 26, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:—
"26. Kadaiyan (in the districts of Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi,
Ramanathapuram, Pudukottai, Thanjavur, Tiruvarur and Nagapattinam)";
(c) entries 28, 35, 49, 54 and 72 shall be omitted.
