PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 117 OF THE 
CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[CoPy of letter No. F. 5(4)-B/65, dated the 27th April, 1965 from 
Shri T. T. Krishnamachari, Minister of Finance to the Secretary, 
Lok Sabba.] 
The President having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to provide for the authorisation of appropriation of 
moneys out of the Consolidated Fund of India to meet the amounts 
spent on certain services during the financial year ended on the 31st 
day of March, 1963, in excess of the amounts granted for the said 
services and for that year recommends under clauses (1) and (3) of 
article ll7 of the Constitution read with sub-clause (2) of article 115 
thereOf, the introduction of the Appropriation (No.4) Bill, 1965, in 
the Lok Sabha and also recommends to the Lok Sabha the considera-
tion of the Bill.
