This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the Constiw-· 
tiOD of India, read with -artic1e 115 thereof; to ·provide for the appropriation .•

tion of the Appropriation (No. 4') Bill, 1984 in Lok Sabha a'1d also re-
comm.ends to Lok Sabha the consideration of the Bill under articl. 
117(1) and (3) of the Constitution read with article 115(2) hereof.

tary Demands for Grants for 1964-tl5 have been voted.
