A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2009-10 for the
purposes of Railways.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixtieth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (Railways) No. 4 Act, 2009.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and
applied sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting
in the aggregate to the sum of seven hundred thirty-one crores, thirty lakhs and sixty
thousand rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of
payment during the financial year 2009-10, in respect of the services relating to
Railways specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated
Fund of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed
in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
