4. The duties proposed in the Bill are 4 annas per sq. yd. on 
coarse and medium cloth and 6 annas per sq. rd. on fine and super-
fine cloth. It is not intended, however, imme(ilately to enforce these 
rates to the full extent. Some increases are being made now and 
it is the intention that a constant watch be kept ()n the movement of 
prices and to the extent that excessive profits are made in relation 
to the fair ex-mill price as worked out on the basis of the formula 
of ·the Tariff Commission, an increasing percentage of such profits 
may be taken away as excise duty. Correspondingly, if prices indi-
cate a downward trend, necessary adjustments will be made in the 
excise duty.
