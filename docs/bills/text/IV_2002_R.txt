THE CONSTITUTION (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2002
A
BILL
further to amend the Representation of the People Act 1951.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-third Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Amendment) Act, 2002.
2. After article 21 of the Constitution, the following articles shall be inserted,
namely:—
21A. All [citizens] families shall have the right to have a dwelling unit
having minimum of two rooms with basic facilities to be provided by the State
at affordable prices.
21B. All adult citizens shall have the right to work as per their calibre,
qualifications and physical fitness, and earn their livelihood.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Shelter is one of the basic necessities for sustaining the life. Right to live and other
similar rights guaranteed by the Constitution for the well being of citizens would be
worthless if a citizen have no shelter to live in and no employment to earn his livelihood.
Growing unemployment has led to resorting to arms and indulgence in extremist activities
by youths of the country. Hence it is necessary that right to shelter and employment is
guaranteed and made a fundamental right.
Hence, this Bill.

FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM
Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to insert a new article 21A in the Constitution, which
makes the State responsible for providing a two rooms flat with modern facilities to all
citizens. Thus, a huge financial burden will have to be borne by the State in implementing
this obligation under the proposed provision.
It is estimated that the total financial burden would be about rupees 40,000 crores
per annum to be shared between Central and State Governments.
