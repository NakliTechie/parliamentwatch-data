STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

A huge amount of money is allegedly being spent to become the
Chairpersons of panchayats at intermediate or district levels. Instances of
fraud and manipulation have been observed in these elections of the
chairpersons at intermediate or district levels and the principles of democracy
are not followed. It is disgusting that elected members are involved in such
derogatory practices. The candidates are allegedly involved in malpractices
and corruption to become the heads of such bodies, which are supposed to
render services to the people at the grass root level.

The persons elected to such higher posts with aid of money power
cannot be expected to discharge their duties in a faithful manner.

If the Chairpersons of panchayats at block level and district level are
elected directly by the people as Mayors and Corporators are elected directly
by the people, the large scale manipulations and corrupt practices in the elections
to the post of Chairpersons of block and district panchayats can be checked.

Therefore, the Bill seeks to amend the Constitution with a view to
provide that the Chairpersons of panchayats at intermediate and district levels
shall be elected by direct elections.

Hence this Bill.
