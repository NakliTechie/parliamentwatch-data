STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Indian Railway Companies Act, 1895 enables a Railway Company to pay interest
on its paid-up share capital out of capital and treat the amount so paid as the total cost of the
project. A similar provision relating to power of company to pay interest out of capital in
certain cases exists in section 208 of the Companies Act, 1956. The provisions of section 208
of the Companies Act, 1956 are applicable in case of all the companies registered under the
Companies Act, 1956 including the Railway Companies. Consequent upon enactment of the
Companies Act, 1956 the Indian Railway Companies Act, 1895 has become redundant.

2. The Bill, therefore, seeks to repeal the Indian Railway Companies Act, 1895.
