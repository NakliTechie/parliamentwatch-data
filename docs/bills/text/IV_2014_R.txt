A
BILL
further to amend the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950 to modify the list
of Scheduled Castes in the States of Haryana, Karnataka and Odisha and the
Constitution (Dadra and Nagar Haveli) Scheduled Castes Order, 1962.
B
E  it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-SixthYear of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1.  This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Orders
(Amendment) Act, 2015.
2. In the Schedule to the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950,—
(a) in PART V. —Haryana, for entry 19, substitute,—
“19. Kabirpanthi, Julaha, Kabirpanthi Julaha”;
(b) in PART VII.—Karnataka, for entry 23, substitute,—
“23.  Bhovi, Od, Odde, V addar, Waddar, V oddar, Woddar, Bovi (Non-
Besta), Kalluvaddar, Mannuvaddar”;
