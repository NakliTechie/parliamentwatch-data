M emorandum explaining the modifications contained in the Bill to replace
the Prevention oj Illixnt Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Ordinance^  1988.
The Prevention of Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic  
Substances Bill, 1988, which seeks to repeal and replace the Prevention  
of Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Ordinance,  
1988, proposes to make the modifications as set out in paragraphs 2 and 3,  
apart from modifications of a consequential or drafting nature, in the  
provisions contained in the said Ordinance.
2. In clause 10(1) of the Bill [which corresponds to section 10(1) of  
the Ordinance] the maximum period of detention without obtaining the  
opinion of an Advisory Board is six months as against one year in the  
Ordinance. This has been done to bring the period in consonance with  
the period specified in sub-clause (2). In the Explanation to clause  
10(1), in clause (i), the reference to the coastal States have been omitted  
so as to widen the scope of the Indian customs waters. Further a new  
sub-clause (vi) has been added to the Explanation so as to empower the  
Central Government to specify, by notification in the Official Gazette,  
any other area or customs station to be area vulnerable to illicit traffic.
3. In clause 15 of the Bill [which corresponds to section 15 of the  
Ordinance] it has been provided that no order of detention shall be made  
on any of the grounds specified in sub-section (1) of section 3 of the  
Conservation of Foreign Exchange and Prevention of Smuggling Activi­
ties Act, 1974 on which an order of detention may be made under section  
3 of the Jamrtiu and Kashmir Prevention of Illicit Traffic in Narcotic  
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Ordinance, 1988. This is because  
the Jammu and Kashmir Ordinance closely follows the Central Ordi­
nance and the same had been promulgated a day after the Central Ordi­
nance was promulgated. As the Conservation of Foreign Exchange and  
Pr^^ve^fttidti of Smuggling Activities Act, 1974 applies to the State of  
Jammu and Kashmir also, it is necessary to provide that no order of  
detention shall be made on any of the grounds specified in section 3(1)  
of that Act on which an order of detention may be made under section 3  
of the Jammu and Kashmir Ordinance.
4. Other modifications contained in the Bill are of clarificatory and  
Consequental in nature.
