THE ST. JOHN AMBULANCE ASSOCIATION (INDIA) TRANSFER

A
BILL
to repeal the St. John Ambulance Association (India) Transfer of Funds Act, 1956.

Be it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—

1. This Act may be called the St. John Ambulance Association (India) Transfer of
Funds (Repeal) Act, 2001.

2. The St. John Ambulance Association (India) Transfer of Funds Act, 1956 is
hereby repealed.

Short title.

Repeal of Act
21 of 1956.


STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The St. John Ambulance Association (India) Transfer of Funds Act, 1956 was enacted
to provide for the transfer of a portion of the funds of the St. John Ambulance Association
(India) to St. John Ambulance Association (Pakistan). The Act has served its purpose and
has become redundant. In 1998, a Commission headed by Shri P. C. Jain for review of
administrative laws, regulations and procedure also recommended repeal of this Act.

2. In view of the above, the St. John Ambulance Asssociation (India) Transfer of

Funds (Repeal) Bill, 2001 seeks to repeal the St. John Ambulance Association (India)
Transfer of Funds Act, 1956.

C. P. THAKUR.
