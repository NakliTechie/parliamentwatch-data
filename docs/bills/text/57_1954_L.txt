STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The object of the present Bill is to amend the First Schedule to the 
Indian Tariff Act, 1934 (XXXII of 1934) in order to grant protection 
to the Automobile Leaf Spring industry and to continue or discontinue 
protection to certain other industries on the advice of the Tariff Com-
mission.
