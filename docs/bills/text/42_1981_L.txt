STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of artieles 204(1) and 
206 o'f the Constitution, read with the Proclamatfon issued 
under article 356 of the Constitution in respect of the State of 
Manipur on the 28th February, 1981, to provide for the appro-
priation out of the Consolidated Funa of the State of Manipur 
of . the moneys required to meet the expQ,llditure charged on 
the Consolidated Fund of the State of Manipur and the grants 
made in advance by the Lok Sabha in respect of the estimated 
expenditure of the Government of Manipur, for a part of the 
financial year 1981-82. 
R. VENKA TARAMAN .

Appropriation (Vote on Account) Bill, 1981, in and the consi-
deration of the Bill by Lok Sabha.
