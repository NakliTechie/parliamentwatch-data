STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
The Rights of Persons with Disability Act, 2016 was enacted to expand the scope of
the then existing law to ensure equitable rights for persons with disabilities. Under the
existing provisions of the Act, several terminological and structural issues cause uncertainty
and hinder the dissemination of such rights to those deserving.
Hence this Bill.
