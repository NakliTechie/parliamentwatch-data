BILL
further to amend the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1.  (1) This Act may be called the Maternity Benefit (Amendment)  Act, 2008.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
2.  In the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, for section 8, the following section shall be
substituted, namely:—
“8. (1) Every woman entitled to maternity benefit under this Act shall also be
entitled to receive from her employer a medical bonus of one thousand rupees, if no
pre-natal confinement and post-natal care is provided for by the employer free of
charge.
