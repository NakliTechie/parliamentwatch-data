STA TEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
With about two-third of our population dependent on agriculture and agriculture
sector accounting for more than twenty percent of the Gross Domestic Product, the importance
of agriculture in our country cannot be over-emphasized.
At present agriculture is a State subject included in List II of the Seventh Schedule to
the Constitution as item No. 14. As such, the Union Government does not have direct
legislative or executive control. Recently a number of problems have come up in the agriculture
sector, which have affected the agricultural produce, and in the recent years, agriculture has
registered a slow and tardy growth. In order to address the agriculture related problems, it is
necessary that uniform measures should be followed for promoting agriculture and also for
assisting the agriculturists throughout the country. For this it is essential that agriculture
should be transferred from List II (State List) to List III (Concurrent List) in the Seventh
Schedule to the Constitution so that the Union Government may play a more proactive role
in the agrarian sector and put in place farmer-friendly schemes uniformly applicable throughout
the country.
Hence this Bill.
