FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM 
Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to amend article 334 of the Constitution to 
provide for the continuance of the representation of the Anglo-Indian 
community in the Lok Sabha and in the Legislative Assemblies of the 
States by nomination for a further period of ten years. It is estimated 
that the recurring expenditure on the salaries and allowances and other 
concessions, of the two representatives of the Anglo-Indian community 
in the Lok Sabha under article 331, will be of the order of Rs. one 
lakh per annum. 
2. There will be no non-recurring expenditure.
