This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 204 (I) of the Constitu-

the Consolidated Fund of the State of West Bengal and the grants made 
by the Lok Sabha in respect of the estim~ted expenditure of thE" 
Government of West Bengal for the financial year. 1970-71.
