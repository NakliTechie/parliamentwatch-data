THE KARNATAKA APPROPRIATION (VOTE ON ACCOUNT)
BILL, 2008
A
BILL
to provide for the withdrawal of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the
State of Karnataka for the services of a part of the financial year 2008-09.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of  the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Karnataka Appropriation (Vote on Account) Act, 2008.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Karnataka there may be
withdrawn sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in
the aggregate to the sum of  twenty-nine thousand and twenty-seven crores, thirty lakhs
and seventy-seven thousand  rupees towards defraying the several charges which will
come in course of payment during the financial year 2008-09 in respect of the services
specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be withdrawn from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the
State of Karnataka by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed
in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
