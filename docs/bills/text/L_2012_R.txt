2
STATEMENT  OF  OBJECTS  AND  REASONS
The sub-section (3) of Section 1 of the Employees' Provident Funds and
Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 provides its applicability to all the establishments
having engaged twenty or more persons.
In India millions of employees work in such establishments where less than
twenty persons are engaged and such persons are deprived of various social security
benefits such as pension, gratuity and provident fund. They should also be entitled
to such benefits under the Act for its proper implementation.
The Bill proposing amendments to Section 1 of the Act seeks to achieve the
aforesaid objectives.
Hence, this Bill.
