A
BILL
further to amend the Central Universities Act, 2009.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-fifth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Central Universities (Amendment) Act, 2014.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
2.  After section 3A of the Central Universities Act, 2009 (hereinafter referred to as the
principal Act), the following section shall be inserted, namely:—
"3B.  (1) The Central University of Bihar established under sub-section (4) of
section 3 shall be known as the Central University of South Bihar, having its territorial
jurisdiction extending to the territory in the south of River Ganges in the State of Bihar,
as specified in the First Schedule to this Act.
