STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Bill seeks to replace the Indian Tariff (Amendment) Ordi-
nance, 1965 (7 of 1965), and the Indian Tariff (Amendment) Ordi-
nance, 1966 (1 of 1966), promulgated by the President respectively 
on the 31st December, 1965 and the 1st February, 1966. 
2. The Indian Tariff (Amendment) Ordinance, 1965, amended the 
First Schedule to the Indian Tariff Act, 1934 (32 of 1934) in respect 
of Items Nos. 72 (35), 72 (36) and 72 (37), thereby de-protecting the 
Ball Bearings Industry with effect from the 1st January, 1966. The 
Indian Tariff (Amendment) Ordinance, 1966, amended the said 
Schedule by inserting a new item as Item No. 27 (10) with a view 
to levy protective duty on petroleum, crude, at the rate of 20 per 
cent. ad valorem.
