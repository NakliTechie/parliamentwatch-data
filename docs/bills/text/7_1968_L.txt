STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND R!ASONS 
Under section 109 (2) of the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 
If51, the Corporation has to de'terinine the rates of municipal taxes 
to be levied in the following year before the fifteenth day of Feb-
ruary each year. The difficult ways and means position of the Cor-
poration rendered it urgently necessary to confer on the Corpora-
tion before that date powers 'to impose general tax at a higher rate 
while ensuring at the same time that the burden of the tax is dis-
tdbuted on a graduated scale according to the prinCiple of ability 
to pay. Accordingly an Ordinance was promulgated by the Presi-
dent on the 3rd February, 1968 raising the maximum ceiling rate 
of general tax from 20 per cent to 30 per cent and enabling the 
Corporation to levy the tax on a graduated. scale. 
2. The Bill seeks to replace the Ordinance by an Act of Parlia-
