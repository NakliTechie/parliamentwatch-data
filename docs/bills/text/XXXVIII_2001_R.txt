STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Drinking water is a basic necessity for sustaining life. Right to life and other
guarantees for the well-being of the citizens enshrined in the Constitution are meaningless
for the millions of persons who have no access to safe drinking water. It is necessary that
right to safe drinking water is guaranteed and made a fundamental right.

Hence this Bill.
