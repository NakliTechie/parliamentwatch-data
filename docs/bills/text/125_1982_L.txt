STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is a sequel to the Sugar Cess (Amendment) BUl, 1982. For 
the purpose of generating the necessary funds for maintaining buffer 
stocks of sugar, the Sugar Cess (Amendment) Bill, 1982, seeks to amend 
the Sugar Cess Act, 1982, for increasing the rate at which cess may be 
levied under that Act on sugar. This Bill seeka to amend the Sugar 
Development Fund Act, 19~, to provide that the Sugar Development 
Fund under -the Act, to which the proceeds of the duty of excise levied 
and collected under the Sugar Cess Act, 1982 as reduced by the cost of 
collection. are required to be credited after due appropriation made by 
Parliament by law, may be utilised also for the purpose of defraying 
some holding costs and interest charges to sugar mills for building up 
and maintenance of buffer stocks of sugar. 
2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
