A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated
Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2011-12  for the purposes of
Railways.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation  (Railways) No. 2  Act, 2011.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied sums
not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the
sum of one lakh ninety-seven thousand five hundred one crores, sixty-seven lakhs and
thirty-three thousand rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in
course of payment during the financial year 2011-12, in respect of the services relating to
Railways specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
