STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

‘The tribal community, ‘Kurumans’ is listed as a Scheduled Tribe in the Constitution
(Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950. There are other tribal communities known by various other
similar names like Kurumba, Kurumban, Kuruma, etc. living in the States of Tamil Nadu,
‘Andhra Pradesh and Kerala. The Revenue Officials of these States were issuing community
certificates to the persons belonging to these communities till mid-nineties since verification
of old records relating to year 1880 and earlier periods reveals that these people were termed
as Kurumans, a Scheduled Tribe in view of their social, economicand cultural backwardness.

Recently some people represented against treating Kurumba, Kurumbar, Kurumban as
Kuromans listed in Scheduled Tribes Order, 1950. On the basis of this unsubstantiated
information, the Revenue authorities stopped issuing them the community certificates. Later
on, Tamil Nadu Government issued order directing revenue authority to treat these people 85
Scheduled Tribes. But the Ministry of Home Affairs of Government of India, opined that

such an order ४

violation of the established convention, as the Order can be issued only

after necessary amendments are made in the Constitu ‘Scheduled Tribes) Order.

‘Therefore, to give persons belonging to Kurumba, Kurumban, Kurombar and Kuruma
tribes social justice and also considering that people of these tribes are generally inter-
related with people belonging to Kuruman tribes by marriage, it is proposed to amend the

Order suitably to include these tribes.
