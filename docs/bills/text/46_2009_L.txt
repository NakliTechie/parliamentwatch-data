STA TEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Democracy is considered to be the best of all types of governance and voting is
an instrument to ensure democracy. “Majority is always right” is the guiding principle
of this form of governance. Every democratic system is based on the participation of
the citizens, who are the real soul of democracy. They give legitimacy to this system.
Therefore, high turnout during elections is desirable. India is the largest democracy in
the world. However, it has been observed during general elections in the recent past
that the number of actual voters casting votes is far less than the eligible voters. It is a
disturbing trend and is an obstacle in establishing sound democratic system in the
country. There have been efforts in the past to increase voter turnout and encourage
voting at elections but desired results have not been achieved.
It is, therefore, necessary that a duty is cast upon every citizen to exercise his
franchise during elections. The Bill seeks to amend the Constitution with a view to
make it fundamental duty of every eligible voter to cast vote at elections to the House
of the People, Legislative Assemblies of States and local bodies.
Hence this Bill.
