STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

‘The tribal communities of Naik, Nayak, Beda, Bedar and Valmiki in
the State of Karnataka were not included in the list of Scheduled Tribes
of that State and accordingly these tribal communities did not derive
the benefits of various safeguards provided for the Scheduled Tribes
in the Constitution. The State Government of Karnataka had recom-
mended the inclusion of these communities in the list of Scheduled
Tribes in relation to that State. There was persistent demand for the
inclusion of these communities in the list of Scheduled Tribes in that
State. As those Tribes were included in the Constitution (Scheduled
Tribes) Orders (Amendment) Ordinance, 1991 (Ord. 3 of 1991), which
was promulgated on the 19th day of April, 1991, it is proposed to bring
this legislation into force from that date.

2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above objects.

FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to include Naik, Nayak, Beda, Bedar and
Valmiki in the State of Karnataka in the list of Scheduled Tribes of that
State. This shall involve additional recurring and non-recurring expe
diture for the benefits to be provided to the persons belonging to these
communities under the various existing schemes. It is not possible to
estimate at this stage the likely expenditure to be involved on this
account. However, the expenditure, whether recurring or non-recurring,
will be met out of the budgetary grants of the Ministry of Welfare.

2. The Bill, when enacted, will not involve any other recurring oF
non-recurring expenditure.
