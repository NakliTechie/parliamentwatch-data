STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
In pursuance of resolutions passed by the State Legislatures con-
cerned under article 252 of the Constitution, this Bill seeks to make 
the provisions of the Estate Duty (Amendment) Act, 1958 (33 of 
1958), applicable to the levy of estate duty on agricultural lands in all 
the States except West Bengal, Orissa and Jammu and Kashmir. 
The Bill also seeks to make the provisions of the principal Act, 
namely, the Estate Duty Act, 1953, inapplicable to the levy of estate 
duty on agricultural lands in the State of Orissa from 1st April, 1959, 
in accordance with the resolution passed by the Orissa State Legisla-
tive Assembly. 
2. Advantage is being taken of this opportunity to remove any 
doubts as to the applicability of the three earlier Acts which made 
certain amendments in the Estate Duty Act, 1953, in so far as such 
amendments relate to estate duty on agricultural land.
