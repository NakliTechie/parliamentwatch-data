¥ STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Consumer Protection Act enacted in 1986 suffers from certain loopholes. Some
of the existing provisions are cumbersome and the Act is without adequate teeth. The Bill
seeks to provide more teeth to the Act and simplify certain provisions.

FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

Clause 3 of the Bill provides that the Central Council shall meet at least four |
in a year. Clause 4 provides for establishment of separate district forum in every city
having @ population of more than one lakh. The Bill, therefore, if enacted, will in
expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of India. It is likely to involve an annual
ring expenditure of about rupees fifty crore.

A non-recurring expenditure of about rupees one hundred crore is also likely to be
involved.
