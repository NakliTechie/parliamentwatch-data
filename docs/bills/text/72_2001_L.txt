Be it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—

1. This Act may be called the Cinematograph (Amendment) Act, 2001. Short title,

152, 2. In section 58 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952, after sub-section (1), the following
5 sub-section shall be inserted, namely:—
“(1A) A film shall not be certified for public exhibition if it contains scenes Amendment of
depicting song and dance and fight sequences in places of worship or scenes hurting section SB.
religious sentiments.”.


STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

In the recent past, many films have been released depicting scenes of song and
dance sequences in places of worship. Places of religious worship should not be used for
such scenes, Religious sentiments should not be hurt. No film should be certified if it
depicts scenes likely to hurt religious sentiments. The Bill seeks accordingly to amend the
Cinematograph Act. के

Hence this Bill.
