STA TEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of sections 29(1) (a) and 30 
of the Government of Union Territories Act, 1963 and the Order of 
the President issued on the 3rd December, 1966 under section 51 of 
that Act to provide for the appropriation out of the Consolidated 
Fund of the Union Territory of Goa, Daman and Diu of the moneys 
required to meet the supplementary grants made by the Lok Sabha 
for expenditure of the Government of Goa; Daman and Diu for the 
ftnancial year 1966-67. 
MORARJI DESAI.

MENT OF UNION TERRITORIES ACT, 1963
