STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the 
Constitution of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provitle for 
the appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the 
moneys required to meet the supplementary expenditure charged on 
the Comolidatett Fund of India and the grants rnade by the Lok Sabha 
for expenditure of the Central Government, excluding Railways, for 
the financial year 1968-69. 
MORABJI DESAI. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 117 OF 
THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. F.5(12)-B/68, dated the 5th August, 1968 from 
Shri Morarji Desai, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance 
to the Secretary, Lok Sabha.] 
The President having been informed of the subject matter of the 
proposed Bill to authorise payment and appropriation of certain fur-
ther sum's from and out of the Consolidated Fund of India for the 
services of the year en'ciing on the 31st day of March, 1969, recom-
mends the introduction of the Appropriation (No.3) Bill, 1968 in the 
Lok Sabha and also recommends to the Sabha the consideration of 
the Constitution read with article 115 thereof.

plementary Demands for Grants for 1968-69 have been voted.
