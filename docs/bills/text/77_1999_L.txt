STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS,

‘The wibe Deswalli Majhees are Santhal descendants. The tribe was |
such in the year 1921. In the year 1941 Deswalli Majhees were declared as ‘Tribes’
after that in the year 1952 and 1955 they were accepted as 'Santhal Descendants! and
got benefits and legitimate dues guaranteed by the Government of India. Things chang
from the year 1961 onwards. The tribes were denied ail the facilities meant for them at
till today they have been suffering in many ways.

Therefore, to give them justice and in view of their economic, educational
social backwardness, it is proposed in the Bill that the Deswalli Majhees tribe be incl
in the list of Scheduled Tribes for the State of West Bengal.
