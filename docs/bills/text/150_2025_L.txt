A 
BILL 
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the 
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2025-26. 
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-sixth Year of the Republic of 
India as follows:—    
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (No. 4) Act, 2025.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and
applied sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting 
in the aggregate to the sum of one lakh thirty-two thousand two hundred sixty-eight 
crore and eighty-five lakh rupees towards defraying the several charges which will 
come in course of payment during the financial year 2025 -26 in respect of the 
services specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
