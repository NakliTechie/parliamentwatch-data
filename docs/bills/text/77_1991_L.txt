STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Commissioner, Municipal Corporation of Delhi is required
under sub-section (3) inserted (section 2 of Act 10 of 1989) of section 126
of the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957 to issue notices to the
persons affected by revisions in the assessment list for property tax be-
fore the Ist day of April, 1991, The number of properties due for re-
assessment being large, the Commissioner was not in a position to issue
notices based on systematic surveys and proper scrutiny of documents.
With a view to ensuring that notices were issued after due verfication,
it was felt essential to extend time up to 31-3-1992 for the purpose of
initiating assessrient.

2. Similarly, section 67A (inserted by section 3 of Act 10 of 1989) of
the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, as in foree in New Delhi, enabled the
New Delhi Municipal Committee to amend the assessment lists for the
years commencing on the Ist day of April, 1983, the 1st day of April, 1989
and the Ist day of April, 1999 after giving notices to the persons affected
by the amendment of assessment, at any time before the Ist day of April,
1991. In order to ensure that notices for the aforesaid years and also
for the year commencing on the Ist day of April, 1991, were issued
after due verification, it was found necessary to extend time for issuing
notices up to the time before the Ist day of April, 1992 and thereby
enabling amendment in the assessment list,

3. In view of the above, an Ordinance was promulgated on thea
30th day of March, 1991 to amend the Deihi Municipal Corporation
Act, 1957 and the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, as in force in New Delhi.
4, The Bill seeks to replace the aforesaid Ordinance.
