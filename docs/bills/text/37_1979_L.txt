STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of section 29 (1) of the Govern-
ment of Uni on Territories Act, ] 963, read with section 31 thereof and 
the Order of the President made on the 12th November, 1978 under 
section 51 of that Act to provide for the appropriation out of the Con-
solidated Fund of the Union territory of Pondicherry of the moneys 
required to meet the expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund 
and the grants made in advance by the Lok Sabha in respect of the 
estimated expenditure of the Government of Pondicherry for a part 
of the financial year 1979-80. 
SATISH AGARWAL. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER THE GOVERNMENT OF 
UNION TERRITORIES ACT, 1963 
[Copy of letter No. F. 3(132)-B(S) 178, dated the 12th March., 1979 from 
Shri Satish Agarwal, Minister of State in the Minmtry of Finance to the 
Secretary, Lok Sabha.] 
The President, having been informed of the subject matter of the pro-
posed Bill to authorise payment and Appropriation Qf certain sums from 
and out of the Consolidated Fund of the Union Territory of Pondicherry 
for the services of a part of the financial year 1979-80 recommends, under 
sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 23 and section 31 of the Government 
of Union Territories Act, 1963, read with the Order made by him on the 
12th November, 1978 under section 51 of the said Act, the introduction 
of the Pondicherry Appropriation (Vote-on-Account) Bill, 1979 in and 
the consideration of the Bill by Lok Sabha. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in Lok Sabha immediately after the 
Demands for Grants 'on account' for expenditure of the Union Territory 
of Pondicherry for a part of the financial year 1979-80 have been voted.
