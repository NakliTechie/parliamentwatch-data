STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The object of the present Bill is to amend the First Schedule to 
the Indian Tariff Act, 1934 (XXXII of 1934) in order to grant pro-
tection to the Power and Distribution Transformers industry and to 
continue or discdntinue protection to certain other industries on the 
advice of the Tariff Commission.
