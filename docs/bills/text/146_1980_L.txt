I I MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION 
Clause 10 of the Bill empowers the Administrator to make rules for 
carrying out the provisions of the Bill. The matters in respect of which 
rules may be made relate to the form in which removal of ears of unclaim-
ed bodies may be authorised, preservation of removed ears, the manner 
in which publicity may be given to the provisions of the legislation and 
any other matter which is I'equired to be or may be prescribed. 
2. The matters in respect of which rules may be made are of adminis-
trative detail and procedure, and as such, the delegation of legislative 
power is of normal character.
