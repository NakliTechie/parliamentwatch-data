Financial Memorandum to the Monopolies and Restric­

Clause 3 of the Bill seeks to amend section 67 of the 
Act to empower the Central Government to make rules in 
relation to the conditions of service of the Membes of 
the Monopolies and Restrictive Trade Practices Commission 
retrospectively to a date not earlier than the 1st day 
of January 1986. It is proposed to revise the scales of 
pay and allowances of the Chairman and Members of the 
Commission from the 1st day of January 1986 in the light 
of the revision of scales of pay of Central Government 
officers consequent upon the recommend Rations of the Fourth 
Pay Commission. The Monopolies and Restrictive Trade Practi 
ces Commission (Conditions of Service of Chairman and 
Members) Rules/ 1970 were amended on 1-1.1988 to revise 
the said scales of pay from 1.1.1988/ as no retrospective 
effect could be given in the absence of such power. Now 
the scales of pay of the Chairman and Members of the Commi­
ssion will be revised from 1.1.1986 so as to give the 
benefit of such revised scales for the period f i ^ ^ 1.1.198^ 
to 31.12.1987. The additional expenditure for M f t i e  said 
period on pay and allowances of members is *ie*timated at 
Rs. 90,750/-.
The Bill/ if enacted, will not involve any other ex­
penditure of a recurring or non-recurring nature.
