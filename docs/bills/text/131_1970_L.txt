STATEMENT ···OF ,OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the Constitu· 
tion of India read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the appropria· 
tion out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required to meet 
the supplementary expenditure granted by the Lok Sabha for expenditure 
of the Central Government on Railways for the financial year 1970-71.
