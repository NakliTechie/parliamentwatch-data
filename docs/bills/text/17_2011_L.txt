A
BILL
to authorise payment and  appropriation of certain sums from and out  of  the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2011-2012.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-second Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation ( No. 2) Act, 2011.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied sums
not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the  aggregate  to the
sum of forty-six lakh six hundred five crore and ninety lakh
 rupees towards  defraying the
several charges which will come in course of payment during the financial year 2011-2012, in
respect of the services  specified in column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund
of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule in relation to the said year.
