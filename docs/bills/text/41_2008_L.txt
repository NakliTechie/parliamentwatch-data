BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act,
2008.
2. In section 2 of the  Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956, for the word
“twenty-five”, the word “thirty” shall be substituted.
