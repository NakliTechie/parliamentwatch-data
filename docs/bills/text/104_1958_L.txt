STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Poisons Act, 1919, which deals with the importation, posses-
sion and sale of poisons, does not extend to the territories comprised 
in the former Part B States. After the passing of the States Re-
organisation Act, 1956, it was found that in certain States like 
Andhra Pradesh and Madras, the Central Poisons Act was in force 
in the greater part of the State, but local Acts like the Hyderabad 
Poisons Act and the Travancore-Cochin Poisons Act continued to be 
in force in other parts which were included as a result of the re-
organisation. In consultation with the State Governments it is now 
proposed, in the interests of uniformity, to extend the Central 
Poisons Act to the territories comprised in the former Part B States 
and to repeal any corresponding laws in force therein. The Bill 
seeks to achieve this object.
