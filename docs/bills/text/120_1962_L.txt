STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Bill aeeks to amend the" fucllan Tariff Act, 1934 (32 of 19M) 
with a view to providing for automatic levy of countervailing duty 
on imported articles where an excise duty is leviable on similar 
articles produced indigenously. The Bill also provides for applying 
the normal rates of duty to certain items on which the duties were 
originally reduced due to commitments under the General Agreement 
on Tari1!s and Trade (GATT) but where such commitments no longer 
exist. 
Opporlunity 1& also being taken for making the Tariff Schedule 
more specific and rational by providing component parts of various 
articles being assessed under the same items as the articles them-
