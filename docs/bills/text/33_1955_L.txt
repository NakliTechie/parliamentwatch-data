STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Clause (v) of sub-section (n of section 4 of the Chartered 
Accountants Act, 1949, empowers the Council of the Institute of 
Chartered Accountants of India to recognise examinations and train-
ing conducted outside India as equivalent to those prescribed for 
members of the Institute for purposes of enrolment on its register. 
Since such recognition is generally to be granted only on a recipro-
cal basis, it is considered necessary, on grounds of public policy, 
that the Central Government should also have similar power for 
recognising foreign qualifications as equivalent to Indian ones. It 
is, therefore, proposed to amend the clause referred to so as to vest 
the Central Government also with power to recognise foreign 
qualifications for purposes of enrolment on the register of the 
Institute.
