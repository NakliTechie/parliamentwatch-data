STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Section 10 (1) of the Indian Lighthouse Act, 1927 (17 of 1927), 
empowers the Central Government, by notification in the Official 
Gazette, to prescribe rates, not exceeding four annas per ton, at which 
light-dues shall be payable by ships arriving at or departing from 
ports in India. The rate now prescribed under that section is four 
annas per ton. The existing maximum lim't of four annas per ton 
is not adequate for the services rendered and for the improvement 
and development of lighthouses. The Bill therefore proposes to 
increase the maximum limit to fifty naye paise per ton.
