THE  APPROPRIATION (No. 4) BILL, 2021
(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2021-22.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-second Year of the Republic of India
as follows:—
1.  This Act may be called the Appropriation (No. 4) Act, 2021.
2.  From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied
sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the
aggregate to the sum of one lakh eighty-seven thousand two hundred two crore and
forty-one lakh rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course
of payment during the financial year 2021-22 in respect of the services specified in
column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated
Fund of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed
in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
