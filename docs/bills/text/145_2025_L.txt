STATEMENT  OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
There have been persistent demands from the Soura-speaking and Ho-speaking people 
for the inclusion of the Soura and Ho language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution. 
At present Soura is a language spoken by Soura people which is an ethnic group in eastern 
India, mainly concentrated in the State of Odisha. Soura is also recognized as an endangered 
language by UNESCO. On the other hand, Ho is spoken by the Ho tribal people living in the 
Kolhan region encompassing the State of Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal and Assam. These 
languages have a rich ethnic and phylogenetic vocabulary which needs to be preserved. The 
Bill seeks to give effect to the same.
The Bill, therefore, seeks to amend the Constitution with a view to include “Ho” and 
“Soura” language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.
Hence this Bill.
