STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Bill is introduced in pursuance of aricles 204(1) and 205, 
read with article 238 of the Constitution and the Proclamation issued 
by the President on the 23rd March, 1956, in exercise of the powers 
conferred on him by article 356 thereof to provide for appropriation 
out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Travancore-Cochin of 
the moneys required to IQ.eet the Supplementary expenditure charged 
on the Consolidated Fund and the grants made by the House of the 
People for expenditure of the Government of Travancore-Cochin 
for the financial year 1Q56-57.
