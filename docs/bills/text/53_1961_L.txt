STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Newspaper (Price and Page) Act, 1956, which was enacted 
in pursuance of recommendations ()f the Press Commissioll, seeks to 
provide for the regul~ltion of the price charged for newspapers in 
relation to their pages and of matters connected therewith. Under 
section 1 (3) of the Act, it will cease to have effect on September 6, 
1961. 
The .A:ct enables the Central Government to make an Order pro-
viding for the regulation of the prices charged for newspapers in 
relation, to their maximum or minimum number of pages, sizes or 
areas and for the space to be allotted for' advertising matter in 
relation to other matters therein. The Daily Newspaper (Price and 
~ge) Order, 1960, was accordingly issued on October 24, 1960. 
Before it could come into force, the validity of the Act and the 
Order were cpallenged in the Supreme Court, which stayed their 
enforcement. The Supreme Court's decision on the petition has yet 
to be announced. 
The conditions under which the above legislation was enacted 
still obtain imd there has been no opportunity even to watch its 
effect because it has really yet to come into operation. The Bill, 
therefore, seeks to continue the life of the Newspaper (Price ,nd 
Page) Act, 1956.
