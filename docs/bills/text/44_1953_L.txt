STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The object of this Bill is to fix the rates of estate duty under 
clause 34 (1) of the Bill to provide for the levy and collection of an 
estate duty now under consideration in the House of the People. The 
Bill also fixes the rates under clause 80 (1) of the Estate Duty Bill. 
Provision has been made for rebate in respect of the value of agri-
cultural land included in small estates. 
2. Notes on clauses are also appended to the Bill.

Notes on Clauses 
Clause I.-The Bill is called the Estate Duty Rates Bill so that its 
connection with the main Act will be apparent from the short title 
itself. 
Clause 2.-The language of this clause follows the language of 
clause 5 of the Estate Duty Bill. In order to prevent fragmentation 
of small holdings of agricultural land, a reduction of 25% of the duty 
appropriate to agricultural land included in the estate where the 
principal value of the estate does not exceed Rs. 2,00,000, is considered 
necessary. The proviso to this clause gives effect to this intention. 
Clause 34 (2) of the Estate Duty Bill lays down how estate duty is 
determined in the case of an estate which includes both interest in 
joint family property and also other kinds of property. Having regard 
to this provision it is necessary to provide that the rebate for agri-
cultural land in such cases will be one-fourth of the duty payable on 
the agricultural land at an average rate. 
qause 3.-This clause is a useful provision. 
The Schedule.-As regards the Schedule, the rates of duty are on 
the slab system in Parts I and II. So far as Part IT! is concerned, if 
the principal value of the shares exceeds Rs. 5,000, a duty is to be 
.charged at a fiat rate of 71%.
