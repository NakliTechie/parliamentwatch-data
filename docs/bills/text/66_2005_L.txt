STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

With about two-third of our population dependent on agriculture | agriculture
sector accounting for twenty-one percent of the Gross Domestic Product, the importance of
agriculture in our country cannot be over-emphasized.

At present agriculture is a State subject included in List II of the Seventh Schedule to.
the Constitution as item No. 14. As such, the Union Government does not have direct
legislative or executive control. Recently a number of problems have come up in the agriculture
sector which have affected the agricultural produce and in the current year, agriculture has
registered a mere 1.1 percent growth. In order to address the agriculture related problems, it
is necessary that uniform measures should be followed for promoting agriculture and also
for assisting the agriculturists throughout the country. For this it is essential that agriculture
should be transferred from List II (State List) to List III (Concurrent List) in the Seventh
Schedule to the Constitution so that the Union Government may put in place farmer-friendly
schemes uniformly applicable throughout the country.

Hence this Bill.
