STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act , 2009 was 
enacted with the good intention of providing free and compulsory education to the 
children of India.  However, it suffers from some flaws that undermine its purpose. 
The exemption of schools established, owned or controlled by the appropriate 
Government or the local authority from the recognition criteria under section 18 of 
the Act, leaves many schools out of the requirements necessary for quality 
education. There is a need for amendment in the Act to ensure quality education in 
all the schools. 
2. Secondly, in evaluating the performance of schools, apart from the
“inputs”, the addition of evaluation of learning outcomes can be a tool for 
significantly improving the quality of learning. The principal Act is completely 
silent on learning outcomes.  The current Bill provides for the right to quality 
education while ensuring quality learning outcomes.  
The Bill seeks to achieve the above objectives.
