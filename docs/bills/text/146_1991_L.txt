tion of India to provide for the appropriation out of the Consolida
Fund of India of the moneys required to meet the expenditure char;
on the Consolidated Fund and the grants made by Lok Sabha
expenditure of the Central Government, excluding Railways, for
financial year 1991-92,

MANMOHAN SINGH.

from and out of the Consolidated Fund of India for the Services of the
financial year 1991-92 recommends under article 117(1) and (3) of the
Constitution, the introduction of the Appropriation (No. 4) Bill, 1991
in Lok Sabha and also the consideration of the Bill,
