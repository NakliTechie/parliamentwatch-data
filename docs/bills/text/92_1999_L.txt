STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Under Article 1 of the Constitution, the name of our country is “India,
Bharat, shall be a Union of States”.

It will be appropriate if the name of our country is known as “Bharat” instead
“India, that is Bharat”.

It is to be noted that the two Houses of Parliament in India were formerly known
“House of the People” and “Council of States”. The nomenclatures were changed to “]
Sabha” and “Rajya Sabha” which have since become popular in our country as also abro:

The Bill accordingly seeks to amend the Constitution with a view to changing
nomenclature of our country. : :

Hence this Bill.
