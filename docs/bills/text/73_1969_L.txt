STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bil1 is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the Constitu-
tion of India, read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the appropria-
tion out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required to meet 
the expenditure incurred in excess of the grants made by the Lok Sabha 
for expenditure of the Central Government on Railways for the finan-
cial year ended on the 31st day of March, 1968. 
RAM SUBHAG SINGH. 
RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 117 OF THE CONSTITUTION 
OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. 69-B-4083, dated the 1st August, 1969 from Dr. 
Ram Sub hag Singh, Minister of Railways to the Secretary, Lok Sabha.} 
The Chief Justice of India discharging the functions of the President 
having been informed of the subject matter of the proposed Appropria-
tion Bill providing for the appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund 
of India of the moneys required to meet the expenditure incurred in 
excess of the Grants made by the Lok Sabha for 1967-68 recommends 
under clauses (1) and (3) of article 117 of Constitution the introduction in 
and consideration by the Lok Sabha of the Appropriation Bill.
