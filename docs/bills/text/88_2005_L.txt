_ STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Arunachal Pradesh is a multi tribal State having twenty major tribes
However, during British regime names given to the tribes have no bearing with
names of local tribes. Names of some tribes are derogatory and names of some
tribes are misspelt. For example, in the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order
1950 in "Part XVIII.—-Arunachal Pradesh" in entry 1. "Abor" is a derogatory —
word which is to be replaced by "Adi" which has already been given in entry 16.

"Aka" tribes is to replaced by "Hrusso" which has been given in entry 13 of the
List. In entry 4, the word “Dafla" is to be replaced by "Nyishi" including "Bangru
and "Puroik" as sub-tribes

Similarly, many major tribes like Monpa, Sajolang, Tangsang, Yobin, etc.
have not been included in the list and need to be included in the Schedule.

Therefore, it is necessary that names of those tribes which are not in use
should be omitted and incorrect names should be corrected and duplicate entries
should be removed as per the recommendations made by the State Government
of Arunachal Pradesh. It is accordingly proposed to amend the Constitution
(Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950.
