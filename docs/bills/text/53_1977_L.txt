STATEMENT OJ' OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Payment of Wages Act, 1936, regulates the payment of wages to 
certain classes of persons employed in industry. Sub-section (2) of 
section 7 of the Act provides for certain deductions specified in that sub-
section from the wages of an employed person, in accordance with the 
provisions of the Act. 
To promote the welfare of its employees, the Central Government bas 
devised an insurance scheme to which contributions will be made by the 
employees concerned. In order to permit deductions for contributions 
to that scheme from the wages of the employees covered by the Payment 
of Wages Act, 1936, it is proposed to amend sub-section (2) of section 7 
of the Act suitably. 
The Bill seeks to achieve this object.
