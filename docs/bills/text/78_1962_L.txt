priation cut of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys required 
to meet the supplementary expenditure charged on the Consolideted 
Fund of India and the grants made by the Lok Sabha for expenditure 
of the Central Government. excluding Railways, for the financial 
year 1962-63.
