STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the Con-
stitution of India. read with article 116 thereof, to provide for the 
appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India. of the moneys 
required to meet the expenditure charged on the Conso!idated 
Fund and the granb made in advance by the Lok Sabha in respect 
of the estimated expenditure of the Central Government, excluding 
Railways, for a pu't of the financial year 1956-57. 
NEW DELHI; C. D. DESHMUKH. 
The 7th Marc~ 1956.

BILL 
to provide for the withdrawal of certain sums from and out of the

financial year .956-57.

article (117) of the Constitution of India, recommended to Lok
