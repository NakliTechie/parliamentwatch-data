STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
As a result of the partition of the country in August, 1947, it has 
become necessary to divide the funds of the St. John Ambulance 
Association (India) between the Associations in India and Pakistan. 
This Bill is intended to provide legal cover, and to give effect, to 
the agreement reached between the St. John Ambulance Association 
(India) and the St. John Ambulance .. Association (Pakistan) regard-
ing the division of assets of the undivided Association.
