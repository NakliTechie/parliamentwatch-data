2
STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Section 358 of Indian Penal Code, 1860 provides for punishment for assault or use of
criminal force to any person on grave and sudden provocation given by that person. The
said section intends to put a check on the misuse of the exemption of ‘grave and sudden
provocation’ provided under the Code by the person assaulting or using criminal force on
the person who gave him the provocation. However, in the recent times it has been observed
that owing to the meagre punishment provided under section 358 it is being misused for the
personal interest.
The Bill, therefore, seeks to amend section 358 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 with
a view to put a check on the misuse of the safeguard provided under the section for assault
or using criminal force on the person causing grave and sudden provocation.
Hence this Bill.
