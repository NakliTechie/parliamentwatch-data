STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
At present the different States levy varying rates of stamp-duty 
on instruments falling under entry 91 of List I in the Seventh 
Schedule to the Constitution. At times the rates are also high. In 
the interest of uniformity, the Bill seeks to provide-
(1) for the levy of uniform stamp-duty in all States at low 
rates on such instruments; and 
(2) for the levy of stamp-duty on a policy of group insurance, 
for which no specific provision exists at present.
