STATEMENT OF :OBJECTS .t\ND B&ASONS 
Article 338 of the Constitution provides that there shall ".a Special 
Officer for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes whose duty it 
shall be to investigate all matters relating to the safeguatda' provided for 
the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes under the COnstitution aDd to 
report upon the working of those safeguards at prescribed in.tervals. Ac-
oordlngly, the President has appOinted a Special OfBcer designated as the 
Commissioner for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. However. 
considering the magnitude o.f the problem, it has been felt ,that instead of 
having one ofllcer reporting on the safeguards, it will inspire greatet con-
fidence if the matters relating to the Scheduled CaStes and the Scheduled 
Tribes are entrusted to a Commission consisting of persons of eminence 
and status. , " 
2. Similarly, the Constitution provides in article 350B fw a Special 
Officer for Linguistic Minorities. The Government are of the view that 
appointment of a Commission to safeguard the interefits of aU minorities, 
whether based on religion or language, "."ould provide a more satisfactory 
institutional arrangement for achieving the desired objective. A Minorit-
ies Commission was, therefore, set up by an execu1live order. Such a 
Commission would, if set up in pursuance of Constitutional provisions, 
inspire greater confidence among Ithe minorities. 
3. It is, therefore, proposed to amend the Constitution to substitute 
for ar.ticle 338 t~ new articles ,to provide for the aforementioned Com-
missions and to omit article 350B. 
4. 'The Bill seeks to achieve the above objects.
