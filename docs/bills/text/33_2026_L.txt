A 
BILL 
to amend the Industrial Relations Code, 2020. 
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-seventh Year of the Republic of 
India as follows:
— 
1. ( 1) This Act may be called the Industrial Relations Code (Amendment)
Act, 2026. 
(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force from the 21st day of
November, 2025. 
Short title and 
commencement.

Amendment of 
Act 35 of 2020. 
2. In the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, in section 104, for sub-section (1),
the following sub-sections shall be substituted, namely:—
“(1) The following enactments shall stand repealed on and from the date 
appointed in the notification issued under sub -section ( 3) of section 1, 
namely:— 
(a) the Trade Unions Act, 1926;
(b) the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946; and
(c) the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

(1A) Notwithstanding such repeal under sub-section (1), the

other statutory authorities becomes functional under this Code.”.
