STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the ConstI-
tution of India, read with article 116 thereof, to provide for the appropria-
tion from and out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys re-
quired to meet the expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund and 
the grants made in advance by the Lok Babha in respect of the estimated 
expenditure of the Central Government, excluding Railways, for a part 
of the financial year 1983-84. 
PRANAB MUKHERJEE. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 117 OF THE 
CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. F.2(24)-B(D)/83, dated the 14th March, 1983 from 
Shri Pranab Kumar Mukherjee, Minister of Finance to the Secretary, 
Lok Sabha.] 
The President having been informed of the subject matter of the pro-
posed Bill to authorise payment and appropriation of certain sums from 
and out of the Consolidated Fund of India for the services of a part of 
the financial year 1983-84, recommends the introduction of the Appro-
priation (\f.ote on Account) Bill, 1983 in Lok Sabha and also recommends 
to Lok Sabha the consideration of the Bill under article 117 (1) and (3) 
of the Constitution of India read with article 116 (2) thereof. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in Lok Sabha immediately after the 
Demands for Grants, 'on account' for expenditure of th~ Central Gov-
ernment (excluding Railways) for the year 1983-84 have been voted.
