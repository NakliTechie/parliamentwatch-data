STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASO~S 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114 (1) of the Con-
stitution of India read with article 115 thereof, to provide for the 
appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the moneys 
required to meet the expenditure incurred in exces~ of the appro-
priations charged on the Fund and the grants made by the 
House of the People for expenditure of the Central Government, ex-
cluding Railways, for the financial year ended on the 31st day of 
March, 1952.
