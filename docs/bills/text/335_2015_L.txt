THE SUGAR CESS (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2015
A
BILL
further to amend the Sugar Cess Act, 1982.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-sixth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Sugar Cess (Amendment)  Act, 2015.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
2. In the Sugar Cess Act, 1982, in section 3, in sub-section ( 1), for the words
“twenty-five rupees”, the words “two hundred rupees” shall be substituted.
