BILL
to provide for the authorisation of appropriation of moneys out of the Consolidated Fund

ended on the 31st day of March, 2017 in excess of the amounts granted for those
services and for that year.
B
E it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-first Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (No. 3) Act, 2020.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India, the sums specified in column 3 of
the Schedule, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of one lakh ninety thousand two
hundred forty-seven crore eighty-three lakh seventy-six thousand two hundred forty-three
rupees shall be deemed to have been authorised to be paid and applied to meet the amounts
spent for defraying the charges in respect of the services specified in column 2 of the
Schedule during the financial year ended on the 31st day of March, 2017, in excess of the
amounts granted for those services and for that year.
