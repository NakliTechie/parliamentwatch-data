STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The High Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1954 and the 
Supreme Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1958 provide lor the 
conditions of service of the Judges of the High Courts and· the Supreme 
Court respectively. In 1976, these Acts were amended to provide, with 
retrospective effect from 1st October, 1974, for payment of conveyance 
allowance to the Judges at the rate of RI. 300 per- month to every Judge 
subject to the maintenance of a motor car by him. Now it is proposed to 
enltance the conveyance allowance payable to every Judge from Rs. 300 
per month to R.s. 500 per month subject .. to the maintenance of a motor 
car by him. - -
2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
