STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The entry of persons having criminal background in the representative bodies has
become a matter of deep public concern. They get elected to the representative institutions
with huge public mandate through their terror tactics, money and muscle power and even by
misleading common voters. It has been expressed in all quarters to ban their entry into these
institutions. There is a need to expand the ambit of offences enumerated in the Representation
of the People Act for the purpose of disqualification from membership of Parliament and
__ State Legislatures. This will definitely check the entry of the persons into representative
. bodies with criminal background such as those accused of murders, dacoity and kidnapping.

Hence this Bill.
