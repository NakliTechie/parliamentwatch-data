STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
Experience of the working of the Industries (Development and Regu-
lation) Act, 1951, has revealed that offences under this Act rio not gene.. 
rally come to light as soon as they are committed. Further, the investi-
gations for determining as to whether prosecution shoulel be launched in 
a case require considerable time. Consequently, there is difficulty, in a 
number of cases, in launching prosecution for offences under the Act 
within the period of limitation provided in Chapter XXXVI of the Corie 
of Criminal Procedure, 1973. In order to get over this difficulty, it is pro-
posed to include the Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951 
in the Schedule to the Economic Offences (Inapplicability of Uimitation) 
Act, 1974. 
2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
