STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The powers conferred by sub-section (7 A) of section 5 of the 
Indian Income-tax Act, 1922, have always been construed by the 
Income-tax authorities as enabling them to transfer the case of any 
person from one Income-tax Officer to another, and once this is done, 
all assessments in relation to the person whose case is so transferred, 
irrespective of the years to which they relate, are to be made by the 
Income-tax Officer who is in charge of assessing that person. The 
Supreme Court has, however, held in Messrs. Bidi Supply Co., VB. 
The Union of India (Petition No. 271 of 1955) that this sub-section 
only authorises the transfer of a pending proceeding for a particular 
assessment year. 
It has now become necessary to clarify the meaning of the word 
·'case" as understood by the Income-tax authorities, and incidentally 
the amendment is given retrospective operation so as to regularisa 
past actions.
