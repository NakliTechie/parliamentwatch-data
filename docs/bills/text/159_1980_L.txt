STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorised Occupants) Act, 19'71 
was enacted to provide for speedy and summary eviction of unauthorised 
occupants from premises 0'£ the Central GQvernment, companies in which 
not ,less than fifty-one per cent. paid-up share caFital is held by the Central 
Governm~nt, and 'Corporations (other than local authorities) established 
by or under Central Acts and owned or controlled by the Central Gov~ 
emment. So far as the Union territory of 'Delhi is concerned, the Act 
applies also to premises belonging to the Delhi Development Authority, 
the Municipal Corporation of Delhi and other municipal bodies. 
2. The Act does not at present apply in relation to premises beloaging 
to or taken on lease by or on behalf of autonomous and statutory orga-
nisations, such as the major ports, the Bhakra Management Board, Urif .. 
versities and similar institutions set up under a Central Act or subsidiary 
of a company in which the Central Government has at least fifty-one per 
cent. of the paid-up share capital. As considerable difficulties have been 
experienced by these organ'isations in evicting unauthorised occupants 
from their premises, it is proposed to amend the Act so as to cover premises 
belonging to such organisations. 
3. It is also proposed to make the following amendments with a view 
to overcome the difficulties which have been experienced in the workina 
of the Act and to make, the administration of the Act more etfective-
(a) amendment of section 3 of the Act to facilitate the appoint-
ment of officers of the Secretariat of the Lok Sabha and the Secr~ 
tariat of the Rajya Sabha as estate officers for the purpose of dealing 
with public premises under the administrative control of these 
Secretariats; 
(b) amendment of sections 4, 5 and 9 of the Ac.t for reduci.ng the 
total period taken in eviction proceedings by reducmg the period for 
showing cause against notice o'f evicti~n from 10 days to 7 d?ys,

occupant, reducing the period within ~h~ch an unauthonsed occupant 
should vacate the premises after eVictIOn orders hav~ been passed
