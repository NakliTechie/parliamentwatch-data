STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Government of Jammu and Kashmir have agreed to partici-
pate in the scheme of all-India Services and this has necessitated 
the constitution of the Indian Administrative Service and Indian 
Police Service Cadres in that State. Section 3 of the All-India Ser-
vices Act, 1951, empowers the Central Government, in consultation 
with the States concerned, to make rules for the regulation of 
recruitment, etc., to an all-India Service. The object of this Bill is 
to make it clear that the expression "States concerned" in that Act 
includes the State of Jammu ana. Kashmir.
