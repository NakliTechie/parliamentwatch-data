A
BILL
to amend the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. (1) This Act may be called the Food Safety and Standards (Amendment) Act, 2008.
(2) It shall  be deemed to have come into force on the 7th day of February, 2008.
2.  In the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (hereinafter referred to as the principal
Act), in section 3, in sub-section (1), for clause (ze), the following clause shall be substituted,
namely:—
‘(ze) “Member” includes a part-time Member and the Chairperson of the Food
Authority;’.
3.  In section 5 of the principal Act, for sub-sections ( 4) and (5), the following sub-
sections shall be substituted, namely:—
“(4) The Chairperson and the Members including part-time Members other than
the ex officio Members of the Food Authority may be appointed by the Central
Government on the recommendations of the Selection Committee.
