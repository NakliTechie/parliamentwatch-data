STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The Influx from Pakistan (Control) Repealing Act, 1952 was enacted to provide for
the repeal of the Influx from Pakistan (Control) Act, 1949. However a saving clause in
section 3 of the Act stipulated that the repeal shall not affect the continuance in force of any
permit which was intended to continue beyond the 15th day of October, 1952/At Present
the stay and movement of Pakistan nationals in India is regulated by the system of residen-
tial permits in accordance with the Indo-Pak Visa Scheme of 1974 and the system of permits
introduced by the provisions of the Influx from Pakistan (Control) Act, 1949 has lapsed
since long. Hence, the Influx from Pakistan (Control) Repealing Act, 1952 has no relevance
and needs to be repealed.

2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
