A
BILL
to authorise payment and appropriation of certain further sums from and out of the
Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2018-19.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-ninth Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation ( No. 4) Act, 2018.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be paid and applied
sums not exceeding those specified in column 3 of the Schedule amounting in the
aggregate to the sum of eleven thousand six hundred ninety-seven crore and ninety-
two lakh rupees towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of
payment during the financial year 2018-19 in respect of the services specified in column
2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated
Fund of India by this Act shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed
in the Schedule in relation to the said year.
