STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of articles 204(1) and 206 of 
the Constitution and the Proclamation issued under article 356 of 
the Constitution in respect of the State Of Haryana on the 21st 
November, 1967 to provide for the appropriation out of the Consoli-
dated Fund of the State of Haryana of the moneys required to meet 
the expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund of the State and 
the grants made in advance by the Lok Sabha in respect of the 
estimated expenditure of the Government of Haryana for a part of 
the financial year 1961--69. 
MORARJI DESAI. 
PRESIDENT'S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 207 
OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 
[Copy of letter No. F3(5)-B/68, dated the 7th March, 1968 from 
Shri Morarji Desai, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance 
to the Secretary, Lok Sabha.] 
The President having been informed of the subject matter of 
the proposed Bill to provide for the withdrawal of certain sums 
from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Haryana for 
the services of a Part of the financial year 1968-69, recommends 
under clauses (1) and (3) of article 207 of the Constitution of India 
read with article 206 thereof and the Proclamation dated the 21st 
November, 1967 issued under article 3M of the Constitution, the 
introduction of the Haryana Appropriation (Vote on Account) Bill, 
1968, in the Lok sabha and also the consideration of the Bill. 
2. The Bill will be introduced in the Lok Sabha immediately 
after the Demands for Grants, 'on account' for the expenditure of 
the Government of the State of Haryana for the year 1968-69 have 
been voted.
