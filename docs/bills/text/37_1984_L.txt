STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
The Commercial Crops Cess Act, i974, imposing a Cess on Crops of 
Chillies, Cotton, Mustard Seeds, Potatoes, . Rape (Sharshaf, Taramira, 
Toria) , Sugarcane, Tomatoes, Orchards and Vine-yards,· at the rate of 
Rs. 6/- per acre OD irrIgated lands and J;ls. 3/- on unirrigated lands, was 
enacted for five years from Kharif Crop of Agricultural year 1974-75 to 
Rabi 1978-79, in order to meet the expenditure on the Fifth Five Year 
Plan. With a view to meet the financial need of the State Government 
during the Sixth Five Year Plan, the Act was continued for 
another five years from Kharif Crop of Agricultural Year 1978-79 to 
Rabi 1983-84. The existing term will expire after Rabi 1983-84. Keep-
ing in view the tight financial position of the State, it has been considered 
desirable that the Act may be contin~ed for a further period of five years; 
namely, from Kharif Crop of Agricultural year 1984-85 to Rabi Crop of 
1988-89. Hence this Bill.
