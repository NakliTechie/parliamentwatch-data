A
BILL
further to amend the Administrators-General Act, 1963.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-third  Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
1.  (1) This Act may be called the Administrators-General (Amendment)  Act, 2012.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
2.  In  sections 9, 10, 29 and 36 of the Administrators-General Act, 1963, for the words
"two lakhs", wherever they occur, the words "ten lakhs" shall be substituted.
