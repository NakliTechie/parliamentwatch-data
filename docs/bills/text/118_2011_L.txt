STA TEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Despite enactment of the Environment Protection Act, 1986, exploitation of our natural
resources are on the rampant. The pollution of rivers is on the rise. Already with the ever
increasing population, we are unable to provide facilities like drinking water from the depleting
water levels in the rivers.
Moreover with the lifting of sand from riverbanks, rivers are losing their foreshore and
erosion has been intensified. Therefore, a distance limit should be prescribed to prohibit
handling of sand from the riverbanks in order to protect our natural resources and rivers.
Hence this Bill.
