The Supreme Court, in Baij Nath Kedia vs. State of Bihar (C.A. No.

visions of sections 9 and 10 of the Bihar Land Reforms Act, 1950, any 
attempt to regulate those mining leases will fall not in Entry 18 of 
the State List but in Entry 54 of the Union List although it touches 
land and not vice versa; 
(c) the whole field of regulation and development of minor 
minerals have been taken over under the control of the Union.

2. The Government of Bihar have pOinted out that,. as a result of the 
aforesaid judgment, the levy and collection of royalty (In minor minerals

visions of the Bihar Minor Mineral Conces..;ion Rules, 1964, have not only 
become void but they may also be required to refund royalty collected

section 10(2) of the Bihar Land lteforms Act. 1950. as amended by thl!
