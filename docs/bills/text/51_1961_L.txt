STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
At the time the Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 
1951, was enacted, its application could not be extended to the State 
of Jammu end Kashmir, because entry 52 of the Union List in the 
Seventh Schedule to the Constitution, to which the subject matter 
of the Act relates, was not applicable to that State. By the Consti-
tution (Application to Jammu and Kashmir) Amendment Order, 
1961, that entry has been made applicable to the State of Jammu 
and Kashmir. Following this action, it is now proposed to extend 
the application of the Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 
1951 to the State of Jammu and Kashmir, as it is desirable to do so 
in ° the interests of industrial development of Jammu and Kashmir 
in particular and for ensuring uniformity in the industrial develop-
ment of the country as a whole. .. -". '. " . 
The Bill seekS to provide ° for this objective.
