STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
India is one of the most diverse and culturally rich nations of the world. The richness
in culture is contributed by the diversity in languages, religions, dance forms, music,
architecture, food, customs and traditions, etc. The fundamental duty of every citizen
enshrined in article 51(f) to value and preserve the rich heritage of our composite culture. The
valuing and preservation of cultural heritage is best served by the propagation of the cultural
heritage within as well as outside the country. In the process, the citizens would not only
know more about country's cultural heritage but also value it even more. Therefore,
propagation of rich heritage of our composite culture should also be the duty of every
citizen.
The Bill, therefore, proposes to amend the article 51A of the Constitution with a view
to make propagation of rich heritage of our composite culture as one of the fundamental
duties of the citizens.
Hence this Bill.
