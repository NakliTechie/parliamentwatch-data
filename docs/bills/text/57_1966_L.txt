STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
One of the important recommendations made by the Election 
Commission in its R~port on the Third General Elections in India 
iii 1962, and accepted by the Government relates to the abolition of 
election tribunals and trial of election petitions by High Courts. 
If the proposal for a legislation to amend the Representation of 
the People Act; 1951, containing, inter alia, provisions for the trial 
of election petitions by High Courts instead of by election tribunals, 
as at present, is accepted by Parliament, it would be necessary to 
make a minor amendment in clause (1) of article 324 of'the Consti-
tution for the purpose of deleting therefrom the words ", including 
the appoinUnent of election trib19lals for the decision of doubts and 
disputes arising out of or in connection with elections to Parliament 
and to the Legisla~es of States".
