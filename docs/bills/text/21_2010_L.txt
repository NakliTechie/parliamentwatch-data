(AS PASSED BY THE HOUSES OF PARLIAMENT)
A
BILL
to provide for the withdrawal  of certain sums from and out of the  Consolidated Fund of
India for the services of a part of the financial year 2010-11 for the purposes of Railways.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-first Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Appropriation (Railways) Vote on Account Act, 2010.
2. From and out of the Consolidated Fund of India there may be withdrawn  sums not
exceeding those specified in column  3 of the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the
sum of thirty thousand four hundred sixty-one crores, thirty-one lakhs and six thousand
rupees towards  defraying the several charges which will come in course of payment during
the financial year 2010-11, in respect of the services relating to Railways specified  in
column 2 of the Schedule.
3. The sums authorised to  be withdrawn from and out of the Consolidated Fund of
India  by this Act  shall be appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the
Schedule  in relation to the said year.
