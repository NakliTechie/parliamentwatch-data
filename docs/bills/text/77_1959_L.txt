STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
To put down effectively the evil of kidnapping of children for 
exploiting them for begging, the provisions existing in the Indian 
Penal Code are not quite adequate. There is also no special provi-
sion for deterrent punishment for the greater evil of maiming of 
children so as to make them objects of pity. 
2. The Bill makes kidnapping or obtaining custody of a minor, 
and the maiming of a minor for employing him for begging, specific 
offences and provides for deterrent punishment. The Bill also makes 
consequential amendments in the Second Schedule to the Code of 
Criminal Procedure, 1898.
