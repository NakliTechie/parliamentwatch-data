BILL 
further to amend the Constitution (Scheduled Castes ) Order, 1950 and the 
 Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950 to modify the list of Scheduled 
 Castes and Scheduled Tribes in relation to the State of Odisha. 
 BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy -fifth Year of the Republic of 
India as follows:— 
 1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Castes and Scheduled 
Tribes) Orders (Amendment) Act, 2024. 
 2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,— 
 (a) “Scheduled Castes Order” means the Constitution (Scheduled 
Castes) Order, 1950; 
 (b) “Scheduled Tribes Order” means the Constitution (Scheduled 
Tribes) Order, 1950.
