Background 
The press advertisements of the Ministries and Departments of 
the Government of India are routed through Directorate of Advertising 
and Visual Publicity (DAVP), which is an attached office of the Ministry 
of Information and Broadcasting.  A number of autonomous bodies and 
public sector enterprises also channelise their advertisements through 
the DAVP.  The DAVP does publicity for various Ministries/Department 
of the Governments of India through the medium of (i) newsp aper 
advertising (ii) outdoor publicity (iii) printed publicity and audio -visual 
publicity. 
 
1.2 While handling an advertising campaign, a media list is prepared 
by DAVP keeping in view the target area for which the campaign is 
also aimed at. Care is also taken to have a media mix of small, 
medium and big newspapers and advertisements are released to 
them.  Payments are made to those newspapers to whom 
advertisements are released on presentation of bills by them after the 
advertisement is published. 
 

 6 
 
1.3 In the year 1990-91, the Estimates Committee had examined the 
subject ‘Policy for Government Advertisement’ pertaining to the 
Ministry of Information & Broadcasting and presented a Report thereon 
to Lok Sabha on 5 th March, 1992.  In order to act as a check against 
the misuse of power by the bureaucrats and to result in openness of 
the activities, the Committee had inter-alia made the following 
recommendation* with regard to quantum of advertisements and the 
total amount paid to each of the newspaper:- 
“The C ommittee desire that the Ministry should evolve an 
effective, impartial and fair system of empanelment under which 
all genuine newspapers/journals feel encouraged to approach 
DAVP directly.  They also desire that DAVP should publish and 
incorporate in thei r Annual Report the quantum of 
advertisements and the total amount paid to each of the 
newspaper”. 
 
1.4 Accepting the above recommendation of the Committee, the 
Ministry have since then been printing their Annual Report in two 
volumes.  Volume -II of the An nual Report is solely dedicated to the 
details of quantum of advertisements and the total amount paid to each 
of the newspapers by DAVP. 
 
*  Para 2.52 of 8th Report, 10th Lok Sabha

Discontinuation of printing of Volume-II of Annual Report 
 
1.5 The Ministr y of Information & Broadcasting vide their office 
memorandum no. 13/166/2007 -PPC dated 19 th August, 2008 
(Annexure-I) has submitted a memorandum requesting to grant 
permission to discontinue the printing of Volume -II of their Annual 
Report w.e.f. the year 2008-09 onwards on the following grounds:- 
“In the year 1995-96, facility of internet was not accessible easily, 
whereas all the information provided in Volume-II of the Ministry’s 
Annual Report (i.e. details of quantum of advertisements and the 
total amount paid to each newspaper by DAVP) is available on 
the website of DAVP ( www.davp.nic.in) and the same can be 
accessed very easily”.

1.8 While considering the request of the Ministry of Information and 
Broadcasting to grant permission to discontinue the publishing of 
Volume-II of their Annual Report, the Committee found some 
differences between the existing format and the p roposed electronic 
format.  In volume -II of their Annual Report, the Ministry has been 
publishing information on the total amount paid to each newspaper on 
annual basis as recommended by the Estimates Committee (1991 -92) 
but in the electronic format the in formation is proposed to be 
maintained on a continuous basis in the website of DAVP i.e. 
www.davp.nic.in.  Moreover, the crucial information of quantum of 
advertisement (space allotted) to each newspaper has been lef t out in 
the proposed electronic format. 
 
1.9 The Committee note that maintaining of details about 
quantum of advertisements and total amount paid to each of the  

 9 
newspapers in electronic format is less expensive and also easily 
accessible to all.  They, t herefore, accede to the request of the 
Ministry to maintain the same in electronic form.  The Committee 
however, desire that the Ministry should make available the 
information in their website in the same format in which the 
information was hitherto published by them every year in Volume-
II of their Annual Report and a specific reference should also be 
made about the website at the end of their Annual Report  
Volume-I.
