INTRODUCTION 
 
 
I, the Chairman of the Estimates Committee (2009 -2010), having been authorized 
by the Committee to present the Report on their behalf, present this Third Report on action 
taken by the Government on the recommendations contained in the Nineteenth Report of 
Estimates Committee (Fourteenth Lok Sabha) on the subject „Discontinuation of Printing of 
Volume II of the Annual Report‟ pertaining to the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. 
 
2. The Nineteenth Report (Fourteenth Lok Sabha) was presented to Lok Sabha on 
17th December, 2008.  The Government furnished their reply indicating action taken on the 
recommendations contained in that Report on 27th July, 2009.    The Draft Report was 
considered and adopted by the Estimates Committee (2009 -2010) at their si tting held on 
5th October, 2009.

REPORT 
 
1.1 This Report of the Committee deals with the action taken by the Government 
on the recommendation contained in the Nineteenth Report (Fourteenth Lok 
Sabha) on the subject „Discontinuation of Printing of Volume II of the Annual 
Report‟ of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. 
 
1.2 The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting had submitted a memorandum 
to the Estimates Committee requesting to grant permission to discontinue the 
printing of Volume II of their Annual Report w.e.f. the year 2008 -2009 onwards on 
the grounds that the information is easily accessible on the website of Directorate of 
Audio Visual Publicity (DAVP). The Committee examined the memorandum and 
presented their Nineteenth Report thereon on 17 th December, 2008. The Report 
contained the following recommendation of the Committee:- 
“The Committee note that maintaining of details about quantum of 
advertisements and total amount paid to each of the newspapers in 
electronic format is less expensive and also easily accessible to all. They, 
therefore, accede to the request of the Ministry to maintain the same in 
electronic form. The Committee however, desire that the Ministry should 
make available the information in their website in the same format in which 
the information was hitherto publishe d by them every year in Volume -II of 
their Annual Report and a specific reference should also be made about the 
website at the end of their Annual Report Volume-I” 
 
1.3 The Ministry of information and Broadcasting while accepting the 
recommendation has furnished the following action taken reply:- 
“As per the recommendations of the Estimates Committee, the Ministry of 
Information and Broadcasting has discontinued the Volume II of their Annual 
Report w.e.f. the Year 2008 -2009 (present year) and uploaded the c ontents 
of Volume II, both in Hindi and English languages, in its website 
www.mib.gov.in and www.mib.nic.in in the same format in which the 
information was published previously by the Ministry in the Volume II of their 
Annual Report. A reference to that effect has also been mentioned at the end 
of the Annual Report Volume I”. 
 

1.4 The Committee note that the Ministry has uploaded the contents of 
Volume II of its Annual Report, 2008 -09 in the homepage of Directorate of 
Audio Visual Publicity (DAVP) which is a link site to the main website of the 
Ministry.  In this regard, they feel t hat there should be a mention in the main 
website that the contents of Volume II of its Annual Report are currently 
available in the homepage of DAVP.  In the Volume II of Ministry’s Annual 
Report 2007 -08, the State -wise information of newspapers was start ed with 
State’s name at the top and ended with the number of newspapers at the 
bottom.  These details are absent in the present electronic form.  The 
Committee recommend that these details should invariably be added in the 
website to facilitate the visitor s to the website make a clear distinction 
between the information of two States.  They further note that a slip 
containing an addendum has been attached at the end of the Volume I of its 
Annual Report 2008-09 in regard to discontinuation of Volume II of th e Annual 
Report.  The Committee expect that this information would be printed, next 
year onwards, at the end of the Annual Report.
