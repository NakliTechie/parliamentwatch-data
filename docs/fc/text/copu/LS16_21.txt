(mechanized, sophisticated) which will ensure supply of hygienic meals to trains.

undertaken by IRCTC. These kitchens will be owned, 
operated and managed by IRCTC. IRCTC shall develop different types of kitchens 
keeping in view supply of food and usage assessed.  IRCTC shall prepare a detailed 
concession agreement for setting up/develop
ment of the kitchens. Meals for all 
mobile units will be picked up from the nominated kitchens owned, operated and 
managed by IRCTC. IRCTC will engage service providers from hospitality industry

all refreshment rooms, Jan
