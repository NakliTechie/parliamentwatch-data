DEFINING SUBURBAN TRAIN SERVICES:
1. The suburban train services are provided by seven Zonal Railways. On an

Various Railway Zones, viz., Central, Eastern, Southern, South Eastern, South
Centrat ‘and. Wesfern and Kelkata Metro, are providing dedicated suburban
services in the country covering about 72.86 percent of the total passengers on
fhe Indian Railway system spread over 1763 km during the period under review.
The defining feature of Suburban Train: Service as identified by the Railways is
that they are meant for short distances, normally up to 150 km. to facilitate rapid:
movement of passengers within the cities, suburbs and extended suburbs.
Further, the Railways have contended that consideration of routes for notification
as suburban routes is. based on the pattern of traffic, availability of. rail
infrastructure ete. and suburban sections are-notified on detailed examination of
the techno-economic and financial viability derived from traffic studies carried

traffic in the urban conglomerates. Further it was also contended that passenger हे

service levels of current passengerfEMU services on the route also need to be
considered while notifying a section as “Suburban”. The Committee observe
that, although Sub-urban Train Services are bearing the major burden of
passengers carried by the Railways at 72.86 percent, the Railways have more or
