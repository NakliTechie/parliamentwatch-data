ensure speedy resolution of disputes; they also identify litigi ous provisions in the Act

Glossary

Finance.

Income Tax or Deputy Commissioner of Income Tax or Joint Commis sioner of

or assigned to an AO under the Income tax Act, 1961.

Law.

Departments/Ministries or between Public Sector Enterprises or between a
