This Report for the year ended 31 March 2014 has been prepared for submission
to the Governor of the State of Himachal Pradesh under Article 151 of the
Constitution of India.

The Report contains significant results of the performance audit and compliance
audit of the Departments of the Government of Himachal Pradesh under the
General, Social and Economic services, including Departments of Excise and
Taxation, Revenue, Transport, Forest and Mines and Geology.

The instances mentioned in this Report are those, which came to notice in the
course of test audit done during the period 2013-14 as well as those which came
to notice in earlier years, but could not be reported in the previous Audit Reports;
instances relating to the period subsequent to 2013-14 have also been included,
wherever necessary.

The audit has been conducted in conformity with the Auditing Standards issued
by the Comptroller and Auditor General of India.



OVERVIEW



This Report contains 27 paragraphs and one Performance audit on ‘Arrears under
Sales Tax/ VAT’, involving financial effect of %72.17 crore relating to
underassessment of tax, non/ short levy of state excise, non/ short levy of stamp duty
and registration fee, non/ short levy of passenger and goods tax and non/ short levy

The Principal Accountant General (Audit), Himachal Pradesh (PAG) conducts
periodical inspection of the Government Departments to test check the
transactions and verify the maintenance of important accounts and other records
as prescribed in the rules and procedures. These inspections are followed up with
the inspection reports (IRs) incorporating irregularities detected during the
inspection and not settled on the spot, which are issued to the heads of the offices
inspected with copies to the next higher authorities for taking prompt corrective
action. The heads of the offices/ Government are required to promptly comply
with the observations contained in the IRs, rectify the defects and omissions and
report compliance through initial reply to the PAG within four weeks from the
date of receipt of the IRs. Serious financial irregularities are reported to the
Heads of the Department and the Government.

The Excise and Taxation Department is responsible for collection of Sales Tax/
Value Added Tax. The Himachal Pradesh Value Added Tax (HPVAT) Act,
2005 and the other Acts as well as the Rules framed thereunder governed the
levy, assessment and collection of Sales Tax/ VAT in the State since April 1*
2005. The Himachal Pradesh General Sales Tax Act, 1968 (HPGST Act) was in
existence up to 31 March 2005. The Central Sales Tax (CST) Act, 1956 and the
tules framed there under are in operation for inter-state sales. Under the HPVAT
Act, every registered dealer liable to pay tax due from him within the time
specified in the tax demand notice (TDN) (not less than 15 days and not
exceeding 30 days). In case of failure on the part of the assessee to pay the tax
within the date mentioned in the TDN, the Department can recover the amount
which remains unpaid as if it was arrears of land revenue. Powers have been
delegated! to the Department to recover the Government dues as arrears of land
revenue (ALR) under Section 28 of the Himachal Pradesh Land Revenue
(HPLR) Act, 1954. Some provisions of Sale Tax/ VAT Act and HPLR Act for
the purpose of effecting recovery of Government dues are given in Appendix-I.
