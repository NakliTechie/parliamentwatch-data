2.  While scrutinizing these Orders, the Committee note d that there was a 
considerable time lag between issue  of Notificatio ns and their laying in some 
cases. The annexed statement also indicates the per iod of delay in cases where 
Notifications have not been laid as per the time sc hedule prescribed by the 
Committee. The Committee observed that out of the t otal 133 delayed 
Notifications there were 119 Notifications where no  delay statement was 
appended to. However, the bulk of delayed Notificat ions were from the Ministry 
of  Road Transport and Highways which comes to arou nd 64.6% of the total 
delayed Notifications. 
 
The observations/recommendations of the Committee i n respect of the 
specific Ministries/Departments where delay in layi ng of Notifications has been 
noticed, are as follows: -  
 
(i) Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers (Department o f 
Pharmaceuticals)  
  
 Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers (Department of Pharmaceuticals) 
have laid 4 notifications (Sl. Nos. 19 to 22),  out of which 2 notifications (Sl. Nos. 
19 & 21) have been delayed. Both of the delayed not ifications have been delayed 
by 6 months and there is no delay statement attache d to any of these delayed 
notifications.  
 
 The Committee is not happy over the delay of 6 mon ths in laying of  
two notifications  by the Ministry of Chemicals and  Fertilizers (Department 
of Pharmaceuticals). Further there is no delay stat ement appended thereto 
giving details as to what caused such a delay. The Committee, therefore, 
impresses upon the Ministry to take care that such instances of delay do not 
recur in future and in case of any delay in laying,  a delay statement should 
invariably be appended thereto. 
  
(ii)  Ministry of Coal  
 
Ministry of   Coal have laid 4 Notifications (Sl. N os. 23-26). One of them 
(Sl. No. 25) has been delayed by 29 months  though  accompanied with  a delay 
statement. The reason given by the Ministry for del ay is that due to excessive 
work involving Coal investigation with CBI, SC, CBC , PAC, PMO etc., the 
Notifications could not be laid in time:-  
 

The Committee expresses its unhappiness over the fa ct that it took 2 
years and five months in laying of  one (out of 4) Notifications laid by the 
Ministry of Coal on the Table of the House. The rea sons cited for delay is 
that due to excessive work involved in reconstituti on of different branches in 
the Ministry of Coal and timely disposal of work fo r  Supreme Court, CBC, 
Public Accounts Committee, Central Bureau of Invest igation, Prime 
Minister’s Office and Attorney General relating to Coal allocation, the laying 
of Notification could not be accomplished timely. T he Committee would like 
to emphasise here that the work related to laying o f Notifications is not as 
complicated as the work relating to CBI enquiry in Coal allocation and 
Ministry was not left with sufficient time to accom plish the  Parliamentary 
work i.e. laying of Notification. The Ministry shou ld have accorded top 
priority to the Parliamentary work. The Committee a dvises the Ministry of 
Coal to endeavor to stick to the laying schedule st rictly so as to inform the 
Parliament about the Notification in time as this is a case of unusual delay. 
 
(iii) Ministry of Communications and Information Te chnology 
 
(a)   (Department of Electronics and Information Technology) 
  
 Ministry of Communications and Information Technology  (Department of  
Electronics and Information Technology)  have laid 2 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 31 - 
32).  Out of these, one Notification (Sl. No. 31) h as been delayed by 30 months 
and has no delay statement  appended to it. 
  
  The Committee takes a serious view, not only about the delay in 
laying of one of its Notification on the table of t he House, but also of its 
failure to append a delay statement with them. The Ministry/Department 
must know about the possible implications of failin g to meet this 
requirement. It must ensure to append a delay state ment in all such cases in 
future so that Parliament can be apprised of the reasons therefor.  
 
(b)  (Department of Telecommunications) 
 
Ministry of Communications and Information Technolo gy (Department of 
Telecommunications) have laid 9 Notifications (Sl. No. 35 to 43). Out  of these 
one Notification (Sl. No. 35) has been delayed by 9  months and has no delay 
statement appended to it.  
 
The Committee does not appreciate the delay in laying of Notifications 
on the part of Ministry of Communications and Infor mation Technology  
(Department of  Telecommunications) and would sugge st them to stick to the 
laying schedule as given in Committee’s 131 
st  Report. 
 
(iv) Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate C hange  

  
 Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Chang e have laid 9 
Notifications (Sl. Nos. 104 to 112) on the Table of  the House during the Session. 
One of them  (Sl. No. 108) has been delayed by 6 mo nths and has no delay 
statement appended thereto. 
    
 The Committee observes that Ministry of Environment , Forests and 
Climate Change did not act promptly to lay the Noti fication (Sl. No. 108) on 
the Table of the House. Had they taken prompt actio n the delay would not 
have occured. The Committee, therefore,  recommends  that the Ministry of 
Environment, Forests and Climate Change  should act  promptly to avoid 
such delay and incase of delays in laying of Notifi cations, delay statements 
should invariably be accompanied with them.  
  
 
(v) Ministry of Finance

 The Committee are not happy with the delay in layin g of Notifications

(b)    (Department of Financial Services) 
 
Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Servic es) have laid 22 
Notifications (Sl. Nos. 148 to 169), out of which 1 0 Notifications have been 
delayed.  Out of 10 delayed Notifications,  6  Noti fications (Sl. Nos. 157 to 162) 
have  been delayed from 6 to 7 months and have no d elay statements attached to 
them. 4 Notifications (Sl. Nos. 148 to 151) have be en delayed  each by 6 months 
with delay statement appended thereto. The reasons forwarded by the Ministry for 
delay is as under:- 
 
“Gazette Notifications dated 12.12.2013 regarding a mendment in 
Regulations 50(1) & 50(6) of Andhra Bank (Employees ’) Pension Regulations, 

1995 and Regulation 4(1) of the Andhra Bank Officer Employees’ (Acceptance of 
Jobs in Private Sector concerns after retirement) R egulations, 2000 were 
published in the Gazette of India, No. 9 & 10 dated  08.01.2014. As the Bank 
furnished the requisite copies of Gazette Notificat ion after the stipulated period, 
clarification was sought from the Andhra Bank. 
The Bank has reported that amendments were publishe d in the Gazette on 
08.01.2014 and the copy of the Gazette Notification  were received on 17 th  
February, 2014. The Bank submitted the requisite nu mber of copies to this

on 25.02.2014 after the stipulated time period. 
In view of above, the said Notification could not b e laid on the Table of 
the both the Houses during the last Session of Parl iament ending on 21.02.2014. 
Delay in this regard is deeply regretted and may please be condoned.” 
 
The Committee takes a serious view not only about t he delay in laying 
of ten Notifications  by the Ministry of Finance (D epartment of Financial 
Services) on the Table of the House but also of  it s failure to append the 
required delay statement. The Committee observes th at Parliamentary 
sessions are declared much in advance and had the M inistry of Finance 
(Department of Financial Services) taken prompt ste ps for laying of the 
notifications, in coordination with Banks for suppl y of Gazette Notifications,  
the delay could have been well avoided. It reflects  the bad management 
planning on the part of the Ministry. The Committee , therefore,  
recommends that the Ministry should complete the pr ocedural formalities at 
the earliest to avoid such kind of delays in future.

 (viii) Ministry of Power 
 
 The Ministry of power have laid 8 notifications ( Sl. Nos. 417-424) out of 
which 3 notifications (Sl. Nos. 418-420) have been delayed. The delay period 
ranges  from 6 to 8 months and all of delayed Notif ications have delay statements 
appended. The reasons for delay  given by the Ministry is  as under:- 
 
 “The Notification Nos. L-1/(5)/2013-CERC and  L-1/ 132/2013-CERC 
were published in the Gazette of India Extraordinar y dated 14 
th  November, 2013 
and 7 th  January, 2014. Copies of these Notifications could  not be laid on the table 
of the Rajya Sabha before conclusion of the Budget Session 2014 as printed 
copies of the same were received only on 8 th  January, 2014 and 30 th  January, 2014

Publication. The CERC forwarded the same to Ministry of Power on 5 th  February,

The Committee does not accept the late receipt of p rinted copies of 
Notifications as a valid reason for  delayed laying  of Notifications etc. The 
Committee does not accept this as a convincing reas on in respect of laying of 
statutory papers before the Parliament. The Ministr y of Power  should, 
therefore,  in future ensure that all its papers ar e laid before the House 
within the time frame as prescribed in the Committe e’s 131 
st  Report. The 
Ministry should take it up with the Ministry of Urb an Development for late 
receiving of printed copies of the Notifications. 
 
The Committee, therefore,  reiterates its earlier d irection to all the 
Ministries that in case there is delay in procuring  printed copies in time, they 
may obtain G.S.R. and S.O. numbers from the Governm ent of India Press 
and prepare requisite number of copies in cyclostyl ed form for laying in the 
Parliament as suggested by Secretary, Ministry of  Urban Development, the 
controlling Ministry of Government of India Press, in its meeting held on 30  
July, 2013. The Committee also once again recommend s that this process 
may be adopted by Ministries suo moto  on immediate basis so as to avoid 
such delays henceforth.

Committee, therefore, urge the Ministry of Urban De velopment to be more 
careful in future, and to lay the papers in time on  the Table of the Houses to  
avoid unreasonable delay in this regard.

 “In addition to the concern expressed by the Commi ttee over delay in 
laying of Notifications on the Table of the House, the Committee also express 
their deep concern over delay in framing of rules/r egulations under different 
Acts. The Committee in its 47 
th  report presented to the House on 14.9.1981 
has recommended that the rule-making process should be completed within a 
period of six months of the coming into force on an  Act. The Committee has 
further recommended that in case a Ministry is not able to adhere to this 
time limit, it should seek extension of time for co mpleting the rule-making 
process. The recommendation of the committee reads as follows:- 
 
  “The Committee feels that the rule-making process could well 
have been completed within the time limit of six mo nths which the 
Committee has laid down for making of rules under a n Act after 
coming into force of that Act. Where however, a Ministry does not find
