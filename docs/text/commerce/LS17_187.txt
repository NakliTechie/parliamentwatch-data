2. During the course of scrutiny of these Orders, the Committee has noted 
some cases where there has been a cons iderable time lag between the date of 
notification of the Order a nd the date of its laying. The annexed statement also 
indicates the period of delay in cases where notifications have not been laid as per 
the schedule prescribed by the Committee and where such notifications were 
accompanied by a statement indicating the reasons for the delay in laying. In 
some of the cases the dela y has been quite long. The 
observations/recommendations of the Co mmittee in respect of the specific 
Ministries/Departments are as follows: -    
  
(i) Ministry of Agriculture (Depar tment of Agriculture Research and 
Education) 
  
 The Ministry of Agriculture (Depar tment of Agriculture Research and 
Education) have laid 4 notif ications with delay of 10 m onths in each case. All of 
them have been accompanied with delay statements. The reason for delay given 
by the Ministry is that the time taken to  get sufficient number of copies printed 
caused delay. 
  
 The Committee finds that the reply given by the Ministry as to the 
reasons of delay is not convincing and  appears to be evasive. Delay could be 
avoided, had precaution been taken by the Ministry in this regard. The 
Committee hopes that the Ministry of Ag riculture  would cut on such delays 
in future particularly on account of delay in receiving printed copies of 
notifications by evolving an effectiv e mechanism and would adhere to the 
time schedule as prescribed in Committee’s 131
st Report. 
 
(ii) Ministry of Commerce and Industry (Department of Commerce)  
 
The Department of Commerce have laid  five notifications out of which 
two notification (Sl. No. 26-27) have b een delayed. Delay period extends upto 5 
months and 8 months respectively.  There is no delay statement  appended with 
either of the notifications delayed. 
  
 The Committee emphasises that the Ministry of Commerce and 
Industry (Department of Commerce), strict ly adhere to the time schedule as 
recommended by it in its 131 st Report  in laying of the Subordinate 
Legislation before Parliament and in case of any delay in laying of the 
notifications, a delay statement sh ould also invariably be appended 
therewith. 
 
 

 
 
(iii) Ministry of Communicati on and Information Technology 
 
Ministry of Communication and Info rmation Technology have laid 7 
notifications and there is de lay in case of 1 notificat ion (Sl. No. 37) which has 
been delayed by 10 months. There is no delay statement  appended with it. 
 
The Committee is of the strong  views that the Ministry of 
Communication and Information Technol ogy  should take appropriate steps 
to prevent any delay in future for laying of Notifications on the table of the  
House and append delay statements where there has been a delay in laying of 
the notifications. 
 
(iv) Ministry of Consumer Affairs,  Food and Public Distribution  
 
(Department of Consumer Affairs) & (Department of Food and Public 
Distribution) 
 
Department of Consumer Affairs have laid four notifications two of which 
have been delayed. The delay period range s from four to six months. Department 
of Food and Public Distribution  have laid  fourteen notifications out of which two 
notifications (Sl. No. 57 & 58) have been delayed by five and nine months. There 
is no delay statement  appended with any of them. 
 
 The Committee is not happy over the delay in laying of notifications. 
Moreover there is no delay statem ent attached thereto. The Committee 
reiterates its recommendation that in case of delay in laying, notifications 
should invariably be accompanied with delay statement. The Ministry should 
take care that repetition of such delay is avoided in future.  
 
(v) Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
 
  Ministry of Corporate Affairs have laid 27 notifications out of which  11 
notifications (Sl. Nos. 71-74, 76-78, 85-87 & 89) have been delayed. Delay period 
ranges over 1 year to 3 years in case of  nine notifica tions and these have been 
accompanied with delay statements and tw o notifications have  delay period of 
four and five months without any dela y statement thereto. The reason for delay 
given by the Ministry is as under:- 
  
 “The provisions of the Company Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 2006, 
Section 18(5B) and Section 40 of the Co mpany Secretaries Act, 1980 came into 

force w.e.f. 17.11.2006 and 8.8.2006 respectively. Prior to the said amendments 
the Annual Accounts of the Institute of  Company Secretaries of India were 
required to be published in the Gazette of  India under Section 18(5) of the  old  
Act and were  not required  to  be laid  before  both  Houses of Parliament. After 
insertion of both the Section 18(5) and 40, the notifications issued by the Institute 
publishing the Annual Report and Account s for the Financial year ended on 31 st 
March, 2006 onwards were required to be laid before both Houses of Parliament 
under Section 40 of the Company Secretaries Act, 1980. 
 
 The notification with reference to  Annual Report and Accounts for the 
years ended 31
st March, 2006, 2007 and 2008. Therefor e the notifications could 
not be laid before both the Houses within the prescribed period as the Institute has 
followed previous practice. .” 
 
 The Committee notes the reply give n by the Ministry  of Corporate 
Affairs. The Committee cautions the Mi nistry to take the first available 
opportunity for laying of their notifications during the session. 
 
(vi) Ministry of Environment and Forest  
   
 Ministry of Environment and Forest  have laid 29 notifications. Out of 
these, three Notifications have bee delaye d two of which  have delay period of 5 
and 8 months without any delay statemen t appended thereto and one Notification 
is has a delay period of 1 year and 6 m onths alongwith delay statement appended 
thereto. The Ministry have cited admi nistrative reasons for not laying the 
notifications in time. 
 
 The Committee hopes that the Minist ry would avoid such instances of 
delay in laying of subordinate legisl ation in future and would remove lacuna, 
if any administrative or any oth er causing delay. The Committee also 
impress upon the Ministry to take note of the fact that in case there is a delay 
in the laying of any Statutory Notifica tions, the same should be accompanied 
by a delay statement while it is being laid . The Committee also advise the 
Ministry to avoid recurrence of delays due to administrative reasons. 
  
(vii)   Ministry of Finance 
 
 (A)  (Department of Economic Affairs) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs) have laid 22 
notifications out of which  3 notifica tion (Sl. Nos. 122, 129 and 139) have been 

delayed. All of these have delay of five to seventh months. No delay statement is 
appended with any of them. 
  
 The Committee would like the Ministry of Finance (Department of 
Economic Affairs) to take necessary st eps to obviate such delays and incase 
of delays in laying of notifications,  delay statements should invariably be 
accompanied with them. 
 
 
(B)  Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) 
 
 The Ministry of Finance (Departm ent of Revenue) have laid 107 
notifications out of  which 5 notification (Sl. Nos. 235-239) have delay of 5 to 6 
months without delay statement. 4 notifications have delay period of 11 months to 
1 year with delay statement. A total of  9 notifications have been delayed. The 
reason for delay given by the Ministry is  non receipt of the published copies of 
notifications and dissolu tion of Lok Sabha on 5
th May, 2008. Another reason 
given by the Ministry is that the notification coul d not be laid due to 
authentication of notification as we ll as explanatory memorandum to the 
notification made by Minister on 12.08.2009. 
  
 The Committee would like to imp ress upon the Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Revenue) that it is their responsibility to ensure that copies of 
notifications are received timely from the Government of India Press, 
notifications are authenticated and ex planatory memoranda signed well in 
time and for this purpose they may be evolve suitable mechanism of 
check/counter check so that such in stances do not recur. The Committee 
hopes that the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) would take 
action accordingly, in order to comply  with the prescribed schedule in the 
matter of laying of Statutory Notifications. 
 
(viii) Ministry of Health & Family Welfare 
 
(Department of Ayush) 
 
 The Ministry of Health & Family We lfare (Department of Ayush) have 
laid 2 notifications out of which 1 has been delayed by seven month and  no delay 
statement is appended with it. 
  
 The Committee directs the Ministry of  Health & Family Welfare 
(Department of Ayush) to take correctiv e measures to obviate such delay in 

future and in case such delays become  unavoidable  in future, it should 
invariably be accompanied by statements giving the reasons for the delay. 
 
(ix)      Ministry of Home Affairs  
 
 
The Ministry of Home Affairs have laid 19 notifications out of which 
three have been delayed. The delay peri od ranges from 5 months to 10 months. 
There is no delay statement with one notification. 2 notifications have delay 
statement appended thereto. The Ministry  have given reason for delay as non-
availability of requisite number of printed copies from the Press. 
 
 The Committee takes serious note of the fact that delay was caused 
due to printing of copies of notifica tions by Government Press. Ministries 
have been taking recourse of  these v ery frequently. This possibility  should 
have been assessed well before and ade quate arrangements should have been 
made in respect thereof. The Committee advise the Ministry of Home Affairs 
to take care in future so that such delays don’t recur in future. 
 
(x) Ministry of Human Resource Development 
  
(Department of Higher Education) 
 
The Ministry of Human Resource De velopment (Department of Higher 
Education) have laid 5 notifications out of which three notifications (Sl. Nos. 282-
284) have been delayed. The delay period ranges from 4 to 5 months. All of them 
have no delay statement appended. 
 
 The Committee recommends that the Mi nistry should devise suitable 
mechanism to avoid such instances of delay in future and in case of any delay 
a delay statement should invariably be appended at least. 
 
(xi) Ministry of Labour and Employment 
 
 The Ministry have laid 8 notificati ons out of  which 1 notification has 
been delayed by nine months and no  delay statement is appended with it.  
  
 The Committee desires that the Mini stry of Labour and Employment 
should keep vigilant to avoid any delay in future and in case of any delay, a 
delay statement should invariably be appended at least. 
 
 

(xii) Ministry of  Law and Justice 
  
(Legislative Department)  
 
 Ministry of Law and Justice (Legislative Department)  have laid 16 
notifications and all of th em have been delayed by 6 months to 1 year and 2 
months. There is no delay statement appended with them. 
 
 The Committee expresses its unhappi ness over the inordinate delay 
that has taken place in the laying of the notifications. The Committee is also 
takes serious note of the fact that the Ministry has not provided any delay 
statement for the delayed notifications.   In absence of any delay statement 
Committee are not in a position to as  certain what caused this inordinate 
delay. The Committee therefore  recommends  that the Ministry should take 
all necessary precaution in future so as to avoid recurrence of such situations 
and append delay statements where there has been delay. 
 
 
 
 
(xiii)     Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions

 Ministry of Personnel, Public Grieva nces and Pensions (Department of

 The Committee hopes that the Ministry of Personnel, Public 
Grievances and Pensions (Department of Personnel and Training) would

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natura l Gas have laid 13 notifications out

The Committee reiterates its recommendation contained in its 131
st 
Report that in case of delay in layi ng, notifications should be accompanied 

with delay statement. The Ministry shou ld take care that repetition of such 
delay is avoided in future.  
 
(xv) Ministry of Railways  
 
 Ministry of Railways have laid thr ee notifications on the table of the 
House. One notification has been delayed the delay period covers the span of ten 
months. The reasons of delay given by the Ministry is as under:- 
 
“The Notification GSR-127(E) had to be  laid on the table of both Houses 
of Parliament after issue of Gazette Notification. 
 
 However in the instance case, at the time the Notification was issued, Lok 
Sabha had been dissolved and it was cons tituted after the General Election in 
May, 2009. In the first session lasting 9 days (1 st to 9th June) Parliament business 
was Presidential address and oath taki ng by the Cabinet. Th e second session of 
the Parliament from 2 nd July, 2009 to 7 th August, 2009 was the Budget session, 
with Parliament business relating to discussions on both the Railways and General 
Budgets. The delay caused is thus inadvertent and is deeply regretted.” 
 
 
  
The Committee is not convinced by the reason given by the Ministry 
that the notification could not be laid because of the discussion on the 
Railways and General Budgets during the Budget Session from 2
nd July, 2009 
to 7th August, 2009 as laying of notificati ons was also done during the Budget 
Session period. The Committee fails to understand why notification of the 
Ministry of Railways could not be  laid  due to the discussion on the Railways 
and General Budgets. The Committee, therefore, recommends that adequate 
precaution should be exercised to avoid recurrence of su ch instances in 
future. 
 
(xvi) Ministry of Road  Transport & Highways 
 
  The Ministry of Road Transport & Highways  have laid 381 notifications  
on the Table of the House. 77 notificati ons have been delayed. Out of the 77 
notifications which have been delayed, 21 notifications  have been accompanied 
by delay statements and 56 notifications ha ve no delay statements appended with 
them. The delay period ranges from 4 mont hs to 11  months. The reason given by 
the Ministry is that it took some time to complete the procedural formalities and 
therefore, papers could not be laid in time. 
 

 The Committee recommends that the Mi nistry should initiate urgent 
measure so as to avoid del ay in completing the procedural formalities so that 
such delays can be eliminated. 
  
(xvii) Ministry of Textiles 
 
 The Ministry of Textiles have laid 3 notifications. All of them have been

 The Committee desires that the Mini stry of Textiles should take

Table of the House in future and in variably  append the delay statement 
where there is delay in laying of the notifications. 
 
(xviii)  Ministry of Urban Development 
 
The Ministry of Urban Development have laid 4 notifications out of which

The Committee urges the Ministry of Urban Development to take care

3. The Committee impresses upon all the Ministries/Departments to take 
immediate steps to ensure timely and proper laying of subordinate legislation. The 
Committee, accordingly, wishes to bring to the notice of all 
Ministries/Departments of the Government  of India, particularly, the defaulting 
ones, the following recommendati ons, as contained in its 131
st
,135
th and 152 nd 
Reports: 
 “Orders required to be laid before th e House should be laid (i) if the 
House is not in Session, during the Session immediatel y following the date of 
publication of the Order in the Official Gazette and (ii) if the House is in 
Session, on the date of the publication of the Order, during its continuance 
and in case the time lag between the da te of publication and the date of the 
closing of the Session is less than 15 clear days, before the expiry of the 
Session immediately following  the said Session. All the 
Ministries/Departments of Government of India should henceforth comply 
with this schedule in the matter of layi ng of the Orders issued under various 
Acts of Parliament on the Table of the Rajya Sabha. 

(131st Report) 
(Presented on 15 December 2000) 
 “The Committee has, time and again, emphasized that it is essential that 
in case there is a delay in the laying of Statut ory Orders, the concerned 
Ministry/Department should invariably append a delay statement along with 
the notifications being laid so as to a pprise Parliament of the causes of delay. 
The Committee desires all Ministries/Dep artments to take a note of this, 
since the intension is that through the process of formulation of a delay 
statement the Ministry is able to intr ospect, identify bottlenecks and devise 
remedial measures”. 
(135th Report) 
(Presented on 27 July 2001) 
 “In the opinion of the Committee, every  notification should be assigned a 
distinctive number in order to give it a separate identity and similarly, no 
notification should be published/laid which does not carry a notification 
number. The Committee expects all Mini stries/Departments of Government 
of India to exercise caution in this re gard. In case where there are more than 
one notification under a single file, the Ministries/Departments should ensure 
that the file number on each notificati on should be suitably distinguished 
from one another.
