2.  While scrutinizing these Orders, the Committee note d 
that there was a considerable time lag between issu e  of 

Notifications and their laying in some cases. The a nnexed 
statement also indicates the period of delay in cas es where 
Notifications have not been laid as per the time sc hedule 
prescribed by the Committee. The Committee observed  that 
out of the total of 135 delayed Notifications, ther e were 96 
Notifications where no delay statement was appended  to. 
However, the bulk of delayed Notifications were fro m the 
Ministry of  Road Transport and Highways which come s to 
around 40.7% of the total delayed Notifications. 
 
The observations/recommendations of the Committee 
in respect of the specific Ministries/Departments w here delay 
in laying of Notifications has been noticed, are as follows: -  
 
(i) Ministry of AYUSH  
 
 The  Ministry of AYUSH have laid only one 
Notification (Sl. No. 19) which has been delayed by 5 months 
and is accompanied with delay statement. The reason  given 
by the Ministry for delay is as under: 
 “Laying of the Regulation made under the provision s 
of Homoeopathy Central Council Act, 1973 namely 
“Homoeopathy (Degree Course) Amendment Regulations,  
2015” vide  gazette Notification no. 12-13/2006-CCH(Pt.V) 
published in Part-III, Section 4, of Gazette of Ind ia dated the 
14 
th  July, 2015 published by Central Council of 
Homoeopathy, a statutory body under the Ministry of  
AYUSH (erstwhile Department of AYUSH under Ministry  of 
Health and Family Welfare). The reasons for delay i n laying 
the notified Regulation in both the Houses of Parli ament are 
given below:- 
 
(i)  The Regulation notified by CCH on 14 
th  July, 
2015. 

(ii)  CCH furnished the notified copies of the 
regulation to the Department on 17.08.2015. 
(iii)  The copies could not be laid by 13.08.2015 
(the last date of the session) as delay occurred 
in completing the formalities for laying. 
 
 The Committee takes serious note of such a long 
delay of 5 months and find the reasons of delay giv en by 
the Ministry unconvincing and evasive. In view of t he fact 
that it is a statutory obligation to lay the 
notifications/orders/rules on the Table of the Hous e in 
time, the situation should have been assessed well in 
advance and adequate arrangements should have been 
made in respect thereof. The Committee advises the 
Ministry of AYUSH to take precaution in future so t hat 
such delay does not recur and time schedule prescri bed in 
Committee’s  131 
st  Report be strictly adhered to. 
 
(ii) Ministry of Chemical and Fertilizers 
 (Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals)  
 
Ministry of Chemical and Fertilizers (Department of  
Chemicals and Petrochemicals) has laid  one Notification (Sl. 
No. 20) and has no delay statement appended thereto . The 
delay period is of astounding 21 months. 
 
The Committee is constrained to note that not only 
has there been inordinate delay in  laying of the o nly one 
Notification by the Ministry of Chemical and Fertil izers 
(Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals) on the  
Table of the House but also gross failure of that t o have 
appended the delay statement with it. The 
Ministry/Department must ensure that Notifications 
should be laid in time on the Table of the House. I t also 

must ensure to append a delay statement in all such  cases 
in future. 
 
 
(iii) Ministry of Civil Aviation  
 
 Ministry of Civil Aviation have laid only one 
Notification (Sl. No. 22) which has been delayed by  8 
months. The Ministry have appended delay statement with 
the Notification and the reasons given by the Minis try are as 
under:- 
 “The Gazette Notification to make amendment in rul e 
2 and to insert a new rule 9A in the Aircraft (Carr iage of 
Dangerous Goods) Rules, 2003 vide  G.S.R. No. 296 (E), 
dated the 16 
th  April, 2015 published by the Ministry of Civil 
Aviation was to be laid in both the Houses of Parli ament. The 
authenticated copies of Gazette Notification along with 
bilingual explanatory notes were sent to Lok Sabha/ Rajya 
Sabha Secretariat for laying them on the Table of t he Houses 
of Parliament during the Monsoon Session of Parliam ent 
2015. However, due to the fact that no question day  was left 
for Ministry of Civil Aviation, the papers could no t be laid on 
the Table of the Houses of Parliament. Therefore, n ow 
Gazette Notification is being submitted along with the 
explanatory notes before both the Houses of Parliament.” 
 
The Committee, time and again, have emphasized 
that laying of rules/regulations/orders is a statut ory 
obligation of the Ministries and should be complete d on 
priority within specified time-frame. The Ministry of Civil 
Aviation should understand the implications of such  
failures. However, the Ministry of Civil Aviation h ave 
failed to comply with the recommendations of the 
Committee in this regard by delaying it for 8 month s and 

then sending on the day when the laying could not b e 
undertaken as session was nearing its end. This is in 
violation of the recommendation of the Committee in  this 
regard. The Committee emphasizes that Ministry shou ld 
adhere to the time schedule in laying of Notificati ons as 
prescribed in Committee’s 131 st  Report.

(vii)  Ministry of Defence 
  
The Ministry of Defence have laid only one 
Notification (Sl. No. 77) which has been delayed by  6 
months. The Ministry have appended delay statement with 
the Notification and the reasons given by the Minis try is as 
under:- 
“The Pune Cantonment Board (Local Body Tax) Bye-
laws, 2015 were sent to the Government of India Pre ss for 
publication in the Official Gazette of India on 04. 06.2015. It 
took some time to get the published copies of the N otification 
from the Government of India Press. The Sitting of the Eighth 
(5 
th ) Session of Sixteenth Lok Sabha and Two Hundred an d 
Thirty Six (236 th ) Session of Rajya Sabha concluded on 13 th  
August, 2015. 
2. As per the procedure for laying of Papers on the  Table 
of Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha issued from time to time b y Lok 
Saha/Rajya Sabha Secretariat, the papers to be laid  before the 
Houses are required to be forwarded to them at leas t three 
days in advance before the date on which these were  
proposed to be laid on the Table. It is requested t hat the 
marginal delay on the part of Ministry of Defence m ay kindly 
be condoned.” 
 
 The Committee finds that laying of Notifications 
was delayed due to late receipt of published copies  of 
Notification in the Ministry. The Committee does no t 
accept this as a convincing reason at all for such delays in 

laying of statutory papers. The Committee observes that it 
is the Ministry’s prime responsibility to ensure th at 
Subordinate Legislation is laid before the Parliame nt 
within the prescribed schedule and accordingly, it is for 
the Ministry only to ensure that they receive the p rinted 
copies  timely.  
 The Committee is not inclined to accept the late 
availability of printed copies of Notifications fro m the 
Government of India Press as the reason for delay i n 
laying of the Notifications. The Committee directs all the 
Ministries that, in case, there is delay in procuring printed 
copies in time, they may obtain G.S.R. and S.O. num bers 
from the Government of India Press and prepare 
requisite number of copies in cyclostyled form for laying 
in the Parliament as suggested by Secretary, Minist ry of  
Urban Development, the controlling Ministry of 
Government of India Press, in its meeting held on 3 0  
July, 2013. The Committee also recommends that this  
process may be adopted by Ministries suo moto  on 
immediate basis so as to avoid such delays henceforth.

 As the Bank furnished the requisite copies of Gaze tte 
Notification after the stipulated period, clarifica tion was

 The Bank has reported that the amendment to 
Regulation was notified in the Gazette on 22.07.201 1 and has

 In view of the above, the said Notification could not 
be laid on the Table of both the Houses on earlier occasions. 

Delay in this regard is deeply regretted and may pl ease be

(2) “The Notification dated 08.10.2014 regarding

 As the Bank furnished the requisite copies of Gaze tte

 The Committee are surprised at the length of time
