REPORT 
 
 
 The Department of Public Enterprises (of the Ministry of Heavy Industries and 
Public Enterprises) is the nodal department for all Central Public Sector Enterprises 
(CPSEs) and formulates policy pertaining to the role of CPSEs in the economy and also 
lays down policy guidelines for performance improvement, autonomy and financial 
delegation, personnel management and other related areas.  The DPE is also the interface 
between the administrative Ministries and the CPSEs.  
 
2. There are approximately 246 PSUs out of which 213 are functional, these 246 
PSUs are distributed among 38 different Min istries.  So, it is a vast colla ge of enterprises 
distributed among different sections of the Government. 
 
3. The intention of the Government in creating the DPE was to f ind an enabling 
mechanism which would be able to coordinate and which would also bring some 
cohesion as to where and in what direction the public enterprises should move and how 
there could also be an independent third party evaluation of these PSEs.   
  
 The Department tries to continuously improve the management of PSEs by way 
of professionalization  of the management, goal setting and reviewing performance of 
PSEs, evolving guidelines for corporate governance and social responsibility and 
strengthening the institutional mechanism for revival of sick units. 
  
4. As per the Allocation of Business Rules of the Central Government, the following 
subjects have been allocated to the Department of Public Enterprises:- 
 
(i) Co-ordination of matters of general policy o f non -financial nature 
affecting all public sector industrial and commercial undertakings. 
(ii) Matters relating to Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) mechanism of 
improving the performance of Public Sector Undertakings. 
(iii) Matters relating to Permanent Machinery o f Arbitration for the Public 
Sector Enterprises. 
(iv) Matter relating to Counselling, Retraining and Redeployment of 
employees in Central Public Sector Undertakings (CPSUs) under 
Voluntary Retirement Scheme (VRS).  
 
“The Committee desires that in order to play a meaningful and effective role 
in getting the policies and guidelines implemented by the PSEs the Department of 
Public Enterprises should ask for the compliance report from the PSEs about the 
implementation of the policies and guidelines formulated by it from time to time and 
separate paragraph thereon may be incorporated in the Annual Report of the 
Ministry”.

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING ( MoU) 
 
26. The MoU system has started several years ago and it has become robust and 
entrenched over the last 3-4 years.  The PSEs and their mother Ministries are subscribing 
to the MoUs.  The MoUs are made at the b eginning of the performance year in which 
targets are set which are measurable, quantifiable and could be monitored. 
 
27. The Secretary of the DPE informed that earlier the MoU was signed between the 
mother Ministry and the CPSE and because of relationship  that existed between them 
often soft targets were being set for own CPSEs.  Now, the DPE being a third party it 
takes an objective view of the performance and try to set benchmarks that are not only 
limited to that particular enterprises but related to the industry across where it operates. 
 
 
 
9 
 


28. The evaluation of the performance of MoU is done at the end of the year in which 
mother Ministry and the CPSE sit in conjunction with the Task Force of DPE consisting 
of 5-6 experts in finance, domain experts,  retired bureaucrats etc.  In the evaluation 50% 
marks are given for the financial performance and 50% for non -financial performance 
such as corporate social responsibility, sustainable development, human resource etc. on 
that basis PSEs are graded as excellent, very good, good etc. 
   
29. The Department of Public Enterprises is the nodal agency for the matter 
pertaining to MoU between the PSEs and the administrative Ministries, the Committee 
noted that 144 PSEs in 2007 -08, 147 PSEs in 2008 -09 and 197 PSEs in 2009-10 had 
signed MoU.    
 
30. The Department informed the Committee that the performance of the CPSEs is 
evaluated on the basis of achievements against MoU targets.  CPSE submits self -
evaluation report and audited data in Sept -Oct in the following yea r to the one which is 
evaluated and finalized by DPE with expertise of the Task Force Syndicate groups.  The 
ratings of the CPSEs during the year 2007-08 and 2008-09 were as follows:
