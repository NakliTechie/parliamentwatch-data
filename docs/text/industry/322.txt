Recommendation/Observation 
1. The Committee welcomes the goal of the Government for making 
India a $5 trillion economy by the Year 2024 -25 and opines that the target  
can be achieved by growth and development of the MSME sector. As MSME 
sector is vital for fostering entrepreneurship and generating self -
employment opportunities at comparatively lower capital cost and are 
complementary to large industries as ancillary units, formulation of targeted 
policies in the areas of infrastructure development, technological 
upgradation and forward linkages could help MSMEs to achieve their full 
potential and propel the Indian economy to a higher growth trajectory. The 
Committee rec ommends that Government need to play a proactive role in 
promoting MSME Sector by handholding the MSMEs at the grassroots level 
and for this, a top down approach needs to be implemented right down to 
the last tier of the MSME sector in letter, spirit and a ction. In this regard, a 
roadmap should be devised to achieve the desired results.  
(Para 10) 
 
Action Taken Replies 
The Government implements various schemes, programmes and 
initiatives for the promotion, development and enhancing the competiveness 
of MSME  sector in the country. A number of recent initiatives have been 
taken to strengthen, support, finance, revive and reconstruct MSMEs. These 
include:  
(i) Rs. 50,000 crore equity infusion through Self Reliant India Fund. This 
scheme has a provision of Rs.10 , 000 crore from Government of India and 
Rs.40,000 Crore leverage through Private Equity/Venture Capital funds. 
This initiative is aimed at providing growth capital to the deserving and 
eligible units of MSME sector.  
(ii) New revised criteria for classification of MSMEs. 
(iii) No global tenders for procurement up to Rs. 200 crore. 
(iv) “Udyam Registration” for MSMEs, for Ease of Doing Business w.e.f. 
1.7.2020 

 4 
(v) Launching of an online Portal “Champions” in June, 2020 to cover 
many aspects of e -governance including grievance redress and handholding 
of MSMEs. 
(vi) Inclusion of Retail and Wholesale traders as MSMEs w.e.f. 2.7. 2021. 
(vii) Non-tax benefits extended for 3 years in case of an upward change in 
status of MSMEs. 
(viii) Launch of Udyam Assist Platfo rm on 11.1.2023 to bring Informal 
Micro Enterprises (IMEs) under the formal ambit for availing the benefits 
under Priority Sector Lending.  
(ix) The Ministry of MSME , vide notification no. S.O. 5670(E) dated 
9.11.2018, had made it mandatory for all Centra l Government  Ministries/ 
Departments/ CPSUs to procure at least 25% of their annual procurement 
from MSEs, including 3% from MSEs owned by women entrepreneurs. 
(x) Field offices of Ministry of MSME organize various awareness 
campaigns for UDYAM registrati on and benefits of Government schemes 
/programmes, in coordination with MSME/ Industry Departments of 
States/UTs concerned and other MSME stakeholders through physical 
workshops, social media, print media & electronic media for creating wide 
publicity for increasing participation from all stake holders.  
(xi) In order to address the issue of delayed payment to MSMEs, RBI has 
issued guidelines for setting up and operating the Trade Receivables 
Discounting System (TReDS) to facilitate the financing of trade r eceivables 
of MSMEs from corporate and other buyers, including government 
departments and public sector undertakings (PSUs) through multiple 
financiers electronically.  
(xii).  Budget 2023-24, has announced the infusion of Rs. 9,000 crore in the 
corpus of CGTMSE to enable additional credit of Rs. 2.00 lakh crore with a 
reduced cost of credit.

Recommendation/Observation  
6. The Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) 
is the flagship scheme of the Ministry of MSME and the main o bjectives of 
the scheme are generation of sustainable and continuous self -employment 
opportunities in urban and rural areas; providing adequate employment to 

 8 
the rural and urban unemployed youth, traditional and prospective artisans 
through the establishme nt of micro -enterprises; and facilitating the 
participation of financial institutions for encouraging higher credit flow to 
the micro sector. The Committee notes that the RE for the FY 2022 -23 for 
the PMEGP scheme was Rs. 2484.00 crore and the Actual Expenditure up to 
the 13th February, 2023 is Rs. 2000.00 crores which is 80.51 per cent of the 
RE allocations. The Committee is of the view that the Ministry should strive 
to utilize fully the balance amount of 19.49 per cent in the remaining period 
of FY 2022 -23 in consonance with the Financial Rules and considering the 
importance of the scheme, the Ministry should also utilize the enhanced 
allocation of Rs. 2700.00 crore for the FY2023 -24 in order to make the 
Programme efficacious. 
(Para 44) 
Action Taken Replies 
PMEGP is one of the flagship schemes of the Ministry of MSME 
capable of generating large scale employment opportunities for the people, 
especially in rural areas of the country and it significantly contributes 
towards Government‟s endeavor to create employment opportunities.  
 PMEGP Scheme since its inception has created employment 
opportunities for 71 lakh people through establishment of more than 8.75 
lakh micro enterprises in rural as well as urban areas across the country.  
  During FY 2022-23, initially BE allocation was Rs.2500 cr ore, which 
was reduced to Rs.2484 crore. However, keeping in view the performance of 
the scheme and increase in number of projects sanctioned by the Banks 
during 2022-23, the RE allocation was enhanced to Rs.2733.21 cr ore, which 
has been fully utilized during 2022-23. 
  For the FY 2023 -24, BE allocation of Rs.2700 cr ore has been made. 
All efforts will be made for timely utilization of this fund.

discontinuing the role of District Level Task Force Committee

Recommendation/Observation 
9. The Committee notes that the BE for SFURTI Scheme for the FY 
2022-23 was Rs. 33 4.00  crore which was reduced drastically to Rs. 1.95 
crore only at the RE Stage due to the introduction of a new system of 
accounting according to which the amount was reduced to Rs. 1.95 crore 
and the Ministry concentrated in utilizing the unspent balanc e of Rs. 200.00 
crore lying with different implementing and nodal agencies.  In this regard, 
the Committee was apprised that in the old system, the money used to be 
kept in banks and so the amount remained unutilized whereas in the new 
system the money com es to the Central Nodal Agency (CNA) where the 
unspent balance is first collected and then depending upon the actual 
expenditure in the field, the money is released, thereby helping in 
disbursement of money. The Committee is of the view that the new system  of 
accounting should be accessible to the beneficiary actually in need and a 
clear set of guidelines from the top to the bottom should be put in place for 
accessibility of the intended beneficiary. The Committee appreciates the BE 
of Rs. 280.00 crore for the SFURTI Scheme for the FY 2023 -2024 and 
recommends that the allocation should be utilized optimally for employment 
generation and benefit of the artisans.  
(Para 51) 
Action Taken Replies  
Under the new fund flow mechanism introduced by Dep artment of 
Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, the account of the Central Nodal Agency 
(CNA) has been mapped with the accounts of the Sub Agencies (SA) i.e, 
the Nodal Agencies (NA) for effective implementation of SFURTI projects. 

 13 
Besides, all the accounts of the Implement ing Agencies (IA)/Special 
Purpose Vehicle (SPV) are also being mapped with the Sub Agency 
accounts to extend desired benefits of the scheme to the beneficiaries. Also, 
the scheme is under review for continuation beyond 30.9.2022. 136 clusters 
are proposed to be set up during the FY 2023 -24. Under the revised fund 
flow mechanism, the BE of Rs 280.00 crore for the FY 2023 -24 will be 
utilized towards development of the 136 clusters directly benefitting 81600 
artisans as well as in meeting the requirements of already approved clusters.

Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) was announced 
as part of the Aatma Nirbhar Bharat Package in 2020 with the objective to 
help MSMEs/small businesses to meet their operational liabilities and 
resume businesses in view of the distress caused by the COVID-19 crisis, by 
providing Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) 100 per cent guarantee in 
respect of the credit facility extended by them to eligible borrowers.  
ECLGS is not a beneficiary -oriented scheme; rather it is a guarantee 
scheme which provides guarantee to MLIs to encourag e them to lend to 
eligible borrowers under the scheme. The amount disbursed under the 
scheme to National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Limited (NCGTC), 
the agency operating the scheme, is to be utilized to meet the claims arising 
out of defaults under t he scheme, if any. Since the launch of ECLGS, actual 
defaults under the scheme has been less than that estimated at the time of 
introduction of the scheme and hence the utilisation of funds for meeting the 
claims arising out of the guarantee issued has bee n on the lower side. 
Lending to borrowers under ECLGS by MLIs by availing guarantees from 

 24 
NCGTC has no correlation to utilisation of funds. Loans have continued to 
be extended to eligible borrowers under the Scheme by the MLIs and 
guarantees were issued by  NCGTC upon application made by MLIs. Thus, 
borrowers have not been adversely impacted due to the lower utilisation of 
funds.

crore was allocated at the BE Stage, as per the request of t he implementing

Rs. 10,500 crore at RE stage.  Funds allocated at RE stage have been 100%
