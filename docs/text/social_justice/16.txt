INTRODUCTION 
 
 I, the Chairperson, Standing Committee on Social Justice and 
Empowerment (2025-26) having been authorized by the Committee to submit the 
Report on their behalf, do present this Sixteenth Report on the Action taken by 
the Government on the Observations/Recommendations contained in the Eighth 
Report (Eighteenth Lok Sabha) on ‘Demands for Grants (2025-26)’ of the Ministry 
of Minority Affairs. 
 
2. The Eighth Report was presented to Lok Sabha and laid in Rajya Sabha 
on 19.03.2025. The Ministry of Minority Affairs have furnished their replies 
indicating action taken on the recommendations contained in that Report on 
19.06.2025. The Report was considered and adopted by the Standing Committee 
on Social Justice and Empowerment at their sitting held on 9.12.2025. 
 
3. An analysis of the Action taken by the Government on the 
Observations/Recommendations contained in the Eighth Report (Eighteenth Lok 
Sabha) on ‘Demands for Grants (2025-26)’ of the Ministry of Minority Affairs is 
given in Appendix.

BUDGETARY ALLOCATION AND EXPENDITURE 
Recommendation (Para No.2.6 and 3.10) 
5. The Committee, in their original Report, had recommended as follows:- 
“The Committee note that the actual expenditure of the Ministry of Minority 
Affairs has gone down considerably during previous years. The Ministry could 
spend only ₹837.68 crore and ₹1,032.65 crore out of ₹2,612.66 (RE) crore 
and ₹2,608.93 (RE) crore during the year 2022-23 and 2023-24. The 
situation did not improve in 2024-25 as the Ministry were able to spend only 
₹919.10 crore out of budgetary allocation of ₹3,183.24 crore (BE) by 31 
December, 2024. The Committee have been apprised that the Ministry have 
not been able to spend the budgetary allocation as several Schemes such as 
Free Coaching and Allied Scheme, Nai Udaan, Scheme of Providing Education 
in Madrasas/ Minorities (SPEMM), etc, have been discontinued and PM-VIKAS 
Scheme launched in 2023-24 did not initiate. Further, the amount allocated 
during 2022-23 to 2024-25 under various Scholarship Schemes of the 
Ministry could not be spent because these Schemes are under consideration 
for approval since 2022-23 as they are being harmonised with the Schemes 
of other line Ministries, viz. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment and 
Ministry of Tribal Affairs. The Committee strongly believe that the Ministry of 
Minority Affairs are making all efforts to achieve the mission of the Ministry 
by streamlining and strengthening the prevailing System so that the 
deserving Minorities can avail the benefits of the Schemes and the funds are 
not misused. The Committee feel that the steps taken by the Ministry were 
necessary in order to harmonize the system with the welfare Schemes of 
Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment and Ministry of Tribal Affairs and 
hope that these steps will improve the implementation of the Schemes and 
enhance the socio-economic condition of six notified minority communities in 
the country. Since the Ministry of Minority Affairs are in process of 
streamlining/strengthening the prevailing System, the Committee expect that 
the necessary approvals will be obtained by the Ministry at the earliest so 
that the allocated Budget of ₹3,350.00 crore for 2025-26 is fruitfully utilised. 
The Committee, therefore, recommend the Ministry of Minority Affairs to 
vigorously pursue with the concerned Ministries/ Departments for the 
pending approvals and keep the administrative machinery of the Ministry 
ready for immediate implementation of the Schemes as soon as they are 
approved.” 


3 
 
6. The Ministry of Minority Affairs, in their Action Taken Reply, have submitted 
as under:- 
 “The Observations of Hon’ble Committee have been noted for compliance.  
Under the Nai Udaan scheme, financial support was provided to candidates 
who qualify in the preliminary examinations conducted by Union Public 
Service Commission (UPSC), Staff Selection Commission (SSC), State Public 
Service Commissions (SPSCs). No other Ministry / Department provide such 
support to their beneficiaries. Hence, in order to align with the practices and 
schemes followed by other Ministries and to attain uniformity, it had been 
decided to discontinue this scheme w.e.f. 2022-23. 
Under Free Coaching and Allied Schemes (Naya Savera), coaching were 
provided to students from minority community to empower and prepare 
them for competitive and entrance examinations. The New Education Policy 
2020 does not support coaching programs; thus, the Ministry decide to 
discontinue the scheme w.e.f. 2022-23. 
Scholarship: 
After taking cognizance of media reports in the year 2020 regarding 
siphoning of Scholarship fund, the matter was referred to the Central Bureau 
of Investigation. Also, States were shared suggestive measures to 
incorporate in implementation of Scholarship Schemes. 
In the year 2023, a third party evaluation study, conducted by this Ministry, 
highlighted some irregularities, wherein out of sampled 1,572 institutes, 830 
institutes were found to be either fake/ partially fake or non-operational. 
Observing the seriousness of the issue, Aadhaar based bio-metric 
authentication drive was conducted in which about 27 lakh students and 
verifying officials were to be verified. After the drive, about 18.87 lakh 
students were finally verified for the year 2022-23. Subsequently, in order to 
make the system more robust, Aadhaar Payment Bridge System has been 
introduced for disbursement of scholarships.  
In the year 2024-25, a special drive for verification was held in the North 
Eastern States wherein, fake beneficiaries and verifying authorities were 
recorded. These were for the academic year 2021-22 and 2022-23. This 
exhibited that due diligence was not done by the States in implementing the 
Scholarship Schemes of Minority Affairs in some institutes. States have filed 
FIRs in this regard. Besides, the States have been given additional list of 
suspicious institutes (based on red flags generated by National Informatics 
Centers- National Scholarship Portal) to get them verified/ inspected and 

4 
 
take punitive actions against fraudulent elements. However, reports from the 
states are still awaited.  
In order to prevent duplicity of benefits, MoMA has also asked all the States 
to integrate their State Scholarship Portals with the National Scholarship 
Portal so that de-duplication can be done and one-student one-benefit could 
be ensured. However, the efforts by the States are yet to take a logical 
shape. In such circumstances, approval of Schemes could not be accorded 
by the competent authority and thus, the Ministry could not spend allocated 
funds under the three Scholarship Schemes. This Ministry endeavors to 
implement these schemes at the earliest.” 
 
Recommendation (Para No.3.10) 
7. The Committee, in their original Report, had recommended as follows:- 
“The Committee find that the actual expenditure under various educational 
Schemes was very insignificant in comparison to the budgetary allocation as 
several Schemes such as Free Coaching and Allied Scheme, Nai Udaan, 
Scheme of Providing Education in Madrasas/Minorities (SPEMM), Maulana 
Azad National Fellowship, etc. have been discontinued and Pre-Matric and 
Post-Matric Scholarship Schemes are pending for approval since 2022-23. 
The Committee feel that since the Schemes discontinued were in operation 
for several years, their sudden discontinuation would deprive the targeted 
beneficiaries from the benefits of these Schemes. The Committee have no 
doubt about the intention of the Ministry but strongly desire that the 
substitutes of these Schemes even if at all available elsewhere, the 
beneficiaries should be made aware of such Schemes by publicizing them. 
Further, the Ministry should take necessary measures for the Scholarship 
Schemes pending for approval as the financial aid provided under these 
Schemes is very important and any more delay will adversely affect the 
educational prospects of the students belonging to Minority Communities. 
The Committee may like to be apprised of the initiatives taken by the 
Ministry of Minority Affairs in getting the approvals of these Schemes.”

9. While examining the budgetary allocation, the Committee found 
that the Ministry of Minority Affairs was not able to spend the budgetary 
allocation made during 2022-23 to 2024-25 under various Scholarship 
Schemes as they were in the process of harmonization of the Schemes of 
the Ministry with the Schemes of other Ministries, viz. Ministry of Social 
Justice & Empowerment and Ministry of Tribal Affairs. Hence, the Ministry 
were recommended to vigorously pursue with the concerned Ministries/ 
Departments for the approvals pending for the early harmonization of 
Schemes so that the Budget of ₹3,350.00 crore allocated for 2025-26 is 
fruitfully utilized. The Committee, however, do not find any commitment 


6 
 
for the allocated Budget from the action taken reply of the Ministry. The 
Committee expected that the Ministry would take suitable action to stop 
the misuse of the Schemes by the State Governments, but the Committee 
find that problem still persists and the Ministry have not been able to 
establish the required mechanism in the States due to lack of cooperation 
from States. Reiterating their earlier recommendation the Committee 
desire that strong measures needs to be taken for the State cooperation 
to stop misuse of Schemes in their States and also take measures for 
harmonizing of the Schemes of the Ministry with other Ministries without 
any further delay. The Committee would like to be apprised of the 
outcome of the steps taken by them in this regard.

CHAPTER-II 
OBSERVATIONS/RECOMMENDATIONS WHICH HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED BY 
THE GOVERNMENT 
Recommendation (Para No. 4.8) 
The Committee note that the Ministry of Minority Affairs is implementing Pradhan 
Mantri Virasaat Ka Samvardhan since 2023-24 by converging five erstwhile 
Schemes namely Seekho Aur Kamao, USTTAD, Hamari Dharohar, Nai Roshni and 
Nai Manzil. The Committee find that the Ministry spent ₹83.51 crore out of Revised 
Estimate of ₹169.00 crore in 2022-23. After the convergence of independent 
Schemes in PM-VIKAS, the Ministry could spend only 209.42 crore out of RE of 
₹325.86 crore during 2023-24 and ₹124.08 crore out of ₹230.00 crore upto 31 
December, 2024 during 2024-25 to meet the committed liability of 2023-24 and 
2024-25 relating to the erstwhile Schemes. The PM-VIKAS Scheme, however, could 
not be implemented during these years as the guidelines of the Schemes could not 
be approved. The Committee feel that the Ministry should have timely initiated the 
proposal for approval of guidelines to avoid the extraordinary delay. The Committee 
now expect that the guidelines have since been approved, the targeted 
beneficiaries will not have to wait anymore to avail the benefit of the Scheme. The 
Committee hope that the approval of the proposal to integrate the portal of the 
Ministry with National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) etc. will not be delayed 
and the tie up proposed under the Scheme with National Institute of Design (NID), 
National Institute of Fashion Technology and Expert Promotion Council for 
Handicrafts will be finalized and executed at the earliest. The Committee would like 
the Ministry to sanction the proposals received for approval without any delay and 
take necessary action to ensure that the proposals are approved in a time bound 
manner. The Committee may like to be intimated of the action taken in this regard.        
 
Reply of the Government 
The observation of the Hon’ble Committee has been noted for compliance. 
Since the approval of the PM VIKAS guidelines, the Ministry has signed 
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with its knowledge partners including 
National Institute of Design (NID), National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT), 
National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) and Export Promotion Council for 
Handicrafts (EPCH) under the scheme. 
The Ministry has held Screening and Empowered committee meetings with 
various stakeholders under the PM VIKAS scheme to deliberate and approve the 

8 
 
proposals received for implementation of the scheme by two (02) institutions 
namely Delhi Sikh Gurudwara Management Committee (DSGMC) and State Institute 
of Hotel Management (SIHM), Manipur for 31,600 and 7,950 beneficiaries 
respectively. 
The Ministry has also signed an MOU with DSGMC and allocated the project 
of 31,600 candidates under PM VIKAS Scheme. 
Ministry of Minority Affairs  OM No. G-20014/02/2025- Budget  Dated   
19.06.2025 
 
Recommendation (Para No. 5.10) 
The Committee note that the Pradhan Mantri Jan Vikas Karyakram (PMJVK) is being 
implemented since 2017-18 with an objective to develop community infrastructure 
and basic amenities in the identified areas for improving the quality of life of the 
people in the Minority Concentrated Areas. The Committee find that the areas 
covered under PMJVK were increased from 90 Districts to 308 Districts in 33 
States/UTs w.e.f. May, 2018. On the scrutiny of budgetary allocation, the 
Committee observed that an actual expenditure of ₹222.66 crore out of ₹500.00 
crore (RE) and ₹189.23 crore out of ₹550.00 crore (RE) could only be made due to 
the introduction of SNA during 2022-23 and 2023-24 respectively. The Committee 
are happy to find that the Ministry of Minority Affairs have successfully spent 
₹618.00 crore (till 31.12.2024) out of ₹908.90 crore (RE) allocated under the 
Scheme during 2024-25 since the problems faced due to the introduction of SNA 
system has now been resolved. The Committee hope that BE for 2025-26 has since 
been substantially increased to ₹1913.97 crore from ₹910.90 crore in 2024-25, the 
Ministry should make an action plan to utilize the allocated funds so that they may 
not be reduced at the RE stage. The Committee would like to recommend the 
Ministry to popularize the Scheme so that the proposals are received from 
concerned Organisations and State Governments and take necessary measures to 
approve the proposals received in a time bound manner. They would also like to 
suggest the Ministry to provide the technical and administrative support to the 
implementing Agencies so that they are able to send complete proposals and the 
unnecessary delay due to the receipt of incomplete proposals is avoided. 
 
Reply of the Government 
The observation of the Hon’ble Committee has been noted for compliance. 
It may be informed that the Ministry of Minority Affairs is putting all efforts 
to make the States/Union Territories compliant to the PFMS and SNA Guidelines, 
reduction in the unspent balances available in the States/UTs treasuries and the 

9 
 
completion of old approved projects. Regular Regional Review Meetings/ re-
conciliation are being done to pursue the States/UTs for speedy completion of the 
approved project units and to release the funds to compliant States/UTs.  
The BE for 2025-26 in respect of PMJVK Scheme has been increased from 
₹910.90 crore to ₹1913.97 crore in the financial year of 2024-25. As per the 
recommendations of the committee, the Ministry has already worked out and made 
an action plan to utilize the allocated funds for PMJVK Scheme timely.   Due to this 
an expenditure of ₹204.28 crore (upto 16.6.2025) has already been incurred under 
the scheme and released to the eligible States/UTs for implementation of projects 
approved under PMJVK. Further, many States/UTs are eligible for release of funds 
and it is expected that remaining ₹809 crore may be released to them during 2nd 
quarter. 
With a vision to usher the digitization of the scheme for effective monitoring 
and evaluation of works taken up at the grassroots level, the Ministry has also 
introduced new modules i.e. QPR reports, Drop module, UC module, big ticket 
projects module etc. in the PMJVK Portal. A two day’s National Workshop was also 
held on 07.05.2025 & 08.05.2025, in which States/UTs/CGOs were given hands-on 
training for effective utilization of these newly developed modules of PMJVK Portal. 
Further, a dedicated PMU team of PMJVK also deployed for regular monitoring 
and frequent communication with the respective Nodal Officers of the States/ UTs/ 
CGOs. These States/UTs/CGOs are also given technical & administrative support to 
ensure timely resolution of all their queries.

Schemes. The Committee have no doubt about the intention of the Ministry but 
strongly desire that the substitutes of these Schemes even if at all available 
elsewhere, the beneficiaries should be made aware of such Schemes by publicizing 
them. Further, the Ministry should take necessary measures for the Scholarship 
Schemes pending for approval as the financial aid provided under these Schemes is 
very important and any more delay will adversely affect the educational prospects 
of the students belonging to Minority Communities. The Committee may like to be 
apprised of the initiatives taken by the Ministry of Minority Affairs in getting the 
approvals of these Schemes.  
 
Reply of the Government 
After taking cognizance of media reports in the year 2020 regarding 
siphoning of Scholarship fund, the matter was referred to the Central Bureau of 
Investigation. Also, States were shared suggestive measures to incorporate in 
implementation of Scholarship Schemes. 
In the year 2023, a third party evaluation study, conducted by this Ministry, 
highlighted some irregularities, wherein out of sampled 1,572 institutes, 830 
institutes were found to be either fake/ partially fake or non-operational. Observing 
the seriousness of the issue, Aadhaar based bio-metric authentication drive was 
conducted in which about 27 lakh students and verifying officials were to be 
verified. After the drive, about 18.87 lakh students were finally verified for the year 

14 
 
2022-23. Subsequently, in order to make the system more robust, Aadhaar 
Payment Bridge System has been introduced for disbursement of scholarships. 
In the year 2024-25, a special drive for verification was held in the North 
Eastern States wherein, fake beneficiaries and verifying authorities were recorded. 
These were for the academic year 2021-22 and 2022-23. This exhibited that due 
diligence was not done by the States in implementing the Scholarship Schemes of 
Minority Affairs in some institutes. States have filed FIRs in this regard. Besides, the 
States have been given additional list of suspicious institutes (based on red flags 
generated by National Informatics Centers- National Scholarship Portal) to get them 
verified/ inspected and take punitive actions against fraudulent elements. However, 
reports from the states are still awaited. 
In order to prevent duplicity of benefits, MoMA has also asked all the States 
to integrate their State Scholarship Portals with the National Scholarship Portal so 
that de-duplication can be done and one-student one-benefit could be 
ensured. However, the efforts by the States are yet to take a logical shape. In such 
circumstances, approval of Schemes could not be accorded by the competent 
authority. Therefore, it is evident that the Ministry is making efforts vigorously and 
has taken several steps to implement these schemes at the earliest.
