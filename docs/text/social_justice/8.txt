CHAPTER – I 
 
INTRODUCTORY 
 
 
 The mandate of the Ministry of Minority Affairs includes formulation of overall 
policy and planning, coordination, evaluation and review of the regulatory and 
development programmes for the benefit of the minority communities namely Jains, 
Parsis, Buddhists, Sikhs, Christians and Muslims.  
1.2 The vision of the Ministry is to create an enabling environment for strengthening 
the multi-facial, multi-cultural, multi-lingual and multi-religious character of the nation. 
The mission of the Ministry is to improve the socio-economic condition of the Minority 
communities through an affirmative action and inclusive development so that every 
citizen has equal opportunity and to participate accordingly in building a dynamic 
nation, to facilitate equitable share for Minority communities in education, employment, 
economic activities and to ensure their upliftment.  
1.3 T he following Schemes are being implemented by the Ministry for the welfare 
of Minority Communities: 
 
 
 
I. Pradhan Mantri Jan Vikas Karyakram (PMJVK), a Centrally Sponsored 
Scheme of the Ministry provides for development of infrastructure projects in 
Education, Health, Skill Development Sectors and Women-centric projects, for 
improving the socio-economic conditions of people in the identified areas. 
 
II. The Ministry has designed Pradhan Mantri Virasat Ka Samvardhan (PM 
VIKAS), an umbrella scheme by converging five of the existing schemes of the 
Ministry namely –Seekho Aur Kamao, Upgrading the Skills and Training in 
Traditional Arts / Crafts for Development (USTTAD), Hamari Dharohar, Nai 
Roshni and Nai Manzil.  
 
III. The Ministry of Minority Affairs implements three scholarship schemes namely 
Pre-Matric, Post-Matric and Merit-cum-Means based Scholarship Schemes for 
the educational empowerment of notified minority communities. The schemes 
intend to lower the financial burden of financially weaker sections of religious 
minority communities. As part of an overall exercise to harmonize all the 
scholarship schemes of various Ministries/ Departments, the schemes will be 
brought under the ambit of an umbrella scheme. 
  

2 
 
IV. Jiyo Parsi is a Central Sector Scheme concerning the decline of Parsi 
population in India. The scheme has three components, Medical Assistance, 
Advocacy and Health of Community. Since inception, the scheme has enabled 
birth of more than 400 Parsi children till 31.03.2024. 
 
V. The National Minorities Development & Finance Corporation (NMDFC) was 
incorporated on 30.09.1994 as a ‘Not for Profit’ company under Section 25 of the 
Companies Act, 1956 (now Section 8 of Companies Act, 2013). NMDFC provides 
concessional credit viz., Term Loan, Education Loan, Micro Finance & Virasat 
implemented through the State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) for self-
employment and income generating activities for the socio-economic 
development of the ‘backward sections’ amongst the notified Minorities.

2.4 On being asked about the Schemes and Programmes that have been 
discontinued in previous years and are likely to be discontinued in future alongwith the 
alternate measures taken in this regard , the Ministry of Minority Affairs submitted vide 
written reply that : 
 
“(i) With the approval of competent authority, 02 schemes namely (a) Free 
Coaching & Allied Scheme, and (b) Nai Udaan, were discontinued from beginning 
of FY 2022-23. Under the Free Coaching and Allied Schemes (Naya Savera) 
wherein coaching was provided to minority youth was discontinued as the New 
Education Policy 2020, does not support coaching programs. Under the Nai 
Udaan scheme financial support was provided to candidates who qualify in the 
preliminary examinations conducted by Union Public Service Commission 
(UPSC), Staff Selection Commission (SSC), State Public Service Commissions 
(SPSCs). As no other Ministry / Department was providing such support to their 
beneficiaries, thus, in order to attain uniformity, the Ministry, decided to 
discontinue the Scheme w.e.f. 2022-23. 
 
(ii) From year 2022-23 onwards, the Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme is being 
harmonized with the Schemes of other line ministries, Ministry of Social Justice & 
Empowerment, Ministry of Tribal Affairs, etc. Accordingly, the coverage under the 
Pre-Matric Scheme has been restricted to class IX-X, whereas Schemes like 
Padho Pardesh and Maulana Azad National Fellowship have been discontinued 
since 2022-23 due to the fact that there was an overlap of these two schemes 
with the schemes of other Ministries/ Departments. 
 
(iii) The Scheme of Providing Education in Madarsas/Minorities (SPEMM), 
comprising 2 sub-components namely (i) SPQEM, and (ii) IDMI, was under 
implementation of Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of 
Education from 1993-94 to 2020-21. Thereafter, the scheme was transferred to 
the Ministry of Minority Affairs in 2021-22. As the Scheme was approved upto FY 
2021-22, thus, the Ministry of Minority Affairs implemented the scheme upto 2021-
22 only.”

2.5 As regards to policy changes likely to be introduced in 2025-26 for the effective 
implementation of the Schemes and Programmes of the Ministry so that the welfare of 
the notified Minority communities is not compromised, the Committee were informed 
vide written reply that: 
(i) “As per the felt needs on the basis of experience gained during 
implementation and execution of PMJVK and also based on the

carried out in the scheme’ guidelines.  The latest amendments in the PMJVK’

across the country irrespective of the declared Minority Concentration Areas 
along with some more changes.  In future also the scheme would be subject to

(ii) In respect of Scholarship Schemes, as of now, Ministry is making efforts to 
get the schemes approved for implementation at the earliest. 
 (iii) The Ministry has designed and constructed the PM VIKAS scheme 
 considering the specific requirements of the notified minority communities. The 
 scheme aims at providing comprehensive skill development, traditional training, 
 leadership and entrepreneurship promotion and education support to the notified 
 minority communities.”

2.6. The Committee note that the actual expenditure of the Ministry of 
Minority Affairs has gone down considerably during previous years. The 
Ministry could spend only ₹837.68 crore and ₹1,032.65 crore out of 
₹2,612.66 (RE) crore and ₹2,608.93 (RE) crore during the year 2022-23 and 
2023-24 respectively. The situation did not improve in 2024-25 as the 
Ministry were able to spend only ₹919.10 crore out of budgetary allocation 
of ₹3,183.24 crore (BE) by 31 December, 2024. The Committee have been 
apprised that the Ministry have not been able to spend the budgetary 
allocation as several Schemes such as ‘Free Coaching and Allied Scheme’, 
‘Nai Udaan’, ‘Scheme of Providing Education in Madrasas/ Minorities 
(SPEMM)’, etc, have been discontinued and PM-VIKAS launched in 2023-24 
did not initiate. Further, the amount allocated during 2022-23 to 2024-25 
under various  Scholarship Schemes of the Ministry could not be spent 
because these Schemes are under consideration for approval since 2022-
23 as they are being harmonised with the Schemes of other line Ministries, 
viz. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment and Ministry of Tribal 
Affairs. The Committee strongly believe that the Ministry of Minority Affairs 
are making all efforts to achieve the mission of the Ministry by streamlining 
and strengthening the  prevailing System so that the deserving Minorities 
can avail the benefits of the Schemes and  the funds are not misused. The 
Committee feel that the steps taken by the Ministry were necessary in order 
to harmonize the system with the welfare Schemes of Ministry of Social 
Justice & Empowerment and Ministry of Tribal Affairs and hope that these 
steps will improve the implementation of the Schemes and enhance the  
socio-economic condition of six notified minority communities in the 
country. Since the Ministry of Minority Affairs are in process of 
streamlining/strengthening the prevailing System,  the Committee expect 
that  the necessary approvals will be obtained by the Ministry at the earliest 
so that the allocated Budget of ₹3,350.00 crore for 2025-26 is fruitfully 
utilised. The Committee, therefore, recommend the Ministry of Minority

Affairs to vigorously pursue with the concerned Ministries/ Departments for 
the pending approvals and keep the administrative machinery of the 
Ministry ready for immediate implementation of the Schemes as soon as 
they are approved.

3.4 On being enquired about the reasons due to which allocations on Pre-Matric 
Scheme, Post-matric scheme, Merit cum means scholarship and Maulana Azad 
National Fellowship for Minority students came down drastically between 2022-23  
to 2025-26, the representative of the Ministry during the deliberation informed the 
committee as under:  
 
“Under the scholarship components, we have primarily three scholarships – Pre-Matric, 
Post-Matric, and Merit-cum Means scholarship schemes.  These schemes have been 
since 2007-08. 
Regarding these scholarship schemes, approval of the schemes beyond 2021-22 is 
under  consideration. Salient features regarding the scheme, is eligibility criteria. We 
have 50 per cent qualifying marks. I would like to tell you that our scholarship schemes 
are being harmonised with OBC scholarships of Ministry of Social Justice and 
Empowerment regarding rate, annual parental income, funding pattern, and earmarking 
for girls. Sir, 30 per cent of scholarships are earmarked for girls. We have introduced a 
new payment system (Aadhar Payment Bridge System) which was done for 2022-23 
renewal scholarship. 
As far as budgetary allocation is concerned, everything was smooth till 2021-22, 
and, then, after that, we are awaiting pending approval from the Cabinet. We have spent 
some money on the committed liability part. The reason behind the same is this. There 
were media reports in 2020, and the matter was referred to CBI, and then, we had asked 
the States to investigate the cases. Five States have taken active action under these 
cases. If you see here, in Assam, 15 FIRs have been registered and 43 persons have 
been arrested. Cases are ongoing and we are monitoring it on regular basis.” 
 
3.5 As regards to the need to revise the Scheme and the reasons due to 
which extraordinary time being consumed in approval of the Scheme, the Ministry in 
written submission stated that: 
  
“Like Ministry of Minority Affairs, there are other Ministries/ Departments who are 
implementing similar Scholarship Schemes. While drafting the proposal for approval 
of the Scholarship Schemes for the period beyond 2022-23, it was felt to harmonize 
the parameters of the Schemes with similar schemes of other Ministries/ 
Departments so as to main stream the Scheme. This was attempted for ease of 
choosing the Scholarship Scheme by an applicant among all the Central 
Scholarship Schemes. 
  

12 
 
Meanwhile, irregularities were highlighted during the third party evaluation studies. 
To plug out such fraudulent elements from the system, Ministry asked the States to 
take corrective measures including registering FIRs and conduct thorough enquiry. 
Final report from any of the States are yet to be received. Such closure report along 
with conclusive corrective measures taken by the States will make it easier to get 
the scheme approved. In the meanwhile, Ministry also conducted Aadhaar based 
bio-metric authentication drive for all the applicants for the year 2022-23. This entire 
process has consumed some time and hence, delayed the approval of Scheme.” 
 
3.6 On being asked about the option available with Ministry for the Minority students 
who were dependent on the Pre & Post Matric Scholarship Scheme so that the 
education of such needy students is not disrupted, the Ministry of Minority Affairs inter-
alia submitted vide written reply that: 
  
“Although, the Pre-Matric and Post Matric Scholarship Schemes for Minorities have 
not been implemented beyond 2021-22, other Scholarship Schemes are open for 
most of the minority students who also belong to such social categories and 
communities including minorities.” 
  
  
3.7 On being enquired about the irregularities etc due to which Minorities are being 
deprived of Merit cum Means Scholarship, Educational loans for overseas studies and 
support for students clearing Prelims, the Ministry of Minority Affairs  inter-alia submitted 
vide written reply that : 
  
“While implementing the three Scholarship Schemes for Minorities since

CHAPTER-V 
PRADHAN MANTRI JAN VIKAS KARYAKRAM (PMJVK) 
 
  The Pradhan Mantri Jan Vikas Karyakram (PMJVK), erstwhile Multi Sectoral 
Development Programme (MsDP) initiated in the year 2008-09, is a Centrally Sponsored 
Scheme(CSS) being implemented from financial year 2017-18 with the objective to 
develop community infrastructure and basic amenities in the identified areas for 
improving the quality of life of the people in these areas. The Minority Concentrated 
Areas (MCAs) were identified on the basis of concentration of minority population and 
backwardness in terms of identified indicators. The areas covered under PMJVK were 
increased from 90 Districts originally, to 308 Districts in 33 States/ UTs w.e.f. May, 2018. 
The scheme has been revised with the approval of the Cabinet in the year 2022 for 
continuation over the 15th Finance Commission Cycle i.e. upto 2025-26. The Scheme is 
now implementable in all Districts across the Country including all the Aspirational 
Districts subject to fulfilling of crieteria that the place of infra-development under the 
scheme should have more than 25% Minority Population within the catchment area of 15 
kms.  
  
5.2 As per procedure the proposals under PMJVK are recommended by the State 
Level Committee (SLC) headed by the Chief Secretary of the respective States/Union 
Territories (UTs) based on demand for infrastructure development in the identified areas. 
The proposals recommended by the SLC are considered by the Screening 
Committee(SC) chaired by JS(PMJVK) and then considered and approved by 
Empowered Committee(EC) chaired by Secretary, Ministry of Minority Affairs after due 
consultation with the concerned Central Ministries, projects under PMJVK are 
implemented and managed by concerned State/UT Government/CGOs. 
  
5.3 Education, health, skill development and women-oriented projects are the priority 
sector under PMJVK. The type of works undertaken under PMJVK include construction of 
schools, additional classrooms, hostels, computer labs/digital classrooms, science 
laboratories in schools, drinking water facilities and toilets in schools, colleges, 
dispensaries, hospitals, Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs), Polytechnics, working women 
hostels, sports facilities, public/ community toilets etc. The projects are implemented on a 
fund sharing arrangement between the Centre and the State Government/UT 
Administration in the ratio of 90:10 for North East States, Hilly States (J&K, Himachal 
Pradesh, Uttarakhand)/ UTs without Legislation, 100% for UTs without Legislature and 
60:40 for all other States/ UTs/ UTs with Legislation.   Proposals received from Central 
Government Organisations are supported 100%.

STATEMENT OF OBSERVATIONS/RECOMMENDATIONS 
 
Sl.No Para No. Observations/ Recommendations 
1. 2.6 The Committee note that the actual expenditure of the 
Ministry of Minority Affairs has gone down considerably 
during previous years. The Ministry could spend only 
₹837.68 crore and ₹1,032.65 crore out of ₹2,612.66 (RE) 
crore and ₹2,608.93 (RE) crore during the year 2022-23 
and 2023-24. The situation did not improve in 2024-25 as 
the Ministry were able to spend only ₹919.10 crore out of 
budgetary allocation of ₹3,183.24 crore (BE) by 31 
December, 2024. The Committee have been apprised that 
the Ministry have not been able to spend the budgetary 
allocation as several Schemes such as ‘Free Coaching 
and Allied Scheme’, ‘Nai Udaan’, ‘Scheme of Providing 
Education in Madrasas/ Minorities (SPEMM)’, etc, have 
been discontinued and PM-VIKAS launched in 2023-24 
did not initiate. Further, the amount allocated during 
2022-23 to 2024-25 under various  Scholarship Schemes 
of the Ministry could not be spent because these 
Schemes are under consideration for approval since 
2022-23 as they are being harmonised with the Schemes 
of other line Ministries, viz. Ministry of Social Justice & 
Empowerment and Ministry of Tribal Affairs. The 
Committee strongly believe that the Ministry of Minority 
Affairs are making all efforts to achieve the mission of 
the Ministry by streamlining and strengthening the  

32 
 
prevailing System so that the deserving Minorities can 
avail the benefits of the Schemes and  the funds are not 
misused. The Committee feel that the steps taken by the 
Ministry were necessary in order to harmonize the 
system with the welfare Schemes of Ministry of Social 
Justice & Empowerment and Ministry of Tribal Affairs 
and hope that these steps will improve the 
implementation of the Schemes and enhance the  socio-
economic condition of six notified minority communities 
in the country. Since the Ministry of Minority Affairs are 
in process of streamlining/strengthening the prevailing 
System,  the Committee expect that  the necessary 
approvals will be obtained by the Ministry at the earliest 
so that the allocated Budget of ₹3,350.00 crore for 2025-
26 is fruitfully utilised. The Committee, therefore, 
recommend the Ministry of Minority Affairs to vigorously 
pursue with the concerned Ministries/ Departments for 
the pending approvals and keep the administrative 
machinery of the Ministry ready for immediate 
implementation of the Schemes as soon as they are 
approved. 
2. 3.10 The Committee find that the actual expenditure under 
various educational Schemes was very insignificant in 
comparison to the budgetary allocation as several 
Schemes such as ‘Free Coaching and Allied Scheme’, 
‘Nai Udaan’, ‘Scheme of Providing Education in 
Madrasas/ Minorities (SPEMM)’, ‘Maulana Azad National 
Fellowship’, etc. have been discontinued and Pre-Matric 
and Post-Matric Scholarship Schemes are pending for 

33 
 
approval since 2022-23. The Committee feel that since 
the Schemes discontinued were in operation for several 
years, their sudden discontinuation would deprive the 
targeted beneficiaries from the benefits of these 
Schemes. The Committee have no doubt about the 
intention of the Ministry but strongly desire that the 
substitutes of these Schemes, even if, at all, available 
elsewhere, the beneficiaries should be made aware of 
such Schemes by publicizing them. Further, the Ministry 
should take necessary measures for the Scholarship 
Schemes pending for approval as the financial aid 
provided under these Schemes is very important and any 
more delay will adversely affect the educational 
prospects of the students belonging to Minority 
Communities. The Committee may like to be apprised of 
the initiatives taken by the Ministry of Minority Affairs in 
getting the approvals of these Schemes. 
3. 4.8 The Committee note that the Ministry of Minority Affairs 
have been implementing  Pradhan Mantri Virasaat Ka 
Samvardhan since 2023-24 by converging five erstwhile 
Schemes namely ‘Seekho Aur Kamao’, ‘USTTAD’, 
‘Hamari Dharohar’, ‘Nai Roshni’ and ‘Nai Manzil’. The 
Committee find that the Ministry spent ₹83.51 crore out of 
Revised Estimate of ₹169.00 crore in 2022-23. After the 
convergence of independent Schemes in PM-VIKAS, the 
Ministry could spend only ₹209.42 crore out of RE of 
₹325.86 crore during 2023-24 and ͅ₹124.08 crore out of 
₹230.00 crore upto 31 December, 2024 during 2024-25 to 
meet the committed liability of 2023-24 and 2024-25,

respectively, relating to the erstwhile Schemes.  The PM-
VIKAS Scheme, however, could not be implemented 
during these years as the guidelines of the Schemes 
could not be approved. The Committee feel that the 
Ministry should have timely initiated the proposal for 
approval of guidelines to avoid the extraordinary delay. 
The Committee now expect that the guidelines have 
since been approved, the targeted beneficiaries will not 
have to wait anymore to avail the benefit of the Scheme. 
The Committee hope that the approval of the proposal to 
integrate the portal of the Ministry with National Institute

approved in a time bound manner. The Committee may 
like to be intimated of the action taken in this regard.
