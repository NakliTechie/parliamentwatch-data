authorised by the Committee to present the Report on their behalf, present this Tenth

Survey and Mapping  
Recommendation Serial No. 3 
5.  The Committee observe that for the year 2024-25, the Budget sought for Survey and 
Mapping (Mission-I) activities of GSI is ₹192.00 crore whereas Budget Allocated was ₹74.75 
crore hence there is a shortfall of ₹117.25 crore under the head. For the year 2023-24 the BE 
was ₹135 crore and RE was ₹123 crore. Hence, there is a decrease of 39.42 % under the 
head Survey and Mapping (Mission-I) for 2024-25 as compared to 2023-24. Now, the Ministry 
has sought an additional amount of ₹101.25 crore at RE stage for 2024-25. The Committee 
feel that since, Survey and Mapping is crucial for creation and updating of national 
geoscientific database through ground, marine and airborne surveys, shortfall in funds may 
hinder the implementation of this important programme. The Committee, therefore, while 
desiring to know the reasons for decrease in allocation also emphasize that the Ministry 
should ensure optimal utilization of additional funds sought in 2024-25 and also make 
available adequate funds for this programme at the BE stage in future. 
6. In their action taken reply, the Ministry of Mines have stated as under:- 
In the current F.Y. 2024-25, GSI received ₹171.30 crore in RE with an increase 
of ₹47.90 crore than the RE allocation for F.Y. 2023-24 (₹123.40 crore). The enhanced 
fund under the head Survey and Mapping (Mission-I) will be utilized for smooth 
implementation and execution of all the activities under Mission-I. It is expected that 
the budget grant received at RE stage for Survey and Mapping will be optimally 
utilized by 31.03.2025. 
  
7.     The Committee in their original Report had observed that there was shortfall of 
₹117.25 crore and also decrease of 39.42% in terms of budget allocation under this 
head during 2024-25 and 2023-24 respectively. The Committee, are however, satisfied 
to note from the action taken replies furnished by the Ministry that GSI has received 
₹171.30 crore in RE 2024-25 which is now an increase of ₹47.90 crore taken RE for 
financial year 2023-24. In view of the forgoing, the Committee would like to impress 
upon the Ministry to ensure that GSI make concerted efforts to optimally utilize the 
funds under the head Survey and Mapping (Mission-I). The Committee may be 
apprised of the progress made in the matter.

R&D 
Recommendation Serial No.4 
 
8. As per the information furnished to the Committee, under R&D Head (M-IV) of GSI 
there is a decrease of 37.06% against last year’s allocation. During 2023-24 the BE was 
₹17.40 crore and actual expenditure was ₹17.30 crore. Now BE for the year 2024-25 is 
₹10.95 crore. Since, R&D plays a very crucial role in the development of any Organisation 
the Committee feel that a review on allocation for the purpose may be carried out.   
9. In their action taken reply, the Ministry of Mines have stated as under:- 
GSI undertakes various R&D activities with an aim to supplement mineral 
exploration and also other public good geoscience research projects like landslide 
studies, environment geology projects paleontological studies etc. Allocation for R&D 
has been increased to ₹11.05 crore in RE-2024-25. Moreover, the allocation for R&D 
activities in FY-2025-26 is increased to ₹13.00 crore for carrying out the R&D activities 
in GSI. 
  
10.    The Committee, observe that though the R&D budget has been increased at RE 
for 2024-25 to ₹11.05 crore as compared to BE of ₹10.95 crore but the increase is 
miniscule i.e. ₹0.1 crore only especially in view of the fact that BE 2023-24 was ₹17.40 
crore and the same was almost fully utlised as expenditure incurred was ₹17.30 crore. 
The Committee would therefore, while reiterating their earlier recommendation that, 
R&D plays a very crucial role in the development of any Organization feel that a review 
on utilization of funds may be carried out and the budget may be enhanced for R&D 
activities so that GSI can perform their best to undertake research projects like 
landslide studies, environment geology projects, paleontological studies etc.  
Mine Surveillance System (MSS)  
Recommendation Serial No.8 
11.  The Committee note that in technological and modernization scheme, IBM implements 
Mine Surveillance System (MSS) under which it generates preliminary triggers for major 
minerals and send them to State Governments for field verification. The Committee note that 
during 2022-23, MSS generated 61 preliminary triggers for major minerals and uploaded on 
the portal for further transmission to the State Governments. Field verification was received in 
respect of 24 triggers and out of these, unauthorized mining has been confirmed in seven 
major minerals by the State Govts.  Similarly, during 2023-24 also MSS generated 138 
triggers, sent 40 triggers to State Governments for field verification and in seven cases 
unauthorized mining have been detected.  The Committee while appreciating the contribution 

 
4  
of IBM in detecting illegal mining and thereby plugging the leakages to the Governments 
revenues, they suggest IBM may request State Govts to carryout field verification of all the 
triggers generated to detect and confirm the illegal mining. This, the committee believe, will 
go a long way in improving the revenues of the Government in a significant manner. Further, 
it also helps in deterring illegal mining in future.   
12. In their action taken reply, the Ministry of Mines have stated as under:- 
 
“IBM has been following the matter with the concerned state 
governments to speed up the field verification of the triggers communicated to 
them. IBM has issued a letter to all the concerned State Governments on 
13.10.2022 requesting them to carry out field verification of the triggers at the 
earliest. Subsequently, IBM also requested Ministry of Mines on 30.09.2023 
to issue a letter to all the concerned State Governments advising them to 
complete the pending field verifications. Instructions have been issued to all Regions 
in this regard from HQ after a V.C. was held on 29.11.2024, in this regard to contact 
State Governments requesting to undertake the verification of all triggers at the 
earliest. Accordingly, the matter is being pursued with them.” 
 
13.   The Committee while appreciating the contribution of IBM in detecting illegal 
mining and thereby plugging the leakages to the Government revenues, had 
suggested that IBM may request State Govts to carryout field verification of all the 
triggers generated to detect and confirm the illegal mining. In their action taken reply 
the Ministry has submitted that IBM has been following the matter with concerned 
State Government to speed up the field verification of triggers. IBM has issued a letter 
to all State Government on 13.10.2022 and had also requested Ministry of Mines on 
30.09.2023 to issue letter to all State Government advising them to complete the 
pending field verifications and instructions have been issued to all the regions in this 
regard, from HQ after a VC was held on 29.11.2024. The Committee note that despite 
clear recommendations given by the Committee in their report presented on 4.12.2024, 
the Ministry did not take any action on the same and has furnished details of action 
taken by them prior to presentation of the Report.  The Committee while taking note of 
the same would reiterate their earlier recommendation and recommend the Ministry to 
issue letters to all concerned State Governments also requested by IBM vide their 
letter dated 30.09.2023 to complete field verifications of the triggers communicated as 
the same will curb illegal mining and also improve the revenues of the concerned 
Government. The Committee would like to be apprised of the action taken in this 
regard.

Utilisation of allocated resources  
 
Recommendation Serial No:1   
1. The Committee note that the budgetary allocations made to the Ministry of Mines 
including its attached/subordinate offices, such as Geological Survey of India (GSI), 
Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM), National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET), etc.  have 
been utilized for various projects, schemes, programmes etc. during the last three 
financial years beginning from 2021-22. The actual utilization is in the range of 90-
99.50% of the allocations made at Revised Estimates (RE) stage during the said period.

manner for achieving the intended objectives. They hope that the trend will continue in 
future also and suggest that suitable measures be taken to ensure their utilization in 
future also.

Ministry is grateful for the observations of Hon’ble committee and it will ensure continued 
optimal utilization of allocated resources in future also. All Organisations and Divisions of this 
ministry are regularly reminded to comply with Monthly and Quarterly Expenditure plans to 
ensure proper utilization of available provisions. For the current F.Y. 2024-25 also, 
expenditure of ₹2922.00 cr (which is 93.26% of Revised Estimate (RE) of ₹3133.00 cr) has 
already been incurred.

Reply of the Government:  
 The Baseline geoscience data generated from Specialized Thematic Mapping (STM), 
National Geochemical Mapping (NGCM), National Geophysical Mapping (NGPM), 
National Aero-Geophysical Mapping Programme (NAGMP) and Marine and Coastal 
surveys are of paramount importance as a primary step towards comprehensive 
understanding of the geological set-up that is inter-alia essential for identifying areas 
with potential mineralized zones. That apart, these studies help in understanding 
various earth-science processes that has application in natural hazard mitigation and 
other geoscientific issues.  
 GSI has geologically mapped 99.27% of the mappable part of the country (31.23 lakh 
sq km) on 1: 50,000 scale under systematic geological mapping. 
 GSI increased its momentum manifold to acquire the baseline geoscience data using 
the state-of-the-art instruments and modern technologies. 
Key achievements of GSI:

km. area has been covered under STM under STM on 1:25K scale.

mapping of 21.39 lakh sq. km in the targeted priority areas to derive anomalous 
zone/s of elemental concentration which may be prospective for future mineral

social concerns.  
 National Geophysical Mapping (NGPM): This geophysical mapping program (1:50K 
scale) has covered an area of 15.71 lakh sq. km till December 2024. NGPM surveys

subject matter of the Point No.9 is under the purview of concerned State Governments.

incorporate the mandates of Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY),  a 
scheme of  the Ministry of Mines for the welfare of people & affected areas by using the funds 
accrued under DMF. At least 70% the DMF fund have to be utilized for "High Priority Areas" 
like Drinking water supply, Environment preservation & pollution control measure, Health 
care, Education, etc. and rest of the fund are utilized for "Other Priority Areas", such as 
Physical infrastructure, Irrigation, Energy & watershed development and Measures for 
enhancing environmental quality.

guidelines after incorporating the suggestions from stakeholders, for improving the impact, 
enhancing the effectiveness and widening the scope of the PMKKKY scheme.

11.  The Committee note that in technological and modernization scheme, IBM implements 
Mine Surveillance System (MSS) under which it generates preliminary triggers for major 
minerals and send them to State Governments for field verification. The Committee note that 
during 2022-23, MSS generated 61 preliminary triggers for major minerals and uploaded on 
the portal for further transmission to the State Governments. Field verification was received in 
respect of 24 triggers and out of these, unauthorized mining has been confirmed in seven 
major minerals by the State Govts.  Similarly, during 2023-24 also MSS generated 138 
triggers, sent 40 triggers to State Governments for field verification and in seven cases 
unauthorized mining have been detected.  The Committee while appreciating the contribution 
of IBM in detecting illegal mining and thereby plugging the leakages to the Governments 
revenues, they suggest IBM may request State Govts to carryout field verification of all the 
triggers generated to detect and confirm the illegal mining. This, the committee believe, will 
go a long way in improving the revenues of the Government in a significant manner. Further, 
it also helps in deterring illegal mining in future.
