Report 
 
The Action Taken Report of the Committee deals with the action taken by the Ministry of 
Development of North Eastern Region on the recommendations contained in the Two Hundred Twenty 
Fifth Report of the Committee on Demands for Grants (2020-21) of the Ministry.  The Two Hundred 
Twenty Fifth Report was presented to Rajya Sabha /laid on the Table of Lok Sabha, on 5th March, 2020. 
 
2. Action Taken Notes as received from the Ministry of DoNER in respect of the recommendations 
contained in the Two Hundred Twenty Fifth Report have been categorized as follows: 
 
Chapter I: The recommendations/observations, which have been accepted by the Government: 
Para Nos. 2.2.10, 2.6.4, 3.2.12, 3.5.7, 4.4.9, 6.1.8, 6.6.7, 6.10.7, 6.11.4, 6.15.6 (of  Two Hundred 
Twenty Fifth Report)    
Total recommendations- 10  
 
The Committee is pleased to note that the Ministry has accepted its recommendations as 
mentioned in Chapter-I of the Report. 
 
Chapter II: The recommendations/observations which the Committee does not desire to 
pursue in view of the Government’s replies: Para Nos. 3.6.7, 4.1.11, 4.3.8, 6.15.4 (ibid)  
Total recommendations - 04 
 
The Committee is convinced with the explanations furnished by the Ministry and, 
therefore, does not want to pursue the recommendations further. 
 
Chapter III: The recommendations/observations in respect of which the Committee is not 
satisfied with the replies of the Ministry: Para Nos. 2.2.5, 2.7.4, 3.2.14, 5.2.8, 6.3.6, 6.9.4, 6.14.5 
(ibid)  
Total recommendations - 07  
 
The Committee expresses its concern over the non-implementation of its 
recommendations contained in Chapter-III and desires that the Ministry should furnish 
convincing action taken steps in respect of those recommendations.  
 
Chapter IV: The recommendations/observations in respect of which final replies of the 
Government have not been received: Para Nos. 3.2.17, 3.3.4, 3.14.4, 4.2.5, 5.1.9, 6.5.9, 6.8.2, 
6.12.11, 6.14.4, 6.16.4 (ibid)  
Total Recommendations - 10  
 
The Committee desires that the Ministry should furnish pointed and detailed Action 
Taken Notes in respect of the observations/recommendations made in all chapters of the 
Report.  
 
3. The details of the ATNs have been analyzed in the succeeding chapters.

Chapter- I 
 
Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the Government 
 
 
1.1 Utilisation of Funds during the Last Three Years 
  
Recommendation 
 
1.1.1 The Committee appreciates the role of the Ministry in recommending reallocation of resources 
within the overall pool to the other Ministries and recommends that the Ministry must strengthen the 
aforesaid mechanism for better coordination among the other Ministries/Departments and better 
utilization of funds allocated. The Committee further recommends that the Ministry should strengthen 
the project management system and all pending projects should be completed by the Ministry as a 
Special Mission mode drive. 
 (Para 2.2.10 of 225th Report) 
Action Taken  
 
1.1.2 As already informed, Ministry of DoNER regularly monitors and reviews the expenditure of Non 
exempt Ministries/Departments under 10% Gross Budgetary Support (GBS) on a quarterly basis. All 
Non exempt Ministries are advised to regularly monitor the expenditure against 10% GBS for North 
East and if necessary modify existing schemes and formulate NE specific Schemes. The Ministry is also 
empowered to recommend reallocation of likely surrenders of their Statement 11 allocations to those 
Ministries/Departments who have the absorptive capacity to implement additional schemes/programmes 
within the financial year. During the financial year 2019-2020, three review meetings were held on 06 th 
August, 2019, 11 th October, 2019 and 16 th January, 2020 respectively to assess the utilization of the 
10% GBS earmarked for NER and necessary proposal for additional allocations for inclusion in the 
supplementary demands were sent to the Ministry of Finance. This is a step towards minimizing the 
accruals to NLCPR of the unspent balances of 10% GBS at the end of the year. Besides, efforts are also 
made to collect the actual expenditures figures of the non exempted Ministries on a real time basis in 
the PFMS for better monitoring. The proposal of Ministry of DoNER for specific coding of expenditure 
in sanction orders to be issued by non exempt Ministries, as proposed by CGA from the Financial Year 
2020-21, that would ensure real time expenditure figures for NER, thereby greatly helping in 
monitoring and expenditure of earmarked funds was taken up with Secretary, Department of 
Expenditure. The recommendation of Committee on strengthening the project management system and 
completion of all pending projects as a Special Mission mode drive is noted. 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.1.3 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.  
 
 
1.2 Revenue Section 
 
Recommendation 
 
1.2.1 The Committee notes that there is a shortfall of Rs. 2165.49 crore in the allocation in BE 2020-
21 vis-à-vis the amounts projected i.e. Rs. 4495.63 crore by the Ministry for its various schemes. The 
shortfall is too steep, particularly under the schemes of the NEC and North East Special Infrastructure 
Development Scheme and will adversely affect the infrastructure development in the North Eastern 

3 
 
Region. The Committee, therefore, strongly recommends that the increased allocation at RE stage in 
2020-21 may be considered by the Ministry of Finance if allocated funds at BE stage are utilized fully. 
 
 (Para 2.6.4 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.2.2 The recommendation of the Committee is noted for compliance. The requirement of additional 
funds will be taken up with the Ministry of Finance at the RE stage in FY 2020-21 based on various 
factors like progress in expenditure, reduction of outstanding UCs, Unspent balances, projections and 
demands of the MDoNER etc., and other related considerations. 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.2.3 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.  
 
1.3 Schemes of NEC 
 
Recommendation 
 
1.3.1 The Committee appreciates the role of the North Eastern Council (NEC) in formulation of 
regional plans for integrated and balanced socio-economic development of the NER. Therefore, it is 
pertinent that NEC must synchronise the efforts made under central projects/schemes and efforts of the 
State Government of NE regions to achieve the objectives of inclusive growth and equitable distribution 
of benefits of socio-economic development to the lower rungs of the society. The Committee also takes 
note of the efforts made by the NEC to liquidate the huge committed liabilities against ongoing and 
completed projects which depend on receipt of Utilization Certificates from state governments and 
implementing agencies respectively. The Committee recommends that the Ministry must put all its 
efforts for clearing of liabilities of on-going projects and undertake strict measures to resolve the i ssues 
of non-receipt of Utilization Certificates from the state governments for the future projects. 
 
 (Para 3.2.12 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.3.2 During the last two years, the NEC has been concentrating primarily on liquidating the 
committed liabilities under "Schemes of NEC" and no major new liability has been created under the 
scheme. Regular reviews with the State Governments/ implementing agencies are being done to stress 
on the need to furnish the utilization certificates on time and complete the projects.  Such efforts will 
continue in the future as well. 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.3.3 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.  
 
 
1.4 Non-Lapsable Central Pool of Resources (NLCPR-state)  
 
Recommendation 
 
1.4.1 The Committee notes that as Non-Lapsable Central Pool of Resources (NLCPR) Scheme has 
been restructured into a new scheme North East Special Infrastructure Development Scheme (NESIDS) 
w.e.f. 15 th December, 2017 no new projects are being sanctioned under NLCPR-State scheme and Rs. 

4 
 
407.16 crore, allocated in 2020-21 under NLCPR-State scheme, is for meeting the expenditure towards 
the committed liabilities of the ongoing projects. The Committee also notes that the projected demand 
under NLCPR-State was Rs.1000.00 crore. In absence of any clarification furnished by the Ministry, the  
Committee fails to understand whether the projected demand of Rs.1000.00 crore was for meeting the 
committed liabilities or for the implementation of the projects. Therefore, the Committee desires that the 
Ministry may clarify the same and feels that if the projected demand was for meeting the expenditure 
towards the committed liabilities of the ongoing projects, then the Government should make a one-time 
provision towards meeting the committed liability so that the implementation of the projects could be 
carried out without the burden of a large committed liability and the Government must strengthen the 
monitoring mechanism and continuously review the projects that are pending. 
 (Para 3.5.7 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.4.2 The projected demand of Rs.1000.00 crore was for releasing the funds against ongoing projects 
only under NLCPR-State scheme. Continuous efforts are being made by the Ministry for timely 
implementation of projects under NLCPR scheme and meeting the committed liabilities in respect of the 
ongoing projects. The ongoing projects under NLCPR schemes are regularly reviewed in the meetings of 
Inter Ministerial Committee (IMC)/North East Special Infrastructure Development Scheme (NESIDS) 
Committee; through video conferencing with the senior officers of the State Government and during 
visits of senior officers of Ministry of DoNER to NE States. During the F.Y. 2019-20, Rs.379.19 crore 
was released in respect of the ongoing projects under NLCPR scheme. With the rigorous exercise of 
review of ongoing projects and continuous follow up with the State Governments, 62 ongoing projects 
costing Rs.841.34 crore were completed in the F.Y. 2019-20. 
  
Further Recommendation 
 
1.4.3 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.  
 
 
1.5 Social and Infrastructure Development Fund (SIDF) 
 
Recommendation 
 
1.5.1 The Committee notes the reasons for delay in utilisation/slow progress of projects but takes 
strong exception to the low utilisation i.e 44.66% in 2018-19 and 26.05 % upto 31 st January, 2020 in 
2019-20 under the Social and Infrastructure Development Fund (SIDF). Therefore, the Committee 
recommends that effort need to be made by the Ministry of DoNER to accelerate the utilisation of funds 
under the SIDF. The Committee  also recommends that M/o DONER must take a lead in coordinating 
with   the State Governments  for formulating infrastructure projects so that all projects are submitted in 
time for final approval and entire allocation is utilized.  
(Para 4.4.9 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.5.2 In compliance of the recommendations of the Hon'ble Committee, this Ministry has been 
continuously raising issue of slow utilization of funds with the respective North Eastern State 
Governments during IMC meetings and during visits of Chief Nodal Officers/ Nodal Officers. This has 
resulted in better expenditure position in this Financial Year under SIDF i.e. release of Rs.27.89 crore. 
Out of revised estimates of Rs.40.00 crore under SIDF. The Ministry is making all-out efforts for timely 
submission of documents by the State Government to expedite the release of pending instalments. The 
total expenditure of Ministry, for the Financial Year 2019-20, was more than 99.5% of the revised 
estimates. This has been achieved by putting in place a robust system in which the officials of the 

5 
 
Ministry regularly interact with the State Governments to ensure that the proposals which are sent to 
the Ministry for approval/sanction are properly formulated in respect of the guidelines of the schemes, 
and submitted in time, to ensure that the time taken for approvals is minimised, and the funds allocated 
are utilised timely. 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.5.3 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.  
 
 
1.6 Vision 2020 
 
Recommendation 
 
1.6.1 The Committee recommends that specific monitoring mechanism must be put in place to ensure 
timely implementation of projects undertaken under Vision 2020 Document. The Ministry of DoNER 
should expedite the process of implementation of the projects so that the development process in th e 
region is accelerated to achieve the objectives of mission of Vision 2020. The Committee is of the 
considered view that a perceptible change with regard to development of the North-Eastern region needs 
to be felt. Therefore, strict adherence to the timeline within the stipulated cost needs to be ensured while 
executing implementing projects/schemes. 
(Para 6.1.8 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.6.2 The Ministry of DoNER in pursuance of achieving the objectives of mission of Vision 2020 is 
implementing projects in infrastructure sectors of road, power, water supply, health and education, etc., 
bridging the gaps in NER. The Ministry is in agreement with the Committee that timely implementation 
of projects under Vision 2020 Document would fulfill its objectives. In that direction, close coordination 
and communication between MDoNER and NE State Governments for project implementation is being 
strengthened. Supervision and due diligence which plays an important factor in the progress of projects 
is also being expedited. The steps taken to strengthen the monitoring mechanism are (i) Online portal 
for specific schemes/projects, (ii) Payment through PFMS, (iii) Geo-tagging of project sites, (iv) 
Monitoring of physical progress through geo tagged photographs, (v) ‗Just in time‘ release of funds 
(linked to physical and financial progress), and (vi) Super-check of progress of projects by Technical 
Wing of the Ministry, on sample basis. North East Space Application Centre, Shillong is actively 
engaged with MDoNER and NEC for project monitoring through satellite imagery, geo-tagging, etc.  
 
1.6.3 NEC in particular, in line with the NER Vision 2020 Document has considered and sanctioned 
project proposals of the State Governments of NER. NEC has a robust system in which all the projects 
are regularly inspected through field inspections by officers of NEC and Regional Level Monitors 
(RLM). In addition, quarterly review meetings are conducted with the State Governments of NER and 
implementing agencies to ensure timely completion of projects as well as to avoid time and cost 
overrun.  These efforts no doubt, would ensure timely completion of projects within the stipulated cost.  
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.6.4 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.

1.7 Civil Aviation  
 
Recommendation 
  
1.7.1 The Committee hopes that with the completion of ongoing/new projects would smoothen the air 
connectivity and operational efficacy of Civil Aviation in NER. The Committee recommends that these 
ongoing projects be completed in the stipulated time frame and without cost overruns. The Committee 
desires that the Ministry of DoNER should coordinate with the Ministry of Civil Aviation and the State 
Governments concerned and remove the bottlenecks, if any, in implementation of the projects 
undertaken. 
 (Para 6.6.7 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.7.2 Ministry of DoNER coordinates with the Ministry of Civil Aviation regarding the status of 
implementation of the ongoing/new projects undertaken by Ministry of Civil Aviation and its 
bottlenecks, if any, on a regular basis that would smoothen the air connectivity and operational efficacy 
of Civil Aviation in NER. While the projects are reviewed by the concerned sectoral Ministry i.e. 
Ministry of Civil Aviation internally, an Inter-Ministerial Committee on Civil Aviation (IMC) was 
constituted in order to fast track the development of infrastructure projects on airports in NER. IMC-
Civil Aviation also envisages for greater synergy in ongoing efforts in development of aviation sector in 
NER and Tourism and review issues involved in implementation of schemes/programmes and their 
convergence. Since the constitution of IMC-Civil Aviation, 5 IMC meetings have been held and the 5 th 
IMC was held on 16 th October, 2019. Besides, the ongoing/new infrastructure projects are also 
monitored at the level of Secretary, Ministry of DoNER through the quarterly review of projects above 
Rs 100 crore. 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.7.3 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry. 
 
1.8 Tourism 
 
Recommendation 
 
1.8.1 The Committee takes note of the projects related to tourism promotion that are being 
implemented under the NESIDS Scheme and hopes that with concerted efforts to implement these 
projects, niche and high end tourism will be developed in the NER. The Committee is however, not 
happy to note that the educational tourism has not been explored in an organized manner by NE 
States. The Committee therefore recommends that Ministry must take steps to explore educational 
tourism with an aim to make education more accessible and providing qualified Human Resource for 
Tourism and Allied services for North Eastern Region States. This will help in making North East 
India a preferred destination for visitors and tourists and generate a pool of qualified youth for 
handling tourism activities there. 
(Para 6.10.7 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.8.2 The recommendations of the Committee are noted. Further, it is submitted that the objectives 
of NESIDS include filling up gaps in specified sectors of physical infrastructure relating to water 
supply, power and connectivity to enhance tourism. The physical infrastructure which will be created 
with the projects sanctioned under this scheme will play a crucial role in increasing tourist inflow to 
the North Eastern Region. The scope of NESIDS also includes infrastructure projects in the field of 

7 
 
primary and secondary education, and it has always been an endeavour of the Ministry to give due 
importance to projects received in this sector, from the North Eastern States. Due focus would also be 
given to infrastructure projects in the field of primary and secondary education, which promote 
educational tourism, upon receipt of such project proposals from the State Governments. 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.8.3 The Committee note the reply of the Ministry and desires to be apprised about the 
progress made in this direction.  
 
 
1.9 North East Industrial Development Scheme (NEIDS) 
 
Recommendation 
 
1.9.1 The Committee is displeased to note that no fund has been sanctioned under NEIDS and 
respective State Level Committees of NEIDS have not recommended release of funds to any 
company. The Committee therefore recommends that a strategy should be chalked out to implement the 
programme/action plan to boost integrated and balanced industrialization in NER and to promote 
employment in the North East States. 
(Para 6.11.4 ibid) 
Action Taken 
 
1.9.2 NEIDS 2017 Scheme guidelines require all eligible industrial units to register with Department 
for Promotion of Industry & Internal Trade (DPIIT) through the portal and applicants have to submit 
applications along with DPRs.  As on date, 104 industrial units have been registered by the empowered 
committee based on the recommendations of the State Government. Units should start commercial 
production within 18 months of registration and thereafter file their claims within one year from their 
date of commercial production under Central Capital Investment Incentive for Access to Credit 
(CCIIAC).  After constant follow up and requisite hand holding by District industries Centres, only 67 
claims that were able to start their commercial production could be registered on the web portal for 
processing. 
  
1.9.3 As no claims were received from State Governments, no allocation was initially made in NEIDS 
Scheme for BE 2019-20.  At RE stage, after constant follow-up, State Level Committee (SLC) of Assam 
confirmed about some claims being ready for consideration and a token amount of Rs. 1.00 crore was 
allocated.  Govt. of Assam conducted the first meeting of SLC and considered the claims of six industrial 
units on 17 th March, 2020.  Based on the recommendations of the SLC, Rs.1.00 crore was released as 
advance to the 6 Industrial units in March, 2019 through NEDFi before the mandatory Pre-Audit. In the 
current financial year (2020-21), an allocation of Rs.100 crore has been made and efforts are being 
made to speed up the process of disbursal of incentives, by revisiting and simplifying stages involved in 
processing of the claims. 
  
1.9.4 In order to speed up the processes, all stakeholders are being engaged through symposiums and 
workshops to build up their capacity. A mega Symposium was held on 30 th November, 2019 on 
―Accelerating Investment & Industrial Development in North -Eastern Region‖ with Invest India and 
Ease of Doing Business (EoDB) Divisions of DPIIT. Participants included Heads of Industries 
Departments of all the 8 NER States, APEDA and NER representatives from CII, FINER, ASSOCHAM, 
FICCI, etc., besides manufacturing and services sector units located in North East Region and other 
stakeholders. Regular meetings through Video Conferencing are held with the State Governments to 
ensure that claims are processed and placed before SLCs for approval.    

8 
 
Further Recommendation 
 
1.9.5 The Committee notes the reply of the Ministry.

region. 
  
3.5.5 As on date, 248 JSSs are functional throughout the country. With the objective to expand the JSS

3.5.6 One of the schemes being implemented by MSDE is Apprenticeship Training under ―The

skilled manpower for industries: 
  
(i) To   regulate   and promote the   program  of  training  of apprentices in the industry; and 
(ii) To  utilize   the  facilities  available in industry for imparting practical training with  a view  
to  meeting  the  requirements  of  skilled manpower for industry. 
  
3.5.7 As per the Apprentices Act, 1961 it is obligatory on the part of employers/establishments having 
a manpower strength of 30 or more to engage apprentices in a band of 2.5% to 15% of total manpower 
strength of the establishments including contractual staff. 
  
3.5.8 Under this scheme so far, 245 establishments of NE region have registered on 
―apprenticeshipindia.org.in‖ portal and are engaging apprentices. Currently 2437  apprentices are

19-20.
