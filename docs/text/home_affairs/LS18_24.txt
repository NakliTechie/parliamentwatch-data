REPORT 
I, the Chairman of the Committee on Home Affairs, having been authorised by the 
Committee, present this Twenty-fourth Report of the Committee relating to the Election 
Commission (Charging of Expenses on the Consolidated Fund of India) BiW, 1994. The Bill 
seeks to provide for charging of expenses of Election Commission on the Consolidated Fund 
of India. 
2. In pursuance of the Rules relating to the department-related Parliamentary Standing 
Committees, the Chairman, Rajya Sabha in consultation with the Speaker, Lok Sabha, 
referred•• the Bill, as introduced in the Lok Sabha on the 16th December, 1994 and pending 
therein, to the Committee, for examination and report. 
3.The Committee took up consideration of the Bill in its meeting held on the 13th 
March, 1995. It considered the Report in its meetings held on the 19th July and lOth 
November and adopted the same on the lOth November, 1995. In this connection, the 
Committee heard the representative of the Ministry of Law, Justice and Company Affairs 
and considered the relevant information furnished by the Ministry including the extracts 
from the Constituent Assembly Debates regarding the views expressed therein on the 
question of making expenditure of the Election Commission 'charged' expenditure. 
4. From a perusal of the said debates, it appears that the Constituent Assembly, after 
due deliberations, in its wisdom decided that the expenditure of certain institutions/offices as 
mentioned in Article 112 of the Constitution, shall be charged on the Consolidated Fund of 
India and thought it proper not ·to include the Election Commission therein. 
5. The representative of the Legislative Department of the Min.istry of Law, Justice and 
Company Affairs also informed the Committee that the question of making the expenditure 
of Election Commission 'charged' expenditure was also considered in 1990 by a Committee 
headed by the then Law Minister Shri Dinesh Goswami. It did not think it necessary to 
make the expenditure of the Election Commission as 'charged' expenditure. The Goswami 
Committee was of the view that the expenditure of Election Commission should be 
continued as 'voted'. Hence, no recommendation was made by the said Committee to bring 
about any legislation in this regard. 
6. The Committee, after taking into consideration the background note on the Bill, the 
deposition of the representative of the Minsitry of Law, Justice and Company Affairs and 
other information given by the Ministry, does not agree with the principle of the Bill that
