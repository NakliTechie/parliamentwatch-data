Report  
 The Ministry of Culture deals with the preservatio n and conservation of ancient cultural 
heritage and promotion of art and culture, both tan gible and intangible, in the country. The 
Ministry functions through two attached offices, si x subordinate offices and thirty five 
autonomous organizations which are fully funded by the Government. There are seven Zonal 
Cultural Centres for promoting folk and traditional  arts of different regions of the country. There 
are also four National Missions namely National Mis sion for Manuscripts, National Mission for 
Monuments and Antiquities, National Mission on Libraries and Gandhi Heritage Sites Mission. 
2. The Ministry is responsible for the protection, development and promotion of both 
tangible and intangible heritage of culture and kno wledge heritage. In addition, the Ministry is 
mandated to preserve Gandhian heritage and for comm emoration of important historical events 
and centenaries of great personalities. With regard to tangible heritage, the Ministry takes care of 
all the Centrally protected monuments of national i mportance, which is achieved through 
Archeological Survey of India. Similarly, the Ministry is also promoting a museum movement in 
the country and majority of the museums are under i ts administrative control. The Ministry also 
promotes regional museums through grant-in-aids. In  the field of intangible heritage, the 
Ministry extends financial support to individuals, group of individuals and cultural organizations 
engaged in performing visual and literary arts. Sim ilarly, the Ministry through its organizations, 
is engaged in recognizing excellence in the field o f art and culture by way of awards given by 
institutions like Sahitya Akademi, Lalit Kala Akade mi and Sangeet Natak Akademi. The 
National School of Drama is involved in promoting a  vibrant theatre movement of contemporary 
relevance in the context of our traditions and cultural diversity. 
3. In knowledge heritage, the Ministry is the custo dian of all the major libraries in the 
country. It also extends grant-in-aid for library d evelopment and is also responsible for all policy 
matters regarding library development. Through Nati onal Archives of India, Ministry is 
responsible for maintenance of all archival records  of the country. The Ministry is also involved 
in protection and promotion of Buddhist and Tibetan  Culture. The Ministry has a well-planned 
capacity-building programme for all those who want to excel in the field of Indian as well as 
1

 
Asian art and culture. The Ministry is also respons ible for implementation of various UNESCO 
conventions in the field of culture.

Audit Paras 
20. The Committee notes that despite two extensions  given by the Greater Noida Authority, 
ASI failed to construct the Institute of Archaeolog y on the land acquired by it in March 2000. 
This led to cancellation of allotment of the plot b y the Authority in November, 2012 and 
6

 
imposition of penalty.  The lackadaisical approach of ASI also led to avoidable payment of Rs. 
2.61 crore and blocking of funds of Rs. 3 crore besides non-achievement of project objectives.  
21. The Committee could understand that Ministry of  Culture delayed, for more than a 
decade, the approval of construction agency for the  National Museum Institute Building, 
resulting in escalation of costs by Rs. 75.40 crore  and payment of penalty of Rs. 52.80 lakh to 
NOIDA authority. 
22. The Committee recommends that an Inquiry Commit tee may be constituted to look 
into the facts and circumstances which led to the d elay in construction of the Institute of 
Archaeology. Based on the findings, action may be taken against those officials found guilty 
and a report in this regard may be submitted to the  Committee. The Committee strongly 
recommends that effective and periodic monitoring o f the various Organizations under the 
Ministry should be done in order to avoid such delays.  
Archaeological Survey of India 
23. The Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) was es tablished in 1861. It functions as an 
attached office of the Ministry of Culture. ASI is engaged in protection, preservation and 
conservation of 3686 protected monuments of nationa l importance including 21 World heritage 
Monuments and 44 site museums spread all over India which are directly under the ASI.  
24. The Ministry, in its written reply, has stated that while intimating ceilings of BE 2017-18, 
Ministry of Finance has allocated an amount of Rs. 924.37 crore in favour of Archaeological 
Survey of India. Out of this, Rs. 200.00 crore has been exclusively allocated for boundary walls 
of ASI protected monuments. The allocated amount of  Rs. 924.37 crore consists of Rs. 42.00 
crore under Capital Head for Building Projects and Rs. 882.37 crore under Revenue Head. The 
allocation for 2017-18 includes all items of expend iture which were earlier classified as Plan and 
Non-Plan. 
25. The Committee was informed that ASI has been al located an amount of Rs. 924.37 crore 
for the year 2017-18 which includes Rs. 200 crore f or construction of boundary walls for ASI 
protected monuments.  
26. The Secretary, Ministry of Culture while deposi ng before the Committee stated that time 
limit that has been fixed for constructon of bounda ry walls for ASI protected monuments is six 
7

 
months from the date of award of the tender and tha t the tender has already been floated. If the 
work is not completed within six months, there is a provision for penalty.  
27. The Committee welcomes the initiative taken to construct boundary walls for ASI 
protected monuments. The Committee hopes that this will go a long way in preventing 
encroachment of monuments. The Committee desires th at a roadmap may be drawn for 
the construction of boundary walls for all the ASI protected monuments and that existing 
timelines may be strictly adhered to. The Committee  may be informed of the progress 
made in the matter. 
28. As regards the non-implementation of e-Governance i n ASI, the Secretary apprised the 
Committee that mindset change and management of change are the issues in this regard.  
29. The Committee is unhappy to note the non-implem entation of e-Governance in ASI. 
The Committee is of the view that in this era of ra pid advancement in technology, it is 
essential that e-Governance  is adopted in a vital Organization like ASI. The Committee 
recommends that the Ministry should take proactive steps to ensure that e-Governance is 
implemented in ASI. The Committee may be apprised of the action taken in this regard.   
30. The Committee finds that e-ticketing has not be en introduced in all ASI protected 
monuments. 
31. The Committee observes that e-ticketing enables  the visitors to plan  their visit to the 
monuments in advance and help them to avoid the has sle of waiting at the ticket counters. 
Therefore, the Committee desires that the facility of e-ticketing may be introduced in all 
ASI protected monuments. 
32. The Ministry, in its written reply, has stated that Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) 
has implemented ‘Swachh Bharat Abhiyan’ at all Centrally protected monuments so that they are 
maintained in a good state of preservation and clea nliness. A Swachh Prayatan Mobile App is 
developed by the Department of Tourism, Government of India for 100 Adarsh Smarak on which 
public/visitors can upload their complaints through photographs along with the monument which 
are redressed by the concerned field officer. 
33. The Committee appreciates the initiative taken by the Department and hopes that 
the App would not only ensure greater accountabilit y of the concerned agencies but also 
ensure a clean environment around the Centrally protected monuments.  
34. The Ministry has been informed that Archaeologi cal Survey of India has identified 100 
monuments as Adarsh Monuments for providing tourist  amenities and other facilities like toilets, 
8

 
drinking water, wi-fi services, cafeteria, interpre tation centre, etc.  However, such facilities 
would be provided in a phased manner.  
35. The Committee welcomes the initiatives taken un der Adarsh Smarak and desires 
that such improved facilities may be provided in al l the ASI monuments in a phased 
manner.

Encroachment of Monuments 
38. The Ministry, in its written reply, has stated that nine and twenty-eight Demolition 
Orders have been issued by the Archaeological Surve y of India so far for the removal of 
unauthorized construction within the prohibited/reg ulated areas of Zaffar Mahal and Qutub 
complex respectively. 
39. The Committee desires that ASI should ensure th at the demolition orders are 
executed timely and that the encroachments are removed. 
40. In response to a pointed query as to whether le gal opinion has been received from the 
Ministry of Law on the powers of ASI for demolition  with regard to action on District 
Authorities in case of laxity or non-co-operation o n their part in executing the demolition order, 
the Ministry has replied that legal opinion is being sought. 
41. The Committee recommends that this process of s eeking legal opinion may be 
expedited so that further course of action can be taken with regard to District Authorities. 
42. The Committee emphasizes the need for greater c o-ordination between ASI and the 
State Governments in the matter of prevention and r emoval of encroachments at the 
monuments. The Committee impresses upon the Ministr y to persuade the State 
Governments to form State level Co-ordination Commi ttees for prevention and removal of 
encroachments at the monuments. The Committee also recommends that a separate wing 
may be created within the Ministry of Culture and A SI to oversee the removal of 
encroachments in an effective way. 
9

 
43. In response to a query by the Committee regardi ng the number of encroachments 
removed during the last five years, the Committee w as informed  that during the last five 
years, 50 number of encroachment has been removed from Asa fi Imambara, Lucknow under the 
jurisdiction of  Lucknow Circle.  In case of monuments under Raipur Circle, 16 encro achments 
have been removed.  Few part of encroachment from G rihaneshwar Mandir, Ellora have been 
removed under Aurangabad Circle. However, informati on from other field offices of ASI is 
being collected. 
44. The Committee notes that the Ministry and ASI, New Delhi has stock of information 
on removal of encroachment of only three circles. T his shows the lackadaisical attitude of 
the Ministry and ASI towards removal of encroachmen ts. The Committee is aware of the 
fact that a large number of monuments are encroache d and many of them are with active 
connivance of anti-social elements. The Committee, therefore, recommends that they 
should obtain quarterly report of the progress made  from each of the circles regarding 
removal of encroachments. The Committee may be appr ised about the status of removal of 
encroachment from all the circles of ASI in the Action taken Report.

Committee desires that the proposals may be approve d in a time bound manner so that the 
Ministry of Finance may be requested to provide the requisite funds in time.
