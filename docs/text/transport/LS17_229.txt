RECOMMENDATIONS/OBSERVATIONS OF THE COMMITTEE 
5.  The Committee notes that even though Planning Commi ssion had made a very 
ambitious plan for tourism promotion in the country  during the 12 
th  plan Period by making  
an outlay of Rs. 15190 crore, the sanctions made du ring the first three years of the Twelfth  
Plan period was  Rs 2930.00 crore only, which is ju st 19.28% of the  total outlay. If the 
actual spending is taken into consideration, it sta nds at Rs.2566.48 crore which is 16.89% of 
the 12 th  Plan outlay.  The Committee finds that expenditure and allocation have not been upto 
the mark.  It is a matter of concern as the targets  also could not have been achieved due to 
less spending. The Committee, therefore, is constra ined to observe that such a pattern does 

not augur well for Tourism, as it has highest poten tial for employment as well as revenue 
generation for our economy.               
6. The Government does not make adequate allocation  for this Sector. The Committee is 
afraid that if such a trend continues, the country may not be able to sustain the current tourist 
inflow, not to speak of increasing it upto the desi red level.  It, therefore, may result in the 
decline of not only the tourism industry as a whole  but also so many other sectors associated 
with it.            
      (Recommendation at Para No.9 & 10 of the 222 
nd  Report)

8. Ministry of Tourism recognizes the fact that the  tourism sector has the potential for 
employment and revenue generation for our country. Hence it is the endeavour of Ministry of 
Tourism to help the States for development of world  class tourist infrastructure. In view of 
this, Government of India announced two new schemes  in Budget 2014 namely Swadesh 
Darshan and PRASAD.  Under Swadesh Darshan the aim is holistic development of theme 
based integrated tourist circuits in the country wh ile under PRASAD the objective is to 
augment infrastructural facilities and beautify the  pilgrimage centers of the country so that 
tourists can visit these places more comfortably an d their experience remain memorable. 
Ministry of Tourism regularly requests Ministry of Finance for adequate Budget allocation so 
that the various Schemes for promotion of tourism c an be implemented more 
comprehensively. However Ministry of Finance during  the current financial year (2015-16) 
has given additional amount of Rs. 100 crore under Swadesh Darshan Scheme which is a 
new Scheme for integrated development of theme base d tourist circuits launched during 
2014-15 pursuant to the Budget 2014-15 announcement s. The Ministry of Tourism is taking 
all necessary steps to sustain the current tourist inflow and adequate growth in the 
forthcoming years through its various promotional e fforts and utilize the allocated funds 
under various Schemes.

10. The Committee notes the reply. The Committee, howe ver, is not satisfied with

abatement would be 5.6% and net increase would be 0 .66%, which is again a very nominal 
increase.
