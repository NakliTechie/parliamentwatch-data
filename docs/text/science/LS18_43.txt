INTRODUCTION
I, the Chairman of the Committee on Science and Technology, Environment and Forests, having been authorised by the Committee
 to submit the report on its behalf, present this Forty-Third Report, on the Action Taken by the Department of Biotechnology on the
 recommendations contained in the Thirty-Fourth Report of the Committee.
2. The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Biotechnology on the recommendations
 contained in the Thirty-Fourth Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science and Technology,
 Envi­ronment and Forests on the Demands for Grants (1996-97) of the Department of Biotechnology which was presented to the
 Rajya Sabha on 27th August, 1996.

 recommendations of the Committee, were received from the Department on 21st January, 1997.

REPORT
The Report of the Committee deals with the Action Taken by the Department of Biotechnology on the recommendations contained
 in the Thirty-Fourth Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science and Technology, Environment
 and Forests on the Demands for Grants (1996-97) of the Department of Biotechnology which was presented to the Rajya Sabha on 27th
 August, 1996. There are 14 recommendations contained in the Thirty-Fourth Report of the Committee. Action Taken notes on the
 Recommendations of the Committee, were' received from the Department on
21st January, 1997.                                                                                             
On scrutiny of the Action Taken Report as received from the Department of Biotechnology, the Committee finds that 11
 recommendations have been fully implemented. The Committee is not satisfied with the action take n by the Ministry in regard to its
 recommendation Nos. 17 & 21 and in case of one reply, the Department has been asked to take expeditious action.
The Committee's recommendation, action taken thereon and comments of the Committee on the action taken by the Department of
 Biotechnology are given in the succeeding paragraphs:-
RECOMMENDATIONS:
The Committee was appraised that Biotechnology based programmes have also been initiated for upliftment of rural women and
 for providing opportunities to women scientists and steps have been taken for development of appropriate technologies and their
 transfer and, demonstrations for providing income and employment generation in rural areas. The technologies developed by DBT
 which are ready for transfer for the benefit of women include Biofertilizers, Biopesticides, Sericulture, Plant tissue culture,
 Vermicomposting, Spirulina production, Mushroom cultivation, Medicinal and aromatic plants and Aquaculture, poultry and livestock.
(para 17)
Action Taken by the Department
The Department has further initiated action to augment the programmes for upliftment of Rural women and for providing
 opportunities to women scientists during the 9th plan period. The issue has been discussed in detail by the Working Group for the
 formulation of 9th plan. A separate budget head for special programmes for women has been proposed in the plan document. The
 technologies developed for the Plant tissue culture especially for a number of forest trees, horticulture and plantation crops would
 directly benefit the women further in terms of employment opportunities and income generation. Demonstration trials at the farmers'
 field are already been conducted in which the participation of women is further encouraged. Similarly, there involvement of rural
 women 
in the production and use or biopesticides, vermicomposting, mushrooms, spirulina, aquaculture, poultry and livestock and
 sericulture products.

COMMENTS
The Committee is not satisfied with the Ministry's  reply as no detail of the programmes have been given. Nor has it been indicated
 as to where these programmes are being conducted or are proposed to be conducted and the number of women who have benefited
 from the programmes. It is also not clear how the women entrepreneurs market their products and solve their problems in this regard.
 The Committee would like to have a comprehensive status note on the subject.
RECOMMENDATION
The Committee recommends that efforts should be made to open at least one centre during the year 1996-97 in each state/union
 territory. The DBT should make out a case for allocation of more funds under major head 3425 and sub-head 119.13 and efforts 
be
 made for transfer of technology for both BGA and Rhizobium.
(Para 21)
Action Taken by the Department
The technology development and demonstration programme in Biofertilizers was started in the year 1990 which was subsequently
 converted' into Mission Mode Programme. Technologies for the production of Rhizobial and algal Biofertilizers are in the process of
 transfer for commercial production. Steps have been taken to locate the production centres all over the country and first two centres
 have been established in the state of Madhya Pradesh. The centres located in Jammu &  Kashmir, Punjab, Delhi, West Bengal, Uttar
 Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra where technology development programme is in progress are also producing Biofertilizers for
 demonstration and awareness building programme. In addition, these centres are selling the produce at very nominal rate  to the farmers
 of the state and adjoining areas.
COMMENTS
The Committee is not satisfied with the slow pace of the progress in the transfer of technology for commercial production of
 Rhizobial and Algal biofertilizers. The Department should clearly indicate the time schedule by which the production centres would be
 established and commercial production start.

RECOMMENDATION:
The Committee satisfied with the efforts made by the DBT for initiatives taken to promote research and development programmes,
 demonstration activities, technology transfer, patenting of inventions and conservation of natural resources, especially genetic diversity
 using biotechnology tools. The Committee notes that with the involvement of the state level organisations initiatives have been taken to
 promote this field in the States of Haryana, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh and Orissa. A beginning should therefore be made to involve all
 States and Union Territories and direct linkage be established with the socio­economic ministries and departments and with other
 organisations involved in scientific work and joint proposal be mooted. The Committee recommends that DBT should identify the rural
 areas in each State/Union Territory for the purpose. The Panchayats should be involved to make the projects result oriented.
(Para-25)
Action taken by the Department
The Department has already initiated a programme for the demonstration of biotechnologies like Biofertilizers, Biopesticides,
 Sericulture, Aquaculture, Mushroom Cultivation, Spirulina Production, Vermicomposting, Cultivation of Medicinal and Aromatic
 Plants etc. in the rural areas for the upliftment of SCIST population and weaker sections. Various projects under this programme are
 being implemented with the help of Science/Medical/Agricultural/Colleges/Agricultural UniversitieS/1ITslVoluntary
 Organisations/NGOs with the direct involvement of target population. These projects are at different stares of implementation and
 hence its impact can be assessed only after the completion of the projects, approximately by 
1999-2000. Under this programme an
 Expert Committee has been constituted for generation of new projects, identification of new technologies and the implementing
 agencies and to ensure successful implementation of the projects with regular monitoring. The Deptt. has prepared a 10 year profile
 starting from the financial year 1997-98. Under this programme it has been proposed to undertake the following activities, in addition to
 the already identified ones, in different parts of the country: (i) Biological software for substainable agriculture covering biofertilizers,
 bio-control agents, vermicomposting, organic manure, micro nutrient improvement etc. (ii) Bio-resource survey with the involvement
 of local people specifically in the conservation of biological resources which are of economic importance to the nation. (iii) Training
 for the use of agro-technology packages for biomass production as an economic activity. (iv) Establishment of biotechnology
 demonstration, application and training centres for target population in different regions of the country where there is a larger
 concentration of the target population. At least 
10 such centres would be established in West Bengal, Orissa. North­ Eastern Hills,
 Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh in the Agriculture Universities/Krishi Vigyan Kendras/existing NFOs who
 are involved in such type of programmes. The large scale demonstration trials of Tissue Culture raised plants are being conducted at
 various locations in different states. The plantlets are raised by two Tissue Culture Pilot Plant facilities set up by Deptt. of
 Biotechnology. The State Forest Deptt./AgenciesIVoluntary Organisations are conducting these trials. The Ministry of Environment
 and  Forest has been associated and provides assistance to the State Forest Departments. The field trials of tissue culture raised
 plantation crops are being conducted in collaboration with Spices Board, Coffee Board and related user Agencies/Ministries. The
 Deptt. has also supported hardening facilities in different agroclimatic regions in collaboration with various State S&T councils who
 coordinate the field level activities in the States of West Bengal, Madhya Pradesh and Arunachal Pradesh. Efforts are being made
 through the assistance of Biotech Consortium India Ltd. (BCIL) to transfer Biopesticides technology to the Industries. So far 5
 industries have shown interest in the product.

RECOMMENDATION:
According to the "Background Note" the sanctioned staff strength of DBT as on 01.03.1996 is 251. The note is however
 silent on the vacancies as on date. The Committee reiterates its earlier recommendation made in its 21st report regarding the
 appointments and adequate promotion avenues for the scientists. The Committee further recommends that a total Cadre
 Review including the scientists' feeder cadre be taken up at the earliest and an independent Cadre Controlling Cell be set up.
 The Committee observes that DBT should have its own Recruitment Rules, for Scientists and Support Staff.
(Para 38)
Action taken by the Department
The Department has 44 vacant posts as on date out of these five positions fall in group 'A' and none in group 'B'
 (Gazetted). Efforts are being made to fill up these posts. As regards promotional avenues of Scientists are concerned, they are
 covered under the Flexible Compementing Scheme, according to which they are eligible for promotion after every five years
 of completion of satisfactory service upto the post of Advisor in the scale of Rs. 5900­7300. Nineteen Scientific Officers have
 got promotion after January, 1995 and position of promotion is up-to-date as on date. The recruitment rules for all the
 scientific posts have since been notified and some amendments to bring these rules at par with the Recruitment rules of
 Department of Science and Technology are under active consideration in consultation with Department of Personnel and
 Training and Union Public Service Commission. The Department itself is the Cadre Controlling Authority in respect of
 Scientific posts and is following the norms laid down in the Recruitment Rules which were notified in April, 1989. The non-
scientific posts are covered under the Central Staffing Scheme/Central Sectt./Central Sectt. Clerical Service for which the
 Cadre Controlling authority is Department of Personnel and Training.
