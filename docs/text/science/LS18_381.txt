REPORT 
 
 
The Action Taken Report (ATR) of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee 
on Science and Technology, Environment, Forests and Climate Change deals with the action taken by  
the Government  on the recommendations/observations of the Committee contained in its ‘Three 
Hundred Seventy Fourth Report’ on ‘Demands for Grants (2023 -24) of the Department of 
Biotechnology’. The Report was presented to Rajya Sabha on the 15 th March, 2023 and laid on the 
Table of Lok Sabha on the 20th March, 2023.  
2. Action Taken Note s (ATNs) ha ve been received from the Department of Biotechnology  in 
respect of all the 21 recommendations/observations contained in the ‘Three Hundred Seventy Fourth 
Report’. The replies of the Department of Biotechnology have been categorized as follows:-  
Chapter I: Recommendations/Observations which have been accepted by the Government: 
Para Nos. 2.15, 3.5, 3.6, 4.9, 4.10, 5.6 and 6.2. 
 Total recommendations – 07  
Chapter II:    Recommendations/Observations which the Committee does not desire to pursue 
in view of the Government’s replies: 
Para Nos. 2.4, 2.9, 2.19, 3.4, 4.3, 4.4, 4.8, 5.7, 6.6, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 7.4 and 7.5. 
 Total recommendations – 14   
Chapter III: Recommendations/Observations in respect of which replies of the Government 
have not been accepted by the Committee: ‘Nil’ 
Chapter IV:   Recommendations/Observations in respect of which final replies of the 
Government are still awaited: ‘Nil’ 
 
3. The details of the ATNs have been analyzed in the succeeding Chapters.

Recommendations/Observations 
 
1.1 The Commi ttee recognises the important contributions of the Department of Biotechnology 
across crucial sectors and the need for an additional allocation of at least Rs. 350 crore, over and 
above to the allocation of Rs. 2683.86 crore in B.E. 2023 -24, to sustain its  key programmes. The

Finance/Department of Expenditure for seeking the requisite additional allocation of funds at the R.E.

the pace of expenditure may request for an additional allocation at the R.E. stage.

Sector Undertaking Biotechnology Industry Res earch Assistance Council (BIRAC) provides equity 
based funding to startups.  BIRAC has supported more than 140 startups through equity support under 
SEED and LEAP schemes. Out of these startups, more than 60% have raised follow on funding 
amounting to more  than INR 1000 Cr. There is an increasing number of startups getting investments 
from Angels, High Net Worth Individuals (HNIs), and early -stage Venture Capitals (VCs) contributing 
to the growth story of biotech industry.

HUMAN GENETICS & GENOME ANALYSIS 
 
Recommendations/Observations 
 
 
2.31 The Committee recognizes the immense potential that genetic research and gene therapy hold 
for altering the treatment paradigm of many genetic diseases, including those that are currently 
incurable. However, as it is a relatively new field of medical science, the many challenges still 
associated with it are not lost on the Committee. Despite the existing challenges, such as issues 
regarding delivery methods, safety and effectiveness concerns, potential long -term effects of genetic 
modification, high costs of developing and administering the therapy making it inaccessible to masses, 
etc., the potential benefits are far too significant to ignore and, therefore, it is highly essential to 
prioritize research in the area.                (Para 7.4 ibid) 
                 
Action Taken 
 
2.32 The Department has been facilitating research in this domain to make cell and gene therapy 
affordable. Since past one decade, the Department supported gene therapy programme, which led to the 
first-in-human gene therapy phase-I clinical trial for Hemophilia A patients.  Proof -of-concept studies 
have also been completed for development of novel lentiviral vectors which can be employed for gene 
addition (beta or gamma globin genes); gene modulation (BCL11A gene); and gene editin g approaches 
using CRISPR-Cas9 and base editing technologies. 
 
Recommendations/Observations 
 
2.33 The Committee appreciates the ongoing efforts of scientists and researchers in this field. The 
Committee recommends the Department to undertake deeper researc h in collaboration with other 
institutes under the government and private sector, explore avenues for using latest emerging 
technologies including Artificial Intelligence (AI), in order to fully explore the untapped potential of 
genetic research. The Commi ttee also recommends that adequate funds be earmarked for the purpose, 
so that financial constraints do not hinder the much-needed R&D. The Committee would also like to be
